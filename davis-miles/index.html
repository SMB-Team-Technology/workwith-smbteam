Here Is Where Davis Miles PLLC Stands Today

Davis Miles PLLC is a multi-office Arizona firm with locations in Tempe, Mesa, and Glendale, and its Arizona family law practice is projected to reach $785,161 in revenue this year against a stated goal of $1,000,000. The firm already invests roughly $20,000 a month in Google Ads plus Meta ads, yet leads have declined since March 2026 and fewer than half of free consultations are being kept — which tells us the next gain comes from better execution and a tighter funnel, not just more budget.

Stage 6 is where the $1,000,000 Arizona family law goal stops depending on constant firefighting with vendors and starts coming from systems that perform predictably — and it is exactly where we are taking you.

Where You Are Today

Stage 4: Small Business Manager
  • Multiple paid channels are running today — Google Ads, Local Service Ads, and Meta — but lead volume has been declining since March 2026
  • Fewer than half of free 15-minute consultations are kept, driven by unqualified “shopper” leads and the attorney approach on the call
  • Lawmatics lead data is not connected to the case management software, so the true return on roughly $20,000 a month in ad spend cannot be tracked accurately
  • Revenue is projected at $785,161 against a $1,000,000 target — a gap of about $215,000 to close

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between $785,161 and $1,000,000 is closable without starting over — it takes a lead engine that performs, a consultation process that converts, and numbers you can actually trust.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Davis Miles PLLC
Davis Miles PLLC is operating at Stage 4: it has real lead generation in market and a leadership layer that includes a COO, but the systems behind those channels are not yet converting at the level the $1,000,000 goal requires. The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The homepage headline — “Welcome to the Only Law Firm You Will Ever Need” — speaks to every practice area at once and says nothing about family law or the Tempe, Mesa, and Glendale markets the firm is targeting

The Positive

The Gap
  • Every extra click between a visitor and a consultation request costs signed cases, and roughly $20,000 a month in ad spend is sending traffic to this site
  • Without video, prospects in a stressful family law situation have little chance to see and hear the attorneys before they call
  • Without city-specific pages, the firm is not competing for neighborhood-level searches that Edwards & Petersen already targets in Mesa and Chandler
A website that converts as hard as your ad budget works is one of the fastest ways to close the $215,000 gap between $785,161 and $1,000,000.

Intake Assessment

Davis Miles relies on free 15-minute consultations as its conversion step, and fewer than half of them are kept — a major leak between a paid lead and a signed retainer. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • Fewer than half of the free 15-minute consultations are kept, so most booked time never turns into a conversation with an attorney
  • Unqualified “shopper” leads are reaching the consultation stage, which uses attorney time on people who were never likely to retain the firm
  • The attorney approach on consultations was named as a contributing factor, so results depend on how each conversation is handled
  • Lawmatics is not connected to the case management software, so you cannot trace a lead through to a signed case or measure true return on ad spend
Blind Spot: When unqualified leads show up as booked consultations, it can mean qualification is happening too late — after the attorney has already invested time — rather than before the calendar slot is offered.
The Positive
  • Lawmatics is already in place as a lead and intake platform, so the foundation for structured follow-up exists
  • The firm knows its own numbers: a 20% consultation-to-retainer conversion rate and a $5,000 average retainer, which makes the cost of every missed consultation measurable
The Gap
  • At a 20% conversion rate and a $5,000 average retainer, every consultation that is kept represents roughly $1,000 in expected revenue — and more than half are being lost before they start
  • Without lead-to-case tracking, there is no way to tell which channels produce signed clients and which only produce shoppers
  • Roughly $20,000 a month in ad spend is feeding a funnel that loses more than half its consultations at the last step
Tightening intake is one of the fastest, lowest-cost ways to move from $785,161 toward the $1,000,000 goal.
