Lead Generation

Davis Miles PLLC already has paid channels running in every major lane — Google Ads at roughly $20,000 a month, Local Service Ads, and Meta — yet leads have fallen since March 2026 and the firm is not showing up organically in its own Tempe, Mesa, and Chandler markets. The question is not whether to advertise; it is whether the current setup is built to deliver the 16 to 17 signed cases a month that a $1,000,000 Arizona family law goal at a $5,000 average retainer requires.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The homepage headline — “Welcome to the Only Law Firm You Will Ever Need” — speaks to every practice area at once and says nothing about family law or the Tempe, Mesa, and Glendale markets the firm is targeting
  • Mobile and desktop PageSpeed scores were unavailable when this report was prepared and should be re-tested live before website recommendations are finalized
  • There is no contact form on the homepage — visitors must click through to a separate Contact page to reach the firm
  • No video was observed on the homepage, and no dedicated city pages exist for Chandler, Gilbert, Scottsdale, or Peoria
The Positive
  • A clear primary “Schedule My Consultation” button sits above the fold, and attorney profiles are prominent through “Our Attorneys Make Us Great” and an Attorney Spotlight feature
  • An active blog, “Lawyer Speak,” most recently featuring the firm's attorneys being recognized in The Best Lawyers in America 2027
The Gap
  • Every extra click between a visitor and a consultation request costs signed cases, and roughly $20,000 a month in ad spend is sending traffic to this site
  • Without video, prospects in a stressful family law situation have little chance to see and hear the attorneys before they call
  • Without city-specific pages, the firm is not competing for neighborhood-level searches that Edwards & Petersen already targets in Mesa and Chandler
A website that converts as hard as your ad budget works is one of the fastest ways to close the $215,000 gap between $785,161 and $1,000,000.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The firm did not appear in top visible results for “divorce attorney Tempe AZ,” “child custody lawyer Mesa AZ,” or “family law attorney Chandler AZ” — the three markets closest to its own offices
  • The firm is listed across directories under at least three name variants — Davis Miles PLLC, Davis Miles McGuire Gardner, PLLC, and Davis Miles McGuire Gardner — which splits review signal and authority across listings
  • Yelp shows what appear to be two separate listings for the same Tempe address, one filed under Bankruptcy Law and another under Business Law, rather than Family Law
  • No firm-level Avvo or Justia profile was found, and Google Business Profile review totals and rating could not be confirmed — different sources return materially different figures
Blind Spot: Prospects and Google can see different review totals depending on which version of the firm's name they land on, so reviews earned today may not be adding up to one strong, visible profile.
The Positive
  • The firm has active profiles on FindLaw, LawInfo, Lawyers.com, and Super Lawyers (with four attorneys recognized), and holds the highest Ethics and Legal Ability rating tier on Martindale-Hubbell
The Gap
  • Prospects searching in Tempe, Mesa, and Chandler are finding Hildebrand Law, Edwards & Petersen, Blake & Pulsifer, and Arizona Law Group before they find Davis Miles
  • Fragmented listings and missing city pages mean the firm's real credibility is not translating into local visibility
Edwards & Petersen, PLC — a family-law-only firm with dedicated Mesa and Chandler city landing pages, surfacing in both of those searches where Davis Miles did not.
Showing up first in Tempe, Mesa, and Chandler is how the $1,000,000 goal gets reached without paying for every single lead.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • The firm is actively running Google Ads at roughly $20,000 a month through its current agency, so the issue is performance rather than presence
  • Leads have declined since March 2026, and the firm has said its agency has stayed narrowly focused on PPC despite requests for other channels. Live ad copy could not be captured, so creative was not evaluated here
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Live competitor ad placements could not be observed in this review, so no competitor ad copy is cited
  • Arizona Law Group, Hildebrand Law, PC, Blake & Pulsifer, and Edwards & Petersen, PLC recur across the organic results for the same family law searches, so they are competing for the same prospects the firm's ads are targeting
The Opportunity
  • City-specific family law terms — divorce attorney, child custody lawyer, and family law attorney — for Tempe, Mesa, Glendale, and Chandler
  • Case-type terms such as child custody modification, legal separation, and spousal support paired with each office city
  • Neighboring cities with no dedicated coverage today — Gilbert, Scottsdale, and Peoria — where competitors such as Arizona Law Group already have offices
  • Competitor-name and urgency searches, such as emergency custody and order of protection, that capture prospects who are ready to retain counsel now
Blind Spot: Steady spend with falling leads, plus consultation no-shows from unqualified “shoppers,” can point to campaign structure, keyword targeting, and ad copy — not just intake — so budget alone will not fix it.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • The firm has an active Local Service Ads account managed by its current agency, which the firm described as ineffective account management
  • LSA placements cannot be viewed through available research tools, so Google Screened status and LSA review count were not observed and are not assumed
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • No named local firm could be confirmed holding LSA positions for these searches with available tools, so no competitor LSA figures are cited
The Opportunity
  • The account already exists, so the opportunity is performance — faster response, tighter lead qualification, and steady review growth to rank higher
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Leads that do not match the firm's case types can often be disputed for credit, which matters when shopper calls are part of the problem

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • The firm runs Meta ads at about $75 a day today, with a plan to increase to $150 a day
  • The Meta Ad Library could not be accessed in this review, so creative, formats, and run time were not evaluated
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Arizona Law Group and Edwards & Petersen, PLC — active Meta ads were not observed because Ad Library access was unavailable, so this should be verified live
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Doubling spend to $150 a day works best behind a defined retargeting and lead-qualification structure, so added budget produces consultations that show up rather than more shoppers
