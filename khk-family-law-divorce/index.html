Here Is Where KHK Family Law & Divorce Attorneys Stands Today

KHK Family Law & Divorce Attorneys is a Chicago-area family law firm, formed through the merger of the Kulek and Katz practices, with seven attorneys and a support staff of nine serving clients from Chicago and Lincolnwood. Revenue is projected to grow from $700K in 2025 to more than $2M in 2026, yet the firm is signing about 20 cases a month against a goal of 40, at roughly 15% net profit. The most important finding: KHK has the credentials and the team to win high-value divorce and custody cases across Cook, Lake, and DuPage counties, but its marketing is not built to target those cases and its financials are not yet steering its decisions.

Stage 6 is where Matthew steps into the CEO role with a trusted equity partner running the firm day to day, free to put his energy into his mission to combat attorney burnout — and it is exactly where we are taking you.

  • About 20 signed cases a month today against a goal of 40, on $5K–$10K a month of marketing

  • Revenue is on pace to grow from $700K to more than $2M, but net profit is only about 15% (roughly $300K)
  • Matthew is still leading the firm while preparing to move into a CEO role and bring his top associate in as a 50% equity partner within three years

  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 26/100 (Lighthouse lab test) — the main content takes 19.7 seconds to load and the first content appears at 8.7 seconds
  • The contact form sits at the bottom of the homepage, so a visitor has to scroll the entire page to reach it; the "Get A Free Evaluation" button sends visitors to a separate contact page
  • County-level pages for Lake and DuPage counties were not confirmed, even though the firm is targeting both
Blind Spot: A staging copy of the website appears to be indexed by Google. Duplicate versions of a site can split ranking signals and put an unfinished copy in front of prospects searching for a divorce attorney.
The Positive
  • Individual profile pages exist for all seven attorneys, and a Spanish-language toggle and "Se Habla Español" are shown site-wide
  • Nine practice-area pages (including high-net-worth, LGBTQ+, gray, military, and collaborative divorce), neighborhood landing pages, and a blog with posts dated this month give search engines plenty to work with
The Gap
  • A slow mobile experience means prospects searching for a divorce attorney from their phone are likely to hit "back" and call the next firm on the results page
  • Slow load times can also make every Google Ads and Local Service Ads click less valuable, because paid traffic lands on the same slow pages
  • With the form buried at the bottom, high-intent visitors have to work to reach the firm
A website that converts the traffic KHK already earns is the first step toward 40 signed cases a month — and toward a firm that grows without Matthew carrying every piece of it.
