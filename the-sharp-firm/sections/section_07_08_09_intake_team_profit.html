Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • No CRM or lead-tracking software exists — leads are prioritized manually rather than tracked systematically
  • Lance personally decides callback order (10 minutes for auto/truck calls, batched for med/dental mal) — every lead depends on his personal bandwidth
  • No defined follow-up sequence exists for leads who don't sign on the first call
  • No email or text nurture program exists for leads who aren't ready to commit immediately
Blind Spot: You're personally triaging 5-10 med/dental mal calls a day to reject 99% of them — that's real time spent every single day on cases you've already decided you don't want, time that isn't going toward the truck and auto cases you do want.
The Gap

  • No nurture sequence means leads who aren't ready today simply disappear instead of converting later
You told Dan you want a done-for-you solution — intake is the first place that promise has to show up, because right now it still runs through you.
