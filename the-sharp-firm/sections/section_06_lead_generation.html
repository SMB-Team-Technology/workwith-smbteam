Website Audit

The Problem
  • The site footer still credits "Digital Marketing By Rize Up Media" even though you told Dan you are no longer working with that vendor — suggesting the transition to your new hosting/web vendor is not yet complete
  • PageSpeed mobile and desktop scores could not be verified during this research pass (tooling limitations) — recommend a live scan before kickoff to confirm real numbers
  • The main contact form is buried in the footer under "Start Your Case Today" rather than visible above the fold on the homepage
  • No live chat widget exists anywhere on the site, including the reviews/testimonials page
Blind Spot: With no PPC currently running, any branded searches for "The Sharp Firm" or "Lance Sharp" are wide open — a competitor bidding on your own name would face no resistance right now.
The Positive
  • Site shows clear responsive design signals (mobile menu, modern .webp images) and dedicated attorney bio pages for both Lance and Laura Sharp
  • An active blog and an active YouTube channel already exist — raw content assets most firms your size don't have yet
The Gap
  • A buried contact form means visitors who are ready to reach out have to hunt for the way to do it, and some simply leave instead
  • No live chat means after-hours and weekend visitors — exactly when accident victims are searching — have no way to engage in the moment
  • An unclear vendor transition (old agency's name still on the site) risks looking neglected to a prospect doing their own research on you before calling
A website that quietly loses visitors at the exact moment they're ready to call is one more reason you're stuck fielding the wrong cases instead of the ones you actually want.

  • With a 4.9-star, 97-review Google profile already in place, the firm already meets the reputation bar LSA approval typically looks for
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Specific firms holding LSA placements for Austin car/truck accident searches could not be confirmed with available tooling this cycle — recommend a live, logged-in check before final scoping
  • Google Screened/LSA adoption is broadly common among competing Austin PI firms as a lower-cost-per-lead alternative to standard PPC — a channel The Sharp Firm has not yet entered
The Opportunity
  • LSAs are pay-per-lead, not pay-per-click — a meaningfully different cost structure than the PPC approach that burned you with Rise Up
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly
  • Your existing review count and rating already position you to qualify quickly, without needing the web rebuild or SEO investment a lower-rated firm would need first
Blind Spot: LSA doesn't require winning the PPC bidding war against Thomas J. Henry or Jim Adler at all — it's a separate placement, which means it's one of the fastest realistic paths onto page one that doesn't depend on outspending them.
