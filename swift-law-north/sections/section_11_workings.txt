AD SPEND & ROI PROJECTIONS
---------------------------
Methodology note: Swift Law North is a national, virtual practice with no local service area,
so it does not map cleanly onto any single row in the practice-area ad spend/CPL tables (all of
which assume a local map-pack market). Per the research notes' own adapted-methodology framing,
the following approach was used and should be verified with a human/paid-ads review before
finalizing spend commitments:
  - LSA is excluded entirely (requires a defined local service area SLN does not have).
  - Recommended channels: Google Search (national) + Meta Retargeting + Meta Cold/Lead Gen.
  - CPL: no "Consumer Protection / FCRA" row exists in the CPL table. Used "Business Law" as
    the closest analog (complex/high-value national case type): Google Search $100–$120
    (mid $110), Meta Retargeting $60–$80 (mid $70), Meta Cold $70–$90 (mid $80).
  - Assumed channel mix: 60% Google Search / 20% Meta Retargeting / 20% Meta Cold (search-heavy
    since LSA is unavailable).
  - Blended CPL (no cushion) = 0.6(110) + 0.2(70) + 0.2(80) = $96
  - Blended CPL (conservative, +20% cushion) = $96 x 1.2 = ~$115
  - Average case value: transcript states "wide range, from low tens of thousands to $500k"
    with no single average given, and this vertical has no listed practice-area default.
    Used a disclosed representative estimate of $25,000 (near the stated low end, not the
    $500k outlier) rather than fabricate a precise average. This should be validated by sales
    before quoting to the client.
  - Close rate: not stated on call — defaulted to 15% per standard assumption (per research
    notes STEP 9).
  - Ad spend: conservative $3,000/mo (absolute floor for any paid ads, used in place of the
    local channel-minimums sum since no local-market row applies); aggressive $12,000/mo
    (Growth tier ad spend cap — no dollar revenue goal was stated on the call to run the 20%
    rule against, only a client-volume goal, so the tier cap was used as the aggressive bound).

Conservative scenario:
