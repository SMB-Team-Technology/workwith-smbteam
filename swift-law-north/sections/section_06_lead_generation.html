Lead Generation

Swift Law North has grown to 30-40 new clients a month entirely on referrals and partnerships, without ever running a dollar of paid marketing or building any digital visibility. As a national, virtual practice with no physical office, its lead generation gap does not look like a typical local firm's — there is no map-pack ranking to lose, because there was never a local presence to begin with.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • PageSpeed mobile and desktop scores could not be captured this pass — Google's PageSpeed API returned a quota error on repeated attempts; a live-browser pass is recommended to confirm before finalizing, but the site reads as a lean, single-purpose intake page rather than a fully built-out marketing site
  • No phone number displayed anywhere on the homepage — the only contact path is the intake/survey form
  • No blog, no video, and no live chat — the site has a single attorney bio (founder Aaron Swift) and two intake CTAs above the fold, but little else to build trust before someone fills out a form
  • Footer shows a 2024 copyright, consistent with a site built quickly around SLN's national expansion rather than a mature marketing asset
Blind Spot: A separate education site, consumerlaw101.com, lists Aaron Swift as an instructor — that credibility asset exists but is not connected to or leveraged by the Swift Law North site at all.
The Positive
  • The homepage CTA structure is clear and above the fold — two distinct paths (individual victims vs. business owners) rather than one generic contact form
  • Aaron Swift's bio leads with strong, specific credibility markers — "over 15 years" and "over 1,000 lawsuits" against credit reporting agencies
The Gap
  • No phone number means every urgent, high-intent visitor is funneled into a form and a wait — a common point where anxious prospects abandon and call a competitor instead
  • With no blog, no video, and no content depth, the site has nothing to rank for beyond the homepage itself — it cannot compete for search traffic against firms that have built out dozens of pages
  • An unverified mobile experience is a real risk when the plan is to drive national paid traffic directly at this site
Every dollar spent driving national traffic to a site that cannot yet confirm its own mobile speed or offer a phone number is a dollar at risk of being wasted before it ever reaches the 100+ clients a month Swift Law North is building toward.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Swift Law North has no Google Business Profile, no address, and no service-area page for Google to anchor a local listing to — consistent with a fully national, virtual practice
  • Because there is no local market to rank in, this assessment is adapted into a national organic visibility scan across all three target practice areas — and across nine separate searches spanning credit report errors/FCRA, identity theft/fraud, and illegal debt collection/FDCPA, Swift Law North does not appear once
  • No Avvo, Justia, FindLaw, Yelp, or BBB listing exists under the "Swift Law North" brand — Aaron Swift's only Avvo presence is tied to his original St. Petersburg, FL "Swift Law" brand, not this one
The Positive
  • Because no competing "Swift Law North" listings or mismatched directory profiles exist yet, the brand is a clean slate — there is no bad data or duplicate listing to clean up before building real visibility
The Gap
  • Every one of the nine searches that should surface Swift Law North instead surfaces a competitor — meaning every one of those searchers is currently being handed to someone else
  • With zero third-party directory presence, SLN has no independent trust signals reinforcing paid traffic once it starts
Consumer Attorneys (consumerattorneys.com) — appears across all three of SLN's target practice areas in organic search, showing the kind of broad content coverage SLN has not yet built anywhere.
Reaching 500 new clients a month starts with existing somewhere a prospective client can find you — right now, in this vertical, Swift Law North does not exist in a single organic search.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Swift Law North does not appear to be running paid search ads for any of its three target practice areas — consistent with the transcript's confirmation that the firm has never run paid marketing of any kind
  • Live sponsored search results could not be rendered through available tools this pass — a live-browser confirmation is recommended before finalizing spend, though the "no prior paid marketing" statement on the call is the stronger signal here
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Consumer Attorneys — established, well-resourced national FCRA/consumer-protection firm highly likely to be running paid search across this vertical, based on its dominant organic presence in all three practice areas
  • Francis Mailman Soumilas — a long-established national FCRA specialist firm with the market position to be actively bidding on "credit report errors" and related free-case-review terms
The Opportunity
  • "credit report errors lawsuit lawyer," "identity theft attorney free consultation," and "FDCPA attorney free case review" — the exact terms competitors already own
  • "credit report lawyer free consultation" and "fraud victim lawyer credit report" — high-intent variations with the same zero-visibility gap
  • Competitor-name and brand-defense terms against Consumer Attorneys, Francis Mailman Soumilas, and Atlas Law Center, since SLN currently has no presence to protect or contest
  • Spanish-language versions of every core term — the transcript flags this as a real, less-contested advantage none of the named national competitors are clearly leaning into

Competitor LSA Landscape

Facebook & Instagram Ads

  • No social presence or content strategy was identified for the SLN brand specifically during this scan
Competitor Ad Landscape

  • Educational content ads explaining credit report rights and FCRA violations — capturing prospects before they know to search for a lawyer at all
  • Spanish-language creative across all of the above, matching the national bilingual advantage the transcript identifies as underused
