Intake Assessment

Swift Law North already delegates intake to an outsourced team based in India rather than handling it personally — a real step ahead of many firms this size — but there is no confirmation of after-hours coverage or follow-up speed, and the only stated contact path is a form, with no phone number on the homepage. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating. This is where the biggest immediate ROI lives.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • No after-hours coverage was confirmed — with no phone number on the homepage, prospects with an urgent need have no immediate path to a human
  • The intake team is entirely outsourced and overseas, with no visibility from this call into response times or follow-up sequencing
  • No live chat exists, so anyone who lands on the site outside business hours has only a form to fill out and wait
  • No CRM or intake tracking system was mentioned on the call
Blind Spot: Right now, referral-based leads likely already know and trust Swift Law North before they ever reach intake. Paid traffic will not — those leads will judge the firm entirely on how fast and how well that first response lands, and that has never been tested at volume.
The Positive
  • A dedicated intake team already exists — this is not a solo attorney fielding every call personally
  • A structured intake/survey form is already built specifically for the ID theft/fraud/credit-report path, not a generic contact form
The Gap
  • Every paid lead that goes unanswered after hours is a lead that already cost money to generate and converts to nothing
  • With no phone number, high-intent prospects in an urgent situation have no way to reach a human before deciding whether to look elsewhere
  • Without a defined follow-up sequence, leads that do not convert immediately have no system pulling them back in
If Swift Law North scales its ad spend before confirming intake can actually convert the leads it generates, the 100+ clients a month goal turns into 100+ leads a month with nothing to show for it.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • The intake/survey form for the ID theft/fraud/credit-report path is reachable from the top nav and not buried — a real strength to build on
  • No phone number is displayed anywhere on the homepage, leaving the form as the only path in for every visitor
  • No live chat exists on the site

Team Assessment

Swift Law North already has more team infrastructure than most firms this size — 4 partners, roughly 10 underwriting staff, and an outsourced intake team — but every strategic decision on the call still ran through the partner group directly. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • No dedicated ops leadership was mentioned — the 4-partner group appears to own strategy, growth decisions, and day-to-day direction together
  • ~10 underwriting staff and an outsourced intake team represent real operational capacity, but nothing on the call described a manager layer between that staff and the partners
  • The firm's confidence that it can "handle double" current volume has not been tested against a real scale-up — 30-40/month to 100+/month is a different operational reality
  • Mike, as CTO, is personally driving this growth initiative alongside the sales process — a sign the partner group is still hands-on with functions beyond casework
Blind Spot: Having 14+ people on staff can feel like "the team is handled" — but a team of that size without a dedicated ops layer just means more people for four partners to personally manage, not less.
The Positive
  • Swift Law North already has staff dedicated to underwriting and intake — most firms this size are still doing both personally
  • Four partners sharing ownership of the business is a stronger foundation for eventually stepping back than a true solo practice
The Gap
  • Without dedicated ops leadership, every new client added at scale is more direct load on the same four partners — freedom does not increase as revenue does
  • No accountability structure was described for the underwriting or intake teams beyond the partners themselves
  • Scaling to 500 clients a month with the current structure would mean scaling partner involvement right alongside it, not away from it
Dominating this market at 500 clients a month only means something if the firm can run that volume without the four of you personally holding it together.

Profit Plan Assessment

No firm-wide revenue figure — gross or net — came up anywhere on the call, and case value was described only as "a wide range, from low tens of thousands to $500k" with no average given. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: RED
"Case Value: Wide range, from low tens of thousands to $500k."
The Problem
  • No firm-wide revenue figure was discussed on the call at all — the conversation stayed entirely at the per-case level
  • Case value spans an enormous range with no average and no breakdown of which case types make up the bulk of the firm's revenue
  • No profit margin, take-home, or cost-per-acquisition figures were mentioned
  • With no prior paid marketing, the firm has never had to calculate a cost-per-lead or cost-per-case number to plan against
Blind Spot: A case value range this wide (low tens of thousands to $500k) usually means a small number of cases carry a disproportionate share of total revenue — without tracking which ones, growth effort could easily go toward the wrong case mix.
The Gap
  • Without a firm-wide revenue baseline, there is no way to measure whether new marketing spend is actually moving the needle on profit, not just client count
  • Without per-case-type profitability tracking, the firm cannot tell whether its highest-volume cases are also its most profitable ones
  • Scaling ad spend without a cost-per-case benchmark makes it difficult to know when a campaign is working versus simply generating activity
Chasing 500 clients a month without knowing which cases actually build wealth risks turning a scale-and-dominate vision into a lot more casework for the same take-home the partners have today.
