Here Is Where Swift Law North Stands Today

Swift Law North is a three-year-old, national consumer protection firm — no physical office, incorporated in D.C. — built by founder Aaron Swift on credit report errors, identity theft, and illegal debt collection cases, with case values ranging from the low tens of thousands to $500k. The firm has grown to 30-40 new clients a month purely through referrals and partnerships, has never run a single dollar of paid marketing, and is confident it could handle double that volume today. The single most important finding: SLN already has the team and capacity to scale, but it is entering a national vertical where several well-funded, specialized firms already own the exact "free case review" search intent SLN needs to reach 100+ clients a month.

Stage 6 is where Swift Law North's national reach and bilingual advantage stop being untapped potential and start being the dominant position in this market — and that is exactly where we are taking you.

Where You Are Today

Stage 4: Small Business Manager
  • Referrals and partnerships are the only source of new clients — no paid marketing has ever been run
  • A real team already exists (4 partners, ~10 underwriting staff, an outsourced intake team) but the firm has no digital footprint anywhere to anchor a search for it
  • Zero organic visibility across all three target practice areas — credit report/FCRA, identity theft/fraud, and debt collection/FDCPA
  • Confident it can handle double its current volume, but has no system in place to actually generate that volume

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between 30-40 referrals a month and the 100+ clients a month Swift Law North is aiming for is not a capacity problem — the team can already handle double the volume — it is a visibility problem, and closing it is exactly what this plan is built to do.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.
