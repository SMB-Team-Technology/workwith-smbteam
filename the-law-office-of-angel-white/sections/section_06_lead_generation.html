Lead Generation

The Law Office of Angel White has grown for six years almost entirely through referrals and pay-per-lead platforms — Legal Match and Unbundled Attorney/Access Legal — with no owned SEO, PPC, or paid social presence in Douglasville, and zero online visibility yet in the brand-new Alabama market.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • PageSpeed mobile and desktop scores could not be obtained in this research pass (Google's API returned a quota-exhaustion error) — a live pagespeed.web.dev check is recommended before finalizing scope, though the site's clean, modern layout suggests speed is not the primary drag on conversions right now
  • Above-fold conversion path is phone-call only — the contact form is buried below the fold, so anyone who'd rather type than call has no easy path forward
  • No live chat is present to capture visitors browsing after hours
  • The blog lives on a separate subdomain (blog.aswhitelaw.com) with posting cadence not confirmable in this pass — worth a live check before assuming it's active
Blind Spot: Because your CTA is phone-call only, every visitor who finds you outside business hours — or who simply prefers not to call a stranger about a will — leaves without any way to reach you at all.
The Positive
  • Clean, modern design with no legacy visual debt and a responsive, mobile-friendly layout
  • Attorney bio section featuring a Voyage ATL magazine feature and an educational video ("The basics of an estate plan") — strong trust-building assets already in place
The Gap
  • Every visitor who won't call gets no path to convert — a lead lost before it ever reaches Angel's phone
  • There is no content built for the Alabama market, so visitors there find nothing that speaks to their location
  • Without geo-targeted pages for nearby cities like Villa Rica, Carrollton, or Austell, high-intent nearby searchers land on generic content or don't find the site at all
A website that converts on its own — not just one that looks good — is what lets leads come in even when you're not standing by the phone.

The Gap
