Intake

Fixing intake means every dollar spent on lead generation converts at a higher rate — without you having to be the one who answers the phone.

Self-Managing Team

Until there's a team that can run daily operations without you, the CEO role you're working toward stays theoretical — even as revenue grows.

Profit

  • No profitability, margin, or cost-per-acquisition figures were discussed on the call

Real freedom comes from profit you can plan on, not revenue you hope holds together — and that visibility doesn't exist yet.
