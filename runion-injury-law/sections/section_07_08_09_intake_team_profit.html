Team Assessment

Derick is the firm's litigation engine and its would-be operator at the same time — a split that leaves neither job getting his full attention. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: RED
The Problem
  • No team size or leadership structure was stated on the call
  • Derick is functioning as both lead litigator and de facto operator — the same bottleneck constraining marketing execution
  • The stated goal of working remotely two weeks a month has no operational owner behind it yet
  • The firm has already engaged an outside framework ("Process Driven") to build an operational playbook, but implementation has stalled — the plan exists, the execution capacity does not
Blind Spot: Derick's own competence is part of the obstacle. Being good enough to do all of this himself has let the gap stay unaddressed longer than it would for an owner who couldn't do it all.
The Positive
  • Derick has already identified the gap himself and engaged an outside framework to build an operational playbook — most owners never get this far on their own
The Gap
  • Every month without a dedicated operator is a month the marketing and ops plan Derick already built stays on the shelf
  • Litigation will keep expanding to fill all of Derick's available time unless something structurally changes
  • Revenue growth alone will not produce free time — only a change in who is doing the operating will
Two weeks a month working remotely is not a revenue problem — it is a staffing problem, and it has one fix.

Profit Plan Assessment

Revenue, margins, and case value were not part of this conversation — the call was scoped around Derick's operational bandwidth, not the firm's financial picture. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • Annual revenue, close rate, and average case value were not stated or requested on this call
  • No profitability claims were made by the firm — there is simply no visibility into this yet from the conversation so far
  • Contingency-fee litigation practices often see uneven cash flow tied to case resolution timing — this was not addressed on the call
  • Whatever the current profit picture is, it is being managed by the same person who is also litigating every case and would need to execute any operational plan
Blind Spot: It is hard to sanity-check whether an operational hire pays for itself without a clearer view of the number that would fund it. That visibility should close before or alongside bringing in outside operational help.
The Gap
  • Without clarity on revenue and margins, it is hard to know how large an investment the firm can sustainably make in fixing its operations
  • Time spent on litigation carries a real opportunity cost that never shows up as a line item
  • A clear profit plan would tell Derick exactly how much freedom his numbers can already afford — right now, nobody knows
Freedom is a financial plan as much as it is a time plan — Derick cannot design his way out of litigation until he knows what the numbers allow him to do.
