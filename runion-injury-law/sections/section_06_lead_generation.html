Lead Generation

Runion Injury Law has real lead-generation infrastructure in place — a paid SEO vendor, genuinely localized geo-pages, and an active blog — but by the firm's own account on the discovery call, that spend is producing poor results. The firm does not surface in organic search for its core practice areas, and no paid ad presence was confirmed on any channel this audit could check.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • PageSpeed mobile and desktop scores could not be pulled this session due to an API quota limit — a live speed check is recommended before the proposal call, since it directly affects both rankings and conversion
  • No live chat is present on the site, so after-hours visitors have no way to engage before picking up the phone for a competitor
  • The site's strong bones are currently being fed by an SEO vendor the firm itself describes as producing poor results — the traffic that should be validating this build is not showing up
Blind Spot: The site is being wasted, not broken. Well-built geo-pages and an active blog only pay off when paired with distribution that gets people to them — right now the vendor responsible for that distribution is the one both underperforming and being asked to fix it.
The Positive
  • Modern, mobile-responsive design with an above-fold consultation form and a clear phone CTA
  • Genuine attorney bio ("The Insurance Insider Advantage"), a dedicated video library, and an active blog publishing as recently as August 17, 2026 — trust signals many firms this size skip entirely
The Gap
  • A site engineered this well should be converting far more traffic than an underperforming SEO vendor can currently send it
  • No live chat means after-hours visitors who did find the site have no path to engage before calling someone else
  • Without current speed data, conversion loss from page performance cannot yet be quantified — but it cannot be ruled out either
A website this well-built deserves a lead engine that matches it — building that engine is what lets Derick choose when he touches litigation, instead of litigation choosing for him.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Runion did not appear among top organic results in any of six sampled searches for terms like "Phoenix car accident lawyer" and "Phoenix personal injury attorney"
  • Google Business Profile shows a strong star rating, but a review count that trails well behind the market's most-reviewed competitors
  • A FindLaw attorney profile for Derick Runion is filed under Albuquerque, NM rather than Phoenix — a NAP inconsistency worth auditing given the firm operates two offices
Blind Spot: NAP inconsistencies quietly suppress local rankings. Google's local algorithm cross-references name, address, and phone data across every directory it can find — even one mismatched profile, like the Albuquerque-filed FindLaw listing, can dilute the Phoenix local signal this firm needs most.
The Positive
  • Genuinely localized geo-targeting pages are live for both Albuquerque and Scottsdale — referencing real neighborhoods and state-specific law rather than keyword-swapped templates, and consistent NAP data across the firm's own site, FindLaw, and BBB for the Phoenix location
The Gap
  • Every search this audit ran surfaced the same handful of competitors instead of Runion — prospects actively searching for a Phoenix PI attorney are finding someone else first
  • A smaller review base relative to the market leaders means every future review this firm earns carries less relative weight than the same review would for a competitor already at scale
Zanes Law — maintains a dedicated landing-page structure per city and case type (e.g., a dedicated Phoenix car accident page) that consistently outranks Runion for local search intent.
Closing this visibility gap only matters if someone is free to act on it — right now, that someone is the same person handling every litigation file.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Runion does not appear to be running Google search ads for "Phoenix car accident lawyer" — the firm may not be configured to show for this term, or may be getting outbid by higher-spend competitors
  • The same pattern held for general personal injury search terms — no ad presence was observed
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Zanes Law — maintains dedicated PPC-style landing pages per practice area and city, the infrastructure typical of an active, sustained paid-search campaign
  • Phillips Law Group — a long-documented major legal advertiser in the Phoenix market with sustained top-of-market visibility
The Opportunity
  • "Phoenix car accident lawyer" and "Phoenix personal injury attorney" — the exact terms competitors are currently winning
  • "Albuquerque car accident lawyer" and "Scottsdale car accident lawyer" — geo-pages for both already exist and are ready to receive paid traffic today
  • Competitor conquesting on Phillips Law Group, Zanes Law, and Lamber Goodnow brand terms
  • Neighboring-suburb terms (Tempe, Mesa, Chandler, Glendale) where competitor density is typically lower
Blind Spot: Because the Albuquerque and Scottsdale geo-pages already exist and are genuinely localized, Runion is one paid campaign away from sending bid traffic directly to landing pages competitors would need months to build from scratch.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Runion does not appear to be running Local Service Ads or holding Google Screened status — this could not be independently confirmed live this session
  • No LSA review count was observed for the firm
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Lamber Goodnow Injury Lawyers — industry sources reference this firm as a common Google Screened/LSA presence in the Phoenix PI vertical (not independently verified live this session)
  • Phillips Law Group — also referenced as a common LSA presence in this market (not independently verified live this session)
The Opportunity
  • Appearing in LSA would place Runion above the same competitors currently outranking it organically, with a Google Screened badge that builds instant trust for users comparing multiple firms
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • LSA is priced per qualified lead rather than per click, which caps downside risk relative to broader search campaigns — a lower-risk entry point once a lead gen system is back up and running
Blind Spot: Firms holding Google Screened status effectively get free real estate above every other search result. Right now, that space in the Phoenix PI market is being ceded to competitors by default.
