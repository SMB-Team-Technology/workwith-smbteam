Lead Generation

O'Brien Legal already runs a broad lead generation program — seminars, direct mail, TV, billboards, social, Google Ads and Meta Ads through five or six outside vendors, at about $70,000 a month by Corey's account. The opportunity is not more channels; it is connecting them so every dollar can be traced to a signed client.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The mobile experience is the weak point: Lighthouse rates mobile performance in the "poor" band, which matters for every seminar, direct mail and TV viewer who checks the firm on a phone
  • PageSpeed (Lighthouse) scores: Mobile 46/100 — Desktop 80/100
  • The only form above the fold asks for a name and email to download a free guide; no full intake or scheduling form was observed there, so the main path to a consultation is a phone call
  • Corey records social videos regularly, but no video was detected on the homepage — that content lives only on social
Blind Spot: Offline marketing sends people to a phone first. A direct mail piece, billboard or TV spot typically ends with someone searching the firm's name on a mobile device — so mobile speed quietly sets the first impression for that entire spend.
The Positive
  • Active site with a current blog (roughly one new post a month), four named client testimonials and a Google Reviews badge on the homepage
  • Corey is featured on the homepage with a dedicated "Meet Corey" page, a clear "Schedule Your Free Consultation" call to action and four upcoming seminars listed
The Gap
  • A slow mobile page can lose prospects before they ever see the seminars, testimonials or consultation button
  • Seminar registration, the firm's number one lead source, is not the clear primary action on the page
  • Video of Corey is recorded constantly but is not working on the website to convert visitors
A website that converts the traffic your offline marketing already creates is growth that doesn't require more spend or more hiring.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The homepage shows a Google Reviews badge but no review count, so visitors cannot see how much proof sits behind it
  • Corey described a one-star and a two-star review arriving in the same week, both tied to follow-up lapses — recent negative reviews carry real weight with prospects and in local search
  • Local map-pack positions for estate planning, elder law and probate searches in Scranton, Wilkes-Barre and Moosic were not captured in this review and should be verified live
Blind Spot: Directory listings show two separate entries at the firm's Moosic address, "O'Brien Legal" and "O'Brien Law Group." A possible legacy or duplicate listing can split reviews and confuse local search — worth a manual check.
The Positive
  • Martindale-Hubbell pages exist for the firm and for Corey, he has an Avvo profile, and a Pittsburgh landing page shows the site is already being built for statewide reach
The Gap
  • Statewide expansion will need location pages and local profiles for each new market, not just Pittsburgh
  • Reviews are the easiest local signal to improve, and responding promptly and closing the follow-up loop protects every one already earned
Leeds Law Firm — an established estate planning competitor across Wilkes-Barre and Scranton that surfaces in legal directory results with a visible review history; live review counts should be pulled to benchmark O'Brien Legal against it.
Strong local visibility in every new market is what lets the firm grow statewide without relying on outside vendors alone.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Google Ads and SEO are run by a separate specialist firm, and Corey reports that Google has gone from producing almost no clients to producing a lot
  • Live ad copy and keyword coverage were not captured in this review, so the campaigns themselves have not been evaluated
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Competitor ad copy was not captured live. Leeds Law Firm, Law Office of Scott Lynett and Law Office of David J. Harris are the Scranton and Wilkes-Barre competitors to check for ad activity.
The Opportunity
  • Core terms such as "estate planning attorney Scranton PA" and "elder law attorney Wilkes-Barre PA"
  • Specific-need terms such as "Medicaid planning attorney Northeastern PA" and "probate lawyer Lackawanna County"
  • Searches that follow seminars and direct mail — people who receive a mailer or attend an event often search the firm's name afterward, so brand terms deserve protection
  • Statewide expansion terms for each new Pennsylvania market, starting with Pittsburgh, where a landing page already exists
Blind Spot: Calls from Google Ads land with Answer Connect and a second phone system, so it is hard to tell which calls became signed clients. The campaigns can be performing well and still be impossible to prove.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Whether O'Brien Legal holds a Local Services Ads listing, or Google Screened status, was not captured in this review
  • LSA availability varies by legal category and market, so eligibility for estate planning and elder law in this market should be confirmed first
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • No competitor LSA listings were captured in this review, so no competitor claims are made here.
The Opportunity
  • If LSAs are available for the firm's practice areas, they offer a pay-per-lead channel that sits alongside the seminar and Google programs already running
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • LSA listings display reviews prominently, so a stronger review base and tighter follow-up support this channel as well

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • Corey confirmed the firm runs Meta ads, and a second outside vendor runs Meta ads specifically to drive seminar registrations
  • Live creative, ad counts and formats were not captured in this review because the Meta Ad Library could not be reviewed
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Competitor Meta ad activity was not researched in this audit.
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Seminar registrants who receive only a day-before confirmation call could instead get text and email reminders, which protects the ad spend that already earned the registration
