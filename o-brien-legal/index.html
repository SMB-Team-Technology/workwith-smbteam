Here Is Where O'Brien Legal Stands Today

O'Brien Legal is a four-year-old estate planning, elder law, probate and trust administration firm in Moosic, PA, founded by Corey D. O'Brien and growing from a northeastern Pennsylvania practice to a statewide one, with revenue climbing from $500K in year one to a projected $1.6M this year. A broad, high-spend marketing program — seminars, direct mail, TV, billboards, Google and Meta — keeps interest coming in, but roughly ten disconnected systems mean Corey cannot see which channel drives clients or where inquiries fall through after the first call.

Stage 6 is where O'Brien Legal can reach all 50 states without Corey doubling headcount or carrying every recurring chore himself — and it is exactly where we are taking you.

Where You Are Today

Stage 4: Small Business Manager
  • Seminars, direct mail, TV, billboards, Google Ads and Meta Ads all feed the firm, with seminars the number one lead source
  • Roughly ten systems — Clio, Pipedrive, QuickBooks, phones, Zoom and more — that do not communicate with each other
  • After Answer Connect takes a call there is no systematic follow-up, and the firm cannot say what its close rate is on inbound calls
  • Corey still records about 30 videos per session and covers overflow casework while planning a move from statewide to all 50 states by the end of 2029

  • The firm runs without you

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The mobile experience is the weak point: Lighthouse rates mobile performance in the "poor" band, which matters for every seminar, direct mail and TV viewer who checks the firm on a phone
  • PageSpeed (Lighthouse) scores: Mobile 46/100 — Desktop 80/100
  • The only form above the fold asks for a name and email to download a free guide; no full intake or scheduling form was observed there, so the main path to a consultation is a phone call
  • Corey records social videos regularly, but no video was detected on the homepage — that content lives only on social
Blind Spot: Offline marketing sends people to a phone first. A direct mail piece, billboard or TV spot typically ends with someone searching the firm's name on a mobile device — so mobile speed quietly sets the first impression for that entire spend.
The Positive
  • Active site with a current blog (roughly one new post a month), four named client testimonials and a Google Reviews badge on the homepage
  • Corey is featured on the homepage with a dedicated "Meet Corey" page, a clear "Schedule Your Free Consultation" call to action and four upcoming seminars listed
The Gap
  • A slow mobile page can lose prospects before they ever see the seminars, testimonials or consultation button
  • Seminar registration, the firm's number one lead source, is not the clear primary action on the page
  • Video of Corey is recorded constantly but is not working on the website to convert visitors
A website that converts the traffic your offline marketing already creates is growth that doesn't require more spend or more hiring.

Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

  • Follow-up depends on busy people remembering, which Corey tied to a one-star and a two-star review in the same week
Blind Spot: A 70% close rate once prospects are in the room means the highest-leverage work is getting more of the people already interested into the room — through confirmations, reminders and post-seminar follow-up — rather than buying more registrations.

  • Without close-rate tracking on calls, the firm cannot tell which vendors and channels are producing clients
  • Open follow-up loops show up publicly as reviews, adding to the cost of each missed touch
