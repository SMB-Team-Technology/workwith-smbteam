Profit Plan Assessment

Caress Law is generating $2.2M-$2.7M in revenue this year with a stated 3-year goal of $7.6M, but no profitability, margin, or expense visibility was part of this discovery call. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • Revenue figures and a 3-year growth goal are known, but no margin, expense, or profitability data was discussed on the call
  • Probate and trust administration are still billed hourly, which caps the predictability of profit on that segment of the practice
  • A strategic goal exists to move probate/trust administration to flat fees, but this depends on automation not yet built
  • No stated visibility into cost per case type or practice area profitability
The Positive
  • Revenue is substantial and growing, with a clear, explicitly-stated 3-year revenue goal ($7.6M) that the firm has already backed into from its own growth projections
The Gap
  • Without margin visibility, it is not possible to know whether growth toward $7.6M will translate into proportional profit
  • Hourly billing on probate/trust administration limits how predictable that revenue segment's profit can be until flat fees are enabled
Automation that enables flat-fee probate work is not just an efficiency play — it is the path to more predictable profit, and more time back for Tammi.
