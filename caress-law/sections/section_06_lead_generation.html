Lead Generation

Caress Law has built real credibility in Portland's estate planning market — a 2025 Best of the Rose City win and a 4.4-star reputation — largely on organic search and referral strength rather than any confirmed paid lead generation system. This section documents that competitive landscape as context; the primary focus of this engagement is operational, not marketing.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • No above-the-fold contact form — contact information appears only in the footer
  • Live PageSpeed mobile/desktop scores could not be verified this pass (API access was unavailable during research); a manual PageSpeed check is recommended before final presentation
  • Limited individual attorney bios beyond Tammi's own dedicated bio page — the "Our Team" nav and team photo do not surface detailed bios for other attorneys
  • No geo-targeted landing pages for Beaverton, Lake Oswego, or Vancouver WA despite the firm stating it serves both Oregon and Washington
The Positive
  • Site is mobile responsive per modern layout markers, with current, actively maintained content — most recent blog post dated July 22, 2026
  • Clear practice area pages exist for all three service lines: Estate Planning, Probate & Trust Administration, and Business & Tax Planning
  • Two clear calls to action above the fold ("Contact an Attorney" and "Register for a Workshop") and a 2025 Best of the Rose City award prominently establish credibility
The Gap
  • Prospective clients in Beaverton, Lake Oswego, and Vancouver WA have no dedicated page speaking directly to their location, despite being in the firm's stated service area
  • Without a homepage contact form, visitors who don't scroll to the footer have no immediate way to reach the firm
  • Limited attorney bios beyond Tammi's may make the firm look more solo-dependent than its team framing suggests
A website that converts and represents the full team is one less thing that depends on Tammi personally holding it together.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Google Business Profile shows 4.4 stars across 27 Google reviews (28 total including 1 on Birdeye) — solid, but well behind the review volume of the firm's three most visible named competitors
  • Caress Law does not appear on page one for "estate planning attorney Portland OR," "probate attorney Portland OR," "wills and trusts attorney Portland Oregon," or "trust and estate administration attorney Portland OR"
  • The firm does surface for "estate planning lawyer near me Portland 97225" and for "business succession planning attorney Portland Oregon" — narrower, more specific searches
The Positive
  • 4.4-star rating is competitive against named competitors — ahead of Chenoweth Law Group (3.8 stars) and close to Nay & Friedenberg (4.7) and Fitzwater Law (4.8)
The Gap
  • Absence from the core "estate planning attorney" and "probate attorney" Portland searches means the firm's highest-intent traffic is currently going to competitors first
  • Lower review volume than all three named competitors limits how often Caress Law's profile is trusted at a glance against firms with 3-5x more reviews
Fitzwater Law — surfaces prominently for "estate planning attorney Portland OR" and "probate attorney Portland OR" where Caress Law does not, backed by 85 Google reviews built since 1931.
Closing the local visibility gap means more of the right prospects find Caress Law first — without adding a single hour to Tammi's week.

Google Ads

Ad Presence

  • This matches the transcript: paid advertising was not discussed on the call at all, consistent with no active campaign
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Fitzwater Law & Southwest Portland Law Group — maintain established, SEO-optimized practice pages that would plausibly support paid search, though live ad presence was not confirmed this pass
  • Nay & Friedenberg & Pacific Cascade Legal — strong organic probate visibility that could extend into paid search; not confirmed live this pass
The Opportunity
  • "Probate attorney Portland OR" and "estate planning attorney Portland OR" — the two core terms where Caress Law is currently absent
  • "Business succession planning attorney Portland Oregon" — a term where the firm already surfaces organically alongside Harris & Bowker LLP and Cosgrave Law
  • Neighboring markets — Beaverton, Lake Oswego, Vancouver WA — where no geo-targeted presence exists today
