The Positive

The Gap

A site built to convert — not just to inform — is one less thing standing between Danja and a team she can actually hand leads off to.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Higuera's own Google Business Profile star rating and review count could not be confirmed live this session, but the directory footprint that could be checked (Avvo, Birdeye) is markedly thinner than nearby competitors showing 156 and 144/81 reviews
  • A NAP (name/address/phone) inconsistency exists: the website lists 954.860.8040, while the Yahoo Local listing shows a (216) Cleveland, OH number tied to Danja's prior Ohio licensure
  • No geo-targeting landing pages exist for surrounding cities — Davie, Sunrise, Cooper City, Pembroke Pines, or Fort Lauderdale

Blind Spot: An address inconsistency also exists — Yelp lists a "Davie, FL" variant for the same Executive Park Dr suite that other directories list as "Weston, FL 33331." Search engines treat inconsistent NAP data as a trust-signal problem, which can quietly suppress local rankings even when nothing else is wrong.
The Gap
  • Every month with a stale directory number and thin review footprint is a month that keeps handing family-immigration searches to the firms next door
  • No local search visibility means referrals stay the only lever, capping growth at whatever Danja's personal network can produce
American Immigration Law Center (Caroly Pedersen) — 156 reviews and a dedicated naturalization/citizenship landing page at americanimmigrationusa.com/naturalization, built specifically to capture the family-immigration searches Higuera is not appearing in.
Cleaning up citations and building a real review base is inexpensive, one-time work that keeps paying off every month after — freeing up energy for the team-building Danja actually wants to focus on.
