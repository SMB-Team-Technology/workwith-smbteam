Here Is Where Higuera Immigration Law Stands Today

Stage 6 is the version of this firm where Danja works fewer hours, not more, while it keeps growing — and it starts with exactly the things she asked about on this call.

Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Higuera Immigration Law
Higuera Immigration Law is operating at Stage 3 — a Solo Practitioner whose reputation and referral base are real, but whose growth is capped by the absence of any predictable lead-generation system and a team to hand work off to. The single most urgent gap is not more clients — it is a funded, consistent lead channel paired with the org structure to act on it, since without both, hiring remains a guess rather than a plan. Until that changes, the time Danja is trying to reclaim for her family will keep coming out of her own schedule instead of the firm's systems.
