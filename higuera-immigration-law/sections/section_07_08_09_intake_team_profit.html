Intake Assessment

With no delegated intake team, every business or family immigration inquiry that comes in has to go through Danja personally — and she is already stretched across casework, client relationships, and running the firm. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating. This is where the biggest immediate ROI lives.

Status: RED
The Problem
  • Danja personally handles every intake conversation — no delegation, no dedicated intake team member
  • No CRM or intake tracking system in place — there is no way to see where each lead sits in the process
  • No after-hours coverage — inquiries outside her roughly 30-hour work week likely wait until she is next available
  • No defined follow-up sequence for leads who do not sign immediately
Blind Spot: You may not realize how many after-hours or weekend inquiries go unanswered until you are next available — for a solo practice, that gap is often invisible until a lead has already called the competitor down the hall.
The Gap
  • Every unhandled after-hours inquiry is a lead that may already be calling a competitor down the hall
  • Inconsistent follow-up means paid-for leads, once a funded channel launches, risk going to waste
  • No process means growth adds workload for Danja personally instead of for the firm
Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • The contact form lives in the footer, not on the homepage — a visitor has to actively search for a way to reach the firm
  • No live chat is present — visitors who want an immediate answer have no way to get one
  • There is no lower-commitment engagement option for visitors who aren't ready to book a call yet

Team Assessment

Beyond Danja, this firm has no fixed team — just independent contractors used on an as-needed basis, which means every decision, every client relationship, and every piece of casework still runs through her personally. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: RED
The Problem
  • No fixed employees — only independent contractors used "as needed"
  • No org structure, defined roles, or accountability system in place
  • No hiring sequence identified beyond a general interest in adding a removal-defense IC
  • Danja cannot step away from the firm without work stalling
Blind Spot: Because you have never had a team to delegate to, stepping back can feel abstract rather than achievable. The real blind spot is not knowing what a self-managing team actually looks like day to day — which is exactly why this was the focus of this call.
The Positive
  • Danja is already actively pursuing an independent-contractor attorney for removal/defense work — a real sign she is thinking about delegation, not just casework
The Gap
  • Owner dependency means Danja cannot take real time away from the firm without work stalling — what full ownership dependency costs in time and freedom
  • No hiring sequence means the next hire is a guess rather than a plan
  • No KPIs or accountability structure exist to measure whether delegation is even working
Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Profit Plan Assessment

Danja has named profit constraints directly — she cannot confidently hire without predictable lead flow, which keeps the firm's finances reactive rather than planned. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • No stated profit plan or target margin exists yet
  • Hiring decisions are being held back by uncertain, inconsistent lead flow rather than clear financial visibility
  • No average case value figure is available yet to model return on future marketing spend
  • The revenue growth Danja wants has no funding mechanism attached to it yet
Blind Spot: A predictable, ad-driven lead channel does more than generate cases — it becomes the financial data point that tells you exactly what you can afford to spend on your next hire.
The Positive
  • Danja was direct and specific about her financial constraints on the call rather than avoiding the topic — that kind of transparency makes building an honest profit plan far easier
The Gap
  • Without a profit plan, revenue growth alone will not translate into the freedom Danja is working toward — what lack of financial visibility costs
  • Uncertain lead flow keeps hiring decisions reactive instead of proactive
Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.
