Lead Generation

Erkan & Sullivan's only active lead-generation channel is organic SEO through current vendor Blue Shark, which the firm itself describes as producing "canned," "robotic" content. That single channel is generating just 212 monthly organic visitors against direct competitors pulling in 1,300 to 1,900 — and no paid channel, whether Google Ads, Local Service Ads, or Meta, has ever been turned on.

Website Audit

"Canned," "robotic," and "insulting."
The Problem
  • Content across the site is generic, template-driven copy — the firm's own words for it are "canned," "robotic," and "insulting" — and does nothing to convey Erkan & Sullivan's actual results or credibility
  • PageSpeed scores: not able to be captured this session (Google API daily quota exhausted) — recommend a manual PageSpeed check before assuming site speed isn't costing leads
  • No video content anywhere on the site — for a firm built on courtroom credibility and Super Lawyers-badged partners, there is no face-to-camera trust-building content
  • The site's most powerful asset — a landmark OUI case that entitled 27,000 people to new trials — is not featured anywhere as a dedicated page, press release, or case study
Blind Spot: Blue Shark had this landmark case in hand and never built a single piece of content around it — meaning the firm's biggest brand-building opportunity to date has already gone unused for months.
The Positive
  • Attorney bios are strong — both founding partners are Super Lawyers-badged, and the site includes profiled bios for the full support team
  • The site already includes geo-targeted landing pages (Lowell, Andover, and specific charge types), showing some existing local-SEO investment
The Gap
  • Without a website that reflects the firm's actual caliber, paid traffic sent to the site once PPC launches will convert at a lower rate than it should
  • The landmark case sitting unused means the firm is leaving its single strongest piece of earned credibility off the table with every visitor
  • No video presence means prospects researching multiple firms before a consultation have nothing to build early trust before they call
A website that actually reflects a premium, high-end brand is the foundation everything else — PPC, retargeting, reputation — is built on.
