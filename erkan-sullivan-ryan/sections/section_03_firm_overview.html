Here Is Where Erkan & Sullivan Stands Today

Erkan & Sullivan, P.C. is a two-partner criminal defense firm in Andover, Massachusetts built on a genuinely strong reputation — 154 five-star Google reviews and a landmark OUI case that entitled 27,000 people to new trials. But that reputation is nearly invisible online: the firm's current marketing vendor generates just 212 organic visitors a month, roughly a sixth of what direct competitors pull in, and the firm has never run a paid ad of any kind. The single most important finding is that Erkan & Sullivan's brand is already built — it just has no system turning that brand into a predictable flow of premium, high-margin cases.

Stage 6 is where a firm becomes selective — taking only the premium, high-margin cases that build the Erkan & Sullivan brand, while systems and a real team handle everything else — and that is exactly the market position you are building toward.

Where You Are Today

Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Erkan & Sullivan
Erkan & Sullivan is operating at Stage 4 — a Small Business Manager with one lead-generation system in place, but that system, SEO through Blue Shark, is badly underperforming, driving only 212 organic visitors a month against competitors pulling in 1,300 to 1,900. The single biggest gap holding the firm back is visibility: a firm with a 5.0-star reputation and a landmark case that could anchor a premium brand is nearly invisible in the channels that matter — no PPC, no LSA, no social ads, and organic rankings a fraction of the competition's. Until that visibility gap closes, the growth goals discussed on the call — higher average case values, 25–30 cases a month, and the ability to bring on a new associate — will stay out of reach.
