Intake

  • Intake responsibility isn't clearly delegated beyond the founding partners and admin support — no defined process was discussed on the call

Building a premium brand only pays off if every high-value inquiry it generates gets captured — right now there's no confirmed system ensuring that happens.

Self-Managing Team

  • The two founding partners remain the primary drivers of both casework and firm growth, with no dedicated marketing or business-development role in place

Scaling to 25–30 cases a month and $800/hr partner profitability both depend on a team structure that doesn't yet exist — building it is what turns "dominant brand" from a goal into a functioning firm.

Profit

  • Partners have a clear profitability target ($800/hr) and know the economics of specific matter types, like the $2,000/hr effective rate on clerk's hearings — but no overall revenue baseline was discussed

  • Without a model connecting lead source, case type, and profitability, growth in case volume could just as easily dilute the partner-hour economics the firm is trying to improve
Hitting $800/hr and a premium case mix requires deliberately choosing which cases to take — right now that choice isn't backed by a system that tracks it.
