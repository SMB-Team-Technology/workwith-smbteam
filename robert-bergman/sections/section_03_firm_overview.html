Here Is Where The Law Offices of Robert Bergman Stands Today

Robert Bergman has practiced estate planning in San Jose since 1981 and is a State Bar of California Certified Specialist in Estate Planning, Trust and Probate Law, with a practice built around drafting trusts, special needs plans, and trust administration matters personally. His website is already publishing roughly one new article a day, many aimed at specific Bay Area cities, but on a phone it takes more than 11 seconds to show the main content — the single most important finding in this audit, because it sits between that credibility and the families searching for it.

Stage 6 is where a firm's reputation, systems, and team work together so the owner's expertise is used where it matters most — and it is where this plan is designed to take The Law Offices of Robert Bergman.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • A four-decade reputation and a Certified Specialist credential anchor the practice, with the attorney personally drafting client documents
  • No other attorneys or staff are identified on the public website, so the firm appears to run through one person
  • Marketing is visible online — a steady stream of city-targeted articles and location pages — but mobile site speed (34/100) undercuts it
  • The path from a website visitor to a booked consultation relies on a single scheduling button

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a respected one-attorney practice and a firm that runs on systems is smaller than it looks — closing it starts with putting technology to work on the repeatable tasks that currently depend on you.
