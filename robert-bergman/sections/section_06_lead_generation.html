Lead Generation

The Law Offices of Robert Bergman has a rare asset for an estate planning firm — a Certified Specialist credential, four decades of practice, and a website publishing roughly a new article every day. What this research could not see is the paid side of the picture, so this section shows what is visible today and where the open opportunities are.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • On mobile, the homepage takes about 11.6 seconds to show its main content and blocks interaction for over a second — slow enough that many visitors on phones will leave before it loads
  • PageSpeed scores (Lighthouse performance): Mobile 34/100 — Desktop 84/100
  • The only call to action is a "Schedule a Free Consultation" button — there is no contact form on the homepage for visitors who are not ready to book a time
  • No video was detected anywhere on the site, so visitors cannot hear or see Robert before they decide to reach out
Blind Spot: A steady stream of city-targeted articles brings people to the site from phones, and those are the visitors who meet the slowest version of it. Publishing more content does not help if the page has not loaded by the time they decide to stay.
The Positive
  • Robert's State Bar Certified Specialist credential and admission in 1980 are featured clearly — a strong trust signal in estate planning
  • The blog is actively updated, with ten posts between October 3 and October 13, 2026, covering city-specific topics and subjects like special needs trusts and Medi-Cal
  • Location pages (including San Jose) and consistent name, address, and phone details support local search visibility
  • Desktop performance is solid, and the site appears to be built on a modern, responsive framework
The Gap
  • Visitors arriving on a phone — often the first device families use to search — are the most likely to be lost to slow load times
  • Without a quick, low-pressure way to reach out, a family that is not ready to book a call may simply move on to the next firm
  • Robert's credentials and personal approach are written about but not shown, which leaves a persuasive advantage unused
A site that loads fast and invites action turns the credibility Robert has already earned into booked consultations.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The firm's Google profile is referenced from its own testimonials page, but this research could not view live Google results, so its current rating, recent review activity, and photos were not verified
  • Local map-pack positions for "estate planning attorney San Jose," "living trust attorney San Jose," and "special needs trust attorney San Jose" were not captured — whether Robert appears in the top three for these searches is an open question for the call
  • Competitors such as Cheri Michaelis, Tree of Life Law Firm, and Cross Estate Planning Law show a substantial base of reviews on public directories, which is the main fuel for map-pack rankings
The Positive
  • Name, address, and phone details are consistent across Martindale-Hubbell, Lawyers.com, Yelp, and Yahoo Local
  • A dedicated San Jose location page and city-focused articles give Google clear signals about where the firm serves clients
The Gap
  • Trust administration, Heggstad petitions, and special needs planning are specialized services where a family often searches with a specific need — whether the firm shows up for those searches is not yet confirmed
  • Review volume and recency are the fastest-moving factors in local rankings, and they are not yet being measured against the firms Robert competes with
Law Offices of Cheri E. Michaelis — a long-established San Jose estate planning and trust firm covering the same services, with a large review presence on public directories that gives families immediate social proof.
Being the first firm families see — and trust — when they search is how a respected practice becomes a predictable one.

  • Trust administration / Heggstad petitions / trust modification: no firm ads were observed
  • Special needs planning: no firm ads were observed
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Law Offices of Cheri E. Michaelis — competes for the same estate planning and special needs trust searches; ad activity could not be confirmed in this research
  • Tree of Life Law Firm — promotes free consultations and fixed pricing on its website; ad activity could not be confirmed in this research
The Opportunity
  • High-intent searches such as "living trust attorney San Jose" and "estate planning lawyer Santa Clara County"
  • Specialized searches such as "Heggstad petition attorney," "trust modification petition lawyer," and "special needs trust attorney San Jose" — families searching these terms usually have an immediate need
  • Neighboring cities already covered by the blog — Cupertino, Palo Alto, Los Altos, Fremont — as location-targeted campaigns
  • Searches triggered by life events such as a death in the family or a diagnosis, where speed to respond matters more than price
