Intake Assessment

How The Law Offices of Robert Bergman handles inquiries has not yet been discussed, so this assessment is based on what the website shows visitors. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • The homepage offers one way to act — a "Schedule a Free Consultation" button linked to an online booking system — and no contact form
  • No live chat was detected, so a visitor with a quick question has no immediate way to ask it
  • The listed phone number and email appear to go to the office, but how quickly and by whom they are answered is not yet known
  • Whether inquiries are tracked and followed up when someone does not book right away has not yet been confirmed
Blind Spot: Estate planning inquiries are often triggered by a life event — a diagnosis, a death in the family, a new baby. Families in that moment contact the first firm that responds, not the best-reviewed one.
The Positive
  • The free consultation offer lowers the barrier to a first conversation
  • An online scheduling link lets visitors book without waiting for office hours
The Gap
  • Inquiries that arrive when the office is busy or closed may never turn into consultations
  • A visitor who is not ready to book a call has no lower-commitment way to raise a hand
  • Without follow-up, interested families who do not book immediately are easily lost to the next firm
Every inquiry that turns into a consultation is a family served and revenue earned without adding more marketing spend.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

Team Assessment

The firm's website presents Robert Bergman as the person who personally drafts client documents, and no other team members are identified publicly. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • The site stresses that Robert drafts documents himself rather than delegating to paralegals
  • No other attorneys, paralegals, or office staff are named on the public website
  • Team size and roles have not yet been discussed, so the true picture behind the website is still to be confirmed
  • A practice built around one person's hands is difficult to scale, to step back from, or to hand off
Blind Spot: Being the person clients choose is the firm's strongest asset — and also what makes it hard to step away from. The question is which tasks truly require Robert and which only feel like they do.
The Positive
  • Robert's personal involvement is a genuine selling point that families value in estate planning
  • Decades of experience give the firm well-developed knowledge that can be documented and reused
The Gap
  • Repeatable work that does not require attorney judgment still competes for the attorney's time
  • Without documented processes, growth would mean more hours rather than more leverage
  • The practice's value is tied closely to one person, which limits flexibility in how and when he works
Putting repeatable work in other hands — human or AI — is what lets an owner's time go to the work only he can do.

Profit Plan Assessment

Revenue, fees, and margins have not yet been shared, so there is nothing in this research to confirm or rule out about the firm's profitability. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • Fee structure and pricing have not been discussed, so it is unknown whether work is priced to reflect its value
  • It is not yet known how much of the firm's time goes to higher-value planning work versus administration and trust administration matters
  • The cost of the firm's current marketing and website efforts and what they return are not yet visible
  • Without that visibility, it is hard to say where an additional dollar or hour would pay off most
The Gap
  • Without clear numbers, decisions about technology, staff, or marketing are made on instinct rather than evidence
  • If time is the firm's scarcest resource, any task that can be done faster or by someone else is a direct profit lever
  • A plan that links hours, fees, and spend gives the owner the confidence to invest where it counts
Knowing exactly which work earns the most is what turns experience into a valuable, predictable practice.
