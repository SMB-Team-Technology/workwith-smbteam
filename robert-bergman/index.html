Here Is Where The Law Offices of Robert Bergman Stands Today

Robert Bergman has practiced estate planning in San Jose since 1981 and is a State Bar of California Certified Specialist in Estate Planning, Trust and Probate Law, with a practice built around drafting trusts, special needs plans, and trust administration matters personally. His website is already publishing roughly one new article a day, many aimed at specific Bay Area cities, but on a phone it takes more than 11 seconds to show the main content — the single most important finding in this audit, because it sits between that credibility and the families searching for it.

Stage 6 is where a firm's reputation, systems, and team work together so the owner's expertise is used where it matters most — and it is where this plan is designed to take The Law Offices of Robert Bergman.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • On mobile, the homepage takes about 11.6 seconds to show its main content and blocks interaction for over a second — slow enough that many visitors on phones will leave before it loads
  • PageSpeed scores (Lighthouse performance): Mobile 34/100 — Desktop 84/100
  • The only call to action is a "Schedule a Free Consultation" button — there is no contact form on the homepage for visitors who are not ready to book a time
  • No video was detected anywhere on the site, so visitors cannot hear or see Robert before they decide to reach out
Blind Spot: A steady stream of city-targeted articles brings people to the site from phones, and those are the visitors who meet the slowest version of it. Publishing more content does not help if the page has not loaded by the time they decide to stay.
The Positive
  • Robert's State Bar Certified Specialist credential and admission in 1980 are featured clearly — a strong trust signal in estate planning
  • The blog is actively updated, with ten posts between October 3 and October 13, 2026, covering city-specific topics and subjects like special needs trusts and Medi-Cal
  • Location pages (including San Jose) and consistent name, address, and phone details support local search visibility
  • Desktop performance is solid, and the site appears to be built on a modern, responsive framework
The Gap
  • Visitors arriving on a phone — often the first device families use to search — are the most likely to be lost to slow load times
  • Without a quick, low-pressure way to reach out, a family that is not ready to book a call may simply move on to the next firm
  • Robert's credentials and personal approach are written about but not shown, which leaves a persuasive advantage unused
A site that loads fast and invites action turns the credibility Robert has already earned into booked consultations.
