Lead Generation

CNK Lawfirm has grown to 12–15 new clients a month on referrals alone, with no active marketing because the team has no capacity to absorb more. The website that should support that referral flow is an empty page that Google has been told not to index, so prospects who look the firm up find nothing to confirm their decision.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The homepage contains only a logo — no headline, no body copy, no attorney bio, no navigation menu and no footer
  • There is no call to action and no contact form on the homepage, /contact or /about, which all return equally empty pages
  • Page speed scores were not captured for this report — with no content on the page, the first priority is a site that exists, loads, and can be found
  • The site runs on a blank-canvas WordPress theme and reads as an unfinished install rather than a live marketing site
Blind Spot: The homepage, /contact and /about pages all carry a "noindex, nofollow" instruction in their code, which tells Google not to list them. This setting is commonly left on from a site build, and until it is switched off even a finished site would stay invisible in search.

The Gap
  • Every referral who searches for CNK before calling lands on a blank page, which gives them no reason to trust the firm over the next name on their list
  • No indexed pages means no way to be found for immigration or estate planning searches in Elkridge, Baltimore, Columbia or Ellicott City
  • There is no way for a prospective client to reach the firm online, so any lead the website could have produced is lost
A working website is the first piece of a firm that brings in clients without depending on Caroline's personal network.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • No review presence was found on the Birdeye listings checked, and Google review data could not be confirmed for the firm
  • CNK did not surface in any of the three estate-planning searches run ("estate planning attorney Columbia MD," "wills and trusts attorney Elkridge MD," and "estate planning lawyer near me Howard County Maryland")
  • For immigration searches the firm appeared only in directory listings, and did not surface for "green card lawyer Baltimore MD"
Blind Spot: Two different phone numbers appear across directory listings for the firm. Inconsistent contact details weaken local search signals and can send prospects to a number nobody is watching.
The Positive
  • Attorney profiles already exist on Avvo and Lawyer.com, which gives the firm a base to build directory visibility from
The Gap
  • No geo-targeted pages exist for Baltimore, Columbia, Ellicott City or Laurel, so the firm cannot be found outside its own zip code
  • Several competitors show a visible review base on aggregator listings while CNK shows none, which tips the decision before a prospect ever calls
Adam Afshin Habibi — appears at the top of Elkridge immigration searches with a strong rating signal on directory listings, while CNK shows no comparable review presence.
Showing up in local search is how new cases arrive without Caroline having to be the one generating them.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • CNK does not appear to be running Google search ads for immigration terms such as "immigration lawyer Elkridge MD" — ad placements were not observed
  • The same applies to estate planning terms such as "estate planning attorney Columbia MD" — no ads were observed for this practice area either
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Adam Afshin Habibi — competes for the same Elkridge immigration searches; paid ad activity was not observed in this review
  • Elville and Associates, PC — competes for Howard County estate planning searches; paid ad activity was not observed in this review
The Opportunity
  • Immigration: "immigration lawyer Elkridge MD," "green card lawyer Baltimore MD," and "immigration attorney near me Howard County"
  • Estate planning: "estate planning attorney Columbia MD," "wills and trusts attorney Elkridge MD," and "estate planning lawyer near me Howard County"
  • Neighboring-city terms for Baltimore, Columbia, Ellicott City and Laurel, where CNK has no presence today
  • Searches for the firm's own name and for Caroline Kanja by name, so referrals who look her up find the firm instead of a directory or a competitor
Blind Spot: Paid ads should follow capacity, not lead it. With a team that is still in training and no active marketing today because of capacity, switching on ads before delegation and intake are in place would add volume to the one person already working 80 hours a week.

  • Chidi Onukwugha — LSA placement could not be confirmed for this firm in this review
The Opportunity
  • The Elkridge and Howard County immigration market is a natural fit for pay-per-lead placement, since prospects are searching for help right now
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • LSA placement is shaped by reviews, and CNK has no visible review base yet — building a simple review process comes first and also strengthens local search
Blind Spot: Google Screened status and LSA ranking both depend on review volume and responsiveness, so a review process built now is what makes LSAs viable later.
