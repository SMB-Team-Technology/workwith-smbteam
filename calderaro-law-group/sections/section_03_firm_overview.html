Here Is Where Calderaro Law Group Stands Today

Calderaro Law Group is a 14-person Miami business immigration firm led by attorney Renata Calderaro, built almost entirely on referrals from overseas business owners pursuing E2, EB5, L, and O visas. The firm has a real, provable strength — a 90-95% case approval rate — and a team large enough to run genuine intake and case management, but it has never run a sustained paid marketing campaign; its only paid attempt was a sub-$500/month Instagram test that produced one client before being cancelled after 7-8 months. Revenue has plateaued near $1M against a stated goal of $3.6M by 2027, and the gap is not case-handling capacity — it is that referrals alone cannot reliably fill it.

Stage 6 is where a scalable, predictable lead-generation engine replaces referral luck entirely — and closing the distance between $1M today and $3.6M by 2027 runs directly through building that engine.

Where You Are Today

STAGE 3: SOLO PRACTITIONER
  • New client growth today depends entirely on referrals and word-of-mouth
  • The one paid marketing test ever run — a sub-$500/month Instagram campaign — was cancelled after 7-8 months and produced a single client
  • A 90-95% case approval rate is a real strength, but it is not visible anywhere a prospective overseas client would look before hiring
  • Revenue has plateaued near $1M with no predictable system in place to reach the $3.6M 2027 goal

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The distance between where Calderaro Law Group stands today and Stage 6 is not about team size — Renata already has 14 people and two dedicated intake staff — it is about building the same discipline into how the firm attracts clients that it has already built into how it serves them.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Calderaro Law Group
Calderaro Law Group sits at Stage 3 by lead-generation maturity even though its 14-person team and dedicated intake staff look more advanced — the firm has built real operational capability with nothing systematic feeding it. The single most urgent gap is that new business depends entirely on referrals, in a market where named competitors like Davies & Associates and Kurzban Kurzban Tetzeli & Pratt already have visible paid, SEO, and review presence targeting the same overseas investor-visa buyer. Until that changes, the $3.6M-by-2027 goal is not just a marketing math problem — it is a math problem sitting on top of an empty pipeline.
