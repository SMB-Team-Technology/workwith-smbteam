Intake Assessment

With two dedicated intake staff and a 90-95% case approval rate once a client signs, Calderaro Law Group already has real intake infrastructure — what's missing is a defined process to convert more of what's already coming in. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • No documented follow-up sequence exists for leads who do not sign during the initial consult
  • Close rate sits at ~20% against a stated internal goal of 30%, with no described process for closing that specific gap
  • No CRM or intake tracking system was mentioned — without tracking, the 20%-to-30% goal is hard to measure or improve
  • After-hours coverage runs through WhatsApp, the same channel used as the website's primary marketing CTA — a single point of failure if volume increases
Blind Spot: You may not realize how much of the gap between your 20% actual and 30% target close rate could close simply by adding a defined, timed follow-up sequence — most firms lose deals not on the first call, but on the third one that never happens.
The Positive
  • Two dedicated intake staff are already in place — most firms at this size are still handling intake ad hoc
  • A 90-95% case approval rate once a client signs shows intake is qualifying the right cases
The Gap
  • Every percentage point below the 30% close-rate goal is qualified referral business walking to a competitor for free
  • No tracking system means there is no way to know which follow-up attempts are actually working
  • A single after-hours channel (WhatsApp) risks losing prospects who reach out outside business hours and do not get a fast response
Reaching a 30% close rate from the leads you already have moves you toward $3.6M faster than any new ad spend ever could.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • No native contact form is visible above the fold on the homepage
  • No live chat is present — a WhatsApp click-to-chat button is the sole real-time conversion path
  • That same WhatsApp channel is also used for after-hours business-client communication, per the transcript — the marketing CTA and the operational support line are one and the same tool

Team Assessment

With 14 total staff, including two dedicated intake staff, Renata has built more team infrastructure than most firms at this revenue level — but growth is still bottlenecked by lead flow, not by the team's ability to handle more cases. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • A 14-person team and dedicated intake staff exist, but no dedicated marketing or growth function was described — lead generation still runs entirely through Renata's own referral network
  • No stated org structure beyond intake — it is unclear who, besides Renata, owns growth decisions
  • The firm is near case capacity (90 of 120 slots filled) with 30 open slots and no system in place to fill them
  • Nothing on the call suggests Renata can step away from the practice for an extended period
Blind Spot: A team that already runs cases well can mask the real bottleneck. A strong operational team sitting below its true capacity is just as costly as an understaffed one — 30 open slots is 30 fewer client relationships than this firm is already built to serve.
The Positive
  • 14 staff and a dedicated two-person intake function are real infrastructure most firms this size have not yet built
  • A 90-95% case approval rate shows the team executes well once cases are in the door
The Gap
  • 30 open case slots represent capacity the team is built for but is not being used
  • No dedicated growth or marketing role means every new client still depends on Renata's personal network
  • Nothing described suggests Renata can step back from day-to-day involvement yet
A team built to handle more cases than it currently has is not a freedom problem yet — it is a lead-flow problem. Solve that, and free time becomes possible.

Profit Plan Assessment

Beyond the ~$1M current revenue and the $3.6M 2027 target, no profitability metrics — margin, cost per case, or acquisition cost — came up on this call. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • No profitability or margin figures were discussed on the call — only top-line revenue and the growth target
  • A results-based pricing model was explicitly required by the prospect, tied to recent slow sales — a signal worth tracking as this engagement moves forward
  • No average case value was stated for ROI math to anchor to — flagged for the sales rep to confirm
  • No mention of tracking cost per case type across E2, EB5, L, and O visa work, despite these typically carrying different profit profiles
Blind Spot: Investor-visa case types (E2, EB5, L, O) often carry meaningfully different margins from one another. Without tracking by case type, you cannot know which of your 90-95% approved cases are actually the most profitable ones to scale toward $3.6M.
The Gap
  • Without margin visibility, chasing $3.6M in revenue could mean chasing $3.6M in more work, not more take-home
  • No stated case-value figure makes ROI planning a rougher estimate than it needs to be
  • The results-based pricing requirement suggests real, if unstated, sensitivity to cash flow right now
Hitting $3.6M without a profit plan risks nearly 4x more work for a fraction of the payoff. Building that plan alongside the marketing engine protects what you are actually working toward.
