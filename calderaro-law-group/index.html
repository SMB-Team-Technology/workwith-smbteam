Here Is Where Calderaro Law Group Stands Today

Calderaro Law Group is a 14-person Miami business immigration firm led by attorney Renata Calderaro, built almost entirely on referrals from overseas business owners pursuing E2, EB5, L, and O visas. The firm has a real, provable strength — a 90-95% case approval rate — and a team large enough to run genuine intake and case management, but it has never run a sustained paid marketing campaign; its only paid attempt was a sub-$500/month Instagram test that produced one client before being cancelled after 7-8 months. Revenue has plateaued near $1M against a stated goal of $3.6M by 2027, and the gap is not case-handling capacity — it is that referrals alone cannot reliably fill it.

Stage 6 is where a scalable, predictable lead-generation engine replaces referral luck entirely — and closing the distance between $1M today and $3.6M by 2027 runs directly through building that engine.

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Intake Assessment

With two dedicated intake staff and a 90-95% case approval rate once a client signs, Calderaro Law Group already has real intake infrastructure — what's missing is a defined process to convert more of what's already coming in. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • No documented follow-up sequence exists for leads who do not sign during the initial consult
  • Close rate sits at ~20% against a stated internal goal of 30%, with no described process for closing that specific gap
  • No CRM or intake tracking system was mentioned — without tracking, the 20%-to-30% goal is hard to measure or improve
  • After-hours coverage runs through WhatsApp, the same channel used as the website's primary marketing CTA — a single point of failure if volume increases
Blind Spot: You may not realize how much of the gap between your 20% actual and 30% target close rate could close simply by adding a defined, timed follow-up sequence — most firms lose deals not on the first call, but on the third one that never happens.
The Positive
  • Two dedicated intake staff are already in place — most firms at this size are still handling intake ad hoc
  • A 90-95% case approval rate once a client signs shows intake is qualifying the right cases
The Gap
  • Every percentage point below the 30% close-rate goal is qualified referral business walking to a competitor for free
  • No tracking system means there is no way to know which follow-up attempts are actually working
  • A single after-hours channel (WhatsApp) risks losing prospects who reach out outside business hours and do not get a fast response
Reaching a 30% close rate from the leads you already have moves you toward $3.6M faster than any new ad spend ever could.
