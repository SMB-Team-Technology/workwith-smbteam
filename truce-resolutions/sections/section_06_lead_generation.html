Lead Generation

Truce Resolutions has grown through its first year entirely on Tamara's presentations, referrals and word of mouth, with no paid marketing and a Google presence that is still early-stage. The opportunity is to make the firm easy to find at the moment Solvang-area families search for an estate planner — without a large budget.

Website Audit

"I don't want to spend more money on my website, to be honest."
The Problem
  • The homepage headline and its two top buttons lean on life-transition and Family Conflict & Divorce language, while estate planning is the firm's actual focus
  • Lighthouse performance scores: Mobile 48/100 — Desktop 97/100. On mobile, the main content takes about 9.1 seconds to load
  • No "book a consultation" call to action was observed above the fold, and the contact form sits mid-page
  • No pages aimed at Solvang or the Santa Ynez Valley were observed in the site navigation
Blind Spot: Your firm name was created when you were only doing mediation. Search engines and AI tools reading the site may see a mediation and divorce firm rather than an estate planning firm.
The Positive
  • Desktop performance is excellent, and the site already includes an attorney bio, an embedded video and a blog
  • Tamara can maintain the site herself in GoHighLevel, so improvements can be tweaks rather than a rebuild
The Gap
  • A mobile load time over nine seconds means visitors on phones can leave before they read a word
  • Without a clear consultation button, an interested visitor has to hunt for the next step
  • Without local pages, the site gives Google little reason to connect it to Solvang estate planning searches
A few targeted tweaks — not a rebuild — can make the website work harder so your presentations and referrals turn into booked consultations.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • On a live "estate planning" search near Solvang during the discovery call, the firm did not show in the local map results — it appeared only in Google's AI Overview
  • The Google Business Profile's primary category reads "General Law Firm" rather than Estate Planning
  • The firm has only a small number of Google reviews, and a review QR code exists but requests are not yet a habit
Blind Spot: The happy clients already quoted on your website are your fastest source of Google reviews — a review request costs nothing and is one of the most direct ways to improve local visibility.
The Positive
  • Address and phone number match across the directories checked (State Bar, Personal Family Lawyer, Yahoo Local and Alignable), and the firm already appears in Google's AI Overview
The Gap
  • Map, local and AI search results are where "estate planning near me" searches turn into consultations — today those go to firms that rank or advertise
  • A thin review base gives Google and prospects less proof of trust than an established local firm's profile
Lawvex — has a Solvang office, appears among the Local Service Ads advertisers for Solvang estate planning, and promotes workshops, webinars and community-network involvement on its website — the same local-trust play, backed by paid visibility.
Becoming the go-to estate planner in Solvang starts with being the first name Google and AI tools show when your neighbors search.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • No Google search ads for estate planning terms were observed for Truce Resolutions, and Tamara has told us she has not tried Google Ads yet
  • With no campaigns run so far, there is no search-term or conversion data yet — a small, focused start would begin building it
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Lawvex — appears in the paid Local Service Ads block at the top of the Solvang estate planning search; its specific ad copy was not captured
  • Ashley DeHahn — no paid advertising was found on her website, so paid search for Solvang estate planning terms may be less crowded than it looks
The Opportunity
  • Core local terms such as "estate planning attorney Solvang," "living trust attorney Solvang" and "trust attorney Santa Ynez Valley"
  • Will, trust and probate-avoidance searches from the nearby Santa Ynez Valley communities
  • Prop 19 property-transfer and winery or ranch succession planning searches — a niche a Solvang-based firm can own
  • Searches from Valley residents who would rather work with a local attorney than drive to Santa Barbara
Blind Spot: Google search ads are not the best first step for a budget your size. Local Service Ads charge per lead instead of per click and fit a new firm with a small budget better.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Truce Resolutions was not observed in the Local Service Ads block on the Solvang estate planning search, and Tamara has told us she has not run LSAs
  • Google Screened status was not observed
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Lawvex — the one advertiser in the block with an office in Solvang, according to Tamara; its LSA review count was not confirmed
  • Most of the other advertisers shown are based outside Solvang and simply list it as a service area
The Opportunity
  • As a firm actually located in Solvang, Truce Resolutions has a local-presence advantage over advertisers that only list the town as a service area
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Pay-per-lead pricing fits a new firm with a small budget, and every new Google review strengthens how the listing stacks up
Blind Spot: A couple of the advertisers show as open 24 hours, so an unanswered LSA call can go to the next firm in the list — answering or returning inquiries quickly matters as much as showing up.

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • No paid Facebook or Instagram ads were observed for Truce Resolutions, and Tamara has told us nothing has been spent on marketing
  • Webinars are posted on Facebook organically, but they are not getting much traction
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Lawvex — promotes California estate planning workshops and webinars on its website; its Meta ad activity was not confirmed
  • Ashley DeHahn — no events or paid advertising were found on her website
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Promote the monthly workshop or webinar to a local Santa Ynez Valley audience — invited presentations draw better than ones you offer on your own, and ads can put your event in front of the same kind of audience
