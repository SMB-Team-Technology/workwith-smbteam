Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Every new client comes from presentations, referrals and word of mouth — there is no paid or repeatable lead source in place
  • On a live search for "estate planning" near Solvang, the firm did not appear in the local map results — only in Google's AI Overview
  • The Google Business Profile is categorized as a general law firm instead of Estate Planning, and the review base is still small
A steady flow of consultations is what lets you hand off the day-to-day and focus on being the go-to estate planner in your community.

Intake

  • You convert the prospects who sit down with you — the gap is getting them in the door, not closing them
  • Intake and follow-up still run through you, with one part-time helper just starting to reach out to clients
  • The AI phone agent you tried was turned off because it was not accurate, and no after-hours inquiry coverage was observed
A simple, repeatable intake process is what lets you step out of every first conversation without losing the client.

Self-Managing Team

  • The firm is effectively one attorney who presents, markets, meets clients and manages follow-up
  • The goal is a team of three to five — one to two junior associates, a paralegal and a caseworker — but today only a part-time helper is in training
  • Nobody currently owns marketing, and the firm cannot yet run while the owner steps away
A trained team is what lets you step in and out on your own terms instead of never being able to switch off.

Profit

  • Revenue is projected at roughly $200K–$250K in the first full year of estate planning, with $250K–$300K targeted next
  • At about $6,000 per flat-fee client, reaching $250K takes roughly 6–7 new clients a month versus 3–4 today
  • The marketing budget is limited, so there is little room for trial and error — every dollar needs a clear job
A clear profit plan lets you decide, on your own timeline, whether to keep growing or take your foot off the gas.
