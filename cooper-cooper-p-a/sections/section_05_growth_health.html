Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Paid search and Local Service Ads are already running through a marketing vendor, so the firm has a lead engine, not a blank slate
  • The goal discussed on the call is turning that spend into more retained clients per month, and into visibility in AI search results
  • Probate and guardianship are a growing part of the practice, and local competitors already compete for those searches in Orange Park
A lead engine that reliably fills the calendar is what lets the two of you grow without working harder.

Intake

  • Prospects frequently ask for an attorney by name, so a founder keeps taking consultations
  • The call described roughly two to three of every ten scheduled consultations being retained, against a goal of five in ten
  • A non-attorney sales role is a stated priority, but there is no playbook for building one yet
Moving consultations off a founder's calendar is how Charlean steps out of the consult room and back into the life she described.

Self-Managing Team

  • Six attorneys and a support team, with another attorney joining, show real growth in capacity
  • One founder manages the litigation team while carrying a full caseload, and the other runs the business side
  • You are building systems ahead of the strain, which is the right instinct, but the structure is not in place yet
A team that runs on its own structure is what turns a growing firm into one that does not need both founders every day.

Profit

  • Growth is visible in new attorneys, new space, and new equipment, and that momentum is a real asset
  • The call did not surface a profit target or a view of what each retained client returns on marketing spend
  • Switching marketing partners is also a cash-flow decision, and you want business to stay consistent through any transition
Profit that is planned rather than discovered is what makes growth feel like proof of success instead of more to carry.
