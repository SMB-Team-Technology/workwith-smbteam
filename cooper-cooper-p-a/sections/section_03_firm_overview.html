Here Is Where Cooper & Cooper, P.A. Stands Today

Cooper & Cooper, P.A. is a fast-growing Fleming Island family law and divorce firm, with a growing probate and guardianship practice, led by co-founders Anthony and Charlean Cooper and supported by a team of about eleven. The firm already has a paid marketing engine, a content-rich website, and a growing attorney bench, which makes it a Stage 4 business. The single most important finding is that growth still routes through the two founders, so more marketing alone will not buy them the time they are working toward.

Stage 6 is where the firm keeps growing, and keeps winning clients, without depending on the two of you for every consultation and every decision. It is exactly where we are taking you.

Where You Are Today

Stage 4: Small Business Manager
  • Paid advertising and a marketing vendor are in place, but the goal of turning that spend into more retained clients each month is still open
  • Prospects often ask for an attorney by name, so a founder keeps taking consultations and gets pulled back into casework
  • A non-attorney sales role is a top goal, but there is no proven playbook for building it yet
  • Six attorneys and a support team are in place and still growing, with both founders still deeply involved in daily operations

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a firm that grows because you work harder and a firm that grows while you live your life is a set of systems, and building them ahead of the strain is exactly what you said you want to do.
