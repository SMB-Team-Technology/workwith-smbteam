Lead Generation

Arch Legacy Firm already generates about 60 new estate planning matters a month, supported by self-managed Local Service Ads, an actively updated blog, and a modern website. Lead generation is not what is holding the firm back — which is why this section is about protecting a strong position, not rescuing a weak one.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The homepage "Schedule A Consultation" button leads to a separate scheduling flow — there is no contact form visible above the fold on the homepage
  • Two different phone numbers appear on the firm's own site: (706) 702-5749 on the homepage and (706) 352-9060 on the Contact Us page
  • Page-speed performance has not yet been benchmarked for this report — a live mobile and desktop PageSpeed test is the recommended first step before any site changes
  • A dedicated Athens page exists, but no dedicated Greensboro or Lake Oconee page was found in our searches despite a second office in that area
Blind Spot: Two phone numbers on the same website can split call tracking, confuse Google about which number is correct, and make it harder to know which marketing efforts are actually producing calls.
The Positive
  • The site looks modern and professional, is mobile responsive, and carries a 2026 copyright date
  • A featured video, "How NOT to Leave a Legacy," and a blog updated roughly weekly through September 2026 build trust and keep the site fresh for search engines
The Gap
  • Every extra click between a ready-to-call visitor and a booked consultation costs the firm matters it has already paid to attract
  • Inconsistent phone numbers weaken local search signals and make lead tracking unreliable
  • Without location pages for the Lake Oconee and Greensboro office, the firm is relying on one Athens page to represent three service areas — and a third office is coming next year
A website that books consultations with fewer touches from your team is one more way to grow without adding headcount.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • For estate planning, the firm appeared in all three searches checked ("estate planning attorney Watkinsville GA," "trust attorney Athens GA," and a directory-focused variant)
  • For probate, the firm was not confirmed in the top results for "probate attorney Athens GA," where long-established Athens firms surfaced instead — it did appear for Watkinsville and Oconee County probate searches
  • Recent Google Business Profile activity — photos, posts, and the date of the newest review — was not observed in our review and should be confirmed
Blind Spot: Probate is a newer line for the firm, and Athens is the larger search market. Established Athens firms hold that ground today, and the firm's 4 new probate matters a month could grow faster with a dedicated probate presence in Athens.
The Positive
  • Review volume reported across directories is substantially higher than anything discoverable for the named local competitors, and the firm is BBB Accredited and listed with the Athens Area Chamber of Commerce as a Women Owned Business
The Gap
  • Strong estate planning visibility does not automatically carry over to probate or to the Lake Oconee market
  • Directory and Google Business Profile details are not fully consistent, which can dilute the trust signals the firm has worked hard to earn
Fortson, Bentley & Griffin, P.A.; Smith, Gilliam, Williams & Miles; and Blasingame, Burch, Garrard & Ashley, P.C. — long-established Athens firms (some dating back to 1928 and 1946) that surfaced for "probate attorney Athens GA," where Arch Legacy Firm was not confirmed in the top results.
Winning probate and Lake Oconee searches the same way you already win estate planning is growth that does not require another hire.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Standard Google Search ads were not observed for estate planning or probate searches in our review — the firm does not appear to be running them, or they may not be configured to show for these terms
  • This may simply reflect the firm's choice to rely on Local Service Ads, which are confirmed as running for estate planning
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Robert W. Hughes & Associates, P.C. — holds a paid, sponsored placement on FindLaw's Watkinsville probate and estate administration page, the only paid competitor visibility we found; this is a directory placement rather than a confirmed Google Search ad
  • No other named local competitor was confirmed running Google Search ads in the searches conducted
The Opportunity
  • High-intent estate planning searches such as "estate planning attorney Watkinsville GA," "trust attorney Athens GA," and "living trust attorney Oconee County"
  • Probate searches in the Athens market, where established firms currently hold the visible positions
  • Lake Oconee, Greensboro, and Eatonton-area searches to support the second office and the planned third location
  • Business succession and business planning terms, supporting the firm's planned expansion beyond estate planning

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Local Service Ads are running for estate planning, managed in-house — a real strength, since this is the channel that sits above everything else on Google
  • Google Screened status and the LSA review count were not observed in our review
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • No named local competitor was observed holding an LSA position in our review, so the firm does not appear to be facing direct LSA competition from the firms we identified
The Opportunity
  • Extending LSAs to probate and to the Lake Oconee service area would put the firm in front of more high-intent searchers in the markets where it is least visible today
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • A large base of client reviews positions the firm well to earn and keep strong LSA rankings
Blind Spot: Because the LSAs are managed in-house, settings such as service areas, budget caps, and disputing poor-quality leads are easy to leave unattended — and every wasted lead or missed budget cap is staff time the firm is trying to get back.

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • Active Facebook or Instagram ads were not observed for the firm in our review, and no Meta advertising was mentioned on the call
  • The firm maintains an active Facebook page, so the foundation for social advertising is already in place
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Oconee Law Firm, P.C. and Epps, Holloway, DeLoach & Hoipkemier, LLC — no active Meta advertising was observed for either firm
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • The firm's featured video, "How NOT to Leave a Legacy," is ready-made content that could be adapted into educational ads for families in Oconee County and Lake Oconee
