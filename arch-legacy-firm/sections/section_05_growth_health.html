Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Local Service Ads are running for estate planning, managed in-house, and the firm reports its marketing is producing qualified trust clients
  • A modern website, a roughly weekly blog cadence through September 2026, a featured video, and a dedicated Athens landing page give the firm a solid organic base
  • Standard Google Search ads and Meta ads were not observed, and no dedicated Greensboro or Lake Oconee page was found despite a second office in that area
Demand is not the constraint right now — which means the next investment should add capacity and systems, not more hiring.

Intake

  • Attorneys lead every initial client session, with coordinators managing the rest of the process and paralegals drafting documents
  • After-hours calls are answered by a voice AI service the firm considers subpar in quality
  • Intake and case information is split between GoHighLevel and DecisionVault, which makes recaps, handoffs, and reporting more manual than they need to be
A smoother, more automated intake process is how roughly 60 new matters a month get handled without adding another coordinator.

Self-Managing Team

  • About 18 team members with a COO in place, and five estate planning attorneys projected next year
  • Growth has been met by hiring, and the owner is tired of it — the stated goal is to implement systems so the firm can stop adding headcount
  • A third office planned for next year adds coordination load to a team that is already stretched
Until repeatable work is handled by systems, you cannot reclaim the time and balance you want for yourself and your team.

Profit

  • A flat-fee model with no hourly billing and no trust accounting keeps pricing simple and predictable for clients
  • The only labor-cost figure shared on the call was an estimate from before recent team and price changes, so current margin per matter is not yet clear
  • Expanding into business and tax planning is an acknowledged opportunity the owner says is being left on the table
Strong profit comes from doing more with the same team — not from more hiring — and that is what funds the next vertical.
