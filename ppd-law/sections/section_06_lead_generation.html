Lead Generation

PPD Law already shows up well for most of its core searches in Brandon, with a 4.7-star Google profile and map-result visibility for business, commercial, family, and real estate work — but immigration, a core focus, is missing from the map results entirely. With capacity currently full, the goal here is a steadier, more predictable flow of the right matters rather than simply more volume.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The homepage headline is "Welcome to PPD Law Office" — it names the firm but does not tell a visitor which problem you solve or why to call you
  • PageSpeed (Lighthouse lab test): Mobile 43/100 — Desktop 71/100. In lab testing the main content on mobile took roughly 25 seconds to load
  • The contact form lives on a separate Contact page rather than on the homepage, so a visitor has to take an extra step to reach you
  • The site has only 11 pages, no video, and no pages aimed at the surrounding communities you could serve
Blind Spot: A slow mobile site often costs you matters you never hear about — a prospect who taps your listing from Google and waits too long simply goes back and calls the next firm.
The Positive
  • A clear "Get Started" button sits above the fold on the homepage
  • Dedicated pages exist for real estate, commercial and business law, family law, wills and estates, and immigration, and an attorney bio builds credibility
  • The blog is active in 2026, with the most recent post on September 15, 2026
The Gap
  • Slow mobile loading undermines the trust a strong Google profile has already earned
  • Blog posts are irregular — several months pass between posts — which limits how much search traffic the content can build
  • With no pages for nearby communities, the firm is invisible to people searching from outside Brandon itself
A website that loads fast and converts on its own means growth that does not depend on you personally chasing every inquiry.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • PPD Law appeared in none of the 3 immigration searches we ran ("immigration lawyer Brandon MB," "immigration lawyer Brandon Manitoba," and "work permit lawyer Brandon MB") — even though a client review on your own Google profile mentions an immigration case
  • In immigration, Shivay Immigration Services holds the map results with 240 reviews at 4.9 stars, against PPD Law's 59 reviews at 4.7 stars
  • Business details are not consistent across the web — the postal code differs between the website and the Google profile, and an older street address appears in a Yelp search listing
Blind Spot: Two of the three businesses in the immigration map results are listed by Google as consultants or immigration services, not law firms — a licensed lawyer should be able to out-compete them once the profile is properly built out for immigration.
The Positive
  • PPD Law appeared in the map results in 10 of 13 searches — every business, commercial, family, real estate, and wills search we ran — and ranked #1 for family and divorce lawyer searches
  • A 4.7-star Google rating from 59 reviews gives PPD Law more reviews than every local law-firm competitor we checked except Meighen Haddad LLP (65)
The Gap
  • Immigration searches in Brandon are going to competitors, and a review gap of 59 versus 240 will take a deliberate review-generation effort to close
  • Inconsistent business listings dilute the trust signals Google uses to decide who appears in the map results
Shivay Immigration Services — owns all three immigration map-result searches with 240 reviews at 4.9 stars, which gives it a visibility and credibility lead in a practice area you consider core.
Strong local visibility means the right clients keep finding the firm without you having to go out and find them.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • PPD Law does not appear to be running Google search ads for business, commercial, or corporate law searches in Brandon — we did not observe any sponsored results for these terms
  • The same was true for immigration, family law, and real estate searches — no PPD Law ads were observed, though ad display can vary by searcher and location
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Brandon law firms — no competing firms were observed running Google search ads across the 13 searches we checked, suggesting the paid side of this market is relatively quiet
  • Go To Canada Immigration Lawyer & Business Consultant (Winnipeg) — paying for a listed advertisement on YellowPages.ca for Brandon lawyer searches, a sign that outside immigration firms are already bidding for Brandon clients
The Opportunity
  • Immigration searches such as "immigration lawyer Brandon" and "work permit lawyer Brandon," where PPD Law is not in the map results
  • High-value business and commercial intent such as "business lawyer Brandon," "commercial lawyer Brandon," and "corporate lawyer Brandon," plus real estate closing and divorce searches
  • Neighboring communities such as Neepawa, Virden, Souris, and Carberry, which have no dedicated pages or campaigns today
  • Searches in the languages your site highlights, reaching newcomers looking for a lawyer who speaks their language
Blind Spot: With very little paid competition observed, early advertisers in a small market tend to secure the best positions at lower cost — an advantage that fades as other firms catch on.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • PPD Law was not observed in Local Service Ads in any of the searches we ran, and no Google Screened badge was observed
  • Local Service Ads did not display at all in the Brandon searches we checked, so availability for lawyers in this market should be confirmed before relying on it
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Meighen Haddad LLP — not observed holding a Local Service Ads position; its visibility comes from the map results (65 reviews at 4.5 stars)
  • Shivay Immigration Services — not observed in Local Service Ads; its lead comes from 240 map-result reviews at 4.9 stars
The Opportunity
  • If Local Service Ads are available to lawyers in Brandon, a 4.7-star profile with 59 reviews already gives PPD Law a credible base to qualify with
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Immigration and real estate are practice areas where a call-first listing could surface urgent, ready-to-hire prospects

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • PPD Law's Facebook page has 444 followers and posts regularly, but paid Facebook or Instagram ads were not observed in this review
  • The page currently shows "Not yet rated" with one review, so it is not working as a trust signal for visitors who find you there
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Meighen Haddad LLP — Facebook and Instagram ad activity was not verified in this review
  • Shivay Immigration Services — Facebook and Instagram ad activity was not verified in this review
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Immigration and family-law explainers, such as serving divorce documents outside Canada, that turn your existing blog expertise into reach beyond Brandon
