Here Is Where PPD Law Stands Today

PPD Law is a Brandon, Manitoba firm led by Premmal Patel-David, handling business and commercial, immigration, family law, and real estate matters with three support staff and roughly $500K in annual revenue. The firm already has a real foothold in its market — a 4.7-star Google profile with 59 reviews and a spot in the local map results for most of its core searches — but it is entirely dependent on Premmal, and operations would stop if time away were taken.

You described wanting to move from "Small Business Manager" to "Wealth Multiplier" — a true CEO with time for other ventures — and Stage 6 is the stage where the firm runs without you so that becomes possible.

Where You Are Today

Stage 4: Small Business Manager
  • Business, commercial, immigration, family, and real estate work arrives at an inconsistent 3–8 new files a month, and current capacity is already full
  • A team of three support staff, hired as recent graduates because trained staff are hard to find in Brandon
  • Training and supporting newer staff takes roughly 20% of your day
  • The firm is entirely dependent on you — operations would stop if you took a vacation

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a firm that depends on you and a firm that runs without you is closed with systems, delegation, and technology doing the repeatable work — not with more hours from you.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
PPD Law
Premmal is operating at Stage 4 — a Small Business Manager with a real team, a respected local reputation, and a full caseload, but still the engine behind every decision.
