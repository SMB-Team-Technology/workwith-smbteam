Intake Assessment

Intake wasn't a focus of Gerber Law's discovery call, and no CRM, tracking system, or dedicated intake role was described beyond a single paralegal contact. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating. This is where the biggest immediate ROI lives.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • No intake process, CRM, or lead-tracking system was mentioned on the call — what happens after a referral calls in isn't clear
  • A single paralegal is the only intake-adjacent contact identified — with 85-90% of leads arriving as referrals, there's no confirmed backup if she's unavailable
  • No after-hours coverage was discussed — for a firm competing on a reputation for trust, a referred lead who can't reach anyone after 5pm has options
Blind Spot: You may not realize that referred leads — the ones Gerber Law depends on most — are also the easiest to lose to a slow response, because they usually have someone else's recommendation to fall back on if the firm doesn't answer fast enough.
The Gap
  • Every referral that goes unanswered after hours is a referral that cost 29 years to earn and nothing to lose — the most expensive kind of lost lead there is
  • Without a tracking system, there's no way to know how many referred leads convert versus fall through — the firm can't fix what it can't measure
A firm built on referrals can't afford to lose the leads it already has — closing the intake gap protects the 29 years of trust that built this practice, at no additional marketing cost.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • The "Tell Us About Your Case" contact form is prominent and above the fold — a genuine strength
  • No live chat was observed on the site — not necessarily a gap, since live chat can create response-time expectations firms without round-the-clock coverage struggle to meet
  • No visible chatbot or intake-qualifying tool beyond the standard form and phone number

Team Assessment

After 29 years, Maria Gerber remains the person personally fielding vendor evaluations and marketing decisions — team structure and headcount beyond a single paralegal contact weren't detailed on the call. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • Maria is still personally managing 20 years of vendor relationships and marketing decisions — a role a self-managing team would typically own
  • No ops, marketing, or intake lead was identified in the discovery call
  • Team size and structure weren't detailed beyond a single paralegal contact — for a firm at this revenue level, that's a thin bench
Blind Spot: The fact that Maria is comfortable being the one evaluating every marketing vendor personally is itself the blind spot — after 29 years, that instinct has become the ceiling on how much she can grow without simply working harder.
The Gap
  • Every hour Maria spends evaluating marketing vendors is an hour not spent on casework or strategy only she can do
  • Without a delegated decision-maker, growth to $3M means more of Maria's time, not less
Scaling to $3M while staying the one deciding every marketing move means the growth Maria wants will cost her the freedom she's really after.

Profit Plan Assessment

Gerber Law's revenue is known and growing — consistently above $1M per year, with a stated goal of $3M — but no margin, profitability, or cost-per-case figures were discussed on the call. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"No correlation between spend and case volume."
The Problem
  • 20 years of marketing spend across multiple vendors, by Maria's own account, showed no correlation between spend and case volume — meaning real dollars went out the door without a trackable return
  • No cost-per-case or cost-per-acquisition tracking was mentioned — there's currently no way to know which marketing dollars are working
  • No profitability or margin figures were discussed — revenue is known, but what the firm actually keeps isn't yet visible in the numbers available
Blind Spot: You may not realize that a firm generating over $1M a year with no clear cost-per-case tracking is often more profitable than it looks on paper — or less. Without the number, there's no way to know which, and no way to protect the margin as revenue grows toward $3M.
The Gap
  • Without cost-per-case tracking, doubling revenue to $3M could just as easily double the waste as double the profit
  • Two decades of unmeasured marketing spend is proof the firm has the appetite to invest — what's missing is the system to make sure the next dollar spent counts
Growing to $3M only means something if Maria actually gets to keep more of it — and keep more of her time along the way.
