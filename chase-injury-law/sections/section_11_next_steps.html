Quick Wins for Chase Injury Law

Rebuild the Google Campaign Around Specific Injured-Worker Searches
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
After one week at $50 a day, the campaign has produced 6 clicks and no calls, with ads showing around 25% of the time compared with competitors. A campaign that is not built around the exact problems injured workers search for pays for clicks that do not convert.
Who Is Doing It Better
Lyle B. Masnikoff & Associates — runs dedicated landing pages for Fort Lauderdale and surrounding cities at workerscompfl.net, a sign of an organized, location-specific search campaign.
The Opportunity
Organize ads around denied claims, delayed medical care, stopped benefit checks, pressure to return to work, and settlement questions in each target city, so the firm shows up for the searches closest to signing.
Add Local Service Ads for Top-of-Page Visibility
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
Local Service Ads sit above paid ads, the map pack, and organic results, and you pay when a real prospect calls. We did not observe the firm in LSA results, and the firm has named LSA as a channel it wants to pursue.
The Opportunity
Earning the Google Screened badge puts a trust signal in front of injured workers before they open a single website, and pay-per-lead pricing keeps spend tied to real phone calls.
Fix Mobile Page Speed Before Scaling Ad Spend
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
The website scores 29/100 on mobile, with the main content taking about 19 seconds to load. Many injured workers search from a phone, and a slow page can lose them before they see the call button.
The Opportunity
Improving mobile speed is typically one of the least expensive ways to raise the share of ad clicks that turn into calls, and it makes every future advertising dollar work harder.
Cover Evening and Overnight Inquiries
Intake Quick Win
Why It Matters
Intake is live from 5 AM to 7 PM Florida time, so inquiries that arrive after hours risk going unanswered. On the call, intake was identified as the number one success factor for the plan.
Who Is Doing It Better
Rosenberg & Rosenberg — advertises 24/7 availability to injured workers in South Florida.
The Opportunity
Choosing a coverage option — AI, a virtual assistant, or an answering service — means every new inquiry gets a response, whatever time it comes in.
Update the Google Business Profile Category and Address Setup
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
The profile's primary category was identified on the call as "Law Firm" rather than "Workers' Comp Attorney," and directory listings show a Pompano Beach address while the website shows none and the owners work remotely.
The Opportunity
Setting the right category and a consistent business address or service area gives Google a clear signal about who you serve, which supports your statewide goal.
Track Cost Per Signed Case From Week One
Profit Quick Win
Why It Matters
The plan rests on assumptions about cost per lead and close rate. Until calls and signed cases are tracked back to each channel, the firm cannot tell which spend is earning its place.
The Opportunity
Tracking calls, qualified leads, and signed cases by channel tells you when to scale, when to hold, and what a case really costs — numbers you can use to plan profit.
Build a Review and Testimonial Engine and Refresh the Content
Lead Gen Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
The pages we reviewed show no Google reviews or client testimonials, and the last blog post is from April 7, 2026. Injured workers comparing firms look for proof from past clients, and Google looks for fresh content.
Who Is Doing It Better
Anidjar & Levine — has one of the largest review and brand footprints among the South Florida workers' comp firms we looked at.
The Opportunity
A steady habit of asking satisfied clients for reviews, plus regular posts on the questions injured workers ask, builds trust over time and supports both map and Local Service Ads placement.
Document Marketing and Intake Playbooks Before the Associate Hire
Team Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Two people carry legal work, operations, marketing, and intake today. Without written processes, each new case adds work for the same two people, and a new associate would have to learn it all from scratch.
The Opportunity
Writing down how leads are tracked, answered, and followed up lets Charles own the marketing system and gives a future associate and intake support a clear place to start.
