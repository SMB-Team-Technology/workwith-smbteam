Lead Generation

Chase Injury Law gets about 70% of its clients from referrals, and its first Google ads campaign — one week old — has produced 6 clicks and no calls. To reach 8–10 new cases a month and become Florida's top workers' comp firm, the firm needs an online engine that converts searches into signed cases, not just referrals.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • PageSpeed (Lighthouse) scores: Mobile 29/100 — Desktop 70/100. On mobile, the main content takes about 19 seconds to load
  • The contact page offers Call, Text, and Email buttons, but we did not observe an embedded form there or on the homepage
  • No Google reviews or client testimonials appear on the pages we reviewed, and no video was detected on the homepage
  • The most recent blog post is from April 7, 2026 — about six months without new content
Blind Spot: A page that takes about 19 seconds to load on a phone can lose an injured worker before they ever see the phone number. Six clicks is too small a sample to judge your ads, but a slow mobile site is a known reason paid clicks do not turn into calls.
The Positive
  • The homepage speaks directly to the problem: "Hurt at Work? The insurance company has a team. You deserve one too."
  • A clear "Start Your Free Consultation" button and a click-to-call phone number sit above the fold
  • Michelle's credentials — former prosecutor, 10+ years of experience — are front and center, and the site is mobile responsive
The Gap
  • Every paid click that bounces on a slow mobile page is ad budget spent without a chance at a case
  • Without visible reviews or testimonials, an injured worker comparing firms has no proof from past clients
  • Stale content tells both Google and prospects that the firm is not actively publishing on a topic it wants to own statewide
A fast, trust-building website is the asset you own that makes every dollar of marketing work harder — so you control your growth instead of renting it.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • On the discovery call, the Google Business Profile's primary category was identified as "Law Firm" rather than "Workers' Comp Attorney" — a change we recommended making
  • Directory listings show a Pompano Beach address, while the website shows no street address and the owners work remotely — Google rewards consistent name, address, and phone details, so the address and service-area setup needs to be settled
  • The site lists service-area pages for Florida cities from Tallahassee to Miami, but their depth and quality still need to be reviewed against what each city's searchers expect
Blind Spot: The goal is to be Florida's top workers' comp firm, but Google's local map results are decided city by city. A profile anchored in Broward County does not automatically carry weight in Orlando, Tampa, or Jacksonville.
The Positive
  • Michelle's Florida Bar license is active and her profile lists workers' compensation, which gives a trustworthy, verifiable directory listing to build on
The Gap
  • Without the right primary category and a consistent address, the profile is less likely to be shown for "workers' comp attorney" searches
  • Established Broward firms already hold the review and listing footprint a new profile has to overcome
Anidjar & Levine — has one of the largest review and directory footprints among the South Florida workers' comp firms we looked at, which gives it a strong head start on local trust signals.
A profile that clearly says "workers' comp" in every city you target is what lets the firm grow statewide without you having to be physically present.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Per the discovery call, a Google search campaign launched one week ago and was raised from $25 to $50 a day, producing 6 clicks and 0 calls
  • Ad position is around 25% of competitors, meaning the ads are being shown less often than rival firms bidding on the same searches
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Lyle B. Masnikoff & Associates — dedicated landing pages at workerscompfl.net for Fort Lauderdale and surrounding cities, still live in October 2026, which points to an organized search campaign
  • David Benenfeld Law Offices — a dedicated workers' compensation campaign page at injurylawservice.com, separate from the main website
The Opportunity
  • High-intent searches such as "workers' comp attorney" paired with Fort Lauderdale, Miami, West Palm Beach, Orlando, Tampa, and Jacksonville
  • Problem-specific searches that match what injured workers are living through: denied claim, delayed medical care, benefits checks stopped, pressure to return to work, and settlement questions
  • Urgent, late-night "what do I do now" searches from workers who have just been denied or cut off
  • Competitor-name searches and neighboring-city searches that surface the firm when injured workers compare their options
Blind Spot: Spending more will not fix a campaign that is losing people after the click. Ad position, mobile page speed, and how fast every inquiry is answered all have to work together before cost per signed case comes down.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Local Service Ads are one of the channels the firm wants to pursue, but we did not observe the firm in LSA results during our research
  • Whether the firm has applied for or earned the Google Screened badge was not confirmed
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • We did not confirm which competitors hold Local Service Ads placements, so we are not naming any firm here — checking this live is part of launch planning
The Opportunity
  • A Google Screened badge gives a new firm a trust signal at the very top of the search results, before a prospect reads a single website
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • LSA placement rewards review volume and how quickly calls are answered, which ties directly back to intake coverage and a review-collection habit

Competitor Ad Landscape
