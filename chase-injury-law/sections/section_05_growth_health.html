Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • About 70% of clients come from referrals — a strong asset, but not a system that can deliver 8–10 new cases every month
  • The Google ads test is one week old: $50 a day, 6 clicks, 0 calls, and ad position around 25% of competitors
  • The website scores 29/100 on mobile, which can waste paid clicks before a caller ever reaches you
Predictable online leads are what let Charles run the firm like a business instead of waiting on the next referral.

Intake

  • Intake is covered 5 AM–7 PM Florida time, leaving evening and overnight inquiries exposed
  • No intake solution has been chosen yet — AI, a virtual assistant, and an answering service are all still on the table
  • Paid leads need faster, more structured follow-up than referrals, who arrive already trusting you
Intake that works while you are offline is what lets you build the firm from Portugal without losing cases.

Self-Managing Team

  • Two people carry legal work, operations, marketing, and intake
  • The plan to hire an associate at 75–100 cases is clear, but it depends on case flow that is not yet predictable
  • Marketing knowledge sits outside the firm today — Charles wants to learn it and own it
A team that can absorb growth is what lets you work on the business, not just in it.

Profit

  • Per-case numbers are well understood: about $4,375 in firm fees per signed case at a 25% contingency
  • Real cost per signed case is still unknown because paid advertising has run for only one week
  • The goal is stated in cases, not in dollars, so there is no profit target to measure marketing spend against yet
Knowing what each signed case really costs is what turns marketing spend into profit you can plan around.
