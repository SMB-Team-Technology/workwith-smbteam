Close the After-Hours Gap
Intake Quick Win
Why It Matters
After-hours calls reach a recorded message. Many serious accidents happen at night and on weekends, and competitors that answer around the clock win the people who reach voicemail first.
The Opportunity
A defined after-hours process — so every call and sign-up is answered or followed up quickly — converts leads the firm is already paying to attract through billboards, TV, radio, and reputation.
Build a Steady Google Review Engine
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
The firm's review base is far smaller than several Houston competitors, and the exact Google total needs to be confirmed. Prospects compare reviews before they call, and paper-based files make it harder to ask satisfied clients for them.
Who Is Doing It Better
The Doan Law Firm — a Houston accident and truck-accident firm with a near-perfect rating and a far larger review base, which wins trust at the moment of choice.
The Opportunity

Clean Up Directory and Business Listings
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
One legal directory shows a different phone number than the website, another lists a different website address, and an unclaimed aggregator profile shows hours that do not look accurate. Conflicting details weaken trust with Google and with prospects.
The Opportunity
Consistent name, address, phone, and website details across every directory strengthen local rankings and make sure every call goes to the right number.
Track Every Lead to a Signed Case
Profit Quick Win
Why It Matters
TV, radio, and billboards are difficult to tie to signed cases, so the cost of acquiring a client is not measured today. Growth without that number can mean more spend without more profit.
The Opportunity
Call and form tracking with monthly reporting shows exactly which channels produce signed cases and what each one costs, so the next marketing dollar goes where it works hardest.
Build Local Landing Pages and Fresh, Dated Content
Lead Gen Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
The site lists 11 injury specialties but has no city-specific landing pages for surrounding Houston communities, and its 16 blog posts show no publish dates. Google has little to reward and prospects cannot tell what is current.
The Opportunity
Dedicated pages for each case type and service area, plus a steady schedule of dated articles, build the organic visibility that keeps working month after month.
Build a Documented Intake and Follow-Up Process
Intake Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
There is no formal intake process today — remote sign-ups rely on an online form with no scripted questions, follow-up sequence, or tracking behind it.
The Opportunity
A written process from first contact to signed case means more leads convert and the team no longer depends on any one person's effort to catch them.
Move Case Files from Paper to a Digital System
Team Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Paper files for 40 to 50 active cases a month limit what the team can do. They block digital marketing and review requests, slow follow-up, and keep key information tied to people and filing cabinets.
The Opportunity
A phased move to digital files gives the chief paralegal the case management system he wants, preserves what the attorney values about security, and makes the firm easier to run and hand off.
