Here Is Where The Bonner Law Firm Stands Today

The Bonner Law Firm is a Houston personal injury practice that has served the community for more than 40 years, focused exclusively on car and truck accidents, slip and fall, and dog bite cases. Today, new clients come mainly from word-of-mouth, TV and radio, and billboards, with 40 to 50 active cases moving through the office each month. The most important finding is that a firm with this much history has no consistent, measurable digital system to turn its reputation into a steady flow of new clients.

Stage 6 is where the firm brings in steady, predictable new clients and runs on a team and systems rather than on your effort alone — and it is exactly where we want to take The Bonner Law Firm.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • Lead flow is inconsistent and depends on word-of-mouth plus TV, radio, and billboards
  • There is no formal intake process beyond an online sign-up form, and after-hours calls go to a recorded message
  • Case files are paper-based, which blocks digital marketing, review generation, and easy follow-up
  • A three-person team carries 40 to 50 active cases each month, with no systems that let the firm run without constant owner effort

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between where the firm is and where it wants to be is closable — it starts with a steady lead system, a defined intake process, and a team that does not depend on one person's effort.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
The Bonner Law Firm
The Bonner Law Firm is operating at Stage 3 — a respected, long-established practice whose reputation is stronger than its systems. The most urgent gap is the lack of a predictable lead engine in one of the most heavily advertised personal injury markets in the country, made worse by an intake process that can lose leads after hours. Until that changes, the goal of adding 10 to 15 new clients each month and returning to a strong, steady pace will stay out of reach.
