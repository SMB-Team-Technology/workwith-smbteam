Lead Generation

The Bonner Law Firm has spent more than 40 years building a Houston personal injury practice, yet new clients still arrive mainly through word-of-mouth, TV, radio, and billboards — channels that are hard to measure and produce an inconsistent flow. In one of the most competitive injury markets in the country, there is no digital system working around the clock to capture the people searching for a car, truck, slip and fall, or dog bite attorney right now.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The contact form lives on a separate page rather than on the homepage, so a visitor who is not ready to call has no quick way to reach out
  • Page speed scores could not be captured at the time of this report — a live mobile and desktop speed test is recommended to confirm load times
  • The blog has only 16 posts and none display a publish date, so visitors and Google cannot tell whether the content is current; several posts are seasonal
  • No video was detected on the site, and no city-specific landing pages were observed for the surrounding Houston communities the firm serves
  • Site content is thinner than the leading Houston competitors in areas like attorney credentials and FAQs, as discussed on the call
Blind Spot: The homepage prominently features a Call Now button, but after hours that number reaches a recorded message. A visitor who finds the site on a Saturday night has no obvious way to submit their case, and the contact form is on a different page.
The Positive
  • The site is live, has a clear Call Now button and Contact Us button above the fold, and is set up for mobile devices
  • Practice areas are clearly laid out — 11 personal injury specialties from truck wrecks to wrongful death
  • An attorney bio and About page are in place, and a BBB accreditation badge builds trust
The Gap
  • A website that does not convert after hours or give visitors multiple ways to reach out loses cases the firm never knows about
  • Without fresh, dated content and local landing pages, Google has little reason to rank the firm above larger Houston competitors
  • Thin trust content makes it harder to win the visitor who is comparing three firms side by side
A website that turns every visitor into a conversation is the foundation of a steady, predictable flow of new clients — one that does not depend on word-of-mouth alone.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The firm's Google review count is far smaller than several Houston competitors, and sources disagree on the exact number — the true Google total needs to be confirmed live
  • Directory listings are inconsistent: one legal directory shows a different phone number than the website, and another lists a different website address
  • An aggregator profile for the firm is unclaimed and shows hours that do not look accurate, which can confuse both Google and prospective clients
  • Local map pack positions for car and truck accident, slip and fall, and dog bite searches in Houston were not confirmed in this review
Blind Spot: When phone numbers and website addresses differ across directories, Google has less confidence in which details are correct — and that can hold back map rankings no matter how good the firm's work is.
The Positive
  • The firm is already listed on a wide range of directories, including Avvo, Justia, the State Bar of Texas, and the BBB, where it is accredited — a solid base to clean up and build on
The Gap
  • Prospects comparing Houston injury firms on Google see competitors with far more reviews first — and trust follows the review count
  • Inconsistent business details and no steady review generation keep the firm from competing for map pack visibility
Jim Adler & Associates — promotes a Google review profile many times larger than The Bonner Law Firm's, which gives it a major advantage in map results and trust before a prospect ever calls.
Closing the local visibility gap is how the firm reclaims the Houston searches that are going to better-known competitors today.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Car and truck accidents: Paid search presence for terms like "car accident lawyer Houston" and "18-wheeler accident attorney Houston" was not observed in this review, and Google ad history was not discussed on the call
  • Slip and fall: Paid search presence for "slip and fall lawyer Houston" and "premises liability attorney Houston" was not observed
  • Dog bite: Paid search presence for "dog bite lawyer Houston" was not observed
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Jim Adler & Associates — a leading Houston injury brand competing for the same car and truck accident searches; its ad copy was not captured in this review and is the first thing to map when campaign strategy is built
  • Joe I. Zaid & Associates — operates multiple Houston-area offices and competes for the same high-intent searches; ad messaging to be mapped during campaign strategy
The Opportunity
  • High-intent searches for car accident, truck accident, and 18-wheeler accident attorneys in Houston — the cases the firm already handles every day
  • Separate campaigns for slip and fall, premises liability, and dog bite searches, each sending visitors to a dedicated landing page
  • Urgency and after-hours terms such as accident lawyer searches late at night and on weekends, when people with fresh injuries are searching
  • Surrounding Houston-area cities and competitor firm-name searches, where prospects are already comparing options
Blind Spot: Paid clicks are only worth what happens after the click. With a recorded message after hours and no formal intake process, ad spend on nights and weekends can send prospects straight to competitors who answer — so intake has to be fixed alongside any campaign.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • The firm's presence in Google Local Service Ads for Houston injury searches was not observed in this review
  • Whether the firm holds the Google Screened badge was not confirmed — this is an open opportunity to explore
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Jim Adler & Associates — a large review footprint on Google that positions it strongly for LSA placement; its specific LSA listing was not captured in this review
  • The Doan Law Firm — a near-perfect rating with a far larger review base than The Bonner Law Firm's, which is the kind of profile LSA rankings reward
The Opportunity
  • Google's Local Service Ads put a screened, verified firm in front of Houston accident victims at the exact moment they search
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Because the firm's review base is smaller than its competitors', building reviews alongside LSAs will be key to winning ranking
Blind Spot: Missed calls and slow responses can count against an LSA listing, so a recorded after-hours message does more than lose the lead — it can weaken the listing itself.

Facebook & Instagram Ads

  • The firm's current marketing is traditional media — TV, radio, and billboards — with no clear way to follow up with the people who see it
Competitor Ad Landscape

  • Joe I. Zaid & Associates — multi-office Houston competitor; Meta ad activity to be mapped during campaign strategy
The Opportunity

  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Turning existing blog topics on Texas statute of limitations, truck liability, and dog bites into ads that reach Houstonians researching their options
