Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • The website is two pages (Home and Contact Us) with no practice-area pages, no blog, and no city pages
  • The firm's name did not surface in any of nine Bend-area estate planning, elder law, and probate searches we ran, while the same local competitors appeared repeatedly
  • Google, Local Service Ads, and Meta ad activity was not observed for the firm
A predictable flow of the right clients is what lets a firm stop relying on one person's effort to keep the phones ringing.

Intake

  • Roughly a dozen calls a month are being missed, per our discovery call
  • Calls pass from an outsourced virtual receptionist to a paralegal callback, a hand-off that adds delay and loses prospects
  • No defined intake process or intake KPIs are in place yet
Every call that gets answered and followed up on is revenue the firm already earned the chance to win — without Lin working a single extra hour.

Self-Managing Team

  • One attorney, one paralegal, and an outsourced virtual receptionist — no other roles to hand work to
  • About an hour a day goes to editing Word templates and other low-value administrative work
  • No SOPs, KPIs, or accountability structure exist yet to let anyone else run the work
Until the work lives in systems instead of in Lin's head, a firm that runs itself stays out of reach.

Profit

  • Revenue is about $260,000 a year, a solid base for a solo practice
  • No financial KPIs or regular reporting rhythm were described, and billing is one of the systems still to be built
  • The long-term goal of take-home income above $200,000 a year depends on knowing what the firm keeps, not just what it bills
Profit that is planned instead of discovered is what turns twelve years of work into lasting wealth.
