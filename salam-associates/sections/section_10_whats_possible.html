It is a specific, buildable outcome — and it starts with fixing the operations underneath her, not with spending more on marketing.

1
A coached, accountable team that catches its own mistakes — so a missed radiology bill or a slow follow-up call no longer depends on Fatima noticing it herself.
2
An AI-automated back office — a custom "Fatima GPT" for SOPs, automated case note and document tracking, and an automated client-retention drip campaign — that removes Fatima from the repetitive work currently eating her week.
3
A marketing engine that can finally be turned on safely — once intake and team performance are proven, new leads convert instead of leaking away, and revenue climbs back toward and past the $4M the firm once generated.

This is not a fantasy. SMB Team can make this a reality for Salam & Associates, P.C.
