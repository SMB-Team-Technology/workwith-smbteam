Lead Generation

Salam & Associates does not currently appear in any of the local searches a prospective client in Richardson would run to find a personal injury or car accident lawyer, while at least four named competitors show up consistently. This section documents that competitive picture as context for why the gap matters — per the firm's own stated plan, the recommendation in this audit is to fix intake and team operations first and turn marketing investment back on once the firm can actually convert the volume it would generate.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • PageSpeed scores could not be obtained: the analysis tool was attempted 3 times and returned only a loading/interstitial state each time — no mobile or desktop score is reported here rather than an estimated one
  • Homepage copy is internally inconsistent — the About section claims "more than 34 years" of experience while the footer About Firm section says "more than 25 years," suggesting the site has not been fully reviewed in some time
  • The contact form is buried near the page footer rather than above the fold, adding friction for a visitor ready to reach out
  • The blog's most recent-appearing post title references "During the Pandemic," suggesting the content has not been actively refreshed in several years
Blind Spot: An outdated, unreviewed website is easy to overlook when referrals and reputation already bring in some business — but it becomes the first thing a prospect checks before calling, especially once new lead volume starts arriving from Local SEO or paid channels down the road.
The Positive
  • Attorney bios for Fatima Hassan-Salam and Rebecca Rutan are present on the homepage with clear "Read More" links to full profiles
  • CTAs are visible above the fold, including "Contact Us Now" buttons and both a toll-free and local phone number in the header
The Gap
  • A buried contact form and stale content quietly cost conversions from the traffic the site already gets — before any new marketing spend is even considered
  • Outdated blog content signals to Google (and to prospects) that the firm is not actively engaged online
  • The experience-year inconsistency is a small but avoidable credibility gap for a firm built on 21 years of trust
A website Fatima never has to think about again — clean, consistent, and converting on its own — is part of what makes a firm that runs without her possible.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Google Business Profile shows 90 reviews, per the call — with a stated goal discussed on the call to grow past 200
  • The firm's current primary GBP photo is an accident scene image rather than an office or team photo, which does not build the trust a new prospect needs before calling
  • Across 6 local searches run for "personal injury lawyer/attorney" and "car accident lawyer/attorney" in Richardson, TX, Salam & Associates did not appear in a single result — organic or local-pack-style
  • Named competitors Greening Law, MAS Law, Fielding Law, and Mullen & Mullen each appeared in 2 or more of the 6 searches run
Blind Spot: A wrong primary GBP photo is invisible to the firm internally but is often the very first image a prospect sees before ever reaching the website — an accident scene photo does not say "call us," it says "insurance claim."
The Gap
  • 90 reviews is meaningfully behind every verified named competitor in this market
  • Zero visibility across 6 head-term searches means the firm is not in the consideration set at the exact moment someone searches for help after an accident
MAS Law, Mullen & Mullen, and Fielding Law — all three hold substantially larger, verified review bases than Salam & Associates' 90 (Fielding Law 128+, MAS Law several hundred, Mullen & Mullen 500+), and all three appeared repeatedly in the same local searches where Salam & Associates did not appear at all.
Free fixes like correcting the GBP photo and launching a review-growth push cost nothing and can start today — real marketing investment on top of that comes once intake can actually convert what Local SEO sends.
