Here Is Where Salam & Associates, P.C. Stands Today

The single most important finding: this is not a lead-generation problem yet — slow, broken internal follow-up is estimated to be losing at least 5 cases a month, worth roughly $600,000 a year, and no amount of new marketing spend can fix that leak.

Stage 6 is the stage that finally lets Fatima step back from daily operations, hand the firm off to a team that runs itself, and put her energy into the NGO she wants to build next — and that is exactly where this roadmap is taking her.

Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Salam & Associates, P.C.
Salam & Associates is operating at Stage 3 — a Solo Practitioner firm in which the owner still personally carries three-quarters of the workload and has no scalable lead generation system to fall back on. The single most urgent gap holding the firm back right now is not visibility in the market — it is that slow, inconsistent internal intake follow-up and a lack of team accountability are actively costing the firm cases and revenue it has already paid to generate. Until intake and team performance are fixed, adding marketing spend on top would only accelerate leads into a broken system — which is exactly why this roadmap sequences coaching and AI-driven operations first, marketing second, matching what Fatima already identified as the right order of operations on the call.
