Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Did not appear in any of 6 head-term local searches run for personal injury and car accident terms in Richardson, TX

Intake

  • Slow, inconsistent internal follow-up is estimated to lose at least 5 cases a month — roughly $600,000 a year
  • No after-hours coverage today; the firm is in the process of transitioning intake to Alert Communications, a third-party intake service
  • Intake currently runs through internal staff with no defined process or accountability structure
This is the single fastest path to more revenue without spending another marketing dollar — and it is the leak that has to close before Fatima can trust the firm to run without her.

Self-Managing Team

  • Fatima personally handles roughly 75% of the firm's work, by her own estimate
  • Fatima describes herself as a poor mentor, with no accountability structure built for the rest of the team
  • No leadership layer exists between Fatima and day-to-day casework — every gap in the team currently falls back on her
Until the team can run without Fatima in the middle of every file, she cannot retire from daily operations or start the NGO she wants to run next.

Profit

  • Revenue is on track to fall from $4M to approximately $2M this year — nearly a 50% decline
  • Case volume has dropped from roughly 400/year to roughly 175/year, driven directly by the intake and team issues above, not by a lack of demand
  • Profit has become fundamentally unpredictable rather than planned — a direct symptom of the operational gaps, not a standalone financial problem
Fatima cannot build a predictable exit plan on top of a revenue line that is currently in freefall — stabilizing operations is what makes profit, and eventually retirement, plannable again.
