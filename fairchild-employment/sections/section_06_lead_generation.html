Lead Generation

Fairchild Employment Law already has marketing and SEO in place, and Jillian told us she does not have a problem with leads coming in. The opportunities below are about capturing a larger share of San Diego employment-law searches over time, not about fixing a broken lead engine.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • Lighthouse performance scores (a Google-developed speed test): Mobile 74/100 — Desktop 84/100. Mobile is solid but leaves room to improve, and most prospects searching after a workplace incident are on a phone
  • No video was observed on the homepage — prospects in a sensitive situation often want to see and hear the attorney before they call
  • No city-specific landing pages were observed on the homepage for the surrounding communities the firm serves
  • Blog activity is real but irregular, with long gaps between posts
The Positive
  • A clear above-the-fold call to action — "Schedule a Case Evaluation" with a phone number — and contact forms at the top and bottom of the page
  • Attorney profiles for Jillian and her associate, plus a testimonials page that includes client reviews
  • Recent blog posts on pregnancy-related workplace questions speak directly to what prospects are searching for
The Gap
  • Without video or city pages, the site relies on text alone to build the trust a prospect needs before calling
  • Irregular publishing gives search engines fewer fresh signals than a steady cadence would
  • Mobile speed improvements would help more of the visitors who are already finding the site
A website that builds trust on its own is one less thing that depends on you personally.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The Google Business Profile primary category reads "Law Firm" rather than Employment Attorney or Employment Lawyer, so Google has a weaker signal for the searches the firm wants to win
  • The visibility report shown on the call put the firm at roughly 13% share of local voice for "employment lawyer" versus roughly 76% for Ferraro Vega
  • Google reviews are a work in progress — Jillian told us she has been trying to build them up
  • A MapQuest listing shows a different street address than the website and FindLaw, which suggests an outdated listing that sends mixed signals to search engines
Blind Spot: The Google listing shows the firm as open 24 hours, but there is no after-hours answering in place. A prospect who calls at night expecting an answer and reaches voicemail is likely to call the next firm on the list.
The Positive
  • The Google Business Profile is claimed and actively managed, and the firm's FindLaw listing matches the website address and phone number
The Gap
  • A category change, steady review growth, and cleaning up the outdated address listing are low-cost fixes that directly affect where the firm appears in the local results
  • Closing the gap with the local visibility leader starts with the profile details that Google uses to match searches to firms
Ferraro Vega Employment Lawyers — holds the largest share of local visibility for "employment lawyer" in San Diego, according to the report shown on the call, which means they are capturing the searches that start with the most general term.
Getting found for the right searches means a steadier flow of strong cases without asking more of your time.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Google search ad activity for plaintiff-side employment law terms was not observed in our research, so we cannot say what, if anything, is running today.
  • Jillian described marketing and SEO as already in place and did not identify paid search as a focus.
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Ferraro Vega Employment Lawyers — ad activity was not observed in this review; as the market's local visibility leader, they are the firm to watch
  • Pokala Law and Advantage Law Group — ad activity was not observed in this review
The Opportunity
  • High-intent terms such as "employment lawyer San Diego" and "wrongful termination lawyer San Diego"
  • Case-specific terms such as "pregnancy discrimination attorney San Diego" and "sexual harassment attorney San Diego"
  • Non-obvious: competitor firm names, and nearby communities such as Chula Vista, La Mesa, Escondido, and Carlsbad
  • Non-obvious: urgency-driven searches from people who were just fired or demoted and are looking for answers right now
Blind Spot: Because lead volume is not your bottleneck today, paid search is a later-stage lever. The nearer-term return comes from converting and moving the cases the firm already has.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • The firm's presence in Google Local Service Ads for employment law was not observed in our research.
  • Google Screened status and any LSA review history could not be confirmed, so we are treating this as an open opportunity rather than a confirmed gap.
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Ferraro Vega Employment Lawyers — LSA activity was not observed in this review
  • Pokala Law — LSA activity was not observed in this review
The Opportunity
  • Where Local Service Ads are available for employment law in the San Diego market, they put a firm in front of prospects at the exact moment they search.
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Steady Google review growth strengthens the firm's position for LSA, local map results, and prospects comparing firms side by side.
