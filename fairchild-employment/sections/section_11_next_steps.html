Quick Wins for Fairchild Employment Law, P.C.

Correct the Google Business Profile Primary Category
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
The profile is categorized as "Law Firm" instead of an employment attorney category, so Google has a weaker signal for the "employment lawyer" searches the firm wants to win.
Who Is Doing It Better
Ferraro Vega Employment Lawyers — holds roughly 76% share of local voice for "employment lawyer" versus roughly 13% for Fairchild, per the report shown on the call.
The Opportunity
Switching the primary category to Employment Attorney or Employment Lawyer, and adding relevant secondary categories, is a no-cost change that can improve how often the firm appears for its most important search.
Fix the Outdated Address Listing
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
A MapQuest listing shows a different street address than the website and FindLaw. Conflicting address information weakens trust signals for search engines and can send prospects to the wrong place.
The Opportunity
Updating the old listing so the firm's name, address, and phone number match everywhere strengthens local search consistency.
Build a Steady Google Review Habit
Lead Gen Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Reviews are one of the deciding factors when a prospect in a stressful situation compares firms, and you told us you have been working on building more.
The Opportunity
A simple, repeatable review request at the end of each successful case turns satisfied clients into steady local credibility.
Add After-Hours Answering Coverage
Intake Quick Win
Why It Matters
The Google listing shows the firm as open 24 hours, but no one answers after hours today. Prospects in a workplace dispute often call more than one firm, and the first to answer usually wins the conversation.
The Opportunity
Live answering outside business hours would make sure no strong case goes to voicemail, without adding to your evenings.
Score Every New Case at Intake
Intake Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
On contingency, a case that does not settle quickly can take 12 to 18 months, so the cases the firm accepts determine where the team's time goes for the next year.
The Opportunity
A short, shared scorecard at intake helps your team recognize the strongest cases early and respond to them with the urgency they deserve, without needing you in every step.
Launch a Weekly KPI Scorecard
Profit Quick Win
Why It Matters
You told us you do not have time to build KPIs, which means the firm is steered without a dashboard.
The Opportunity
A handful of weekly numbers suited to a contingency practice — new signed cases, cases by stage, and expected settlement timing — gives you and the team a shared view of how the firm is doing.
Set a Standing Weekly Case-Movement Rhythm
Team Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Working cases up, driving discovery, and pushing the other side toward settlement currently depends heavily on you.
The Opportunity
A short weekly case review with clear owners and next steps lets the associate attorney and paralegal move cases forward between your touchpoints.
Forecast Cash Flow Around Settlement Timing
Profit Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Payroll and case costs such as mediations arrive on a schedule, while contingency income arrives when cases settle.
The Opportunity
Mapping expected settlements against known costs shows several months ahead where cash will be tight, so you can plan instead of react.
