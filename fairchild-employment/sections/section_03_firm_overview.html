Here Is Where Fairchild Employment Law, P.C. Stands Today

Fairchild Employment Law is a San Diego plaintiff-side employment firm led by Jillian Fairchild, handling discrimination, harassment, retaliation, and wrongful termination cases on contingency with an associate attorney, a paralegal, and a virtual intake assistant. Leads are coming in, and the most important finding is that lead flow is not what is holding the firm back — the missing pieces are the operating systems behind those leads: KPIs, intake that runs without the owner, and a repeatable way to move the right cases toward settlement.

Stage 6 is where the firm runs without you having to hold every piece of it — so you can sleep at night, pay every bill without worry, and be present in your free time.

Where You Are Today

Stage 4: Small Business Manager
  • Marketing and SEO are running and you told us you do not have a problem with leads coming in
  • You are still involved in every step of intake and case movement, working about 50 hours a week against a goal of 40 or fewer
  • There is no KPI system for a contingency practice and no consistent way to prioritize the best cases early
  • Cash flow and owner pay are not where they need to be, and the stress of it is a daily weight

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a firm that depends on you for every step and one that lets you sleep at night is closed with systems — KPIs, a trained intake process, and a team that moves cases without you — and that is exactly what this plan is built around.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
