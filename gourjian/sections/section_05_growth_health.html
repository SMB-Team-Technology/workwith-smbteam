Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • 100% of new business comes from personal referrals — zero marketing spend in 21 years of operation
  • The firm does not appear in organic search results for business litigation, corporate transactions, or general counsel services in its own Glendale market
  • Legacy directory listings (FindLaw, Martindale, Yelp) still show the old "Gourjian Law Group" name under a "Bankruptcy Law" category — invisible under the firm's actual, current focus
Every hire Varand wants to make depends on the firm generating more opportunity than referrals alone can deliver — right now, there is no system doing that work for him.

Intake

  • No contact form on the website — every inquiry routes through a single mailto link to one email address
  • No CRM or lead tracking process identified — new business inquiries have no defined path once they arrive
  • A single point of contact creates a bottleneck for a firm whose growth plan depends on adding attorneys and capacity
A referral that reaches out and gets a slow or inconsistent response is a referral that can quietly go find someone else.

Self-Managing Team

  • A "lean, top-heavy structure" of attorneys with minimal dedicated support staff — capable people, but no operational layer running the business side
  • The stated growth plan is to keep hiring revenue-generating attorneys, but there is no repeatable system for attracting, onboarding, or marketing around new hires
  • Profit is currently taken entirely as owner compensation rather than reinvested — a sign the firm still runs through Varand, not around him
Scaling by hiring alone means Varand's growth ceiling is always one more attorney away — a system that builds the firm's visibility takes that ceiling off entirely.

Profit

  • Exceptional financial visibility — a stated 60% profit margin and consistent 20%+ annual growth on ~$3M+ in revenue
  • Profit is currently taken entirely as owner compensation, with no formal plan yet in place to reinvest into growth
  • Varand has already flagged an openness to reinvesting roughly 20% of revenue toward growth — the plan just does not exist yet
The firm already generates the capital to fund its own expansion — the only missing piece is a plan that puts it to work instead of letting it sit as owner comp.
