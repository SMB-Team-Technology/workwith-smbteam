Lead Generation

Gourjian Law has built a 21-year, $3M+ revenue practice entirely on personal referrals — with zero marketing spend, no SEO strategy, and no organic or paid presence for the outside general counsel, real estate, or business litigation work it actually does today. This is not a firm bleeding leads; it is a firm that is simply invisible outside the network it already has.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • No contact form anywhere on the site — the only above-the-fold CTA ("Get in Touch") routes to a single mailto link, with no on-page way to capture or track an inquiry
  • PageSpeed mobile and desktop scores could not be independently verified during this audit (Google's PageSpeed API was unavailable at the time of research) — recommend a live check before the next conversation
  • The "Our Team" page lists all 8 attorneys by name but shows no photos and no bio copy on the index itself — a missed opportunity for a firm whose entire business is built on personal trust and reputation
  • The site's "News Feed" reads as a blog, but the content is syndicated third-party legal news, not firm-authored — there is no actual thought-leadership or authority content today, despite that being the core of the strategy discussed on the call
Blind Spot: The exact "branded play" strategy discussed on the call — building authority through high-quality content and education — has no foundation to build on yet. The site's blog is aggregated wire content, not original writing, so there is currently zero owned content asset to amplify.
The Positive
  • The site itself appears to be a recent build — modern layout, working mobile navigation, and an accessibility widget, all signs of a firm that takes its public presentation seriously
  • The site is mobile responsive, which removes one common barrier to a future content and SEO investment
The Gap
  • Every inquiry that comes through the site today depends on someone remembering to check one inbox — there is no way to know how many opportunities have been missed
  • A modern, well-built site with no original content and no bio pages cannot do the authority-building work Varand described wanting on the call
  • Without a foundation of firm-authored content, any future branded play has to start from zero rather than building on what already exists
A site built to convert trust into contact — and content built to prove the expertise referrals already assume — is what lets Gourjian Law's reputation start working for the firm even when Varand isn't the one making the introduction.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The firm carries far fewer reviews than several competitors in this market on third-party aggregators — exact figures are unconfirmed via a direct Google listing and should be verified live before the next conversation
  • Gourjian Law does not appear in local search results for "business litigation attorney," "corporate transactions lawyer," or "general counsel services for businesses" in Glendale — its own core services
  • Zero page-one organic rankings for any of the firm's actual practice areas — the only visibility the firm has anywhere online is under a legacy "Bankruptcy Law" category the firm no longer practices under
Blind Spot: Nearly every third-party directory (FindLaw, Martindale, Yelp) still lists the firm under its old name, "Gourjian Law Group," and its old bankruptcy-focused category — meaning the small amount of directory visibility the firm does have is actively pointing prospective clients to the wrong practice areas.
The Gap
  • A sophisticated business owner searching for outside general counsel today has no way to find Gourjian Law unless someone already told them to look
  • Every month the directory listings stay inconsistent is a month competitors with cleaner, current listings look more established and easier to find
Babachanian | Legal Counsel — explicitly markets "offsite general counsel" services with dedicated service pages, and outranks Gourjian Law in organic search for the exact terms a prospective client would use.
Fixing NAP consistency and closing the local SEO gap costs nothing in ad spend — it simply makes sure the firm's 21-year reputation shows up correctly wherever someone goes looking for it.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Gourjian Law does not appear to be running Google search ads for "business litigation attorney Glendale CA," "corporate transactions lawyer Glendale CA," or "general counsel services for businesses" — consistent with a firm that has never invested in paid search
  • The firm also does not appear to be running ads for "real estate attorney Glendale CA" — its second core practice area is equally unadvertised
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Novian & Novian, Babachanian, Macias Counsel, KAASS Law, and Lurie & Associates — none observed running paid search ads in this pass; all visibility they have comes from organic and directory listings
The Opportunity
  • "outside general counsel Glendale," "business litigation attorney Glendale," "corporate transactions lawyer" — the exact terms a sophisticated business buyer would use, currently unclaimed by anyone in paid search
  • "real estate attorney Glendale," "real estate litigation lawyer Glendale" — the firm's second core service, equally open
  • Competitor-name conquesting on "Babachanian general counsel" and neighboring markets (Burbank, Pasadena, Los Angeles) where Gourjian Law's target client base also operates
  • Because no local competitor is currently observed bidding on these terms, the cost to establish a presence today is lower than it will be once a competitor like Babachanian invests here
Blind Spot: This is a genuinely low-search-volume, low-PPC-intensity market — the buyer for outside general counsel work rarely searches the way a consumer legal client does. That means traditional PPC volume tactics are a weaker fit here than for most firms SMB Team audits; the bigger opportunity is content and authority that gets shared and referenced, not clicked on from a paid ad.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Gourjian Law does not appear in Local Service Ads or as Google Screened in the searches run for this audit
  • This is expected rather than alarming — LSA inventory for law firms skews heavily toward consumer practice areas like personal injury, bankruptcy, and family law, not transactional outside general counsel work
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • No named competitors in this peer set (Babachanian, KAASS Law, Novian & Novian) were observed holding LSA placements in this pass either
The Opportunity
  • Because LSA is not a natural fit for this practice area, it is not a priority channel for Gourjian Law today — the real-estate side of the practice is the one area where a future LSA test could be worth revisiting
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • No active Gourjian Law ads were observed in the Meta Ad Library in the searches run for this audit
  • Named competitors Babachanian and KAASS Law also show no active ads observed in this pass — Meta advertising is not yet a factor for this specific peer set
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Babachanian and KAASS Law — no active ad formats or campaigns observed; this channel is effectively open in this market
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — visitors who land on the site after a referral conversation but don't reach out are leaving with no follow-up today
  • Video ads featuring Varand and the senior attorneys — builds trust with a sophisticated buyer before a prospect ever calls, reinforcing the personal-relationship model the firm already relies on
  • Educational content ads built from the authority content discussed on the call — the same "branded play" content could be repurposed here to reach business owners who aren't yet in the firm's referral network
  • Because no competitor in this specific peer set is active on Meta today, this is a low-cost way to build brand recognition ahead of the market rather than catching up to it
