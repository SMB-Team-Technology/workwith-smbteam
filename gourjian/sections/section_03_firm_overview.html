Here Is Where Gourjian Law Stands Today

Gourjian Law is a 21-year-old Glendale, CA firm that has quietly built a $3M+ revenue practice on a 60% profit margin — serving sophisticated, long-term business clients in outside general counsel, real estate, and business litigation — without ever spending a dollar on marketing. Every one of those clients came through personal referrals, and the firm today does not appear anywhere in organic or paid search for the practice areas it actually specializes in. The single biggest finding: growth is now bottlenecked not by demand, but by the firm's total invisibility outside its existing referral network.

Stage 6 is the stage where Gourjian Law's growth is no longer limited by how many people happen to already know Varand and his partners — where the firm's 21-year reputation is backed by a visible, repeatable system for building authority, so the firm can keep hiring the caliber of attorneys it needs without straining word-of-mouth alone.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • 100% of new client relationships come from personal referrals — zero paid marketing or SEO investment in 21 years
  • The firm does not appear anywhere in organic search results for its own core services in its own market
  • Directory listings across FindLaw, Martindale, and Yelp still show the legacy "Gourjian Law Group" name under a "Bankruptcy Law" category that no longer reflects the firm's practice
  • Growth is bottlenecked by attorney hiring capacity, not by client demand — but there is no system in place to build the market visibility that hiring plan depends on

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
Closing this gap is not about replacing referrals — it's about giving Gourjian Law's earned reputation a visible presence that keeps generating opportunity even when Varand isn't in the room.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Gourjian Law
Gourjian Law sits at Stage 3 on the growth ladder — not because the firm lacks scale or profitability, but because every dollar of its $3M+ in revenue still traces back to a personal referral. The single biggest gap limiting the firm's next chapter is the complete absence of a visible market presence: no content, no directory consistency, no organic footprint for the practice areas the firm actually handles today. Varand's stated goal is to keep hiring attorneys who can each generate $650K–$700K in revenue — but that hiring plan depends on the firm being able to attract opportunity faster than word-of-mouth alone can deliver it.
