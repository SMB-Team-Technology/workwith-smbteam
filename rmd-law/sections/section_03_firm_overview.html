Here Is Where RMD Law Stands Today

RMD Law is a nine-location personal injury firm anchored in Irvine, California, with six attorneys handling car accidents, truck accidents, and other injury claims across California and a newly-entered Austin, Texas market — with a new employment law practice preparing to launch. The firm runs a genuine $80,000/month marketing budget across five channels, yet new case signings have slipped from roughly 50 per month to 30, and RMD Law's extensive geo-targeted SEO pages are not converting into local map-pack visibility in any market outside its Irvine headquarters.

Stage 6 is where RMD Law's marketing budget finally works as hard as the firm does — funding the multi-market, multi-practice-area expansion Michael is already pushing toward, instead of chasing volatile channels city by city.

Where You Are Today

STAGE 4: SMALL BUSINESS MANAGER
  • New case signings have dropped from roughly 50/month to 30/month even as marketing spend has held near $80,000/month
  • Nearly two-thirds of the marketing budget ($50,000/month) rides on Yelp Ads — a channel the firm itself describes as volatile, swinging between 5 and 20 signups a month
  • Local Service Ads are active but underperforming due to aged accounts and poor ranking, in the firm's own words
  • Extensive geo-targeted SEO pages exist for Sacramento, San Diego, San Bernardino, and Austin, but none of those markets show RMD Law in the local 3-pack

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
Closing that gap is not about spending more — RMD Law already spends aggressively — it is about making every dollar of that $80,000/month budget earn its place, which is exactly the shift that unlocks the multi-market scale Michael is already chasing.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
RMD Law
RMD Law sits at Stage 4 — a Small Business Manager with real scale (nine offices, six attorneys, an in-house CMO) but a marketing budget still being managed channel-by-channel rather than as one coordinated system. The single most urgent gap is that a firm spending $80,000/month cannot yet say, market by market, which of those dollars are actually producing signed cases — which is exactly why signings have slipped from 50/month to 30 despite the spend holding steady. Until that visibility exists, entering new markets like Austin, Seattle, Dallas, and Houston will mean repeating the same expensive trial-and-error that has already left RMD Law invisible in the local pack in Sacramento, San Diego, and San Bernardino.
