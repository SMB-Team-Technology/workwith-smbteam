Here Is Where Zimmer & Rens LLC Stands Today

Zimmer & Rens LLC is a nine-attorney, 16-person firm in Brookfield, Wisconsin built on real estate, business, and estate planning work — and it is now pushing into estate planning at scale, criminal defense, personal injury, and first-party bad faith insurance while opening a second office in La Crosse. The firm's own fractional CFO has already flagged that marketing spend sits well below the roughly 20% of revenue benchmark this kind of expansion needs, and there is currently no after-hours coverage to catch the leads in exactly the two practice areas the firm is betting on first.

Stage 6 is where Zimmer & Rens becomes the multi-office, multi-practice-area platform Paul and Taylor are already building toward — one that runs on systems instead of on the two of them personally — and it is exactly where this expansion is headed.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Intake Assessment

Zimmer & Rens has a working intake process during business hours — a call queue into a receptionist/paralegal, then remote assistants for matter creation — but there is no after-hours coverage at all, and no system in place to maximize how many of the leads that do come in actually convert. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating. This is where the biggest immediate ROI lives.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • No after-hours coverage — calls that come in nights and weekends have nowhere to go
  • Most leads for criminal defense and PI — the two practice areas the firm is prioritizing first — come in outside standard 9-5 business hours, which is exactly when the firm has no one answering
  • Follow-up on daytime leads is timely, but there is no defined process to maximize how many of those leads actually convert to signed clients
  • Intake currently runs through a multi-step handoff — call queue to receptionist/paralegal, then remote assistants for matter creation — with no stated tracking system to catch leads that stall between those steps
Blind Spot: You may not realize how many criminal defense and PI leads are calling after 5pm, getting no answer, and calling the next firm on the list before your team opens the next morning.
The Positive
  • Follow-up on leads that are received is timely — the foundation of a good intake process is already there, it just needs to run around the clock
  • The multi-step handoff (queue → receptionist/paralegal → remote assistant) shows real delegation is already happening rather than one person handling everything personally
The Gap
  • Every after-hours criminal defense or PI call that goes unanswered is a signed case — and the revenue behind it — going to a competitor instead
  • Without a defined conversion process, a meaningful share of daytime leads that do connect with the firm are still being lost to indecision or lack of follow-through
  • Two new consumer-facing practice areas are being launched on top of an intake process that was not built with after-hours consumer urgency in mind
Every new practice area Zimmer & Rens adds without after-hours intake coverage adds risk, not just opportunity — closing that gap protects the very expansion Paul and Taylor are investing in.
