Here's What's Possible for Zimmer & Rens LLC

Paul and Taylor have already done the hard part — built a nine-attorney firm, earned a fractional CFO's trust, and made the call to expand into estate planning, criminal defense, PI, and a second office in La Crosse. What they have not built yet is the marketing and intake engine that can carry all four bets at once without pulling their own attention into every one of them personally. That engine is exactly what turns "we are expanding into four new areas" from a plan into a firm that runs itself across two offices.

1
Estate planning and criminal defense generate their own steady stream of qualified leads — not leftovers from real estate referrals — so the September associate and the new practice areas are busy from day one instead of waiting for word to spread.
2
A criminal defense or PI call that comes in at 9pm on a Friday gets answered — not lost to whichever competitor picks up first — so the two practice areas the firm is betting on hardest stop leaking revenue to Grieve Law and everyone else who is already covering those hours.
3
The La Crosse office opens with search visibility and a lead pipeline already in place — not a blank slate — so Paul and Taylor can point to a second, growing location instead of personally rebuilding what already works in Brookfield.

This is not a fantasy. SMB Team can make this a reality for Zimmer & Rens LLC.
