Here Is Where Zimmer & Rens LLC Stands Today

Zimmer & Rens LLC is a nine-attorney, 16-person firm in Brookfield, Wisconsin built on real estate, business, and estate planning work — and it is now pushing into estate planning at scale, criminal defense, personal injury, and first-party bad faith insurance while opening a second office in La Crosse. The firm's own fractional CFO has already flagged that marketing spend sits well below the roughly 20% of revenue benchmark this kind of expansion needs, and there is currently no after-hours coverage to catch the leads in exactly the two practice areas the firm is betting on first.

Stage 6 is where Zimmer & Rens becomes the multi-office, multi-practice-area platform Paul and Taylor are already building toward — one that runs on systems instead of on the two of them personally — and it is exactly where this expansion is headed.

Where You Are Today

Stage 4: Small Business Manager
  • New client flow still runs almost entirely on organic referrals, repeat clients, and the Wisconsin State Bar Referral Service
  • A low-spend video ad test launched about a month ago, but there is no proven marketing engine behind the four new practice areas the firm is expanding into
  • Leads move through a call queue to a receptionist/paralegal and then remote assistants, with no after-hours coverage — a direct risk for criminal defense and PI, where most leads come in nights and weekends
  • The firm's own fractional CFO has flagged that marketing investment sits well below the ~20% of revenue benchmark needed to support estate planning, criminal defense, PI, and a second office in La Crosse

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
Closing the gap between Stage 4 and Stage 6 means building the lead generation and after-hours intake systems that can carry four new practice areas and a second office — without adding to what Paul and Taylor already manage personally.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Zimmer & Rens LLC
Zimmer & Rens is operating at Stage 4 — a Small Business Manager with a strong, established real estate and business law practice, but no proven marketing engine and no after-hours intake system. The single biggest gap limiting growth right now is that the firm is asking four brand-new practice areas — estate planning, criminal defense, PI, and first-party bad faith insurance — and a new La Crosse office to grow on the same referral-only lead flow that built the original practice, with no coverage to catch leads outside business hours in the two areas that need it most. Until marketing spend catches up to the ~20% of revenue benchmark the firm's own CFO has already flagged, the expansion Paul and Taylor are planning will take longer and cost more than it needs to.
