Lead Generation

Zimmer & Rens has a modern, professionally built website and a genuine team of 9 attorneys behind it — but virtually every new client still arrives through organic referrals, repeat business, and the Wisconsin State Bar Referral Service. There is no proven paid lead generation system in place to carry the four new practice areas the firm is expanding into.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • PageSpeed scores could not be pulled live this session (Google's PageSpeed API returned a quota-exhausted error on repeated attempts) — real mobile/desktop numbers should be confirmed before the proposal call, but sites built the way zrlawyers.com is built typically leave real performance on the table without a dedicated review
  • The active "Insights" blog covers real estate, probate, and eminent domain topics exclusively — there is no content at all yet for estate planning, criminal defense, PI, or first-party bad faith insurance, the four practice areas the firm is actively expanding into
  • No live chat on the site, and the contact form sits one click away via "Contact us" links rather than a native form embedded above the fold
  • The homepage message ("Giving you advice that matters") speaks to the firm's established real estate and business practice — it does not yet speak to a criminal defense or estate planning prospect landing on the site for the first time
The Positive
  • Nine dedicated attorney bio pages and a homepage video featuring partners Paul Zimmer and Taylor Rens — this builds real trust that most firms this size don't invest in
  • An active blog with recent, relevant content — most firms let this go stale entirely
The Gap
  • Every visitor who lands on zrlawyers.com today sees a real estate and business law firm — not a firm that also handles estate planning, criminal defense, PI, and first-party bad faith insurance, which means the site itself is currently working against the expansion
  • Without dedicated practice area pages and content for the four new areas, paid traffic sent to this site for those keywords will convert at a lower rate than it should
The website that built Zimmer & Rens' real estate reputation is not yet the website that can carry estate planning, criminal defense, PI, and a second office in La Crosse — closing that gap is what turns this expansion into revenue instead of just overhead.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Zimmer & Rens' live Google star rating and review count could not be independently verified this session — flagged for a manual pull before the proposal call, but the firm shows no reviews at all on Martindale.com or FindLaw, suggesting a thin third-party review footprint today
  • The firm does not appear to be surfacing in local search results for criminal defense or estate planning searches in the Brookfield/Waukesha market — the two practice areas it is prioritizing first
  • No geo-targeted landing pages found for La Crosse, the firm's newly planned second office — meaning the firm risks opening that office with zero search visibility on day one
The Positive
  • NAP (name, address, phone) is consistent across every directory checked — Martindale, FindLaw, Lawyers.com, and LawInfo all list the same Brookfield address and phone number, and 3 of the firm's attorneys are recognized on Super Lawyers/Rising Stars lists
The Gap
  • Real estate and business law searches are not where the growth opportunity is right now — estate planning and criminal defense are, and the firm currently has no measurable local search presence in either
  • Opening a La Crosse office without geo-targeted content in place means competing for search visibility from a standing start rather than day one
Grieve Law — a Brookfield-based criminal defense specialist with a verified 5.0★ rating and 109 reviews, dominating exactly the search terms Zimmer & Rens needs to win as it enters criminal defense.
Margerie Law is already the name that comes up for estate planning in this market — every month without a dedicated local SEO push is a month that gets harder to close.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • This firm does not appear to be running Google Search ads for criminal defense, estate planning, family law, or real estate terms in the Brookfield/Waukesha market — they may not be configured to show for these terms, or may be getting outbid by established local competitors.
  • The one confirmed paid effort — a low-spend video ad campaign launched about a month ago — is a paid social/video effort, not a Google Search campaign.
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Grieve Law — dominant organic and aggregator presence for criminal defense searches in Brookfield, backed by a 5.0★/109-review footprint that gives them outsized credibility in paid placements too
  • Margerie Law LLC — the name that consistently surfaces for estate planning searches across Brookfield, Elm Grove, and Wauwatosa
The Opportunity
  • "Criminal defense attorney Brookfield WI" and "estate planning attorney Brookfield WI" — the two most obvious, highest-priority keyword targets given the transcript's stated expansion focus
  • "Real estate attorney Brookfield WI" — the firm's most established practice, still worth defending even while deprioritized for new ad spend
  • Competitor-name and "near me" campaigns targeting searchers already comparing Grieve Law or Margerie Law
  • Early geo-targeted campaigns for "La Crosse WI" practice-area terms, launched ahead of the new office opening rather than after

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Zimmer & Rens does not appear in Local Service Ads for any practice area searched — this may be an unclaimed opportunity rather than a deliberate choice.
  • Google Screened status was not observed for the firm.
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Grieve Law and Sterling Lawyers — both large enough, with deep enough review counts (109 and 60+ respectively), to plausibly be running LSA campaigns; this should be confirmed live before the proposal call.
The Opportunity
  • LSA is a largely uncontested channel for this firm today — claiming it now, before competitors expand their own LSA presence, is a low-cost way to get in front of high-intent local searchers.
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Google Screened status would pair well with the firm's existing Super Lawyers recognition to build immediate trust with new-to-the-firm prospects in criminal defense and estate planning, where Zimmer & Rens has no track record yet.

  • Turning the one-month video ad test into a structured, always-on campaign rather than a one-off experiment
