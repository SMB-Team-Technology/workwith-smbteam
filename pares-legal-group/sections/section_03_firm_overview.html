Here Is Where Pares Legal Group Stands Today

Pares Legal Group is an Orlando, Florida immigration practice led by attorney Carlos Parés, with a second office in Puerto Rico, handling asylum, family petitions, business and investor visas, and deportation defense in Spanish and English, with a growing focus on families in Latin America looking for a lawful path to the U.S. Carlos has built his brand through national TV interviews and social media, and by his own account has more leads than the four or five cases a month he takes. The most important finding is not a shortage of attention — it is that no system the firm owns, such as a landing page, an e-book offer, or automated intake, turns that attention into scheduled paid consultations.

Stage 6 is where the firm runs without you — the stage that lets you grow from five cases a month to ten or more while keeping the relaxed, low-stress life you enjoy.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • Leads come from your personal brand — national TV appearances and Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube — rather than a repeatable lead system the firm owns
  • You take four or five cases a month and have more leads than that, so the limit is conversion and capacity, not attention
  • A team of two or three supports you, but the public face, the consultations, and the technology decisions all run through you
  • Intake runs through Clio Grow and Clio Manage, with automation and a secure AI platform still on your to-do list

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a firm that depends on you and one that does not is smaller than it looks — it starts with a landing page that converts your visibility into consultations and an intake process that runs without you.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Pares Legal Group
Pares Legal Group is at Stage 3 — a very small practice built around one attorney's personal brand, supported by a team of two or three. The most urgent gap is that none of the attention that brand earns flows through a system the firm owns: there is no landing page, no e-book offer, and intake still relies on manual effort. Until that changes, growing from five cases a month to ten or twenty would mean more of your own hours, not the relaxed life you enjoy.
