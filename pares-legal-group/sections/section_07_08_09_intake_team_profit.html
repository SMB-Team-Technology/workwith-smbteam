Intake Assessment

Intake runs through Clio Grow and Clio Manage, with a pre-recorded voice line routing callers into the system and a paid-consultation model that filters out casual inquiries. The next step Carlos described is automating intake and onboarding so that growth does not land on his desk. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
"Basically, I'm just trying to integrate some AI and more streamline the process."
The Problem
  • Intake and onboarding are the first things you want to automate, which suggests they still depend on manual effort from you and a very small team
  • Callers reach a pre-recorded voice line before they reach your intake process, and we did not hear about a live answer or after-hours follow-up on the call
  • You are comfortable at four or five cases a month, but moving to ten or twenty multiplies every intake step, and no process built for that volume was described
  • AI is used informally today, through personal Claude and ChatGPT use in the office, with no single secure platform you have chosen for client work
Blind Spot: You told us you want to choose one path and avoid making a mess of your data. The risk with adding AI tool by tool is exactly that: each new tool becomes another place where client information lives.
The Positive
  • Paid consultations at $200 to $300 for about half an hour mean every inquiry arrives with real commitment behind it
  • Leads and clients already live in the Clio family of tools, which gives automation a clear starting point
  • You already have more leads than you take, so improving conversion does not depend on finding new demand first
The Gap
  • Doubling or tripling case volume without a documented, automated intake and onboarding process means the extra work lands on you, which is the opposite of protecting your mental health
  • Every consultation request that waits for a manual follow-up is a prospect who may book with another firm first
  • Without one secure AI platform, the choice is between not using AI and using it informally with client information
A streamlined intake you do not have to touch is how you grow from five cases to ten without giving up the relaxed life you enjoy.

Team Assessment

Pares Legal Group is a very small team of two or three people, with only Carlos shown on the website, and a brand built around him personally. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: AMBER
"Instead of having five, maybe I can go to 10, 20, but never sacrificing my mental health."
The Problem
  • Roles on the team were not described, and only you appear on the website, so the firm's public identity and day-to-day knowledge sit with you
  • Technology decisions and AI experimentation run through you, as the person in the office who uses these tools most
  • Growing from four or five cases to ten or twenty a month has no staffing or process plan yet
  • You describe the brand as you personally, so every consultation, media appearance, and client relationship still depends on your time
Blind Spot: Being the brand is both a strength and a ceiling. If clients are choosing you, the firm only scales as far as your hours unless the work around the consultation is systematized, so your time goes to the part only you can do.
The Positive
  • You already have a small team behind you rather than operating alone
  • You are personally comfortable with technology, which gives AI adoption an in-house champion from day one
The Gap
  • Without documented processes and delegated onboarding, each additional case adds hours to your week instead of adding to a system
  • Your goal of ten to twenty cases at a relaxed pace requires work to move off your desk before volume increases, not after
  • Team AI use is informal today; a shared platform and regular training would let the team handle more repeatable work without you supervising the tools
A team and systems that run the day-to-day are what let you keep the relaxed life you enjoy while the caseload grows.

Profit Plan Assessment

Financial performance was not a focus of the call, so this assessment is based on how the firm earns revenue rather than on financial data. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"I'm a numbered person at end of the day."
The Problem
  • Case fees, margins, and cost per case were not discussed, so there is no baseline yet for what each new case contributes after costs
  • Paid consultations are a newer model for the firm, so there is no track record yet of how many consultations turn into signed cases
  • Adding a landing page, paid campaigns, and an AI platform adds monthly costs, and it is hard to judge what each adds back without tracking cost per consultation and cost per signed case
  • What a signed case is worth to the firm was not stated, so every projection in this report relies on industry defaults for immigration
Blind Spot: Because every consultation is paid, you have a clean, measurable event at the front of the funnel. The value of each booked consultation can be tracked against what it cost to earn it.
The Positive
  • Paid consultations put revenue in the door at first contact instead of giving the first half hour away
  • You told us you do not offer free consultations, which keeps your time tied to people who are serious
The Gap
  • Without a baseline for cost per consultation and cost per signed case, marketing and AI spend cannot be tied to what it returns
  • Growing from five cases to ten or more could add work without adding take-home if the time and cost of each case are not understood
  • A simple monthly view of revenue, costs, and take-home would show how much growth is worth taking on at the pace you want
Knowing your numbers is what lets you grow only as much as your life allows — more income without more hours.
