Standardize Onboarding for New Clients
Intake Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Every paid consultation that becomes a client needs the same steps: confirmation, questionnaire, documents, and follow-up. At four or five cases a month you can do that by hand. At ten or twenty, you cannot without losing your relaxed pace.
The Opportunity
Document the steps once, then use the AI workspace to draft the messages and checklists, so a new case starts the same way every time without needing you.
Write Down Roles and Handoffs for a Ten-Case Month
Team Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Your team of two or three has no defined roles that we heard about, and the brand is centered on you. Growth that is not planned lands on your desk by default.
The Opportunity
Map who handles each step from first inquiry to signed case, and move the repeatable work to the team and the AI workspace first, so your time goes to consultations and the cases you choose.
Track Cost per Booked Consultation and per Signed Case
Profit Quick Win
Why It Matters
Paid consultations are a newer model for the firm, and a landing page, campaigns, and AI will add monthly costs. Without two simple numbers, it is hard to tell what each one adds back.
The Opportunity
Because every consultation is paid, you can measure the value of each booking against what it cost to earn it, and decide how much growth is worth taking on at your pace.
