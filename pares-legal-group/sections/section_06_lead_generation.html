Lead Generation

Pares Legal Group does not have a lead shortage: by Carlos's own account, national TV interviews and social media bring in more leads than the four or five cases a month he takes. What the firm lacks is a destination it owns — a branded landing page, an e-book offer, and a clear path to a paid consultation — that turns that attention into scheduled consultations.

Website Audit

"…just a landing page with the colors of the brand or whatever, the e-book that I would like to do…"
The Problem
  • No landing page built around your brand and the e-book you plan to publish exists yet
  • The contact form sits in a general "Get in touch" section and asks for name, email, phone, and a message, with no offer that gives visitors a reason to share their details before they are ready to pay for a consultation
  • No video was observed on the site, even though you regularly appear on national TV and post on social media
  • No blog or educational content section was observed, so there is little for search engines or prospective clients to find before they book
Blind Spot: A paid consultation is a big first step for someone who has only just heard of you. An e-book gives people a lower-commitment way to learn how you think and raise their hand before they pay.
The Positive
  • A clear "Book a Consult" button sits above the fold, so the path to your calendar is already visible
  • Your bio states more than 10 years of experience, representation before deportation courts and USCIS, and AILA and ABA membership
  • The site lists 12 practice areas, from asylum and family petitions to national interest visas and VAWA
The Gap
  • Visitors who are not ready to pay for a consultation have nowhere to go, so the interest your TV and social presence creates can be lost the moment they leave
  • Without video on the site, prospects cannot see the on-camera credibility you have already built elsewhere
  • Without educational content, the site gives search engines less to work with than the practice-area list alone
A landing page that converts your visibility into booked consultations is the first step toward growing without giving up your relaxed pace.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Your business name appears in different forms across the website, LinkedIn, and Florida business records
  • Search results show two different Orlando street addresses for the firm, which needs to be confirmed and made consistent
  • Your Google Business Profile details — rating, review count, categories, and recent activity — were not captured in this review and should be confirmed at onboarding
  • No landing pages for surrounding cities were observed on the homepage
The Positive
  • National TV appearances and social media are building a name that people can search for directly
The Gap
  • Mixed business details make it harder for Google and for prospective clients to be sure which Pares firm they have found
  • Orlando-area pages in Spanish were not observed, which leaves a way to reach nearby families searching for an immigration attorney untapped
A consistent, verifiable online identity lets the brand you are building on TV and social turn into consultations without you chasing them.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • We did not observe Google search ads for immigration terms in this review. Google results could not be fully checked, so this is not a confirmed finding.
  • On the call, you told us you do not need Google SEO or Google Ads and that your leads come from social media and national TV.
The Opportunity
  • Spanish- and English-language searches such as "immigration lawyer Orlando" and "abogado de inmigración Orlando"
  • Case-specific searches for asylum, VAWA, national interest visas, and deportation defense
  • Searches from people in Latin America researching a lawful move to the U.S., which fits the "soft landing" focus you described
  • Searches for your own name and brand, so people who see you on TV and look you up land on your page instead of a directory
  • Google Ads is optional at this stage. Your stated priority is a landing page and e-book, and nothing here would start without your decision.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • We did not observe the firm in Local Service Ads in this review. Google's local results could not be fully checked, so this is not a confirmed finding.
  • Google Screened status and LSA reviews were not available to confirm.
The Opportunity
  • Orlando immigration searches could be tested in LSA once eligibility and license verification are confirmed.
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Verification for Google Screened depends on consistent business details, so the name and address clean-up in the Local SEO section comes first.

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • Your organic presence on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube is where leads come from today.
  • Paid Facebook and Instagram campaigns were not discussed on the call, and no active ads were confirmed in this review.
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Spanish-language campaigns aimed at Latin American audiences considering a move to the U.S., with your e-book as the offer and clips from your TV interviews as the creative
