Build a Steady Google Review Routine
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
Your clients speak well of you, but the review count appears small next to the firms ranking above you, and review volume is one of the clearest signals prospects use to choose between local tax attorneys.
Who Is Doing It Better
Washington Tax Services Inc — ranks above you in local results with a substantially larger review base.

Upgrade the Website Contact Form
Intake Quick Win
Why It Matters
The only form on your site sits in the footer and asks for just a name and email, so your team learns nothing about the tax issue or its urgency before the first call.
The Opportunity
Adding a phone number, the type of IRS issue, and whether a notice or deadline is involved lets your assistant prioritize urgent matters and arrive at the consultation prepared.
Put AI to Work on Email and First Drafts
Team Quick Win
Why It Matters
Email takes you two to three hours a day and your staff about two more, and you are behind on client work — the time sink that most directly stands between you and a shorter workweek.
The Opportunity
You have already built agents for emails and case evaluation letters; extending that to routine replies and first drafts, reviewed by your team, can return hours to your week.
Define the Associate Attorney Role Before the Hire
Team Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
You are planning to hire an associate attorney around June, and a hire without a defined role, handoffs, and onboarding plan tends to send questions straight back to the owner.
The Opportunity
A clear job description, a case-handoff process, and an onboarding plan written ahead of time let the new attorney take work off your desk faster.
Build a Simple Rolling Cash-Flow View
Profit Quick Win
Why It Matters
You described regular cash crunches you want to even out, and the way to smooth them is to see them coming.
The Opportunity
A forward-looking view of expected fees and expenses over the next several weeks lets you time the associate hire and other investments instead of reacting to a tight month.
Clean Up Directory Listings
Lead Gen Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
The phone number on your website appears to differ from the one on several directory listings, the firm name appears in more than one form, and there appears to be a second FindLaw listing — mixed signals that can weaken your local ranking and confuse prospects.
The Opportunity
Standardizing your name, address, and phone number across every directory strengthens the trust signals Google uses to rank you and ensures every call reaches the right line.
Document the Sales Call So Someone Besides You Can Run It
Intake Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
You take the 15- or 30-minute sales call on every qualified lead, which keeps a closing step that could be shared tied to your calendar as volume grows.
The Opportunity
Writing down your qualifying questions, fee conversation, and next steps creates a process the firm can eventually hand to a trained team member or to the new associate.
