Lead Generation

Your Dallas personal injury practice runs on an organic engine: the website and the activity around it, plus early work on AI search citations. AdWords and Local Service Ads have been tried before but are not running today, so no paid channel is working alongside it.

Website Audit

"we get them through a combination of our website, … and activities related around the website. … That's our main marketing system."
The Problem
  • Mobile speed is the weak spot, and the site is the engine behind your organic leads
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 43/100 — Desktop 91/100 (measured with Google's Lighthouse tool, so they may differ by a few points from pagespeed.web.dev)
  • Paid traffic is mostly mobile, so a slow mobile page can waste part of every advertising dollar sent to it
Blind Spot: The website is your main marketing system, so a mobile page that loads slowly affects every channel at once: organic visitors, paid clicks and AI-referred visitors alike.
The Positive
  • A clear headline, a "Tell us about your case" button and a click-to-call number appear above the fold, with a case form just below
  • The site is mobile responsive, has attorney bios, a video, and a blog that was updated on October 14, 2026
The Gap
  • Mobile visitors, who often arrive from an emergency search or a paid ad, face a slow page right when they are deciding whether to call
  • Before paid spend is added, every extra second of mobile load time raises the cost of each lead
  • An organic engine is only as strong as the page it sends people to
A faster mobile page means each dollar you test on paid channels works harder, so you can scale only what proves itself without overpaying for clicks that never load.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Your Google Business Profile shows 255 reviews at 4.7 stars, but the firms that most often led the local results have far more reviews
  • Personal injury (general): your firm did not appear in the local results for "dallas tx personal injury lawyer," "dallas tx injury lawyer near me" or "dallas tx slip and fall lawyer"
  • Car accidents: not in the local results for "dallas tx car accident lawyer," where Thompson Law (roughly 2,000 reviews, 4.9), Jim Adler & Associates (roughly 4,300 reviews, 4.6) and Mullen & Mullen Law Firm (519 reviews, 4.9) appeared
  • Truck accidents: not in the local results for "dallas tx truck accident attorney," where Rad Law Firm (554 reviews, 4.6), Witherite Law Group (roughly 1,900 reviews, 4.6), Mullen & Mullen (519 reviews, 4.9) and others appeared
  • Wrongful death: not in the local results for "dallas tx wrongful death attorney," where Sneed & Mitchell LLP (51 reviews, 4.9) appeared
Blind Spot: A legacy Yelp listing for a closed firm name sits at the same Dallas address as yours. A stale duplicate can split trust signals and confuse customers who find the old listing first.
The Positive
  • Your profile is complete: correct category, open 24 hours, and the address, phone and website match the site
The Gap
  • Local results are where many Dallas accident victims choose who to call, and those slots are going to firms with far larger review counts
  • The review gap is the biggest structural gap in local search, and it compounds the longer it stays open
Thompson Law — roughly 2,000 Google reviews at 4.9 stars led the local results for car accident, injury lawyer and slip-and-fall searches in the searches we ran.
Closing the review gap steadily gives every channel you add a stronger profile to land on.

Google Ads

Ad Presence

  • On the call you said you have run AdWords before and do not think paid is running today, so this is a gap to fill rather than a new channel to learn.
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Rad Law Firm — sponsored result on car accident searches: "Don't Sign Anything Yet | $1 Billion+ Won For Texans," with "The insurance company already has a lawyer. Get yours." and "Free review, $0 unless we win."
  • Zinda Law Group — advertising on truck accident searches ("Deadlines Approaching Fast / Evidence Lost Every Day"), a Texas-based multi-state firm competing for the same clicks.
The Opportunity
  • Dallas car accident and truck accident searches, where competitors are already paying to appear
  • Slip-and-fall, motorcycle and wrongful death searches, where we saw no named local advertiser
  • Urgency-driven searches such as "accident lawyer free consultation" and "what to do after a crash," plus nearby cities like Fort Worth, where a Fort Worth office page already exists
Blind Spot: Because you have run AdWords before, past results may be coloring expectations. A new campaign built around specific case types and tracked cost per signed case is a different test from a broad one.

Local Service Ads

  • On the call you said you have run LSA in the past and do not think it is running today, so this is an opportunity gap rather than a new channel.
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • No named competitor — no LSA units from specific Dallas firms were visible in our searches, so the competitive picture here is unconfirmed and worth checking live.
The Opportunity
  • With 255 reviews at 4.7 stars, your profile already has a base that can support a Google Screened application.
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Because LSA is billed per lead, it is a natural low-risk place to start, in line with your plan to begin at a budget and add if results are good.
