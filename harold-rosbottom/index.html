The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Website Audit

"we get them through a combination of our website, … and activities related around the website. … That's our main marketing system."
The Problem
  • Mobile speed is the weak spot, and the site is the engine behind your organic leads
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 43/100 — Desktop 91/100 (measured with Google's Lighthouse tool, so they may differ by a few points from pagespeed.web.dev)
  • Paid traffic is mostly mobile, so a slow mobile page can waste part of every advertising dollar sent to it
Blind Spot: The website is your main marketing system, so a mobile page that loads slowly affects every channel at once: organic visitors, paid clicks and AI-referred visitors alike.
The Positive
  • A clear headline, a "Tell us about your case" button and a click-to-call number appear above the fold, with a case form just below
  • The site is mobile responsive, has attorney bios, a video, and a blog that was updated on October 14, 2026
The Gap
  • Mobile visitors, who often arrive from an emergency search or a paid ad, face a slow page right when they are deciding whether to call
  • Before paid spend is added, every extra second of mobile load time raises the cost of each lead
  • An organic engine is only as strong as the page it sends people to
A faster mobile page means each dollar you test on paid channels works harder, so you can scale only what proves itself without overpaying for clicks that never load.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • How quickly calls and web forms are answered, and who answers them, is unknown, and it is the number that decides whether paid leads turn into signed cases
  • We could not tell whether the intake widget on the site covers evenings and weekends
  • It is not known whether every lead, from every channel, is tracked through to a signed case or a lost one
Blind Spot: Paid leads cost more than organic ones and are usually shopping several firms at once. The firm that responds first often wins the case, so a slow callback quietly raises your true cost per signed case.
The Positive
  • The homepage offers a case form, a "Tell us about your case" button and two click-to-call numbers (Dallas and Fort Worth)

The Gap
  • Every paid lead that goes unanswered for even a few minutes is a lead you paid to generate and handed to a competitor
  • Without tracking from first contact to signed case, you cannot tell which channel is really paying for itself
  • At a higher volume of calls and forms, small response gaps add up to many missed cases
Answering every lead fast and tracking it to the end means each dollar you test on a new channel is measured fairly, so you can add budget only where it earns it.
