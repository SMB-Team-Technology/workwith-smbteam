Here Is Where Morgan Law Stands Today

David Morgan has practiced injury law in Idaho for more than two decades, handling auto accident cases out of Canyon County and the Treasure Valley, with a Utah practice as well. Nearly all of his new business has come from referrals, family, friends, and past clients — and apart from a freeway billboard, he has not advertised in about five years. Last night he set up a ChatGPT Ads account and approval was blocked by a website crawler error, a clear sign that the website, not his reputation, is now what is holding growth back.

Stage 6 is where the firm provides for you and your family without depending on who happens to refer you next — and it is exactly where this plan is taking you.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The site does not appear to have been meaningfully updated since about 2019 — the newest blog post we found is from April 2019, and you described the last update the same way
  • OpenAI’s ad verification reported that the site blocks crawler access, which stopped your ChatGPT Ads account from being approved last night
  • Dedicated location pages exist for Boise, Middleton, and Lehi, Utah — but none for Nampa, Meridian, Caldwell, Eastern Idaho, or Twin Falls, the markets you told us you want to win
  • The contact form lives on a separate page, and no video of the attorney was observed on the homepage
Blind Spot: Your trigger domain, morganinjuryfirm.com, redirects to a second domain, morganinjurylawyer.com. Ad platforms and AI crawlers check the landing page they are sent to, so the redirect needs to be part of the crawler fix — not an afterthought.
The Positive
  • A dedicated attorney page showcases your credentials and 20-plus years of experience — a trust signal many firms lack
  • A clear “Book My Free Consultation” button and a 24/7 phone number sit above the fold
The Gap
  • Searchers in Canyon County, Eastern Idaho, and Twin Falls land on a site with no page written for their market
  • Until the crawler block is fixed, ChatGPT Ads — a channel you believe no one in the Boise area is using yet — cannot run to this site
  • A site that looks untouched since 2019 gives a first-time visitor little reason to trust it over a competitor’s
A website that can carry paid traffic and AI search is the foundation for a firm that no longer depends on referrals.

Intake Assessment

Morgan Law gets roughly two to three new cases a month today, and the phone is answered on the first ring — a strong start. The intake process behind that first ring was not described, and it has to be ready for the 10 to 15 cases a month David is aiming for. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • No documented intake process or follow-up sequence was described for new inquiries
  • After-hours and weekend coverage was not discussed, so calls and web inquiries outside the office may go unanswered
  • Going from two to three cases a month to 10 to 15 multiplies inquiry volume, and intake capacity has not yet been planned for it
  • Prospects arriving from search or ChatGPT are ready to act now and will move to the next firm if the first contact is slow or unclear
Blind Spot: Paid traffic arrives with high intent and little patience. A strong first-ring phone answer only helps if the website form, after-hours inquiries, and follow-up are just as fast.
The Positive
  • Your phone system answers the first call on the first ring — many firms lose cases before anyone picks up
  • The website offers a free consultation and a 24/7 phone number, setting an expectation of quick access
The Gap
  • Every inquiry that is not followed up consistently is a case that can go to a competitor — and at your current volume, each one is a meaningful share of the month
  • Without a defined process, new marketing leads could be handled inconsistently as volume rises
  • Without tracking from first contact to signed case, you cannot tell which channels are producing cases at your target cost
Strong intake turns the effort you put into marketing into signed cases — so growth builds security for your family instead of just more phone calls.

Profit Plan Assessment

Growth at Morgan Law is currently measured in cases per month, and David already knows the cost per signed case he can live with, but revenue and profit targets were not stated on the call. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"It was about $1,000 per case is what I was getting."
The Problem
  • Revenue and profit goals were not defined — the target is 10 to 15 cases a month, not a dollar figure
  • Past marketing spend, especially on organic, did not deliver results you could point to
  • No system was described for tracking cost per signed case across channels
  • On a contingency model, ad spend goes out months before fees come in, so cash flow needs to be planned alongside growth
Blind Spot: Cost per signed case is the right yardstick. Tracked by channel, it shows exactly where the next marketing dollar belongs — and where to stop spending.
The Positive
  • You already think in cost per signed case, with a clear ceiling of about $1,000 — a discipline many owners never develop
  • Your early-mover results show you can spot a channel that works and move on it quickly
The Gap
  • Without channel-level tracking, a campaign can look busy and still miss your cost-per-case target
  • Without a profit plan, growing from two to three cases to 10 to 15 may bring more work before it brings more take-home
  • Without clear targets, it is hard to know what to invest in the next level of marketing or hiring
A profit plan turns growth into wealth you can count on — the security you want for your family over the next 20 years.
