Here Is Where Morgan Law Stands Today

David Morgan has practiced injury law in Idaho for more than two decades, handling auto accident cases out of Canyon County and the Treasure Valley, with a Utah practice as well. Nearly all of his new business has come from referrals, family, friends, and past clients — and apart from a freeway billboard, he has not advertised in about five years. Last night he set up a ChatGPT Ads account and approval was blocked by a website crawler error, a clear sign that the website, not his reputation, is now what is holding growth back.

Stage 6 is where the firm provides for you and your family without depending on who happens to refer you next — and it is exactly where this plan is taking you.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • Referrals, family, friends, and past clients are the main source of new cases — roughly two to three a month
  • Apart from a billboard, no advertising in about five years; the earlier PPC and Local Service Ads campaigns were run personally
  • A website that does not appear to have been meaningfully updated since about 2019, and a ChatGPT Ads account blocked at approval
  • A Google review count you describe as low for a firm with decades of history in the market

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a referral-driven practice and a firm that provides for your family for the next 20 years is narrower than it feels — it comes down to a predictable system for bringing in new cases.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Morgan Law
David is operating at Stage 3 — a respected Idaho injury attorney whose reputation has outrun his systems. The most urgent gap is the absence of a predictable lead source: referrals deliver two to three cases a month, while the website, reviews, and ad accounts that should be adding more are dated, low, or blocked. Closing that gap is what stands between today’s volume and the 10 to 15 cases a month he wants.
