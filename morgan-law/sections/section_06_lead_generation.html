Lead Generation

Morgan Law’s lead generation has run almost entirely on referrals and a freeway billboard, alongside self-managed PPC and Local Service Ads campaigns that produced cases in the past. Today a dated website, a review base you describe as low, and an ad account blocked by a crawler error stand between the firm and the 10 to 15 cases a month David wants.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The site does not appear to have been meaningfully updated since about 2019 — the newest blog post we found is from April 2019, and you described the last update the same way
  • OpenAI’s ad verification reported that the site blocks crawler access, which stopped your ChatGPT Ads account from being approved last night
  • Dedicated location pages exist for Boise, Middleton, and Lehi, Utah — but none for Nampa, Meridian, Caldwell, Eastern Idaho, or Twin Falls, the markets you told us you want to win
  • The contact form lives on a separate page, and no video of the attorney was observed on the homepage
Blind Spot: Your trigger domain, morganinjuryfirm.com, redirects to a second domain, morganinjurylawyer.com. Ad platforms and AI crawlers check the landing page they are sent to, so the redirect needs to be part of the crawler fix — not an afterthought.
The Positive
  • A dedicated attorney page showcases your credentials and 20-plus years of experience — a trust signal many firms lack
  • A clear “Book My Free Consultation” button and a 24/7 phone number sit above the fold
The Gap
  • Searchers in Canyon County, Eastern Idaho, and Twin Falls land on a site with no page written for their market
  • Until the crawler block is fixed, ChatGPT Ads — a channel you believe no one in the Boise area is using yet — cannot run to this site
  • A site that looks untouched since 2019 gives a first-time visitor little reason to trust it over a competitor’s
A website that can carry paid traffic and AI search is the foundation for a firm that no longer depends on referrals.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • You described your Google review count as low for a firm that has been in this market for decades, and said it is something you need to work on
  • In the web results we reviewed, Morgan Law did not appear for “car accident lawyer Nampa Idaho” or “personal injury attorney Boise Idaho” — the firm showed up only when we searched for it by name
  • Directory listings show more than one address for the Lehi, Utah office, and the website lists two different phone numbers — possible inconsistencies that weaken local search signals
The Positive
  • Branded searches return your own site along with Avvo, Lawyers.com, and FindLaw listings, so people who already know your name can find you
The Gap
  • People who do not already know your name — the ones searching for an accident lawyer for the first time — are finding other firms first
  • Without market-specific pages and a stronger review base, it is hard to be “the number one guy” in Canyon County, where your firm is based
Andrew Injury Law, PC — based in Nampa with dedicated car, truck, motorcycle, and bus accident pages, giving it a direct local-search advantage in your home market.
Showing up first for your own hometown is the first step toward a firm that grows on a plan instead of on referrals.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • You told us that, apart from the billboard, you have not advertised in roughly the last five years — so the firm does not appear to be capturing paid search traffic today
  • Your earlier self-managed PPC campaigns did produce cases, and being an early mover in Idaho paid off — but you described the click costs and overall spend as hard to sustain
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Litster Frost Injury Lawyers — strong Nampa and Treasure Valley injury visibility, so they already compete for the high-intent searches you want.
  • Hepworth Holzer, LLP — an established Boise firm that appears for Nampa car accident searches. Their paid ad copy was not captured in this review.
The Opportunity
  • Core high-intent terms: “car accident lawyer Nampa,” “Nampa personal injury attorney,” and “Boise car accident attorney”
  • Case-type terms that match what you handle: truck accident, motorcycle accident, and traumatic brain injury searches
  • Neighboring-city terms such as Caldwell, Meridian, and Middleton, plus urgency searches like “hit by a truck what do I do”
  • Expansion markets you named — Eastern Idaho and Twin Falls — where a focused campaign can build presence before larger firms settle in
Blind Spot: A billboard mostly reaches people who already know you and are ready to call. The people searching Google for an accident lawyer for the first time never see it — and they are the ones who grow the firm.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • You told us you do not believe the firm shows up in Local Service Ads today, even in your own hometown
  • Google Screened status and LSA reviews were not observed in this review, so this is an opportunity gap to close rather than a confirmed absence
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Litster Frost Injury Lawyers — competes for the same Treasure Valley injury searches; LSA placements are earned through reviews and responsiveness.
  • Andrew Injury Law, PC — a Nampa-based firm with dedicated car and truck accident pages, which makes it a natural competitor for Canyon County lead spots.
The Opportunity
  • You were among the early movers on LSAs in Idaho — returning with professional management and a stronger review base puts that first-mover instinct back to work
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Every new Google review improves your odds of earning and holding a top LSA position, so reviews and LSAs reinforce each other

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • Active Facebook or Instagram ads for the firm were not observed, and Meta advertising did not come up on the call
  • That leaves a gap in staying in front of the people who visit your site, call once, or search and do not convert right away
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Litster Frost Injury Lawyers — Ad Library activity could not be verified in this review
  • Hepworth Holzer, LLP — Ad Library activity could not be verified in this review
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • A video introducing you, your 20-plus years in Idaho, and your commitment to local families builds the trust that makes a first call easy — especially in Eastern Idaho and Twin Falls, where people may not know your name
