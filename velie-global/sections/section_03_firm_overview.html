Here Is Where Velie Global Law Firm Stands Today

Velie Global Law Firm operates two very different practices under one roof: a tech-driven immigration practice in Norman, OK built on a proven intake system, and a full-service litigation practice in Dallas, TX built almost entirely on word-of-mouth referrals. Jon Velie has grown both from the ground up, but the Dallas office's growth has outpaced its systems, leaving him personally handling daily casework instead of running the firm as its CEO. Pam Velie was recently brought on as Chief Growth Officer to help build the operational foundation the firm needs before it can expand into new markets like New York and San Francisco.

Stage 6 is where Dallas runs the way Norman already does — on defined systems, not on Jon personally — freeing him to lead the firm's expansion instead of carrying its daily casework.

Where You Are Today

Stage 4: Small Business Manager
  • The Norman, OK office has a proven, tech-driven intake system — a visas.ai-driven strategy process plus a dedicated closer — but the Dallas office has none of it
  • The Dallas office runs on referrals only, with an informal, attorney-led intake process and no documented follow-up
  • Jon Velie personally handles daily litigation casework in Dallas, leaving little room to lead firm strategy or the planned expansion into New York and San Francisco
  • Profitability is unknown firm-wide — there is no financial tracking system in place to show what the firm actually keeps, only a stated goal to reach 20–30% margin within 12 months

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between Dallas today and Dallas running itself is not a mystery — it is the exact system Norman already proved works, applied to the office that needs it most before any new market is considered.
