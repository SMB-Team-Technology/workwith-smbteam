Lead Generation

Reneau Bays' marketing today is almost entirely referral-driven — "eat what you kill" partner books with no active paid search, LSA, or social advertising running. Meanwhile, the firm's online presence is split across two unmerged Google Business Profiles and legacy directory listings that still point to a pre-merger identity.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • Practice area pages like the Family Law/Divorce page use titles and headlines that are not geo-optimized ("Divorce — Reneau | Bays" / "Family Law & Divorce") instead of the terms people actually search, like "Oklahoma City Family Law Attorney" or "Oklahoma City Divorce Lawyer"
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile — not yet captured — Desktop — not yet captured (live testing was rate-limited during research; recommend running a live check before finalizing strategy)
  • No contact form is visible on the homepage — visitors must click through to a separate /contact page or rely on phone or email
  • The site lists five surrounding service-area cities (Norman, Edmond, Moore, Midwest City, Yukon) only as a single line of footer text, with zero dedicated, geo-targeted landing pages to actually rank for those markets
Blind Spot: Your practice-area content is actually more substantive than most competitors' — the divorce page covers real procedural depth (filing, temporary orders, discovery, mediation, trial, appeals) — but because the titles and headlines aren't built around the keywords people actually search, none of that depth is showing up in search results.
The Positive
  • Attorney bios are already in place through a dedicated "Our Team" page in the main navigation
  • A clear call-to-action ("Schedule a Consultation") repeats multiple times down the homepage
The Gap
  • Without geo-optimized titles and headlines, the site is invisible in the exact searches — "Oklahoma City family law attorney," "Oklahoma City divorce lawyer" — where Lai & Turner and Cannon & Associates already rank
  • Every visitor searching for a city page that doesn't exist (Norman, Edmond, Moore) is a lead going to a competitor who built one
  • Every day without a live PageSpeed check is a day of guessing instead of knowing whether slow load times are costing mobile leads
Every lead lost to a competitor's better-optimized page is a case Danny needs toward his $250K goal — and a case Roger doesn't have the associates yet to take on anyway.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The firm operates two separate, unmerged Google Business Profiles — one for Roger Reneau/Reneau Law Group, one for Bays Law Firm PC — splitting reviews and search visibility instead of consolidating them into one
  • Roger's GBP is categorized as "Bankruptcy Attorney" rather than "Family Law Attorney," actively working against the practice area that is actually the firm's primary focus
  • Reneau Bays does not appear among top-cited firms in aggregator "best of" roundups for "Oklahoma City divorce lawyer" or "Oklahoma City family law attorney," while Lai & Turner and Cannon & Associates do
Blind Spot: Having two GBPs isn't just confusing for prospects — it actively splits your review count and local ranking signals in half, when competitors are carrying their full weight on one profile.
The Gap
  • Fragmented profiles and NAP inconsistencies across Avvo, Justia, FindLaw, Martindale, and BBB (a legacy "Reneau & Childers" address, mismatched suite numbers) actively confuse both Google's algorithm and prospects trying to verify you're a real, current practice
  • Every month the two GBPs stay unmerged and miscategorized is a month competitors with cleaner, single-brand profiles keep pulling further ahead in local search — and the firm carries far fewer reviews across its two fragmented listings than several named competitors in this market
Lai & Turner Law Firm PLLC — an existing SMB Team client with a dramatically larger review footprint and a single, unified brand presence that is actively out-ranking Reneau Bays' fragmented profiles for the same family law searches.
Merging and correcting these profiles is the fastest, lowest-cost step toward the predictable, referral-independent pipeline both partners need before Danny can hit $250K or Roger can justify bringing on associates.

  • No current paid search activity was identified for probate-related keywords either
Competitor Ad Landscape

The Opportunity
  • "Oklahoma City divorce lawyer" and "Oklahoma City family law attorney" — the exact terms the discovery call flagged Reneau Bays as invisible for
  • "Oklahoma City probate attorney" — a practice area Roger already has real depth in but isn't capturing in search
  • Bidding on "Lai & Turner" and "Cannon & Associates" brand terms to intercept prospects who are already comparing options
  • "Edmond family law attorney" and "Norman divorce lawyer" — neighboring cities the firm already serves but has no dedicated content for
Blind Spot: Every day without Google Ads running, Jimmy Lai — the exact competitor you named as your reason for this call — is capturing searches your own reputation should be winning.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Reneau Bays has not yet implemented Local Services Ads — this is a forward plan already identified on the call ("Start with Google Local Services Ads — family law/probate focus — then PPC as needed"), not a channel in place today
  • Google Screened status has not yet been established
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Lai & Turner Law Firm PLLC — carries a review volume that typically qualifies for strong LSA placement, a bar Reneau Bays' fragmented combined reviews does not yet clear
  • Cannon & Associates — similarly well-reviewed and positioned to compete for LSA placement in the same market
The Opportunity
  • LSA is already the plan you and Dan discussed — family law and probate focus first — and it is the fastest, lowest-risk channel to launch because you only pay for calls, not clicks
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Once merged and optimized, a single unified GBP feeds directly into LSA eligibility — tying the profile cleanup and LSA launch together as one project, not two
Blind Spot: LSA eligibility is tied directly to your GBP review count and category — meaning the GBP cleanup already planned is a prerequisite, not a side project.
