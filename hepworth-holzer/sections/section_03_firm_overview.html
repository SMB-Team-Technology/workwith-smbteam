Here Is Where Hepworth Holzer, LLP Stands Today

Hepworth Holzer, LLP is a six-attorney personal injury firm in Boise, Idaho built primarily on referrals from a strong professional network, supplemented by a modest, self-managed $4,000/month Google and Microsoft Ads budget targeting hyper-specific local keywords. The firm is actively working to shift its case mix away from lower-value pre-litigation matters toward premium trucking and auto litigation, and just ran its first self-directed ChatGPT ad test — a smart, early move that, without refined targeting, produced five inquiries that were the wrong fit. The single most important finding: the firm has no lead-source attribution, so it cannot tell which of its channels — referral, PPC, or the new AI-ad experiment — is actually producing the premium cases it wants more of.

Stage 6 is the stage where the firm's competitive position is no longer dependent on any one partner's day-to-day involvement — and it is exactly the foundation you are trying to build for the next generation of partners at Hepworth Holzer.

Where You Are Today

STAGE 4: Small Business Manager
  • Referrals from a strong professional network are the primary source of new clients, with a self-managed $4K/month Google and Microsoft Ads campaign as the only other running system
  • No lead-source attribution exists, so no one at the firm can say which channel is actually producing the trucking and auto cases it wants more of
  • Kurt personally initiated and is personally driving the firm's first AI-ad experiment, rather than a dedicated marketing function
  • Roughly half of the current 150-160 case load is lower-value pre-litigation work the firm is actively trying to shift away from

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between where Hepworth Holzer stands today and Stage 6 is not a talent gap or a reputation gap — it is a systems and attribution gap, and closing it is exactly what protects the firm's edge for the partners who come after Kurt.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Hepworth Holzer, LLP
Hepworth Holzer is operating at Stage 4 — a Small Business Manager with one real, scalable lead gen system (its self-run PPC) layered on top of a strong referral base, but with Kurt still personally driving every new marketing initiative himself. The single most urgent gap limiting growth right now is the total absence of lead-source attribution: without it, the firm cannot prove out whether its ChatGPT ad experiment, its existing PPC, or referrals are actually the channel winning the premium trucking and auto cases it says it wants more of. Until that gap closes, every new channel the firm tries — including the AI-search white space Kurt is right to want to claim early — will keep repeating the same poor-fit result the first ChatGPT ad test produced.
