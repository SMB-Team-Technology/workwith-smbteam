Intake Assessment

Hepworth Holzer can "immediately handle an influx of up to 50 new cases," so intake capacity is not the constraint — but with no system tagging leads to their source, intake cannot tell whether a case came from referral, existing PPC, or the new ChatGPT ad test. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating. This is where the biggest immediate ROI lives.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • No lead-source attribution at intake — a case cannot be tagged back to referral, PPC, or a new-channel test
  • The first self-directed ChatGPT ad test produced 5 inquiries in one weekend, but intake had no qualification filter tuned for the firm's target case types, and all 5 were poor-fit
  • ~80 of the current ~150-160 cases are lower-value pre-litigation work — consistent with intake accepting whatever comes in rather than filtering for the premium litigation the firm says it wants more of
  • No after-hours coverage details were discussed on the call, but the firm's website shows no live chat, which is one more point where an after-hours inquiry could go unanswered
Blind Spot: The firm's ChatGPT ad test technically "worked" — it generated inquiries — but without a qualification filter tuned to premium case types, generating the wrong leads can look like a failed experiment when it's really an intake targeting problem.
The Positive
  • The firm has enough operational capacity to absorb a significant volume increase without adding staff — a rare position that most growing firms don't have
The Gap
  • Without attribution, every new lead-gen dollar spent — including the entire ChatGPT ad experiment — is generating cases the firm can't confidently evaluate or improve on next time
  • Accepting lower-value pre-litigation cases by default works against the firm's own stated goal of shifting toward premium trucking and auto litigation
An intake process that can tell you where a case came from is what turns "we tried a ChatGPT ad once" into a system the next generation of partners can actually build on.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • Contact form appears above the fold in the header/hero and repeats at multiple points down the homepage — a strong existing conversion structure
  • No live chat observed on the site, meaning after-hours visitors have only the form or phone number to reach the firm
  • "Request Free Video Call" option alongside the standard "Free Case Evaluation" CTA gives prospects a lower-friction way to engage before committing to a full consult

Team Assessment

Hepworth Holzer has real leadership depth — six named attorneys running the firm — but every new growth initiative, including the ChatGPT ad test, is currently being driven by Kurt personally. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • No dedicated marketing, intake, or ops role was mentioned on the call — six attorneys, no non-attorney function named for growth initiatives
  • Kurt personally launched and is personally evaluating the firm's newest channel test rather than delegating it
  • No lead-source attribution means no one on the team, including Kurt, can currently measure whether a new initiative worked
  • Roles beyond "attorney" were not detailed on the call — it's unclear who, if anyone, owns marketing decisions day to day
Blind Spot: Being the one who tests every new channel personally feels like diligence — but it also means the firm's growth ceiling is Kurt's personal bandwidth, which is the opposite of what he says he wants to hand the next generation of partners.
The Positive
  • Six named partners (J. Charles Hepworth, Kurt D. Holzer, John T. Edwards, Andrew LaPorta, Whitney Welsh, Ryan Black) give the firm genuine bench strength most solo and small firms don't have
  • Kurt is already thinking in succession terms — proactively testing new channels "to avoid falling behind competitors and ensure a strong future for the firm's younger partners"
The Gap
  • Owner-driven channel testing costs Kurt the time he's trying to free up for the next generation, and it caps how many new initiatives the firm can run at once
  • Without a dedicated marketing/ops owner, new experiments like the ChatGPT ad will keep repeating the same untargeted first attempt
The firm you're protecting for the next generation of partners needs growth initiatives that don't depend on you personally running point on every one of them.

Profit Plan Assessment

Hepworth Holzer is already making the profit-smart move of shifting case mix toward premium trucking and auto litigation, but with no lead-source attribution, it can't yet prove which dollars are driving that shift. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • No revenue or per-case profitability figures were shared on the call
  • No lead-source attribution means true cost-per-case by channel is unknown — the firm cannot compare what a referral case costs to acquire versus a PPC case versus a ChatGPT-ad case
  • ~80 of ~150-160 current cases are the lower-value pre-litigation work the firm is trying to move away from, without a tracked number on the margin difference
Blind Spot: Shifting toward premium trucking and auto cases is the right instinct, but without cost-per-case-by-channel data, it's possible to spend more acquiring a "premium" case than the case mix improvement is actually worth.
The Positive
  • The firm is already acting on the right profit lever — deliberately shifting case mix toward higher-value litigation — before being told to
The Gap
  • Without attribution, the firm cannot confirm whether its $4K/month ad spend is efficiently driving the case-mix shift it wants, or just adding volume
  • No visibility into whether the ChatGPT ad test's poor-fit result was a targeting problem or a genuinely bad channel fit — the data to tell the difference doesn't exist yet
Knowing your real profit per case by channel is what turns "we're shifting toward premium cases" into a plan the next generation of partners can trust and build on.
