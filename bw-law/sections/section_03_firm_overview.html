Here Is Where BW Law, PLLC Stands Today

Sarah Bai built BW Law, PLLC into a respected Chinese/English bilingual immigration practice in Gainesville with a 5.0-star reputation, referral relationships, and a strong Google Business Profile — and now she is the firm's owner, lead attorney, and de facto operations department all at once. With one part-time legal assistant, a small bench of contractors and partner attorneys for overflow, and no delegated system for reviewing their work, the single most important finding is this: the firm's growth ceiling right now is not leads — it is Sarah's personal bandwidth to hire, train, and oversee a team capable of taking work off her plate.

Stage 6 is the stage where BW Law runs on trusted systems instead of Sarah's personal review of every file — freeing her time for the business development and networking she says she wants more of, instead of babysitting contractor work.

Where You Are Today

STAGE 4: SMALL BUSINESS MANAGER
  • Google Business Profile, referrals, and local networking generate a consistent flow of leads — the firm is not starting from zero
  • Sarah personally reviews all contractor and partner-attorney work before it goes out the door
  • Only one part-time legal assistant exists to support a firm targeting $500k in revenue — there is no hiring system to change that
  • Sarah describes herself as "chief everything officer," working 40-50+ hours a week across legal work, oversight, and admin

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between where BW Law is today and where Sarah wants it to be is not a leads problem — it is a hiring and systems problem, and closing it is exactly what will let her trade contractor quality-control for the growth activities that actually move the firm toward $500k.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
BW Law, PLLC
BW Law is operating at Stage 4 — a Small Business Manager with at least one working lead source (GBP, referrals, and local networking) but no team structure strong enough to support the growth Sarah is targeting. The single most urgent gap limiting growth right now is hiring and delegation: with only one part-time legal assistant and no repeatable process for bringing on and training staff, every contractor's work still runs through Sarah personally. Until that changes, scaling from ~$200k to the stated $500k goal will mean scaling Sarah's hours right along with it — not the outcome she is asking for.
