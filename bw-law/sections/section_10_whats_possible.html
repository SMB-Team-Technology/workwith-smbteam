Here's What's Possible for BW Law, PLLC

Sarah has already done the hardest part — she built a 5.0-star bilingual immigration practice that clients trust and refer their friends and family to. The question is not whether she can grow to $500k. It is whether she can get there without becoming even more of a "chief everything officer" than she already is. With the right hiring systems and the right team behind her, she does not have to choose between growing the firm and getting her time back.

1
Contractors and new hires get trained and reviewed through a system — not through hours of Sarah's personal attention on every file.
2
Sarah spends her reclaimed time on business development and networking — the activities she said she wants more room for — instead of quality-checking contractor work.
3
The path from ~$200k to $500k is backed by a team and a set of systems built to handle the volume — not just a hope that referrals keep pace.

This is not a fantasy. SMB Team can make this a reality for BW Law, PLLC.
