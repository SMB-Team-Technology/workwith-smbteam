Here Is Where Mountain State Attorneys Stands Today

Joshua Kotter runs Mountain State Attorneys as a solo generalist attorney handling business & corporate, criminal defense, estate planning, family law, and several other practice areas out of a single Salt Lake City office, supported by one paralegal. He has grown revenue from roughly $250K in 2024 to a projected $400K in 2026 largely on personal reputation and a 96% close rate, but has no team, no consistent marketing engine, and no systems in place to support his stated goal of reaching $1M+ in revenue within 12 months.

Stage 6 is where the empire Josh describes — thousands of attorneys, decades from now — actually becomes possible, because it is the stage where the firm runs on systems and people instead of on him personally.

Where You Are Today

STAGE 3: SOLO PRACTITIONER
  • Josh personally handles nearly all casework and client intake, with only one paralegal for support
  • Revenue has grown from ~$250K (2024) to a projected ~$400K (2026), but the growth has outpaced the systems needed to sustain it
  • Google LSAs — the firm's only paid lead channel — have been paused for 5+ weeks to manage budget, leaving referrals as the primary source of new clients
  • The firm receives ~6 landlord-tenant calls a day that it currently turns away, an unmonetized opportunity sitting in plain sight

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you

  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
Closing that gap starts with building the foundation — systems, hiring, and intake — before pouring more fuel on the fire, exactly the phased plan Josh is already asking for.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.
