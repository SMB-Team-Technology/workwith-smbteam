Lead Generation

Mountain State Attorneys currently has no active paid lead generation running — Google Local Service Ads, the firm's only tested channel, have been paused for over five weeks to manage budget. With no local search visibility across three of its most consumer-facing practice areas, the firm is relying almost entirely on referrals and the organic overflow of landlord-tenant calls it currently declines.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • PageSpeed scores could not be obtained this session (the API was rate-limited across four attempts) — to be confirmed live before finalizing marketing tactics
  • The contact form ("Send Us a Message") appears mid-page and in the footer, but not above the fold on first load
  • No live chat exists anywhere on the site, and no video content is present
  • Attorney bios are thin — only Josh is featured; the site implies multiple attorneys handle separate practice areas, but none of their bios, credentials, or names are disclosed
Blind Spot: The website implies a multi-attorney team handling eight distinct practice areas, but a prospect researching a specific practice area may not realize there is no attorney bio to build trust in that specialty before they call.
The Positive
  • Josh's own bio is strong — J.D./MBA from a top-30 program, Beta Gamma Sigma honors, and a prior C-suite background — credibility that isn't being leveraged anywhere else on the site
  • Practice-area blogs already exist for five of the firm's areas (Business & Corporate, Criminal Defense, Estate Planning, Family Law, and Securities & Investment)
The Gap
  • A buried contact form and no live chat means visitors who arrive ready to act have to hunt for a way to reach the firm
  • Thin attorney presentation undercuts the credibility Josh has actually earned, at the exact moment a prospect is deciding whether to call
  • No PageSpeed data means the firm cannot yet know whether slow load times are quietly costing it visitors before they ever see the site
A website that matches the credibility Josh has already built is the first brick in the foundation his empire needs.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The firm's own Google Business Profile could not be rendered directly this session, but the best available proxies show a thin presence: Elite Litigators shows 3.9/5 (73% exceptional / 27% substandard), and the Facebook business page shows "not yet rated" with only 1 review
  • Across all 9 local searches run for Family Law, Criminal Defense, and Estate Planning, Mountain State Attorneys did not surface in the results returned
  • No directory presence was confirmed on Justia or FindLaw, and the firm's Avvo profile (Joshua Rich Kotter) shows "Not Yet Reviewed," a 0.0 rating, and no practice areas listed
Blind Spot: A prospect who searches Avvo specifically to vet an attorney before calling will find a blank, unreviewed profile — even though Josh has a 96% close rate once someone actually gets him on the phone.
The Positive
  • NAP (name, address, phone) is consistent between the website and Facebook — a clean foundation to build on once the listings themselves are filled out
The Gap
  • Zero local-pack visibility across three consumer practice areas means the firm is invisible at the exact moment someone searches for help
  • An unreviewed Avvo profile and single-digit review count erode trust before a prospect ever picks up the phone
Susanne Gustin, Attorney at Law — 290 Birdeye reviews and a 4.8★ rating built over years of consistent review generation, precisely what's missing from Mountain State Attorneys' current footprint.
Local visibility is the difference between being found by the clients who fund the empire and being invisible while competitors capture them.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Mountain State Attorneys does not appear to be running Google Search ads for Family Law, Criminal Defense, or Estate Planning based on the searches conducted this session
  • This may reflect being outbid or simply not configured for these terms rather than a confirmed absence — worth confirming live
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • JR Law Group and Intermountain Legal — the most likely spenders in family law search, given their review velocity and "Best of Salt Lake City" recognition
  • Wasatch Defense Lawyers and Susanne Gustin — the most visible organic competitors in criminal defense and plausible ad spenders
The Opportunity
  • "family law attorney Salt Lake City," "criminal defense lawyer Salt Lake City Utah," "estate planning attorney Salt Lake City" — the exact terms the firm did not surface for organically
  • "divorce lawyer," "child custody attorney," "DUI attorney," "wills and trusts lawyer," "probate attorney" — specific-case-type searches that convert at a higher rate than generic terms
  • Competitor-name keywords (bidding on named competitors like Susanne Gustin and JR Law Group) to intercept prospects already comparing options
  • Landlord-tenant referral-adjacent keywords could route the ~6 daily calls the firm already declines toward a referral-fee pipeline instead of a dead end
Blind Spot: Six landlord-tenant calls arrive on this firm's phone every single day with no ad spend at all — the demand already exists; the firm just has not built a way to monetize the calls it declines.
