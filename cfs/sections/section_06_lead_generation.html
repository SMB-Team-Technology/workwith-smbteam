Lead Generation

CFS Attorneys has never run paid advertising, never built a local SEO strategy, and doesn't surface in organic search for either its cannabis-law specialty or its broader business-law work — despite being an active, established boutique in Northampton. New clients come entirely through referral relationships partners have built over years, with no system in place to generate more.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • Contact form is buried at the bottom of the homepage under "Contact Us" — not visible above the fold, adding friction for a prospect ready to reach out
  • PageSpeed scores: not able to be captured this session — Google's API rate-limited every attempt (4 tries); recommend a live check before the proposal call rather than presenting an unverified number
  • Blog has not been updated since March 21, 2023 — over three years of inactivity that undercuts topical authority in cannabis law specifically
  • No live chat and no video content anywhere on the site, despite five attorney bios with photos already in place to build on
Blind Spot: The website's footer copyright reads "2024," suggesting a recent template refresh — but a recent redesign paired with a three-year-dormant blog and no cannabis-first positioning means the update didn't touch what actually drives search visibility.
The Positive
  • Five attorney bios are already live with photos and expertise descriptions — most firms have to build this from scratch
  • Homepage has a clear, above-the-fold CTA ("Learn More" and "Contact Us")
The Gap
  • Every visitor who wants to reach out has to scroll past the entire homepage to find the contact form
  • A dormant blog signals to both prospects and search engines that the firm isn't actively engaged in its own specialty
  • No mechanism exists to capture a visitor who isn't ready to fill out a form right now — no live chat, no click-to-call prominence
A modern, AI-forward firm needs a website that works as hard as the partners do — right now, this one is just a static brochure.

Every month CFS stays invisible in local search is a month a referral-only firm stays entirely dependent on the relationships partners already have — with no new ones being built.
