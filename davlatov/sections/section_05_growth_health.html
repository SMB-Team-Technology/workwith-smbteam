Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • No paid advertising presence has been confirmed on Google, LSA, or Meta for any of the firm's three practice areas
  • Zero search or directory footprint exists in Personal Injury or Family Law — the two areas the firm is counting on to replace shrinking immigration revenue
  • No neighborhood or borough-specific landing pages exist despite the firm claiming to serve "clients worldwide"
Without a real lead generation system, the immigration slowdown becomes Mustafo's problem to personally outwork — not a business problem the firm can solve.

Intake

  • Missed calls are a stated, active issue — described on the call as causing lost leads and wasted marketing spend
  • Lawmatics (the firm's CRM) is in place but underutilized, with no comprehensive system for intake, client management, or document handling
  • No live chat or after-hours capture exists on the website beyond a WhatsApp link
Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Self-Managing Team

  • The firm is described on the call as "entirely dependent on the owner," with Mustafo working 12-hour days
  • No delegation structure exists — casework, intake, and client relationships all route through Mustafo personally
  • There is no manager or lead attorney in place who can run a case or a client relationship without him
Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Profit

  • Revenue of roughly $50K/month "now only covers expenses" — the firm is at breakeven, not profitable
  • Approximately $1M in uncollected client debt is creating a real cash-flow crunch
  • The firm has begun requiring upfront payment on new cases as a defensive measure, but has no broader collections or profit-planning system
A firm that only breaks even cannot fund the freedom Mustafo is working 12-hour days to build.
