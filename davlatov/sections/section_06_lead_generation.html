Lead Generation

Davlatov Law Firm has built its entire client base around directory listings, word of mouth, and a loyal Central Asian/Russian-speaking community — with no confirmed paid advertising system running on any channel. That approach worked while immigration case volume was strong, but it leaves the firm's two planned growth areas, Personal Injury and Family Law, without a single lead generation system in place.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • PageSpeed scores could not be captured during this research pass due to a third-party API quota limit — recommend a quick manual check at pagespeed.web.dev before the proposal call if a hard number is needed
  • No video content exists anywhere on the site, despite Mustafo's personal immigrant background being a strong trust-building story for this client base
  • The blog's most recent posts are dated November 2024 — roughly 20 months stale as of this audit
  • No neighborhood-specific landing pages exist (no dedicated pages for Bensonhurst, Sheepshead Bay, or other Brooklyn areas) despite the firm positioning itself as serving "clients worldwide"
Blind Spot: The phone number listed in the firm's CRM for this deal — a Florida-area-code mobile number — does not match the (718) number listed on the website and directories. If that mobile number is being used for call tracking or client callbacks, calls may not be routing or attributing the way the firm expects.
The Positive
  • The site includes a genuine attorney bio highlighting Mustafo's 10+ years of immigration experience and his own immigrant background — strong trust-building content for this audience
  • A full Russian-language mirror of the site (/ru/) is a real asset most competitors serving this community do not have
The Gap
  • A stale blog and no video content mean prospective Personal Injury and Family Law clients have nothing on the site to build trust in those practice areas specifically
  • Without neighborhood-specific pages, the firm is invisible in local searches from the very Brooklyn neighborhoods it already serves
A website that only tells the immigration story cannot carry the weight of the pivot Mustafo is counting on to reduce his personal dependence on a shrinking practice area.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Google Business Profile shows a 4.7-star rating with roughly 90–190 reviews depending on the source — solid, but already trailing the top named immigration competitor in the market
  • For "personal injury lawyer Brooklyn NY" and "family law attorney Brooklyn NY," Davlatov was not observed appearing in directory or search results reviewed — despite live service pages existing for both
  • No city- or neighborhood-specific landing pages exist, limiting local pack visibility even within the firm's own established immigration category
Blind Spot: A competing immigration practice was found directly on Bay Parkway — the same street as Davlatov's own office — meaning the firm is losing walk-by and hyper-local search visibility in its own backyard, not just to larger citywide firms.
The Positive
  • The firm has a reasonably broad directory footprint for immigration specifically — active profiles on Avvo, Justia, Birdeye, Martindale, and others
The Gap
  • Zero local SEO footprint in Personal Injury or Family Law means every dollar of organic authority the firm has built is locked inside a shrinking practice area
  • A well-established competitor with more reviews is closing in on the one category where Davlatov currently has real standing
Gursoy Law Firm, P.C. — 5.0 stars with 270+ reviews, outranking Davlatov's own immigration listing on both rating and review volume in directory and search results reviewed.
Every month Personal Injury and Family Law stay invisible in local search is a month Mustafo has to personally cover the gap left by fewer immigration cases.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Davlatov Law Firm does not appear to be running visible Google search ads for immigration, Personal Injury, or Family Law terms based on the research completed — this may reflect an actual gap, or live-SERP tooling limitations in this research pass may simply be obscuring it
  • The transcript itself references "wasted marketing spend" tied to missed calls, implying some marketing investment has been made historically — but no platform, budget, or timeframe was confirmed on the call
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Gursoy Law Firm & Alan Lee & Arthur Lee (Immigration) — both rank prominently in organic and directory search, consistent with firms that also tend to invest in paid search visibility in this category
  • Karasik Law Group & The Orlow Firm (Personal Injury) — established, marketing-forward Brooklyn PI practices; PI is historically one of the most heavily paid-search-contested legal categories in NYC
The Opportunity
  • "Personal injury lawyer Brooklyn," "car accident lawyer Brooklyn," and "family law attorney Brooklyn" — all currently uncontested by Davlatov despite live service pages already existing
  • "Immigration lawyer Bensonhurst" and other hyper-local Brooklyn neighborhood terms, where the firm's physical office is a real advantage over citywide competitors
  • Business and employment-based immigration terms — the transcript names this as "a less volatile area with high-value clients," and it is currently uncontested in the research reviewed
  • Competitor-name and "near me" campaigns capturing prospects already comparing Davlatov against Gursoy, Karasik, and Louis Law Firm
Blind Spot: The firm's own client base of 2,000–3,000 existing immigration clients is a warm audience list that paid search cannot reach on its own — but paired with the right retargeting and referral campaign, it is a built-in advantage none of the named competitors have.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • The firm does not appear to be showing in Local Service Ads or carrying the Google Screened badge based on the research completed — live LSA widget rendering was not available in this research pass, so this should be treated as an opportunity gap rather than a confirmed absence
  • No LSA activity was observed for the firm in Immigration, Personal Injury, or Family Law
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Named local competitors in this research pass — live LSA rendering was not available with the tools used, so specific competitor LSA review counts could not be confirmed; recommend a manual spot check before the call
The Opportunity
  • Google Screened is a strong trust signal in the legal category, especially for a firm whose reputation already rests on trust within a specific immigrant community
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • With a 4.7-star rating and 90+ reviews already in place, Davlatov already qualifies on reputation for a strong LSA presence — the gap is enrollment, not credibility
Blind Spot: Pay-per-lead pricing on LSA means the firm would only pay for calls that come in — a meaningful consideration given the firm's current cash-flow constraints and the risk aversion that comes with $1M in uncollected receivables.
