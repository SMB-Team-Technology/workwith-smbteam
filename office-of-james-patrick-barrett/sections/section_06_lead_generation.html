Lead Generation

The firm's website at bllegal.com presents a 35-year Tallahassee injury practice with seven attorneys, video, and multi-million-dollar case results, which is a strong base for lead generation. This review looked at the public web presence only, so the sections below focus on what could be measured and flag what still needs to be confirmed on the call.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The mobile experience is the biggest weakness: the homepage takes about 21 seconds to show its main content on a phone in a Lighthouse mobile test
  • Lighthouse performance scores: Mobile 33/100 — Desktop 94/100. Mobile blocking time of roughly 2 seconds means the page feels frozen while it loads
  • The free consultation form sits mid-page and again lower down, not above the fold, so a visitor has to scroll to find it
  • The form tells visitors that an attorney will reach out within 24 to 48 hours, which sets a slow expectation at the exact moment a prospect is ready to act
Blind Spot: Desktop performance is excellent, so the site looks fast when reviewed on an office computer. Injury searches skew heavily to phones, and that is exactly where the experience falls short.
The Positive
  • Individual pages for seven attorneys, featured case results from $2.0M to $5.8M, a video library and client testimonials build real trust
  • A click-to-call number appears above the fold and is advertised as available 24/7, with a clear no-recovery, no-fee message
The Gap
  • A visitor on a phone who waits that long for the page to appear is likely to return to the search results and call the next firm
  • A 24 to 48 hour promise invites a prospect to keep shopping while competitors answer in minutes
  • Strong credibility on the page is not converting as well as it could when the page is slow to show it
A website that loads fast on every phone turns the reputation the firm has already earned into signed cases, without adding any new marketing spend.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The firm's Google Business Profile, its review count and its position in the Google map pack could not be viewed in this review, so these need a live check before the call
  • The firm name appears in several forms across the web, including "Brooks, LeBoeuf, Foster & Gwartney," "...Gwartney & Hobbs" and "...Hobbs, Fusco & Richards," and a misspelling appears on the site itself
  • The FindLaw listing appears to be categorized under Criminal Law, although the firm's work is personal injury
Blind Spot: Google rewards consistency. When the firm's name and categories differ from one directory to the next, it weakens the signals that help the firm appear in the map results for searches like "car accident lawyer Tallahassee."
The Positive
  • The firm has listings on Super Lawyers, Avvo, Yelp, Lawyers.com, Law.com, LinkedIn and the Florida Bar, and the Tallahassee street address is consistent where it appears
The Gap
  • Without a single consistent name and the right categories, the firm is harder for Google to connect to its practice areas
  • Several Tallahassee competitors appear in the same results, so even small local visibility gaps can send cases elsewhere
Shapiro Law Firm, P.A. — operates a dedicated Tallahassee injury-lawyer website and has focused on car, truck and motorcycle cases since 1997, which supports strong local relevance for the searches the firm most wants to win.
When the firm owns the top of the Tallahassee map results for its core case types, new-client calls come to the phone instead of going to a competitor.

  • Google Tag Manager is installed on the site, and "Google" is an option on the intake form's "How did you hear about us?" question, so there is a basic structure for tracking where cases come from
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Competitor ad copy and spend in Tallahassee were not observed in this review, so no competitor is credited with ad dominance here
  • The firm's market includes Shapiro Law Firm, The Pelham Law Firm, The Masterson Law Firm and a national brand with a local office, so the field for high-intent searches is crowded
The Opportunity
  • Car accident, truck accident and personal injury searches in Tallahassee, where a prospect is ready to call a lawyer today
  • Nursing home abuse, medical malpractice and slip and fall searches tied to the firm's featured results, which fit higher-value cases
  • Competitor-name and brand-name searches, so the firm is present when a prospect compares Tallahassee injury firms
  • Surrounding-city and South Georgia searches that match the firm's stated service area
