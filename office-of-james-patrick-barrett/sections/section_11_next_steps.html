Quick Wins for BL Legal

Fix the Mobile Website Speed
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
The homepage scores 36 out of 100 on mobile speed against 94 on desktop, and takes about 11.5 seconds to show its main content on a phone. Every visitor from search or ads who gives up waiting is a case that never reaches the phone or the form.
The Opportunity
A mobile-first rebuild makes the firm's results, phone number and free consultation form fast and easy to reach on any device, so traffic from every other channel converts at a higher rate.
Strengthen the Map Pack Position and Google Reviews
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
In a live Google search on our call, BL Legal appeared third in the map pack for the main Tallahassee personal injury search. Systemizing Google review requests is an open priority, and the firms above BL Legal have a considerably larger review base to close in on.
Who Is Doing It Better
Fasig Brooks — held the top map pack position in the same search and carries a considerably larger Google review base.
The Opportunity
A consistent review request program, Google Business Profile management and a directory cleanup so the firm name, categories and details match everywhere prospects look.
Put Strategic Paid Search and Local Service Ads Behind Your Highest-Value Cases
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
Google Ads have been run in-house by one attorney, with some success, but not as a coordinated strategy across car and truck accidents, nursing home abuse, medical malpractice, premises liability and wrongful death. Local Service Ads presence was not observed.
The Opportunity
Campaigns built by case type, with Local Service Ads on top, capture high-intent searches at the moment people choose a lawyer, and keep low-value inquiries out of a litigation-focused firm's intake.
Build an SEO and AI-Search Content Foundation by Case Type
Lead Gen Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
SEO is a long game, and the best-performing page in the current reporting attracts the wrong kind of lead. Traffic that does not sign is cost without return.
Who Is Doing It Better
Scott & Wallace LLP — ranked second in the live map pack search, ahead of BL Legal.
The Opportunity
Dedicated pages and content for each high-value case type, written to be found in Google and in AI answer engines, build the authority that brings in the cases the firm actually wants.
Set a Same-Day Response Standard for New Leads
Intake Quick Win
Why It Matters
The website promises an attorney reply within 24 to 48 hours, and signing a client can take a week or two. New digital leads are comparing several firms and tend to sign with the one that responds first.
The Opportunity
A clear internal standard for first contact on every web, phone and ad lead, with the form promise updated to match, protects the cases that marketing is about to generate.
Build an Intake Playbook for Digital Leads, Including After-Hours
Intake Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
The roughly 40% conversion rate reflects referred leads, and after-hours coverage relies on an answering service. Paid and organic leads need a process built for them.
The Opportunity
A defined follow-up cadence and after-hours screening that matches the firm's selective case criteria turn more of every lead into a signed case without another dollar of ad spend.
Name One Internal Owner for the Marketing-to-Intake Handoff
Team Quick Win
Why It Matters
The firm's marketing function is being built from the ground up, and leads can stall between a marketing team and an intake team when no single person owns the handoff.
The Opportunity
One accountable owner, with a short weekly review of signed cases by source alongside the marketing team, keeps every lead moving and gives leadership a clear picture of results.
Track Cost per Signed Case by Case Type
Profit Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Attribution today leans on a "how did you hear about us?" field, and the average fee per case is lifted by a small number of large outcomes. Without cost per signed case by case type, the budget cannot be steered toward what works.
The Opportunity
Reporting by case type, with lead source recorded on every signed case, shows leadership exactly where each marketing dollar returns the most, so the budget can grow as results are proven.
