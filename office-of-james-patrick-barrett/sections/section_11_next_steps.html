Quick Wins for Office of James Patrick Barrett

Make the Website Fast on Phones
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
The homepage scores 33/100 on mobile in a Lighthouse test, with its main content taking about 21 seconds to appear, while desktop scores 94/100. Injury searches skew mobile, so many visitors may leave before they see the firm's results and phone number.
The Opportunity
Bringing mobile speed in line with desktop means more of the visitors the firm already earns stay long enough to call or submit the form.
Replace the 24-to-48-Hour Promise With Same-Day Response
Intake Quick Win
Why It Matters
The consultation form tells visitors that an attorney will reach out within 24 to 48 hours. A prospect choosing a lawyer after an injury is often contacting several firms, and the first to respond tends to earn the case.
The Opportunity
A same-day response standard, with a clear owner for every web inquiry, turns leads the firm already pays to attract into signed cases without adding marketing spend.
Standardize the Firm Name and Listings
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
The firm appears under several name variations across directories, a name misspelling appears on its own site, and the FindLaw listing appears under Criminal Law rather than personal injury. Inconsistent details weaken the signals Google uses to rank local results.
Who Is Doing It Better
Shapiro Law Firm, P.A. — runs a dedicated Tallahassee injury-lawyer website and has focused on car, truck and motorcycle cases since 1997, which supports clear local relevance.
The Opportunity
One consistent firm name, address, phone and category set across every listing gives the firm a stronger chance to appear in the map results for its core case types.
Confirm and Build Out Paid Search, LSA and Meta Ads
Lead Gen Long-Term Play

Give Marketing and Intake Results a Clear Owner
Team Long-Term Play

With seven attorneys carrying cases, growth numbers such as response time, signed cases and cost per case can easily have no single owner.
The Opportunity

Track Cost Per Signed Case

Verify Google Reviews and Start a Review Program

Put the Consultation Form Above the Fold
Intake Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
The free consultation form sits mid-page and again lower on the homepage, so a visitor on a phone has to scroll to find it, and no instant-response option such as click-to-text was seen.
