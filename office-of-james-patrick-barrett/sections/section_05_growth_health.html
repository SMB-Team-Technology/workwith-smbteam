Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • A visible, professionally built website with attorney bios, video and case results gives the firm a strong asset to build on
  • Mobile speed is a real gap: a Lighthouse performance score of 33 out of 100 on mobile, against 94 on desktop
  • Paid search, Local Service Ads and Facebook and Instagram ad activity were not observed in this review, so they need to be confirmed together
A dependable flow of the right cases is what lets a firm choose growth on its own terms instead of waiting on referrals.

Intake

  • The website's own form promises that an attorney will reach out within 24 to 48 hours
  • The contact form sits mid-page rather than above the fold, and no live-response option was seen on the site
  • The phone line is advertised as available 24/7, which is a strong start that a fast written follow-up would complete
Every inquiry answered faster is a case the firm already paid to attract, and it takes no additional marketing spend.

Self-Managing Team

  • Seven attorneys are listed on the website, which points to a real team beyond a single owner
  • How much of day-to-day management, intake and marketing still runs through the owner is not yet known
  • The firm name appears in several different forms across the web, a sign that brand details are not managed in one place
A team that runs on defined roles and clear accountability is what gives an owner the freedom to step back.

Profit

  • Revenue, margins and acquisition cost per case have not been reviewed yet, so this pillar is rated on limited information
  • Contingency-fee work means cash arrives unevenly, which makes planned profit harder without a clear financial picture
  • Knowing the cost to acquire a signed case is the starting point for deciding how much to invest in growth
Profit that is planned rather than discovered is what turns a busy firm into a valuable one.
