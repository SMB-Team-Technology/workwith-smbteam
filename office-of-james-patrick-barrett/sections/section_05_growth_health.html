Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Nearly every case still arrives through referrals, with no coordinated digital program behind the firm's reputation
  • In a live Google search on our call, BL Legal appeared third in the map pack, behind Fasig Brooks and Scott & Wallace
  • The mobile version of the website scores 36 out of 100 on speed, which weakens every visit sent to it from search or ads
A repeatable client-acquisition engine is what lets BL Legal run as the digital pioneer of this market.

Intake

  • Converting a lead into a signed client can stretch over a week or two
  • The website tells visitors an attorney will reach out within 24 to 48 hours
  • The roughly 40% conversion rate reflects the quality of referred leads, not a proven intake process for new digital leads
Faster intake turns every new lead into the signed-case proof that leadership wants to see.

Self-Managing Team

  • Experienced attorneys and an operations leader are in place, but there is no established marketing function yet
  • Marketing strategy, vendor oversight and reporting sit with a small group of leaders rather than a staffed team
  • Reporting on leads and conversions is still being brought up to a standard leadership can fully rely on
A staffed marketing function lets the firm's digital push run without pulling leadership into every vendor decision.

Profit

  • Revenue is tracking toward roughly $10 million this year, but part of the growth came from lateral attorneys' books of business that do not renew on their own
  • Marketing spend cannot yet be tied to signed cases beyond a "how did you hear about us?" field
  • The average fee per case is lifted by a small number of large outcomes, which makes it a loose guide for planning
Tying every dollar to a signed case is what earns the marketing budget room to grow.
