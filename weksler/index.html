Here Is Where Weksler Law Group Stands Today

Ronald Weksler has built Weksler Law Group into a well-regarded estate planning and timeshare law practice in Hollywood, FL, backed by a confirmed 5.0/5.0 average across 79 Avvo client reviews and an Avvo Client's Choice Award every year from 2017 to 2021. But behind that reputation sits a firm with no paid lead generation of any kind — no LSA, no Google Ads, no Meta ads — and a website Ron already knows is overdue for a full rebuild.

Stage 6 is what makes it possible for Ron to keep traveling roughly half the year without every trip turning into a scramble to answer calls across a 12-hour time difference — and it is exactly where we are taking him.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The homepage's primary CTA ("CALL US NOW!") is immediately followed by a second, competing CTA ("Start my Estate Plan") — splitting visitor attention between two different actions
  • PageSpeed testing could not be completed during this research cycle (Google's PageSpeed Insights API quota was exhausted on repeated attempts) — recommend a live mobile/desktop speed test on the proposal call
  • The contact form is buried in a "Contact Us" section near the bottom of the homepage rather than being visible above the fold
  • No blog or dated content exists anywhere on the site, and there is no video despite Ron's decades of credibility
Blind Spot: Two competing calls-to-action above the fold ("CALL US NOW!" and "Start my Estate Plan") force a visitor to choose between two different actions instead of one clear next step — a conversion-rate killer most owners never notice on their own site.
The Positive
  • Ronald Weksler's attorney bio is already published with real credentials (University of Miami School of Law, Florida Bar member in good standing)
  • A clear, toll-free phone CTA sits above the fold on the homepage
The Gap
  • Every visitor who isn't ready to call right now has almost nowhere else to go — no blog, no video, no downloadable guide — so the site converts only the most decided visitors and loses everyone else
  • A buried contact form means visitors who prefer to fill out a form instead of calling may leave before ever reaching it
  • With PageSpeed unverified, there is no way to know today how many mobile visitors are abandoning the site before it even loads
A website that converts on its own — without Ron manually chasing down every lead — is what lets him step away from the business, not just the marketing.
