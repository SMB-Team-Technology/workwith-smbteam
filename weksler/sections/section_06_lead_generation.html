Lead Generation

Weksler Law Group has never run a single paid lead generation campaign — every client today arrives through referrals or organic reputation. That leaves a firm with a genuinely strong reputation invisible on every channel where today's estate planning and timeshare law clients actually search.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The homepage's primary CTA ("CALL US NOW!") is immediately followed by a second, competing CTA ("Start my Estate Plan") — splitting visitor attention between two different actions
  • PageSpeed testing could not be completed during this research cycle (Google's PageSpeed Insights API quota was exhausted on repeated attempts) — recommend a live mobile/desktop speed test on the proposal call
  • The contact form is buried in a "Contact Us" section near the bottom of the homepage rather than being visible above the fold
  • No blog or dated content exists anywhere on the site, and there is no video despite Ron's decades of credibility
Blind Spot: Two competing calls-to-action above the fold ("CALL US NOW!" and "Start my Estate Plan") force a visitor to choose between two different actions instead of one clear next step — a conversion-rate killer most owners never notice on their own site.
The Positive
  • Ronald Weksler's attorney bio is already published with real credentials (University of Miami School of Law, Florida Bar member in good standing)
  • A clear, toll-free phone CTA sits above the fold on the homepage
The Gap
  • Every visitor who isn't ready to call right now has almost nowhere else to go — no blog, no video, no downloadable guide — so the site converts only the most decided visitors and loses everyone else
  • A buried contact form means visitors who prefer to fill out a form instead of calling may leave before ever reaching it
  • With PageSpeed unverified, there is no way to know today how many mobile visitors are abandoning the site before it even loads
A website that converts on its own — without Ron manually chasing down every lead — is what lets him step away from the business, not just the marketing.
