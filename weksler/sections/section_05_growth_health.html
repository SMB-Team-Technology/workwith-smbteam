Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • No paid lead generation of any kind is currently running — no LSA, no Google Ads, no Meta ads
  • All new client flow depends on referrals and organic reputation, with no backup channel if that ever slows down
  • Multiple unused/legacy domains (including floridatimesharelawyer.com) and no city-specific landing pages, while competitors like Finn Law Group build them out for the same markets
Every month without a paid lead engine is a month Ron's growth depends entirely on referrals he can't control or scale — the opposite of the self-managing firm he's building toward.

Intake

  • Inbound calls route to a 24/7 outsourced overseas answering service rather than a trained in-house intake process
  • Ron personally handles high-value consultations and closes, which stalls when he is traveling across the Asia time difference
  • No documented process exists for lead follow-up speed or pipeline tracking beyond the initial call
As long as Ron has to be personally available to close leads, the time freedom he's after stays out of reach no matter how many leads come in.

Self-Managing Team

  • The team consists of Ron, his wife (document prep/support), and a part-time contract helper — no manager or delegate exists for sales or consults
  • Ron cannot fully step away from the business even while traveling internationally, the exact freedom he says he is after
  • No accountability structure or leadership layer exists beyond Ron himself
Until someone besides Ron can close a new client, the firm cannot run without him — no matter how far away he travels.

Profit

  • No fee structure, profit margin, or net revenue figure was shared or appears to be tracked separately from gross revenue
  • The stated revenue goal (“$1M…$10M…the more the merrier”) is directional, with no plan or budget behind it
  • No profitability data by practice area exists to show whether estate planning or timeshare law is actually the more profitable service line
Real financial visibility is what turns Ron's directional goal into a number he can plan around — profit he can count on rather than discover after the fact.
