Intake Assessment

The public intake path for Christine Brown Sports Law runs through a Clio Grow form linked from the website rather than embedded on the page, and no prominent "free consultation" button is visible above the fold. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • The Clio Grow intake form is linked off the homepage rather than embedded above the fold, which adds a step before someone can reach out
  • No prominent "free consultation" button was visible above the fold — only two phone numbers in the header and a Contact Us menu item
  • A /contact-us address returned a "page not found" error when tested, so at least one path to contact may dead-end
  • Who answers calls, how quickly inquiries get a response, and what happens to people who do not sign right away are not visible from the outside and are worth mapping together
Blind Spot: People searching for help with a Title IX accusation or an NCAA eligibility problem are often on a clock, and a visitor who has to leave the page for a separate form may simply call the next firm on the list.
The Positive
  • An intake platform, Clio Grow, is already in place, which is a solid base for a defined follow-up process
  • The header lists two phone numbers, including a toll-free line, so there is a direct way to call
The Gap
  • Every extra click between a motivated visitor and a consultation request is a chance for that athlete, parent, or coach to give up or call a competitor
  • Without a visible consultation button, the strongest content on the site has no clear next step attached to it
  • Without a defined response and follow-up process, inquiries depend on whoever happens to see them first
Intake that runs on a clear process, instead of on whoever is available, is intake you no longer have to cover personally.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • Contact is handled through a Clio Grow intake form that is linked from the site and not embedded above the fold on the homepage
  • No live chat was detected on the site, so after-hours visitors have only the phone numbers and the external form
  • A /contact-us address returned a "page not found" error when tested, so it is worth confirming that every Contact link on the site lands on a working page

Team Assessment

Christine Brown Sports Law has a defined structure on paper — three attorneys, a COO, a finance manager, and two legal assistants — but a visible org chart is not the same as a firm that runs without its founder. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • Whether day-to-day decisions, client communication, and case strategy run through Christine or through her team is not yet clear from the outside
  • The firm launched in 2025, so operating rhythms, accountability measures, and role-level goals are likely still being built
  • Repetitive attorney and staff work such as research summaries, drafting, and intake notes still takes the same hours without AI-assisted systems
Blind Spot: A clear org chart can hide the real test: if Christine stepped away for two weeks, which decisions, client calls, and deadlines would stall? That answer, not the titles on the team page, shows how self-managing the team really is.
The Positive
  • A COO and a finance manager already exist as dedicated leadership and operations roles
  • Three attorneys beyond the founder give the firm legal capacity to share the work
The Gap
  • Until routine work is systematized, the firm's growth will mostly mean more hours for the same people
  • Without clear accountability measures for each role, the COO has little to manage against and more issues escalate to Christine
  • Every decision that only the founder can make is a limit on how far the firm can grow without her
A team that carries the day-to-day is what frees your time for the work only you can do — and for everything outside the firm.

Profit Plan Assessment

The firm's fee structure, revenue, and profit targets are not publicly visible, so the financial baseline for Christine Brown Sports Law still needs to be set together. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • Fee structure and pricing are not described on the website or in the 2025 launch release, so how each type of matter earns for the firm is not yet visible
  • The cost to bring in a new client, and what each matter type contributes to the bottom line, are the numbers a profit plan starts from, and they have not yet been reviewed together
  • Without a stated profit target, it is hard to tell whether adding people or adding marketing spend is the right next investment
The Positive
  • A dedicated finance manager is already on the team, which is a strong starting point for monthly financial reporting
The Gap
  • Without a clear picture of profit by matter type, it is easy to put effort into the work that pays least
  • Without a profit target, growth decisions are made on instinct instead of on numbers
  • Setting the baseline now, in the firm's first years, is far easier than correcting habits later
Profit that is planned instead of discovered is what turns a strong launch into lasting wealth.
