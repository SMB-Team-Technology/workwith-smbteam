Here Is Where Dumas & Sanclemente, PLLC Stands Today

Axel Dumas and Jonathan Sanclemente built Dumas & Sanclemente, PLLC into a two-office personal injury practice across Tampa Bay (Lutz) and South Florida (Fort Lauderdale) entirely on word-of-mouth referrals, without ever running a single paid ad. That referral engine has carried the firm to a roughly $1.2M revenue plateau — but it has a hard ceiling, and the firm's own local search visibility is already being outpaced by competitors building dedicated local-SEO footprints in both of its home markets.

Stage 6 is where Dumas & Sanclemente becomes the go-to personal injury firm across Tampa Bay and South Florida you are working to build — a firm known and chosen in its markets, not just referred into them.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • Unpredictable word-of-mouth referrals are the only source of new business, generating $10k–$20k/mo
  • No paid marketing has ever been run — zero presence in Google Ads, LSA, or Meta Ads across either market
  • The firm did not surface in organic search-snippet visibility for any of its own core searches in Tampa Bay or Fort Lauderdale
  • Growth has stalled at a roughly $1.2M revenue plateau despite two active offices and 250+ litigated cases

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you

  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
Closing the gap from here to Stage 6 does not require more casework — it requires building the systems that turn your existing reputation into a predictable growth engine, which is exactly the plan below.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Dumas & Sanclemente, PLLC
Dumas & Sanclemente is operating at Stage 3 — a firm whose casework and reputation have outpaced its growth systems. The single biggest gap limiting growth right now is lead generation: with zero paid marketing history and no organic search visibility in either Tampa Bay or Fort Lauderdale, the firm is entirely dependent on referrals in markets where named competitors are already building out dedicated local-SEO footprints and review volume. Until that changes, the goal of doubling revenue to $2.5M+ and becoming the go-to PI firm across both markets will remain out of reach no matter how strong the casework is.
