Profit Plan Assessment

No profit or margin figures came up on the call, and revenue has held at a roughly $1.2M plateau rather than grown — meaning the firm has not yet needed to answer what growth would actually do to its bottom line. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • No profit or margin figures were discussed on the call — visibility into cost structure and per-case profitability was not raised
  • Florida's no-fault law and low minimum BI coverage create a "singles" market for this practice area, where individual case values run low and high case volume is required for profitability — a dynamic that makes margin tracking more important, not less
  • No bookkeeping, fractional CFO, or profit-planning engagement is currently in place
  • Revenue has held at a roughly $1.2M plateau, meaning the firm has not had to test whether more revenue actually converts to more take-home
Blind Spot: In a "singles" market where individual case values run low, doubling revenue without knowing your cost per case and cost per acquisition risks doubling your workload while your actual margin stays flat or shrinks.
The Gap
  • Without financial visibility, the firm cannot tell whether the coming investment in marketing and growth is actually paying for itself in profit, not just revenue
  • No profit plan means the $2.5M+ revenue goal could be reached without the take-home income and freedom that goal is meant to produce
Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity — a profit plan is what turns the goal of $2.5M+ into more of what you actually keep, not just more work.
