Lead Generation

Dumas & Sanclemente, PLLC has built a two-office personal injury practice across Tampa Bay and South Florida entirely on word-of-mouth referrals — with no paid advertising, no local SEO strategy, and no predictable system for attracting new clients. Every channel below is currently untapped, in markets where multiple named competitors already have a head start.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • PageSpeed mobile and desktop scores could not be obtained this session — the public PageSpeed Insights API returned HTTP 429 (quota exhausted) across 6 attempts; a manual pagespeed.web.dev check is recommended before the proposal call
  • No video content anywhere on the site, despite the firm's strongest asset being two attorneys with complementary insurance-defense and plaintiff backgrounds
  • Only two geo-targeted landing pages exist (Tampa/Lutz office, Fort Lauderdale office) — no dedicated pages for the broader Tampa Bay, South Florida, or Miami markets the site's own copy references
  • The site's privacy policy confirms Google Ads remarketing/retargeting pixels are installed — passive tracking infrastructure, likely left over from a past web vendor, not evidence of an active campaign
Blind Spot: The site already has Google Ads remarketing pixels installed from a prior vendor setup — meaning the technical groundwork to retarget every visitor who does not convert already exists and is simply not being used.
The Positive
  • Modern, professionally built site with a clear "Free Consultation" CTA and a prominently displayed phone number above the fold
  • Attorney bios frame Axel Dumas's insurance-defense background and Jonathan Sanclemente's plaintiff focus as a specific competitive edge ("we can anticipate what the insurance company will do")
The Gap
  • Without dedicated landing pages for the broader metro areas the firm claims to serve, paid traffic sent to those markets has nowhere strong to land
  • No video content means the firm's biggest credibility asset is never seen or heard by a prospect before they call
  • Unconfirmed mobile page speed is a real risk if it is slow — most paid clicks are on mobile, and slow load times lose clicks before the page even finishes loading
A site built to convert paid traffic — not just referrals — is the foundation everything else in this roadmap depends on for reaching $2.5M+.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Google Business Profile star rating (~4.9★) and review count (~121) are aggregator-sourced approximations, not GBP-verified live — confirm both before quoting them externally
  • The firm did not surface in any of three local 3-pack proxy searches run for "car accident lawyer Tampa," "personal injury attorney Fort Lauderdale," or "truck accident lawyer Lutz FL"
  • At least three different addresses and two different phone numbers are circulating across the firm's own site, BBB, FindLaw, Avvo, Martindale-Hubbell, Justia, and Yelp — a real, citable NAP inconsistency
Blind Spot: Google cross-references your NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across every directory that mentions your firm — right now three different addresses and two different phone numbers are circulating, which is very likely actively suppressing your local map-pack rankings in both markets, even before a single ad is run.
The Positive
  • Your approximate review profile (~121 reviews at ~4.9★, aggregator-sourced) suggests a reputation that already rivals several named competitors — once verified live, it becomes a real asset to lean on in local search
The Gap
  • Being invisible in local search removes the compounding effect of the firm's real courtroom results and reviews, since prospects cannot find the firm to see them
  • Continued NAP inconsistency will keep suppressing rankings even after paid campaigns launch, unless it is corrected first
Holliday Karatinos Law Firm, PLLC — physically located in the same city as your Lutz office, with multiple geo-targeted landing pages (Lutz, Tampa, Florida-wide) and an active reviews program, giving it a more built-out local-SEO footprint in your own market.
Fixing NAP consistency and building out true geo-targeted content is the fastest, lowest-cost way to start closing the visibility gap with Holliday Karatinos and Florin Gray in your own backyard.
