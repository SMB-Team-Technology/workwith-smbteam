Lead Generation

Dynasty Legal Documents currently draws leads from referrals, a self-managed $1,000/mo Google Ads budget, Facebook Ads, and a $800/mo SEO vendor — but none of these are coordinated with each other, and monthly revenue still fluctuates between $20K and $30K.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • PageSpeed scores: not obtained this pass — the API returned a rate-limit error on all 5 attempts; a live scan is recommended before the proposal call rather than assuming performance is fine
  • No dedicated bio page introducing Melissa's credentials, even though testimonials repeatedly name her personally
  • No live chat — after-hours visitors have no way to start a conversation beyond the contact form
  • No video content — the homepage headline claims "Highly Acclaimed" with nothing showing the person behind that reputation
Blind Spot: Testimonials already name Melissa by name, but there's no page introducing who she is or her credentials up front — a missed trust opportunity in a category where prospects are specifically evaluating who will be preparing their legal documents.
The Positive
  • Site is mobile responsive with a modern build — the blog has been actively updated as recently as May 21, 2026
  • A "Request a FREE Consultation" CTA and phone number sit above the fold, paired with a contact form in the same position
The Gap
  • After-hours visitors with no live chat and no bio page to build instant trust are more likely to bounce to a competitor's listing than wait for a callback
  • Every dollar spent driving traffic to a site missing trust signals converts at a lower rate than it should
A website that builds trust in Melissa personally — and captures leads after 5pm — is a direct step toward the predictable revenue that makes hiring a full-time employee possible.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • GBP shows 5.0 stars and 27 reviews, but this could only be sourced from the firm's own embedded reviews widget this pass — the most recent review is flagged as roughly a year old, a slow review velocity worth confirming live
  • NAP inconsistency: two different phone numbers — (602) 349-9629 vs. 602-892-2218 — and two different addresses (Gilbert vs. Mesa) appear across GBP, the website, and directories
  • Absent entirely from "child custody document preparation Gilbert Arizona" results, where Arizona Legal Document Services LLC and Benjamin Legal (FileFamilyLaw.com) surface instead
  • No geo-targeted landing pages for surrounding East Valley cities (Chandler, Queen Creek, Tempe, Scottsdale) despite drawing from greater Maricopa County
Blind Spot: Two active locations (Gilbert and Mesa) listed inconsistently across sources may be splitting local search signal instead of reinforcing it in either market.
The Positive
  • Dynasty does appear in local search for "certified legal document preparer Gilbert AZ," "living trust document preparation Gilbert AZ," and both probate/guardianship searches checked — the foundation is there, it's inconsistent rather than absent
The Gap
  • Missing from the child custody search means families searching for Dynasty's core family law service can't find them at that exact moment of intent
  • No geo pages for neighboring cities means that search traffic defaults to whichever competitor bothered to build the page
Affordable Legal Document Specialists — 23 reviews at 4.9★ on Yelp against Dynasty's 12, in the same zip code and category, plus flexible after-hours availability as a stated differentiator.
Fixing the NAP inconsistency and closing the content gap is foundational — it's what makes every other dollar spent on ads and SEO actually compound instead of leak.

  • No named competitor LSA presence was confirmed this pass, but Google LSAs and Google Screened are broadly active among Gilbert-area legal service providers as a category
The Opportunity
