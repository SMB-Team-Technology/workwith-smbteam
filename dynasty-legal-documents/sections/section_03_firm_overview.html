Here Is Where Dynasty Legal Documents Stands Today

Melissa Moy runs Dynasty Legal Documents, a certified legal document preparation service (not an attorney-based law firm) serving Gilbert and Mesa, Arizona across family law, trusts & wills, guardianship & probate, and business formation document preparation. Monthly revenue currently fluctuates between $20,000 and $30,000, built on referrals plus a patchwork of self-managed Google Ads, Facebook Ads, and a $800/month SEO vendor — with no one besides Melissa running any part of the operation day to day.

Stage 6 is the stage where Dynasty runs without Melissa personally answering every call and managing every vendor — and it's the only path to the full-time hire she's said the practice needs next.

Where You Are Today

STAGE 3: SOLO PRACTITIONER
  • Referrals plus a self-managed $1,000/mo Google Ads budget and a $800/mo SEO vendor generate leads, but revenue still swings $20K–$30K month to month
  • Melissa personally handles intake and casework across two locations (Gilbert and Mesa) with no other team member
  • Two different phone numbers and inconsistent location listings currently work against the marketing spend already going out the door
  • The one attempt at adding headcount — a business development hire — was let go after four months and one client

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between where Dynasty is today and the practice that can support a full-time hire is not about working harder — it's about building systems Melissa isn't yet running personally.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Dynasty Legal Documents
Dynasty Legal Documents is operating at Stage 3 — a Solo Practitioner whose owner is personally responsible for every lead, every intake call, and every case, even while paying three separate vendors for marketing. The single biggest gap limiting growth is the absence of a coordinated, tracked lead-to-case system — not a lack of demand, since referrals, ads, and SEO are all already producing some leads. Until that system exists, Melissa's stated goal of reaching $60,000 a month to finally support a full-time hire will keep depending entirely on her own bandwidth.
