Intake Assessment

Krause & Kinsman already runs an in-house AI call center handling client communications for its 100,000+ existing mass tort clients — but no system yet proactively pushes case-status updates out, so that volume lands as unplanned inbound calls instead. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • No automated system yet proactively pushes case-status updates to the 100,000+ existing mass tort clients — that volume currently arrives as unplanned inbound calls
  • Recurring themes across third-party reviews cite poor communication, delayed callbacks, and slow status updates
  • No practice-area page, contact path, or intake process exists yet for the new IUL vertical — anyone who does find the firm has nowhere to go
  • Website contact form is not visible above the fold, adding friction before a lead even reaches intake
Blind Spot: The in-house AI call center handles volume, but the specific complaint showing up in reviews — slow status updates — is exactly the gap it hasn't closed yet, meaning the tool built to solve this problem hasn't fully solved it.
The Positive
  • An in-house AI call center already exists, handling a portion of intake and status-update volume — most firms this size have not built this yet
  • Aggregate review volume (400-700+ across platforms, ~4.7 stars) shows the underlying service experience is strong even where communication lags
The Gap
  • Every status-update call the team fields manually is time not spent building the IUL vertical or working active mass tort cases
  • Recurring "slow communication" complaints in reviews put future referral and case-acquisition credibility at risk
  • A lead who reaches the site interested in the IUL vertical has no intake path built yet — that interest currently has nowhere to go
Automating client status updates isn't a nice-to-have — it's the difference between the team supporting the IUL launch and the team being consumed by the practice it already has.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • Contact form is not visible above the fold — requires navigating to a dedicated contact page, adding a step between interest and intake
  • No live chat present on the homepage — a visitor with an urgent question has no immediate way to engage
  • No contact path, form, or intake flow exists yet for the new IUL vertical specifically

These findings are also referenced in the Website Audit section.

Team Assessment

Partners Adam Krause and Robert Kinsman are running an 8-10 active mass tort case caseload while also personally driving the launch of a brand-new practice vertical. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • No dedicated social or content team member exists yet to drive the IUL vertical day-to-day — that work currently depends on partner time
  • The AI-avatar approach was specifically chosen because the alternative (hours of filming) was not a realistic use of partner bandwidth
  • Team size and structure beyond the two named partners was not detailed on this call
  • Launching an entirely new practice vertical on top of an existing 8-10 active mass tort caseload adds real capacity strain at the leadership level
Blind Spot: Choosing a 30-second video approval over hours of filming is already a sign the firm knows its own bandwidth is the constraint — the same logic needs to extend to the rest of the IUL launch, not just content production.
The Positive
  • Clear leadership structure with two named, engaged partners driving strategy for both the existing practice and the new vertical
  • The firm already recognized its own time constraint and sought out a solution (AI avatar content) built specifically to protect it
The Gap
  • Every hour Robert or Adam spend personally driving IUL content or strategy is an hour not spent on the mass tort caseload that already generates revenue
  • Without a dedicated owner for the IUL vertical's day-to-day execution, its pace is capped by whatever time partners can give it
  • Scaling to "thousands" of IUL cases, as discussed, will require systems and people beyond what two partners can personally sustain
Until the IUL vertical runs on a system instead of partner bandwidth, growing it will cost Robert and Adam the exact time they built the AI-avatar approach to protect.

Profit Plan Assessment

Revenue and profitability were not a focus of this call — the conversation centered on launching the IUL vertical and automating client communications. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • No revenue figures, profitability targets, or case-value data were shared for the existing mass tort practice or the new IUL vertical
  • No stated close rate or average case value exists yet for IUL cases specifically, since the vertical has not launched
  • A case-volume goal was set (roughly 100 IUL cases in the first 60 days) without a connected revenue or profit target
  • Proposed ad spend ($25K-$50K to start) was discussed as a number the firm is comfortable with, without a stated framework for what return it needs to produce
Blind Spot: A 60-day, 100-case validation goal for a brand-new vertical is a strong operational target — but without a connected revenue or cost-per-case benchmark, the firm won't have a clear signal for whether those first 100 cases were actually profitable to acquire.
The Positive
  • The firm's scale — $700M+ recovered historically, 100,000+ existing clients — points to a well-established, well-capitalized practice
  • The firm is treating the proposed ad spend as a number it can absorb rather than a constraint, suggesting real financial capacity behind the new vertical
The Gap
  • Without a connected revenue or cost-per-case target, the 100-case validation goal cannot be judged as a financial success or failure
  • No visibility into current mass tort profitability makes it harder to know how much the IUL vertical can safely invest before it needs to pay for itself
  • Scaling ad spend from validation ($25K-$50K) to the "thousands of cases" goal discussed will require a profit plan, not just a case-volume plan
A profit plan for the IUL vertical turns "acquire 100 cases" into a number Robert and Adam can trust — proof the new vertical is building wealth, not just activity.
