Lead Generation

Krause & Kinsman's mass tort practice generates a steady stream of business through a mature 130+ firm co-counsel referral network and a strong reputation — but the firm is walking into a brand new practice vertical, IUL/MLM insurance litigation, with no lead-generation system of any kind built for it yet.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • PageSpeed scores could not be obtained live this pass (Google API quota exhausted) — real mobile and desktop numbers should be pulled manually before the proposal call
  • No live chat present on the homepage
  • Contact form is not visible above the fold — requires navigating to a dedicated contact page
  • No dedicated page, content, or CTA exists anywhere on the site for the new IUL/MLM insurance litigation vertical
Blind Spot: The site is well-built for the mass tort brand — modern design, attorney bios, an active blog — but has zero real estate built for the exact vertical the firm most wants to grow right now.
The Positive
  • Modern, mobile-responsive design with a clear "Contact Us Now" CTA and phone number above the fold
  • Individual attorney bio pages for both partners, plus an active blog (most recent post: "Boy Scouts of America Litigation Update," February 2026)
The Gap
  • No page, content, or CTA for the IUL vertical means every visitor curious about that case type has nowhere to land and nothing to convert on
  • No live chat or above-the-fold contact form adds friction for fast-moving, social-native prospects
  • Unverified page speed performance could be quietly costing mobile conversions on an otherwise modern-feeling site
A site that's polished for the mass tort brand but has no home for the IUL vertical means Robert and Adam are launching a new practice area with the one asset it needs most still unbuilt.

The Positive

The Gap
  • Local SEO performance in the existing mass tort practice is not costing the firm meaningfully
  • The real gap is that this channel isn't the one the IUL vertical needs — that opportunity and urgency sit on social, not local search
Peterson & Associates, P.C. — long-tenured (founded 1993), $500M+ recovered, competes for the same local mass tort referral relationships, though does not outperform Krause & Kinsman's own review base.
Local SEO isn't the lever that gets Robert and Adam their next stage of growth — the IUL vertical needs a different channel entirely, and that's where the real opportunity and the real urgency both sit.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Existing mass tort/PI practice: this firm does not appear to be running Google search ads for mass tort or personal injury terms in Kansas City — they may not be configured to show for these terms, or may be getting outbid by competitors with larger local ad budgets
  • New IUL vertical: confirmed by the discovery call that the firm has no search ad presence for IUL/MLM litigation terms, since this vertical has not launched yet
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Peterson & Associates / Dickerson Oxton LLC — both maintain active, SEO-optimized mass tort/PI practice pages that would plausibly support paid search in the Kansas City market
  • RP Legal LLC (investorlosscenter.com) — self-described nation's leading IUL litigation firm, citing 400+ IUL lawsuits and $100M+ recovered, building dedicated content and case-acquisition funnels around the exact MLM/IUL fraud narrative
The Opportunity
  • "mass tort attorney Kansas City," "personal injury lawyer Kansas City" — defensive terms in the existing practice's home market
  • "IUL lawsuit," "Index Universal Life lawsuit," "MLM insurance fraud attorney" — the exact national terms RP Legal and Stoltmann Law are already capturing
  • Competitor-name conquesting on "RP Legal" and "Stoltmann Law" branded searches for prospects already comparing IUL firms
  • Because IUL case acquisition is happening nationally on social, not local search, paid search is a secondary channel for this vertical — the larger opportunity is paid social
Blind Spot: Because IUL case acquisition is happening on TikTok and Instagram rather than Google Search, winning this vertical on search alone would still leave the firm invisible on the channels where MLM-targeted prospects are actually spending their time.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • The firm's LSA/Google Screened presence for mass tort or PI terms was not observed or confirmed live in this research pass — mass tort lead generation typically runs outside LSA, making this a lower-priority channel for this engagement
  • No LSA presence exists for the IUL vertical, consistent with the firm having no marketing infrastructure built for it yet
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Peterson & Associates / Dickerson Oxton LLC — LSA presence not confirmed live in this research pass; recommend manual verification
  • Mass tort and referral-heavy PI firms typically run lead generation outside LSA entirely, consistent with what was observed here
The Opportunity
  • Appearing in LSA would put the firm above every other channel for anyone actively searching for a mass tort or injury attorney in Kansas City at the exact moment they're ready to call — though this is a secondary channel relative to the IUL vertical priority
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • With review volume already in the 400-700+ range, the firm would likely qualify for Google Screened status quickly, converting existing reputation into pay-per-lead volume rather than building it from scratch
Blind Spot: LSAs are a lower priority for this engagement specifically — mass tort case acquisition for both the existing practice and the new IUL vertical runs through referral networks and social content, not pay-per-lead directories.
