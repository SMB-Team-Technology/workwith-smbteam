STEP 3 — ROI PROJECTION — METHODOLOGY NOTE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The standard Leads -> Cases -> Revenue -> Return formula requires a CPL benchmark
and an average case value from the approved tables. Neither exists for mass tort /
IUL litigation: the CPL table (Accident & Injury, Criminal Defense, Family Law,
Estate Planning, Immigration, Bankruptcy, Business Law) and the practice-area
default case-value list have no mass tort / IUL row, and research notes confirm
average case value "not stated for IUL cases specifically; not stated for mass
tort." Forcing a proxy from an unrelated practice area (e.g., Accident & Injury
$5K-$8K) would materially understate real mass tort/IUL case economics and produce
a misleading revenue projection.

Instead, this audit uses the transcript's own stated case-acquisition goal as the
ROI anchor: acquire ~100 IUL cases in the first 60 days (~50 cases/month during
the validation period) to prove out the system before scaling toward thousands of
cases. Case value and projected revenue are left unquoted with an explicit note
that sales must confirm expected per-case value directly with the prospect before
presenting a dollar-figure revenue projection — this avoids fabricating a number
against PRICE INTEGRITY / transcript-first principles.
- Est. cases per month: ~50 cases (client-stated 100-case, 60-day validation goal)
- Est. case value / monthly revenue from ads: not established on the call; no
  comparable benchmark exists in the approved tables — confirm with sales rep
  before quoting a dollar projection to the client
