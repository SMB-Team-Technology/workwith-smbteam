Lead Generation

Grano Law has built its DWI and personal injury business on a website, a steady blog, Local Service Ads and Google search ads — a real foundation that, for years, produced the cases Marc wanted. Over the last couple of months that flow has slowed to a trickle, and the setup behind it has gaps that explain why a few weeks of slowdown can go unnoticed.

Website Audit

"I do feel like we've been doing blog posts. I had her completely revamp the homepage."
The Problem
  • The homepage title leads with the firm name and has no city, county or state, and DWI does not appear in it — so Google gets little signal about where you practice or what you are best at
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 80/100 — Desktop 97/100 (lab test run October 16, 2026). Desktop is excellent; mobile has room to improve for people searching from their phones
  • The Santa Fe DWI/DUI page has the city in its title and headline, but neither contains “lawyer” or “attorney” — the words people actually type when they are looking to hire
  • The homepage headline is the firm name alone, with the practice areas placed in a secondary line beneath it
Blind Spot: A refreshed homepage and regular blogging are real investments, but they are only as valuable as the search signals attached to them. If the page titles and headlines do not carry the city and “DWI attorney” wording, new content can be published for months without pulling in the searchers in Santa Fe and Rio Rancho that you most want.
The Positive
  • Modern, mobile-responsive design with a clear “Request Your Free Case Strategy Session” call-to-action and phone number above the fold
  • Deep practice-area coverage — 18 personal injury pages, 7 criminal defense pages and 6 DWI/DUI pages, including MVD license revocation hearings
  • Dedicated area pages for Santa Fe, Taos, Santa Rosa, Raton, Mora, Española, Rio Rancho and Bernalillo
  • A blog published every 10 to 14 days, alternating between criminal and personal injury topics, and a strong attorney bio with 25+ years of experience, Army JAG service and advanced DWI training
The Gap
  • Without city-plus-attorney wording in titles and headlines, competitors with that structure are better positioned to win the Santa Fe and Rio Rancho searches you want
  • Strong content that is not tied to local search terms leaves the best DWI prospects finding other firms first
  • Mobile speed at 80 leaves some phone searchers — most legal searches — waiting longer than they should
The website already shows the depth of a true DWI specialist — the next step is making sure the right searchers in the right cities can find it.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • On the call’s screen-share, the Google Business Profile showed Monday–Friday 9-to-5 hours, while the website lists Monday–Friday 8-to-5 (Friday until noon) and a 24/7/365 phone line
  • The profile’s main photo is not Marc’s headshot, and “personal injury attorney” appears only as a secondary category — with additional relevant categories still available to add
  • Messaging is turned off on the profile, closing one of the easiest ways for a local searcher to reach you
  • The FindLaw listing was last updated in 2020 and has no reviews
Blind Spot: Your Google Business Profile, Local Service Ads and Google search ads should all tell Google the same story. When hours, photos and categories do not match across them, each one works a little harder for a little less.
The Positive
  • A strong review reputation promoted on the firm’s website, and a long list of directory profiles (Avvo, Justia, FindLaw, Cornell LII, BBB) with consistent name, address and phone number
The Gap
  • An incomplete profile — wrong photo, limited categories, mismatched hours — gives up easy ground in the local results for “DWI attorney” searches
  • Santa Fe and Rio Rancho have far more potential clients than Las Vegas, and the local signals pointing to them are the weakest part of the setup
Eloy I. Trujillo — the Santa Fe DUI lawyer’s page pairs a “Santa Fe, DWI Attorneys” title with a headline built around “experienced Santa Fe lawyers,” the exact city-plus-attorney structure that Google rewards and that your Santa Fe page does not yet have.
A complete, consistent local presence is the quiet foundation of a steady flow of quality DWI cases.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Criminal defense / DWI: a Google search ad for the firm appeared on a re-run of the Santa Fe “DWI attorney” search during the call’s screen-share, with the firm appearing to run without competing search ads on that search
  • No map or location extension showed with the ad, which suggests the search campaign may not be linked to the Google Business Profile — a gap that matters for people searching from their phones
  • Personal injury: we did not observe personal injury search ads for the firm on the searches reviewed, so PI demand may be going to other firms
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • We did not confirm competitor search ads on the Santa Fe “DWI attorney” search we reviewed — a snapshot, not a guarantee. Eloy I. Trujillo and Clark & Ruyle both have dedicated Santa Fe DWI pages and are positioned to compete for the same searches
  • Because competitor ads rotate by time of day and location, a one-time view does not show who is bidding when you are not looking — another reason regular reporting matters
The Opportunity
  • Santa Fe: “DWI attorney Santa Fe,” “DWI lawyer Santa Fe” and “DUI lawyer Santa Fe” — the largest nearby market, and where you said you want your sponsored content to run
  • Rio Rancho and Bernalillo: “DWI attorney Rio Rancho” and nearby city variations, where several firms appear in the results for the same high-intent searches
  • Non-obvious: license-focused searches such as “MVD license revocation hearing” and “DWI license suspension New Mexico” — a topic your site already covers in depth and one that signals an urgent, ready-to-hire searcher
  • Non-obvious: urgency searches like “arrested for DWI” and “DWI bail” in your target cities, plus personal injury searches such as “car accident lawyer Santa Fe” and “personal injury attorney Rio Rancho” once DWI lead flow is steady again
Blind Spot: Ad accounts that are managed by others and reviewed infrequently are easy to leave on autopilot. Without a regular look at search terms, cost per lead and lead quality, a drop in inquiries can run for weeks before anyone can say whether the cause is the ads, the website or the phones.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • The firm appears in Local Service Ads for “DWI attorney” searches in both Las Vegas, NM and Santa Fe
  • The ads stop running at 5 p.m., message leads are turned off, and the photo shown is not Marc’s headshot — all visible on the call’s screen-share. Marc noted that message leads are a “double-edged sword,” which is a fair concern that deserves a response process rather than a switch left off
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Law Office of Adam Oakey, LLC — Albuquerque-based and the only other advertiser we saw in the Las Vegas, NM “DWI attorney” Local Service Ads, so the field in your home market is thin
The Opportunity
  • With only one other advertiser seen in Las Vegas, NM, extending LSA hours beyond 5 p.m. and adding Marc’s headshot are low-cost ways to stand out in a thin field
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Santa Fe and Rio Rancho are the markets where you want more cases, and LSAs aimed at them are a natural fit for a firm with your credentials and reputation
Blind Spot: Local Service Ads that go dark at 5 p.m. quietly send every evening searcher to whichever firm is still showing. Pairing longer hours with a fast-response process makes message leads an asset instead of a risk.

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • We could not confirm active Facebook or Instagram ads for the firm in the Meta Ad Library, so this appears to be an unused channel rather than a confirmed gap
  • The firm does maintain a Facebook page, which gives a foundation to build on
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • We did not confirm active Meta ads from the named Santa Fe or Albuquerque competitors, so social is likely an open lane in this market
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Geo-targeted content aimed at Santa Fe and Rio Rancho, built around Marc’s depth as a DWI specialist and your frequent DWI blog topics
