Lead Generation

Passarini Law currently has no active lead generation system running anywhere — the firm made a deliberate choice to pause all marketing while it builds the operational systems needed to handle more volume. That pause protects the team today, but it also means competitors are the only ones actively building visibility in this market right now.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • passarinilaw.com issues a 301 redirect to passarinilaw.site — a primary domain forwarding to a non-.com address reads as unfinished or mid-migration to a prospective client
  • PageSpeed mobile and desktop scores could not be captured this session — the public PageSpeed API returned a quota error on repeated attempts and the results render client-side; these should be pulled manually before any marketing engagement begins
  • No contact form is visible above the fold — consultation requests are consolidated in a section near the footer
  • No geo-targeted landing pages exist for surrounding cities (Fort Lauderdale, Coral Springs, Deerfield Beach) despite immigration and family law being highly localized search categories
Blind Spot: The domain redirect isn't just a technical detail — every backlink, bookmark, and old citation still pointing at passarinilaw.com now passes through an extra hop to a .site domain, which can quietly work against search visibility even before any new marketing begins.
The Positive
  • An extensive, credibility-building attorney bio for Andrea Passarini covers 20+ years of experience and dual Florida/Brazil licensure
  • Clear calls to action sit above the fold ("Request a Consultation" and "View Practice Areas"), and a linked YouTube channel gives the firm a video presence to build on
The Gap
  • Visitors who land on the homepage have to scroll past several sections to find a way to actually reach the firm
  • The site's practice area depth (immigration, family law, probate, real estate, each broken into subcategories) isn't matched by geo-targeted content competitors could otherwise be outranked on
  • A domain still resolving through a redirect and NAP inconsistencies elsewhere (see Local SEO) compound into a trust-signal gap for a firm otherwise backed by 20+ years of credibility
None of this needs to be fixed today — but when Passarini Law is ready to turn lead generation back on, these are exactly the fixes that determine whether that investment converts into cases Andrea can actually staff.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Passarini Law's live Google Business Profile star rating and review count could not be independently confirmed this session — no synced third-party aggregator count could be located either, itself a signal the profile is thin or not fully claimed on major review platforms
  • Across nine local searches spanning family law, immigration, probate, and real estate in Pompano Beach, Passarini Law did not surface in organic or directory results for any of them
  • Avvo and Justia — the firm's two most client-visible legal directories — both still list Andrea Passarini's location as Hollywood, FL rather than the firm's current Pompano Beach office
Blind Spot: Two of the directories a prospective client is most likely to check when researching an attorney by name are pointing them to the wrong city — a fixable problem that may be quietly suppressing local search visibility right now, independent of anything paid.
The Positive
  • The firm's own website and its underlying business-filing record both correctly reflect the current Pompano Beach registration — the NAP problem is isolated to directory listings, not the firm's core records
The Gap
  • Referral-only visibility disappears the moment a prospective client searches online instead of asking around
  • The two most direct local competitors in Passarini Law's largest practice areas are the ones showing up in that search instead
The Law Office of Melissa Dacunha, P.A. — 87 reviews at 4.8★ on Birdeye, actively visible across both family law and immigration searches, Passarini Law's two largest practice areas.
Fixing the directory NAP inconsistencies costs nothing and doesn't require turning ads back on — it's one of the few lead-gen fixes that can happen in parallel with the operations work, without adding a single new case to the intake queue.
