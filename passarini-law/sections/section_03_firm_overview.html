Here Is Where Passarini Law, PLLC Stands Today

Andrea Passarini has spent more than 20 years building a family law and immigration practice in Pompano Beach, with immigration — driven by complex removal proceedings and Hague Convention cases — now making up roughly half of the firm's caseload and growing. She personally carries the bulk of that work across four practice areas while operating close to 80 hours a week, and the firm has intentionally paused all marketing to avoid exceeding its current capacity of about 10 new cases a month. The demand is there; the operational systems to carry it without Andrea aren't.

Stage 6 is the stage that makes a five-year retirement realistic — not because Andrea works harder to get there, but because the firm no longer runs through her personally.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • Andrea personally handles casework across family law, immigration, probate, and real estate, working close to 80 hours a week
  • The firm has intentionally paused all marketing to avoid exceeding its current capacity of roughly 10 new cases a month
  • Operations are led only part-time by Andrea's son, a financial advisor by trade without a legal-operations background
  • There is no documented workflow covering client intake through off-boarding — everything currently depends on what Andrea knows and does herself

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
Closing that gap starts with the exact work this engagement is built for: turning what currently lives in Andrea's head into playbooks her team can run without her.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Passarini Law, PLLC
Passarini Law is operating at Stage 3 — a Solo Practitioner whose 20+ years of credibility and a fast-growing immigration caseload have outpaced the operational systems behind them. The single biggest thing holding the firm back right now isn't a lack of demand — it's the absence of a documented workflow that lets the team handle intake, casework, and off-boarding without Andrea touching every file personally. Until that exists, doubling or tripling revenue and stepping back toward a five-year retirement will stay two separate goals instead of one connected plan.
