Here Is Where Law Offices of Ricky Malik, P.C. Stands Today

Stage 6 is what finally lets Ricky step out of daily firefighting and into ownership-level work — hiring an associate attorney to take over court work is already a move in that direction, and this plan is built to carry it the rest of the way.
