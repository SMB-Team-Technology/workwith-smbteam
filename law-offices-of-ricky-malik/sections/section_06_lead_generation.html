Lead Generation

Law Offices of Ricky Malik, P.C. already runs three lead sources — SEO, reputation management, and Smith.ai-managed intake — for roughly $5,000/month combined. The problem isn't a lack of a system; it's that the system is producing only marginal year-over-year gains, and past paid ad campaigns were abandoned after generating "worthless leads."

  • No video content on the site despite a strong, trust-building personal story available (16+ years, University of Maryland Immigration Clinic background)

The Positive
  • Modern, current-generation design with a clear "Talk With Ricky" CTA above the fold and repeated in the footer
  • Dedicated attorney bio section and an active blog (most recent post March 2026) — both build trust before a call ever happens
  • 11 practice area pages plus city-specific geo landing pages (Alexandria, Arlington, Woodbridge, Manassas) already in place — a genuine strength most firms this size don't have
The Gap
  • A strong website with no video and no live chat still leaves after-hours and hesitant visitors with only a form to fill out

A website this far along deserves a team that can actually capitalize on it — right now, the site is ahead of the operation behind it.
