Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Word of mouth is the main source of new clients; conferences and online content are expected to grow but are not yet a repeatable system
  • The website homepage still leads with Family Law and Personal Injury and “Aggressively Go the Extra Mile,” not estate planning, business law and IP
  • The blog has a single post, from August 2022, and there are no city pages for Orlando-area searchers
Steady inbound demand for the estate and business work you want is what lets you leave litigation behind without the income wobbling.

Intake

  • The rep agreement, intake form and payment link go out the day after a client says yes
  • A live virtual assistant answers the phones and the firm already uses a case management system with intake tools — a real foundation to build on
  • The main contact form sits on a separate page, not on the first screen of the homepage
Every day an agreement sits unsent is a day a ready-to-sign client waits — and a day you are still the one keeping it moving.

Self-Managing Team

  • The firm runs on contractors by design: contract attorneys, one core paralegal at roughly 30 hours a week, others as needed, and a virtual receptionist
  • Finding meticulous paralegals is hard, and a required publication step was missed on one matter, adding a 90-day wait
  • Paralegal support is bought by the hour or in monthly hour packages, so output tracks the hours purchased
A bench that executes reliably without being checked behind is what lets you step out of the courtroom for good.

Profit

  • This year has been rough during the practice transition, and whether it ends as a best or worst year turns on large probate matters wrapping up by January
  • Fees come from several models at once — statutory-rate probate, flat packages, hourly mediation and client payment plans
  • Paralegal cost grows with every manual task, which narrows the margin on flat-fee package work
Packaged work that is delivered with fewer manual hours is the path to revenue you can plan around instead of waiting on a few large matters.
