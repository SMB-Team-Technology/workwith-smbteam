Lead Generation

LCR Legal's move toward estate planning, business law and intellectual property is not yet reflected online: clients mostly arrive by word of mouth, and the website still leads with family law and personal injury. Google Business Profile, local map results, paid ads and Local Service Ads could not be viewed during our research, so this section focuses on what we could verify and the opportunities that follow.

Website Audit

"I'm actually getting someone to work on my website now because it's going to highlight kind of three pillars of where I work from."
The Problem
  • The homepage headline and subhead (“We Aggressively Go the Extra Mile to Protect Your Family and Business”) and its lead services, Family Law and Personal Injury, do not reflect your move toward estate planning, business law and IP
  • PageSpeed scores (lab test): Mobile 55/100 — Desktop 92/100. On mobile, the largest content takes about 12.5 seconds to load
  • Practice area pages are single short definitions; we found no dedicated estate planning, probate or business law/IP pages with pricing or a clear path to start
  • The main contact form and phone number sit on a separate Contact Us page, and the testimonials on the homepage are two short unnamed quotes
Blind Spot: A redesign built around three pillars can still miss the mark if the new pages do not carry a clear next step. Each practice area page needs its own way to start — a form, a call or a booking path — or the site becomes a better brochure rather than a way to sign clients.
The Positive
  • Your attorney bio is strong: FAMU College of Law, practicing since 2012, a Supreme Court Certified Family Law Mediator, and more than 5,000 pro bono hours
  • The site is live, mobile-responsive, and loads quickly on desktop
The Gap
  • Someone searching for estate planning, probate or business law in Orlando lands on a general definition instead of a page built to turn that search into a consultation
  • Slow mobile loading means visitors on their phones may leave before they ever read your credentials
  • Unnamed testimonials and a blog with one post since August 2022 give new visitors little proof that the firm is active
A website that speaks to the work you actually want to do is the first step toward a practice that grows without litigation.

The Positive

The Gap

The Opportunity

  • Business law and IP searches such as “trademark attorney Orlando” and “business succession planning attorney Orlando” — an area where you have no current matters, so every new inquiry is added growth
  • Neighboring cities such as Winter Park, Kissimmee, Sanford and Apopka, paired with the same estate and business terms

Blind Spot: You told us you expect more clients to come from business conferences and your online content. Those people will look you up by name next, so what appears on the first page for your name and brand matters as much as any keyword.

Competitor LSA Landscape
  • No competitor LSA listings were observed in our research, so no competitor LSA review counts are reported here.
The Opportunity
  • LSAs are a pay-per-lead channel that can put a firm in front of people searching for a lawyer at the moment they are ready to call.
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • LSA visibility depends on Google Screened status and a solid base of Google reviews, so building reviews is the first step before this channel makes sense.
