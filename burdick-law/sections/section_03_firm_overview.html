Here Is Where Burdick Law Stands Today

Vanessa Burdick launched Burdick Law four months ago as a solo criminal defense practice in New York City, handling 70–80 cases in that span entirely through court-appointed (18-B) assignment work. The firm has no website, Google Business Profile, or intake system of any kind — every case today comes through the court, not through any owned marketing asset. The single most important finding is that Burdick Law is trying to build a private, high-value felony and homicide defense practice from a standing start, in one of the most competitive and well-funded legal verticals in the country.
