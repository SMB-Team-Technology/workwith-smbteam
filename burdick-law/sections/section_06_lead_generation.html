Lead Generation

Burdick Law has no owned lead generation asset of any kind — no website, no Google Business Profile, no social presence, and no paid advertising history. Every one of the firm's 70–80 cases in its first four months has come through court assignment, not through anything the firm built or controls.

Website Audit

"No website, Google Business Profile, or formal intake system."
The Problem
  • No website exists for Burdick Law today — there is nothing for a prospective private client to find or evaluate online
  • PageSpeed scores: Not applicable — no website exists to test
  • No attorney bio, no case results, no credentials page — nothing to build trust with a private client before they call
  • No contact form or online scheduling of any kind exists
The Positive
  • Starting from zero means every technical and SEO decision on the future site can be made correctly from day one — there is no legacy debt or bad first impression to undo
The Gap
  • Every private inquiry that would come from a web search today has nowhere to land — it goes to a competitor with a site instead
  • Without a site, there is no way to showcase the case results and credibility needed to win high-value felony and homicide clients
  • Competitors targeting the same niche already have dedicated homicide-defense landing pages built specifically to convert this exact search intent
A website is not a marketing expense here — it is the missing front door standing between Vanessa and the self-managing, high-value practice she described wanting to build.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Not observed — the firm has no website to send paid traffic to, and does not appear to be running Google Ads for "criminal defense attorney NYC" or related terms
  • No ad spend of any kind has occurred yet, consistent with the transcript
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Spodek Law Group, P.C. — operates a network of dedicated city and practice-specific domains (nyccriminalattorneys.com, federallawyers.com, njcriminalattorneys.com) built specifically to dominate high-intent criminal-defense search traffic, and is widely described as advertising aggressively across channels
  • Law Offices of Robert Tsigler, PLLC — homepage and homicide-specific landing page both feature heavy "Available 24/7," "Call Now," and free-consultation CTAs typical of active paid-search landing pages
The Opportunity
  • "Criminal defense attorney NYC" and "criminal defense lawyer Manhattan" — high-intent terms the firm cannot currently capture at all
  • "Homicide defense attorney New York City" and "felony defense lawyer NYC" — the exact terms tied to the firm's target case type
  • Competitor-conquesting terms (searches referencing Spodek or Tsigler by name) — capturing prospects already comparing options
  • Neighboring-borough and urgency terms ("arrested need lawyer now NYC," "Brooklyn homicide attorney") that established competitors are not uniquely positioned to own
Blind Spot: Once Burdick Law has a public identity, competitors or unrelated parties could bid on the firm's own name in Google Ads — protecting brand search early prevents that from happening later.

Local Service Ads

Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Spodek Law Group, P.C. — carries the scale and review volume typically required for LSA eligibility, though explicit LSA participation could not be confirmed without a live Maps/LSA session

The Opportunity

  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Earning Google Screened status early builds the trust badge and review base that will matter once the firm transitions fully into private high-value cases
Blind Spot: LSA eligibility typically requires an established Google Business Profile and review history first — building that foundation is a prerequisite for LSA, not a parallel track that can happen before it.
