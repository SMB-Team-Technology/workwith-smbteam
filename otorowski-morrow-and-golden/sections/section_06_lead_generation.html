Lead Generation

Otorowski Morrow and Golden's new cases come from "some referrals," word of mouth, and an SEO and Super Lawyers advertising effort that Chris is still working to understand — not from a lead-generation system the firm controls. In the Seattle Google Maps searches we ran for medical malpractice, birth injury, personal injury, truck accident, and product liability, the firm did not appear in the top results for any of them.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The homepage code contains hidden, off-screen links to online pharmacy and casino sites — a sign the site may have been compromised — which can damage trust with Google and with visitors
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 63/100 — Desktop 84/100. Mobile largest-content load time (LCP) is 17.8 seconds
  • The site's design dates to roughly 2018 (the footer still reads "2018 ©"), and there is no FAQ section and no video on the homepage
  • The phone number is plain text rather than a tap-to-call link, and the contact form sits on a separate page instead of on the homepage
Blind Spot: You mentioned that the advice you are hearing is that SEO is no longer enough and the firm needs to appear when someone asks ChatGPT. Hidden spam links are one of the things that can keep a site from being trusted or cited by search and AI tools — so fixing them comes before anything else you do online.
The Positive
  • Individual attorney profile pages and roughly 20 case-type pages give the site real depth to build on
  • The firm publishes a blog focused on Washington medical malpractice, with a new post as recently as October 2026
The Gap
  • A slow, hacked-looking mobile site loses visitors before they ever call, and the callers who stay find no FAQ telling them which cases the firm does and does not take
  • Visitors on a phone cannot tap to call, so the easiest path to the firm's intake coordinator is missing
  • Without question-and-answer content, the site gives AI search tools little to quote when someone asks about a Seattle medical malpractice attorney
A clean, fast, trustworthy website is what makes the firm findable — by Google and by ChatGPT — so the right cases come to you.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The Google Business Profile ("Otorowski & Golden PLLC") shows 1.0 stars on just 2 reviews, and its category is simply "Lawyer" rather than medical malpractice or personal injury
  • The firm did not appear in the top 10 to 12 results for "medical malpractice lawyer Seattle WA," "medical malpractice attorney Seattle," "birth injury lawyer Seattle WA," "personal injury lawyer Seattle WA," "truck accident lawyer Seattle WA," or "product liability lawyer Seattle WA"
  • The firm's name and phone number are inconsistent across the web — "Otorowski & Golden" versus "Otorowski Morrow and Golden," and three different phone numbers on the website, Google, and FindLaw — plus a duplicate Yelp listing
  • No owner posts were observed on the Google profile, and you asked on the call how to create a Google Business Profile
Blind Spot: Your 1.0-star rating comes from just two reviews. With so few, a single critical review has an outsized effect — and every competitor we checked has far more reviews to outweigh any one comment.
The Positive
  • The firm is listed on Super Lawyers and FindLaw, and three of its attorneys appear on Super Lawyers and Rising Stars lists
The Gap
  • Moseley Collins Law has 2,048 Google reviews, Davis Law Group 733, and Dubin Law Group 432 — against the firm's 2
  • Inconsistent names and phone numbers make it harder for Google to trust that these listings all belong to the same firm
Moseley Collins Law — 4.9 stars on 2,048 Google reviews, appearing first in the medical malpractice and birth injury map results we checked.
When the firm shows up with real reviews where clients and AI tools look, the right cases find you instead of the other way around.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Medical malpractice: The firm does not appear to be running Google search ads for medical malpractice, birth injury, or surgical error terms in what we could observe. You mentioned you think the firm pays for some advertising through Super Lawyers, but we could not confirm any Google Ads activity.
  • Personal injury: No Google search ads were observed for trucking, auto, or product liability terms either — your opportunity to build the personal injury side you described starts from a blank slate.
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • During your discovery call, a paid ad for "Hospital Malpractice Lawyers" appeared on a live Seattle search — evidence that competitors are paying to appear in this space.
  • Moseley Collins Law and Menzer Law Group were both cited in the AI search results shown live on the call for medical malpractice searches.
The Opportunity
  • Targeted searches such as "medical malpractice lawyer Seattle," "birth injury lawyer Seattle," and "surgical error attorney Washington"
  • Case-specific searches that match the cases you want — "delayed cancer diagnosis lawyer," "failure to diagnose attorney," and "truck accident lawyer Seattle"
  • Non-obvious: add negative keywords for case types you do not take (dental malpractice, black mold, minor-damage claims) so ad budget does not pay for the calls you already turn away
  • Non-obvious: reach the wider market the website lists — Bellevue, Tacoma, Everett, Kitsap Peninsula, and Spokane — where fewer firms may be competing for the same searches
Blind Spot: With about five malpractice calls a day and "most of them" unwanted, broad advertising would simply add to the junk. The goal of paid search for this firm is not more calls — it is more of the right ones.

  • The firm's current Google profile — 1.0 stars on 2 reviews — gives it very little to compete with if it did appear.
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • No competitor Local Service Ads could be captured in our checks, so we cannot name firms holding these spots — this should be verified live before launch.
  • For reference, the firms leading Seattle map results carry far more reviews than the firm: Moseley Collins Law (4.9 stars, 2,048 reviews) and Dubin Law Group (4.7 stars, 432 reviews).
The Opportunity
  • Local Service Ads are most useful for the personal injury cases you want to add — trucking, auto, and product injuries — where callers are ready to hire.
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • With just 2 Google reviews today, building a review base is the first step to competing for these spots.
