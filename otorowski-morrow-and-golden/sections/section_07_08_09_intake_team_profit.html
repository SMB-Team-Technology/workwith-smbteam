Intake Assessment

One intake coordinator fields every call, writes up the caller's story, and routes it to the attorneys — and the firm still turns away roughly 800 cases a year to accept a handful. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
"We get five calls every day. Every day, a malpractice call. And most of them are junk."
The Problem
  • About 800 cases a year are rejected and only 10 to 15 accepted — roughly a 2% acceptance rate — so most of the screening effort ends in a "no"
  • Every call goes through a single intake coordinator, and the summaries then circulate to you and the other attorneys for review
  • Intake details are typed into a FileMaker Pro database you wrote about 20 years ago, and you told us you would rather dictate straight into the software than transfer information by hand
  • The website does not tell callers which cases the firm does not take — dental malpractice, black mold, and minor-damage claims all still generate calls
Blind Spot: A low acceptance rate is not only an intake problem — it is a signal about who is being attracted. When the website and advertising do not screen out the wrong callers, every one of them still costs the firm time on the phone.
The Positive
  • A dedicated coordinator listens to every caller's story, and you can rule out a case in about 60 seconds when damages are clearly too small
  • Selective case acceptance protects the firm from spending heavily on cases that will not justify the cost
The Gap
  • Attorney time spent reviewing summaries for cases that are clearly rejected is time not spent on the cases the firm accepts
  • Information that has to be retyped or transferred between notes and the database slows the process and invites errors
  • Any improvement in lead quality would raise accepted-case flow without adding to the screening load
Spending less time sifting through unwanted calls is how you free up attention for the higher-quality cases you have said you want.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • The contact form is not on the homepage — it lives on a separate Contact page, and "Contact Us" sits in the secondary navigation rather than as a prominent button
  • The phone number in the homepage call to action is plain text, not a tap-to-call link, so mobile visitors cannot call with one tap
  • No live chat or other instant-response option is present, and there is no FAQ to pre-qualify visitors or answer common questions before they call

These findings are also referenced in the Website Audit section.

Team Assessment

After more than 40 years in practice, you remain one of the attorneys who reviews incoming cases, and some of the firm's marketing and vendor information is now "not visible" to you after a change in office management. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: AMBER
"We have an intake coordinator who fields every intake call. He listens to the client's story… types it up, puts it in FileMaker Pro, and then it's circulated to me and other attorneys."
The Problem
  • Intake summaries come back to you and other attorneys for review, keeping senior attorneys inside every screening decision
  • With a recent office manager transition, no one yet owns the firm's marketing, SEO, and vendor relationships end to end
  • The intake database was written by you personally, so a core firm system depends on one person's knowledge
  • You told us "some of this information is not visible" to you, which means decisions about marketing are being made without a clear picture
Blind Spot: A system that "still works pretty well" is easy to leave alone — which is exactly why it can quietly keep the firm dependent on the person who built it.
The Positive
  • The firm already has a dedicated intake coordinator, multiple attorneys, and an outside surgeon who reviews cases as an expert
  • You told us "I think we've got additional capacity right now," so the team can absorb more of the right cases
The Gap
  • Unused capacity means the firm's fixed team costs are not matched by enough of the cases it wants
  • When marketing information is not visible to the owner, he has to rebuild the picture himself before any decision can be made
  • Without clear ownership of marketing and systems, new tools and campaigns will still route back to you
A team that owns marketing, intake, and systems without routing back to you is what turns the firm into something that runs without your daily involvement.

Profit Plan Assessment

Medical malpractice cases are very expensive to pursue and take about three years to resolve, so each case the firm accepts is a long, front-loaded investment, and no marketing budget has been set. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"I think I've got some homework to figure out what the hell we've got going on already."
The Problem
  • When asked whether any budget had been earmarked for marketing, the answer was "No"
  • What the firm already spends on SEO and advertising — including through Super Lawyers — is not clear to you today
  • Cases average about three years to resolve, with roughly 105 cases on the active list and about 20 in suit, so cash is committed well before it comes back
  • No view of what marketing spend returns in signed cases was described on the call
Blind Spot: Without knowing what the current SEO and advertising cost and what they deliver, it is impossible to tell whether the firm is paying for visibility that works — or paying for work that the hidden spam links on the website are undercutting.
The Positive
  • Disciplined case selection: you told us the damages amount is "almost an immediate rule out," which keeps the firm away from costly low-value cases
  • The firm's books are handled by a dedicated person who knows the business
The Gap
  • Without a marketing budget and tracking, there is no way to tell which marketing dollars lead to signed cases
  • With cases this expensive to pursue, attracting the wrong lead mix is costly, not just inconvenient
  • A long case cycle makes it more important to know early whether a marketing investment is working
Knowing exactly what each signed case costs to attract is what lets you invest in the higher-quality cases you want, instead of guessing.
