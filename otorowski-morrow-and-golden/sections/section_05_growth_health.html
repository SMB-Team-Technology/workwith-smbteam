Lead Generation

Intake

  • A dedicated intake coordinator fields every call, and obvious non-fits are ruled out in about 60 seconds
  • About 800 cases a year are rejected and only 10 to 15 accepted — roughly a 2% acceptance rate
  • Every intake summary still circulates to Chris and other attorneys, and the website does not pre-qualify callers
Fewer unwanted calls reaching the phone means more attorney time on the cases that matter.

Self-Managing Team

  • A team with an intake coordinator, multiple attorneys, and an outside expert reviewer — and capacity to take on more
  • A newly appointed office manager is still learning the role, and some marketing details are "not visible" to Chris
  • Intake runs on a database Chris wrote himself about 20 years ago, so a key system depends on one person's knowledge
A team that owns marketing and systems without routing back to Chris is what frees his time.

Profit

  • No marketing budget has been set, and what the firm already pays for SEO and advertising is unclear
  • Cases take about three years to resolve and are expensive to pursue, so every accepted case is a long investment
  • No measure of what marketing returns in signed cases was described on the call
Knowing what each signed case costs to attract is what lets the firm invest in the cases it actually wants.
