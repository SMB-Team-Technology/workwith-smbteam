AD SPEND CALCULATION (tied to the secondary Marketing Starter package only)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Conservative = channel minimum for Family Law, starting with Google PPC only
(firm currently has zero confirmed ad presence in any channel): $3,500/mo

Aggressive = 20% rule:
  No revenue goal stated → default 2x current revenue = $1,140,000
  $1,140,000 x 20% / 12 = $19,000/mo
  x Geographic multiplier (Phoenix/Scottsdale = Tier 2, 1.3x) = $24,700/mo
  No Spanish campaign needed
  - Marketing management fee ($4,997) = $19,703/mo available ad spend
  Reverse-math check: revenue goal $1,140,000/12 = $95,000/mo target / $4,000
  avg. family law case value (default, not stated) = 23.75 cases/mo / 15% default
  close rate = 158 leads/mo x ~$90 blended CPL = ~$14,247/mo. 20% rule ($19,703) is
  higher, so it governs per the "use whichever is higher" rule.
  Tier cap check: Starter cap is $20,000 — $19,703 is under cap, no tier change.

  HOWEVER: the General eligibility rule states total SMB spend (management fees +
  ad spend) must not exceed 35% of MONTHLY REVENUE (current, not goal).
  Monthly revenue = $570,000 / 12 = $47,500. 35% cap = $16,625/mo.
  Management fees alone ($3,297 LAW + $4,997 Marketing = $8,294/mo) already consume
  part of that cap, leaving $16,625 - $8,294 = $8,331/mo maximum for ad spend if
  both packages and full ad spend were live simultaneously.
  DECISION: Aggressive ad spend is capped at $8,300/mo (rounded down from $8,331)
  rather than the raw 20%-rule figure of $19,703/mo, to respect the 35%-of-current-
  revenue spend cap. This is a deliberate reduction — documented here per the
  Budget-Reality principle, even though revenue itself was not flagged as a
  constraint by the prospect.
