Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Zero self-managed PPC, LSA, or Meta advertising has ever been run — the only paid channel is a third-party lead-buying service (LegalMatch), not a firm-owned system
  • Two named local competitors, Doug Atkinson and Brian Foley Law, command an estimated ~100% and ~87% share of voice in the local map-pack the firm depends on most
  • The firm's existing SEO/web vendor relationship has not produced a PPC/LSA presence and has left likely page cannibalization across overlapping geo-targeted city pages unresolved
Every month spent invisible in the map-pack is a month Justin has to personally out-hustle two entrenched competitors instead of stepping back to the 10–20 hour week he's working toward.

Intake

  • Iris personally fields intake calls around the clock with no formal after-hours coverage or overflow answering service — a person carrying the load, not a system
  • The firm has been locked out of its Lawmatics CRM for roughly 2.5 months, leaving intake tracking and reporting incomplete
  • A ~30–32% close rate once a lead reaches Iris shows real underlying strength — the gap is capacity and after-hours coverage, not skill
A defined intake process that doesn't depend on Iris personally answering every call is what lets the firm keep converting leads even while Justin steps back.

Self-Managing Team

  • Justin is the only attorney in the firm, with no hiring process, no defined KPIs, and no plan in place today to bring on a second attorney
  • The firm's own "vacation test" is roughly one week before issues surface — a sign the business cannot yet run without Justin's daily involvement
  • No leadership layer exists beyond Iris's informal ops role, leaving no one positioned to manage a growing caseload without Justin
Until a real team and leadership structure exist, Justin cannot step back to 10–20 hours a week, no matter how much revenue grows.

Profit

  • Revenue is growing toward a "mid-6" figure this year, but there is no profit target distinct from the revenue goal
  • Fee structure is well understood at the case level (~$5,000 average, $4,000–$6,500 range), but that clarity hasn't been rolled up into a firm-wide profit plan
  • The long-term goal of "$3M–$5M gross to net $1M profit" has no defined path connecting today's numbers to that outcome
A defined profit plan is what turns Justin's revenue growth into the kind of income that actually funds stepping back and being present for his family.
