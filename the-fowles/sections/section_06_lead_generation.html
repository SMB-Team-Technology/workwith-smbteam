Lead Generation

The Fowles Law Firm has built its entire client base on referrals, reputation, and organic search, supplemented by a single paid lead-buying service — with no PPC, Local Service Ads, or Meta advertising of its own ever run. That has left two entrenched local competitors controlling nearly all of the map-pack visibility on the highest-intent criminal defense searches in Conroe.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The homepage headline ("Criminal Defense Attorney for Aggressive Defense Representation") and CTA are strong, but the actual contact form is buried below the fold in a lower "Contact us now!" section
  • PageSpeed mobile and desktop scores could not be independently verified this session (PageSpeed API rate limits and no interactive browser tool available) — recommend a live technical check before finalizing a website scope
  • No video content on the site, and the blog's most recent substantive post is roughly 4 months old following a multi-year publishing gap before that
  • Likely page cannibalization: a "Criminal Defense Lawyer in The Woodlands, TX" page and a separate "Drug Crime Lawyer in The Woodlands, TX" page target overlapping intent and geography, alongside a third generic Conroe-area page
Blind Spot: Justin describes himself as happy with the firm's current SEO/website vendor, but the site still has no PPC or LSA presence and likely-overlapping city pages competing against each other in search — the kind of gap that isn't visible from the outside unless someone goes looking for it.
The Positive
  • Mobile responsive, with a clear attorney bio (Justin Fowles, positioned as a "Former Prosecutor") and a prominent "Schedule a Consultation" CTA above the fold
  • Geo-targeted landing pages already exist for Conroe, Montgomery, The Woodlands, Shenandoah, Spring, and Magnolia — the foundation for local reach is already built
The Gap
  • A buried contact form means motivated visitors have to scroll and search before they can reach out — every extra step costs conversions
  • Cannibalized city pages likely split ranking authority instead of concentrating it behind the firm's strongest page
  • With no video and a stalled blog, the site does less than it could to build trust before a prospect ever picks up the phone
A website that converts the traffic already coming to it is the foundation Justin needs before safely adding paid leads on top of it — and eventually stepping back from managing it himself.

The Opportunity
