Here Is Where Trias Mathis PA Stands Today

Larry and Joseph Trias have built Trias Mathis PA into a 7-8 person personal injury, workers' comp, insurance claims, and construction litigation firm spanning four Florida offices in just two years — almost entirely on referral relationships, billboards, and radio. The firm has real assets already in place, including a genuine bilingual advantage in Southwest Florida's Hispanic community and a 19-city geo-landing-page infrastructure, but it does not appear anywhere in organic search, paid search, or local map results for any of its five practice areas in its own home market of Fort Myers.

Stage 6 is what it looks like when growth is no longer a favor you depend on from referral partners — it is a system you built and control, running on terms you set.

Where You Are Today

Stage 4: Small Business Manager
  • Referral companies, billboards, and radio drive nearly all new cases at $17K-$19K/month, and the firm is close to capacity on that channel alone
  • A prior $2K-$3K/month Google Ads test produced weak leads and made the firm risk-averse toward paid digital ever since
  • The firm does not surface in organic or paid search for personal injury, workers' comp, insurance claims, or construction litigation in its own market
  • Directory and profile inconsistencies across Avvo, FindLaw, and BBB are diluting the authority Larry has built over nearly 20 years as an attorney

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
Closing that gap does not mean replacing what already works — it means giving Trias Mathis PA its own digital lead engine, so growth stops depending on someone else's referral.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Trias Mathis PA
Trias Mathis PA is operating at Stage 4 — a Small Business Manager with a working referral and traditional-media engine, but no owned digital presence to fall back on. The single most urgent gap is that the firm is functionally invisible in organic search, paid search, and local map results across all five of its practice areas, while Farah & Farah, Viles & Beckman, and The Dellutri Law Group dominate every channel with hundreds of reviews apiece. Until Trias Mathis builds a lead generation system it controls, the $2.5M goal Larry described will stay tied to the same referral relationships he told us he wants to become independent of.
