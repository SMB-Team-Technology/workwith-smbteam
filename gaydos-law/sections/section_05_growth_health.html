Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • New client flow depends almost entirely on a legacy referral network that you have described as waning
  • Paid ads are currently paused after budget increases failed to produce proportional results
  • Organic visibility is near-zero outside personal injury — estate planning and probate searches rarely surface the firm at all
A referral flywheel that is fading cannot carry the freedom and legacy you are building toward — it can only carry today.

Intake

  • A dedicated reception and intake-paralegal role exists, so intake is not falling entirely on the attorneys
  • There is no CRM or lead tracking system, so no one can see where leads are coming from or where they are falling through the cracks
  • No after-hours coverage was described, meaning inquiries that come in outside business hours have no defined path to a response
Every lead that slips through an untracked intake process is a case that should have carried the Gaydos name — and didn't.

Self-Managing Team

  • An eight-person team is in place, including associate attorneys, a long-tenured office manager, and dedicated intake support
  • A 30-year office manager functions as a genuine de facto operational lead — real, if informal, delegation already exists
  • There is no formal leadership or accountability structure, and Joseph remains the central figure the firm still runs through
You already have people who could carry more of the load — what is missing is the structure that lets you actually step back and trust them to.

Profit

  • No margin tracking or cost-of-acquisition visibility was discussed on the call
  • Revenue was described as plateaued rather than something being actively analyzed or planned around
  • Marketing spend decisions have historically been made without a clear read on what return they produced
Without financial visibility, you cannot know if the firm is actually building the freedom you are working toward — or just staying busy.
