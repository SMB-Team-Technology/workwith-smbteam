Lead Generation

Gaydos Law generates real, high-value cases — but almost exclusively through a legacy referral network built on the family name over 35+ years. Outside of personal injury, the firm has virtually no digital visibility, and the one paid channel it has tried was paused after failing to deliver proportional results.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • On a live, on-call review of the site, Dan identified specific on-page SEO issues — including title tag and H1 problems on key pages — that are actively limiting how the site ranks for practice-area searches
  • Estimated organic traffic is approximately 225 visits per month, compared to an estimated 1,500 to 1,700+ per month for named competitors in the same market
  • No dedicated geo or practice-area landing pages exist (e.g. no "White Oak Personal Injury Lawyer" or dedicated probate page) despite the firm serving seven counties
  • The contact form sits near the bottom of the homepage rather than above the fold, and no video content is used anywhere on the site
Blind Spot: The site is currently managed by an outside vendor and has not been optimized for search — meaning the title tag and heading issues Dan found live on the call have likely been quietly costing organic visibility for some time without anyone flagging it.
The Positive
  • The site already has real trust signals to work with — 35+ years serving Western Pennsylvania, 10,000+ closed cases, and $50,000,000+ recovered for clients are all featured on the homepage
  • Attorney bios are present for the full team, giving prospective clients a way to see who they would actually be working with
The Gap
  • An organic traffic gap of roughly 1,300+ monthly visits compared to competitors means a large volume of prospects in this market are finding other firms first
  • Practice areas with genuine, active volume — like estate planning and probate — have no dedicated page to convert the searches that do occur
  • A below-the-fold contact form adds friction at the exact moment a visitor has decided to reach out
A website that quietly loses visibility every month is not the foundation the next chapter of the Gaydos name can be built on.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The firm's Google Business Profile is active, but has fewer visible reviews than several active competitors in this market
  • The firm surfaces organically for personal injury searches tied to its referral brand, but does not appear in local search results for estate planning, probate, or wills — despite handling six to eight wills every other week and opening eight estates in a recent two-week stretch
  • No geo-targeted landing pages exist for any of the seven counties the firm serves, so local searches in those areas have nothing firm-specific to find
Blind Spot: The firm's estate planning and probate work is a real, active revenue stream today — but because it has almost no search visibility, prospects actively searching for those services in White Oak are finding other firms first, even though Gaydos Law is already doing the work at volume.
The Positive
  • The firm's name/address/phone information is consistent across major directories (Avvo, Justia, FindLaw, Yelp, Martindale, Super Lawyers), which gives any future SEO work a clean foundation to build on rather than a cleanup project first
The Gap
  • Every estate planning or probate search that does not surface Gaydos Law is a client relationship — and a referral source — going to a competitor instead
  • A thinner visible review presence than competitors in the same market makes the firm look smaller online than it actually is in casework volume
Pribanic & Pribanic, LLC — operating from the same street in White Oak, this firm shows a stronger practice-specific search presence in personal injury, including the kind of geo-targeted pages Gaydos Law's site currently lacks.
Being invisible in the practice areas you are already winning work in is the fastest way to keep depending on a referral flow you have called waning.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Google Ads does not appear to be running currently — this is consistent with the firm's own account of having recently paused campaigns after budget increases did not produce proportional results
  • A prior PPC vendor ran ads early with some success before becoming unresponsive, and a second vendor handled local PR and ads without a clearly defined current scope — the firm has tried paid search before without a system to manage or measure it consistently
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Pribanic & Pribanic, LLC — established, direct-geography personal injury specialist in the same immediate market as Gaydos Law
  • TopDog Law — broader regional personal injury marketing presence out of Pittsburgh with visibility across the wider service area
The Opportunity
  • "White Oak personal injury lawyer" and "White Oak estate planning attorney" — direct practice-area-plus-city terms the site does not currently rank for organically
  • "[County name] probate attorney" across the seven counties the firm serves — capturing an active practice area with real case volume but no current landing page
  • Competitor name terms for firms without the same local brand equity — capturing prospects already comparing options in this market
  • "Family law attorney White Oak PA" and "custody lawyer White Oak PA" — practice areas the firm handles but does not consistently surface for
Blind Spot: A paused ad account does not mean a paused opportunity — it means competitors are free to capture search traffic during exactly the window the firm has stepped back from paid visibility.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Gaydos Law does not appear to be running Local Service Ads or carrying the Google Screened badge at this time — this may simply reflect that LSAs have not yet been configured for the firm's practice areas, not a confirmed absence of eligibility
  • Given the firm's decades of case history and $50,000,000+ recovered for clients, the underlying credentials to qualify for Google Screened are almost certainly already in place — the gap is in setup, not qualification
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Pribanic & Pribanic, LLC and TopDog Law — LSA presence for either firm was not confirmed live this session, but both are established enough in this market that an LSA slot would be a natural extension of their existing personal injury visibility
The Opportunity
  • With 35+ years in practice and thousands of closed cases, Gaydos Law has exactly the track record LSA's screening process rewards
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Pay-per-lead pricing is a lower-risk way to test paid visibility again after the mixed results from prior ad vendors, since spend is tied directly to actual calls rather than clicks
Blind Spot: After a frustrating experience with per-click ad spend that did not scale proportionally, a pay-per-lead model like LSA may be a more comfortable way back into paid visibility than another traditional PPC engagement.

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • Gaydos Law does not appear to be running active Facebook or Instagram ads at this time — the firm's Facebook page exists, but active-ad status could not be confirmed
  • This is consistent with the broader pattern on the call: paid channels have been tried inconsistently in the past and are not currently part of an active, managed strategy
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Pribanic & Pribanic and TopDog Law — active Meta ad status for either firm was not observed this session via available tooling, but both maintain a broader regional and local marketing presence that a Meta strategy would naturally complement
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • A legacy-and-family-story angle — the Gaydos name, 35+ years in White Oak, and multi-generational commitment to the community — is a message no regional competitor can authentically replicate, and it is a natural fit for video and educational content ads
