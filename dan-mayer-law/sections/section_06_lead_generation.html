Lead Generation

Dan Mayer Law is built on a narrow, valuable niche: roughly 150 mental health and healthcare practices served, with new clients arriving through about five to ten referrals a month. Content and social posting have paused since the summer helper left, so there is no second system behind the referrals and no way to share the firm's expertise beyond the people who already know Dan.

Website Audit

"...having AI pushing that out, we are not doing that as well as we could."
The Problem
  • The homepage's only button above the fold is "Learn More," with no call or consultation option for a practice owner who is ready to talk
  • PageSpeed scores: 31/100 mobile — 85/100 desktop (lab test run on the live site, which can differ by several points from Google's own tool)
  • The contact form lives on a separate Contact page instead of on the homepage, and there is no video anywhere on the site
  • The blog's most recent posts are dated July 10, 2026, and no pages target surrounding cities such as Towson or Baltimore
The Positive
  • The site is live, mobile-responsive, and built on Squarespace, so improvements do not require a rebuild from scratch
  • Dan's bio, credentials (Maryland and Wisconsin bars), headshot, testimonials page, and a blog on topics practices care about, such as subpoenas, custody, and discharging clients, are already in place
The Gap
  • A mobile score of 31 means many visitors on phones wait on a slow page, and slow pages lose visitors before they read a word
  • Without a clear "talk to Dan" action above the fold, a practice owner who lands on the site has no obvious next step
  • Content that stops being published sends Google and prospects a quiet signal that the firm is less active than it really is
The website should work while you are focused on clients or away from your desk, not wait on you to find time to update it.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Review platforms we found (Birdeye and Avvo) show only a handful of reviews, and the most recent one appeared to be many months old
  • In the generic Towson and Baltimore healthcare-lawyer results we observed, Dan Mayer Law did not appear, while directory pages for other healthcare attorneys did
  • Listings disagree with each other: the website shows a Brooklandville address, several directories show a Baltimore York Road address with different suite numbers, and the firm name appears as Mayer Law, Mayer Law, LLC, Dan Mayer Law, and Daniel Mayer Law
Blind Spot: A different firm, Mayer Law Firm, LLC, which handles physician employment contracts, shows up in results for the same name, so a prospect searching for "Mayer Law" can land on the wrong firm.
The Positive
  • The firm has profiles on Avvo, Martindale-Hubbell, Birdeye, and the Baltimore Magazine attorney directory, including its mental health specialty listing
The Gap
  • Inconsistent name and address details make it harder for Google to trust which listing is the real Dan Mayer Law
  • With very few reviews visible and no city-focused pages, the firm gives Google little to rank when practices search for healthcare counsel nearby
Neil Bloom — a Towson healthcare law attorney with 37 years of experience who is listed in the Justia and Super Lawyers Towson health care directories that surface for generic healthcare-lawyer searches.
A consistent local presence means referrals are backed by a second source of clients that does not depend on Dan's calendar.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Google ads for Healthcare & Mental Health Law searches such as "mental health practice attorney Baltimore" and "healthcare lawyer for therapists Maryland" were not observed for Dan Mayer Law in this audit
  • The same holds for Business Law and Outside Counsel searches such as "business lawyer Baltimore" and "outside counsel healthcare practice Maryland"; ads also did not come up on the discovery call
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Neil Bloom and Louis Close III (Towson) — the most visible healthcare law attorneys in local directories; their ad activity was not observed in this audit
  • David A. Meyerson, M.D., J.D. (Baltimore) — a physician-lawyer in health care law; his ad activity was also not observed
The Opportunity
  • Healthcare law: searches like "mental health practice attorney Baltimore" and "private practice lawyer Towson MD" reach practice owners actively looking for counsel
  • Business law and outside counsel: "business lawyer Baltimore" and "small business attorney Towson MD" for the start-ups and practice owners who need a general counsel
  • Urgency terms such as "subpoena for therapist records" or "psychiatric emergency legal help" catch a practice at the moment it needs a lawyer
  • Neighboring areas (Towson, Brooklandville, and the wider Baltimore County market) and competitor names, so the firm shows up when prospects compare healthcare attorneys

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Local Service Ads for Dan Mayer Law were not observed, and no Google Screened badge was confirmed
  • Since no LSA review count could be confirmed, this is an opportunity gap to evaluate rather than a confirmed absence
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Neil Bloom, Louis Close III, and David A. Meyerson — LSA placement and review counts for these firms were not observed in this audit
  • No competitor LSA review counts are cited here because none could be verified
The Opportunity
  • Where Google offers LSAs for the firm's practice areas, a Google Screened badge adds a trust signal that referrals currently provide only by word of mouth
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Building Google reviews first would position the firm well for LSA, because review volume and rating strongly influence placement
