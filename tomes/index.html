Here Is Where Tomes Law Firm, PC Stands Today

Frances Tomes has run Tomes Law Firm, PC out of Freehold, NJ since 1993, building a five-practice-area firm (bankruptcy, tax resolution, debt defense, business startups, and wills/estates) with a strong local reputation — 99 reviews at 4.9 stars and a steady ~2 bankruptcy cases per week. But the firm's biggest constraint right now isn't leads or reputation — it's that Frances is working 60-hour weeks personally managing a 7-person team that isn't running itself, and two prior attempts to fix that haven't stuck.

Stage 6 is where Tomes Law Firm runs itself — where Frances gets her time and her life back instead of spending it managing staff who need constant oversight.

Where You Are Today

STAGE 4: SMALL BUSINESS MANAGER
  • A 7-person team (5 remote, 2 in-office) still requires Frances's constant oversight — staff has lacked proactivity and follow-through
  • Two prior fixes — a 2-year coaching engagement with How to Manage and a 6-month local part-time COO — are ending without solving the underlying problem
  • Turnover has cost the firm roughly 30 employees since 2020, alongside communication breakdowns (staff coordinating over WhatsApp, leaving Frances out of the loop)
  • Despite a strong reputation and steady case flow, Frances is working 60-hour weeks and cannot step back from day-to-day oversight

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
Closing the gap between a 60-hour week spent managing an inefficient team and a firm that runs itself does not require more clients or more revenue — it requires the operational structure that has been missing through two prior attempts to fix it.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.
