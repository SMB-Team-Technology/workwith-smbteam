The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The site is built entirely around family law, with 12 family law practice areas listed and no personal injury page
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 83/100 — Desktop 61/100 (Lighthouse lab run; desktop is held back by a Total Blocking Time of about 3.3 seconds from heavy scripts)
  • No video was observed on the site, so visitors cannot see or hear Bill before they call
  • Nothing on the site speaks to injured clients, so paid traffic sent to it would have no relevant page to land on
Blind Spot: Because the PI campaign is separate from your family law SEO, dedicated PI pages can be added without disturbing the rankings your current vendor maintains for the practice you depend on.
The Positive
  • Clear "Schedule Your Consultation" call to action above the fold, a contact form on the homepage, and a mobile-responsive layout
  • A founding attorney bio with 25+ years of experience, a dedicated reviews page with 31 client testimonials, and an active blog with a post as recent as October 1, 2026
The Gap
  • An injured prospect who finds the site sees only a family law firm and has no reason to believe Bill handles truck and wrongful death cases
  • Without PI landing pages, every dollar of future ad spend sends clicks to pages that were not built to convert them
  • Slower desktop interactivity can cost conversions from visitors researching on a work computer
A site that turns the right clicks into the right cases means fewer hours chasing leads and a faster path to retirement.

Intake Assessment

Your paralegal calls new leads back and vets them during business hours, then routes the good ones to you, but nothing covers evenings and weekends. Injury clients usually call more than one firm, so a gap in coverage is a gap in signed cases. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating. This is where the biggest immediate ROI lives.

Status: AMBER
"…she only works during working hours."
The Problem
  • Lead screening depends on one paralegal, so every new lead waits for a callback before it reaches you
  • There is no live after-hours or weekend intake, so evening and weekend inquiries wait until the next business day
  • No written screening criteria for the PI cases you want, such as truck accidents and wrongful death, were described on the call
  • The website AI chat agent mentioned on the call could not be confirmed on the live site, so its coverage is unverified
Blind Spot: Injured people often call several firms in a row, and the first one to answer frequently signs the case. An inquiry that goes unanswered overnight is rarely retried.
The Positive
  • You have already committed to closing the after-hours gap
  • Your paralegal brings eight years of PI experience, a strong base to build the screening process around
The Gap
  • Every unanswered after-hours inquiry is a potential case lost before any marketing dollar has a chance to pay off
  • At a target of about 6 signed cases a month, even a few missed calls change the math on the whole campaign
  • Without clear screening criteria, unqualified leads reach your desk and eat the limited hours you want to protect
The right intake process lets you sign only the cases you want, in the limited hours you want to work.
