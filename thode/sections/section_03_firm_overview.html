Here Is Where Thode Law Firm P.C. Stands Today

Bill Thode has practiced law in Denver for 28 years and built a family law practice that is on track for roughly a million dollars this year. He is now launching a personal injury practice for 2027, and today that practice has no web presence, no lead source, and no intake process built for it. The most important finding: the goal of 6 new PI cases a month is realistic, but he is entering a market where established firms already own the visibility.

Stage 6 is where the firm earns for you without needing you in every case and every call — which is what lets you retire on your own terms instead of working longer than you have to.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • Family law, the established practice, depends on an outside SEO vendor whose results the firm describes as okay, not great
  • The new personal injury practice has no dedicated website pages and no PI ad presence observed, so no PI leads are coming in yet
  • Bill is the litigator and decision-maker, supported by one paralegal who covers business hours only
  • The 2027 goal of $500K–$1M in PI revenue has no lead generation system behind it yet

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a family law firm that depends on you and a PI line that funds your retirement is a lead generation and intake system — and that is what this plan builds.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Thode Law Firm P.C.
Bill is operating at Stage 3 — a Solo Practitioner with deep experience, a protected family law base, and a small team that depends on him for every case. The most urgent gap is the absence of any predictable source of personal injury leads, in a Denver market where competitors already dominate the searches that matter. Until a system is in place, the half-million-dollar-plus PI goal and the retirement it is meant to fund remain out of reach.
