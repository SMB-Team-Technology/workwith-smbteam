Intake Assessment

Your paralegal calls new leads back and vets them during business hours, then routes the good ones to you, but nothing covers evenings and weekends. Injury clients usually call more than one firm, so a gap in coverage is a gap in signed cases. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating. This is where the biggest immediate ROI lives.

Status: AMBER
"…she only works during working hours."
The Problem
  • Lead screening depends on one paralegal, so every new lead waits for a callback before it reaches you
  • There is no live after-hours or weekend intake, so evening and weekend inquiries wait until the next business day
  • No written screening criteria for the PI cases you want, such as truck accidents and wrongful death, were described on the call
  • The website AI chat agent mentioned on the call could not be confirmed on the live site, so its coverage is unverified
Blind Spot: Injured people often call several firms in a row, and the first one to answer frequently signs the case. An inquiry that goes unanswered overnight is rarely retried.
The Positive
  • You have already committed to closing the after-hours gap
  • Your paralegal brings eight years of PI experience, a strong base to build the screening process around
The Gap
  • Every unanswered after-hours inquiry is a potential case lost before any marketing dollar has a chance to pay off
  • At a target of about 6 signed cases a month, even a few missed calls change the math on the whole campaign
  • Without clear screening criteria, unqualified leads reach your desk and eat the limited hours you want to protect
The right intake process lets you sign only the cases you want, in the limited hours you want to work.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • The homepage has a contact form capturing name, phone, email, client status, and inquiry details, a solid base for family law inquiries
  • An AI chat agent was described on the call as available 24/7, but it was not detected on the live site during this review
  • There is no injury-specific form or contact path, so a PI prospect would be routed through a family law inquiry form

These findings are also referenced in the Website Audit section.

Team Assessment

You are the only litigator and the final decision-maker, supported by one paralegal now that the junior attorney who handled family law has left. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: RED
"I'm the litigator."
The Problem
  • You plan to run the PI practice personally, from the first call through resolution
  • One paralegal is the only support behind you, with no second set of hands as case volume grows
  • No other attorney was mentioned on the call for either family law or PI
  • With no associate in place, every new case adds directly to your own hours
Blind Spot: Every signed PI case stays on your desk for months or years, so six new cases a month builds a caseload faster than the hours in your week.
The Positive
  • 28 years of courtroom experience means you can handle the largest cases yourself
  • You are deliberate about hiring only when the work requires it, which keeps overhead low while the PI line is new
The Gap
  • Your income is tied to your personal hours, which is the opposite of the limited-hours retirement you described
  • The firm has no path to take on more cases without you working more
  • If you step away for any reason, there is no attorney to keep cases moving
A PI line that does not need you in every task is the difference between retiring and working longer than you want to.

Profit Plan Assessment

You expect roughly a million dollars in revenue this year and have set a 2027 goal of $500K–$1M in PI revenue, but no cost-per-case or return target has been set for the launch. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"If it's a really robust program and I really have good, you know, reasonable expectations of a return on it, I'm willing to finance it."
The Problem
  • No acquisition-cost or return target has been defined for the PI launch
  • PI cases take months to resolve, so marketing spend comes long before the first fees arrive
  • Funding the launch through financing means every dollar of ad spend needs a measurable return
  • A preference for fewer, larger cases has not yet been tied to a target case value that the budget can be judged against
Blind Spot: Cost per signed case, not cost per lead, decides whether a PI launch pays off. A cheap lead for a small case can cost more than an expensive lead for a truck accident case.
The Positive
  • You are willing to invest in growth as long as the return is clear, which is the right standard
  • You expect to finish the year on track for roughly a million dollars in revenue, giving the PI launch an established base
The Gap
  • Without a return target, there is no way to tell whether the first months of spend are working
  • The gap between the first dollar spent and the first fee collected needs a plan so financing does not outrun cash flow
  • Without tracking cost per signed case by case type, the budget cannot be steered toward the cases that pay best
Planned profit, not discovered profit, is what lets you retire on your timeline.
