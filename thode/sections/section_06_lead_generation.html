Lead Generation

Thode Law Firm's family law practice relies on a single outside SEO vendor whose results the firm describes as okay, not great, while the new personal injury practice has no website pages, no ads observed, and no lead source of its own. With a goal of about six signed PI cases a month, lead generation is the gap between the plan and the result.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The site is built entirely around family law, with 12 family law practice areas listed and no personal injury page
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 83/100 — Desktop 61/100 (Lighthouse lab run; desktop is held back by a Total Blocking Time of about 3.3 seconds from heavy scripts)
  • No video was observed on the site, so visitors cannot see or hear Bill before they call
  • Nothing on the site speaks to injured clients, so paid traffic sent to it would have no relevant page to land on
Blind Spot: Because the PI campaign is separate from your family law SEO, dedicated PI pages can be added without disturbing the rankings your current vendor maintains for the practice you depend on.
The Positive
  • Clear "Schedule Your Consultation" call to action above the fold, a contact form on the homepage, and a mobile-responsive layout
  • A founding attorney bio with 25+ years of experience, a dedicated reviews page with 31 client testimonials, and an active blog with a post as recent as October 1, 2026
The Gap
  • An injured prospect who finds the site sees only a family law firm and has no reason to believe Bill handles truck and wrongful death cases
  • Without PI landing pages, every dollar of future ad spend sends clicks to pages that were not built to convert them
  • Slower desktop interactivity can cost conversions from visitors researching on a work computer
A site that turns the right clicks into the right cases means fewer hours chasing leads and a faster path to retirement.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Three different phone numbers appear across the website, FindLaw, and Justia: (303) 225-3343, 303-515-7436, and (303) 330-0425
  • Two addresses (3801 E Florida Ave and 201 Steele St) and at least five business names are in circulation, including Denver Family Lawyers, William D Thode, PC, and the legacy Denver Business Lawyers
  • FindLaw lists the firm under the wrong category (Criminal Defense), and the firm was not observed in local results for divorce, custody, or personal injury searches
Blind Spot: At least three Yelp listings exist for the firm and its owner, and they split your reputation signals across several profiles. A new PI practice inherits that confusion unless the listings are consolidated.
The Positive
  • Listings already exist on Avvo, Justia, FindLaw, Lawyers.com and Yelp, so the foundation is there to clean up rather than build from zero
The Gap
  • Inconsistent name, address, and phone data weakens the trust signals Google uses to decide who appears in the map results
  • The leading PI firms in Denver have very large Google review bases, so a new PI practice needs a deliberate plan to earn reviews from day one
The Wilhite Law Firm — has built a very large Google review base and established personal injury authority in Denver, giving it the trust signals Google rewards in local PI results.
Clean listings and a steady stream of reviews let your reputation work for you, so new cases do not depend on your time.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Google search ads for family law or personal injury terms were not observed from this firm. Live ad results could not be checked directly, so ads may be running through your vendor.
  • The PI practice has no pages on the website yet, so there is currently no landing page that PI search ads could send traffic to
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • The Harris Law Firm — a paid-search landing page for "best divorce lawyer denver" was seen in search results, suggesting active paid search on divorce keywords.
  • Shafner Injury Law, The O'Sullivan Law Firm, Gerash Steiner Blanton, and Bell & Pollock — each has dedicated bicycle, e-bike, or construction accident pages in Denver. Their ad activity was not observed, but they already hold the page presence that supports ads.
The Opportunity
  • Commercial truck accident searches in Denver, the larger-case category you said you prefer
  • Wrongful death and catastrophic injury searches, where a single signed case matters
  • Aurora, Lakewood, and other Denver-area cities, where the same injury searches can be captured outside the most competitive part of the market
  • Urgency terms such as "free consultation" and "what to do after a truck accident" that catch people at the moment they are choosing a lawyer
Blind Spot: Case-type targeting is the lever that keeps campaigns away from cases smaller than you want to handle. Ads can be built around truck, wrongful death, and serious injury so the phone rings for the work you prefer, and your family law campaigns stay separate.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • The firm was not observed in Local Service Ads. Live results could not be checked, so this is framed as an opportunity gap rather than a confirmed absence.
  • Law firm LSAs require the Google Screened badge, which involves license and background verification, and Google Screened status was not confirmed for this firm
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Frank Azar — a high-profile car and truck accident firm with one of the largest review bases in the market, and a likely contender for top-of-page positions on injury searches.
  • The Wilhite Law Firm — a well-reviewed established PI name in Denver that new entrants will be compared against for these placements.
The Opportunity
  • LSAs rank on responsiveness and reviews, so a new PI practice that answers fast and earns reviews early can compete for these spots
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Pay-per-lead pricing lets you test Denver PI demand without committing to click costs on the most expensive truck accident keywords
Blind Spot: LSA leads arrive as phone calls at any hour. A call that goes to voicemail at 8 PM is a case that goes to the next firm, which is why after-hours intake matters before these campaigns launch.

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • No active Facebook or Instagram ads were observed for family law or personal injury. The Meta Ad Library could not be loaded during this review, so this is framed as an opportunity gap.
  • Because the PI practice is new, there is no PI audience and no retargeting pool built yet
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Shafner Injury Law — maintains dedicated bicycle and e-bike accident content in Denver; its Facebook and Instagram ad activity could not be reviewed in this audit.
  • The O'Sullivan Law Firm — focuses on the e-bike and bicycle niche in Denver; its Facebook and Instagram ad activity could not be reviewed in this audit.
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Educational content about what to do after a commercial truck crash, aimed at Denver-area audiences, builds name recognition before a case is ever needed
