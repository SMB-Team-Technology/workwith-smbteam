Here Is Where Allied Defense Law Stands Today

Rebecca Montoya founded Allied Defense Law in Phoenix almost three years ago and runs a solo DUI and criminal defense practice fed by one referring attorney and a paid LegalMatch subscription. The most important finding: she is happy with the leads she gets, but by her own estimate 5–10 of them a month slip away because there is no one to answer and follow up fast enough.

Stage 6 is where the firm no longer depends on you to answer every call and clear every inbox — and for you, the road there starts with taking intake off your plate so you can take on more cases without building a payroll.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • Leads come from one referring attorney and a paid LegalMatch subscription — no advertising of your own
  • You personally handle every intake call, text, email, and follow-up — there is no other attorney, paralegal, receptionist, or admin
  • After-hours calls go to voicemail, and leads you cannot reach quickly often hire whoever answered first
  • Revenue has grown from your first year to a pace you are proud of, but case capacity is capped by your own hours

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a solo practice that does everything by hand and one that runs on systems is smaller than it feels — and you have already told us the first piece to close: intake.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Allied Defense Law
Rebecca is operating at Stage 3 — a Solo Practitioner with a strong hire rate once a consultation actually happens, but every lead, call, and admin task runs through her alone. The most urgent limit on growth is not lead volume; it is the speed of follow-up, because the leads she cannot reach quickly are hiring someone else. Closing that gap is what lets her take on more cases — without adding attorneys or staff she has said she does not want to manage.
