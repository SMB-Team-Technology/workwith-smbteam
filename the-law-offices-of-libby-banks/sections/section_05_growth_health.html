Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Referral partners supply a little over half of new prospects, and the rest depends on a group of independent SEO, pay-per-click, and social contractors
  • You believe internet SEO is falling short of what it used to be, and clients who once said they found you through ChatGPT are no longer mentioning it
  • Third-party SEO data shown on our call put the site at roughly 430 visitors a month with a high bounce rate and no visibility in ChatGPT or Gemini
A predictable flow of new clients that fills the capacity you have already built is what lets the firm become profitable without leaning on you.

Intake

  • A dedicated intake person, a very high show rate, and roughly 70% of consults signing are a strong foundation to build on
  • Tracing a signed client back to the marketing that produced it still means manual digging, so spend cannot yet be tied cleanly to results
  • The front-desk role is in replacement mode, and about one in ten consults goes quiet before signing
Every additional consult you convert adds profit without adding another marketing dollar — a faster path to a firm that does not depend on you.

Self-Managing Team

  • Two associate attorneys, now close to two years in, have not yet generated business at the level their added capacity was meant to support
  • No minimums or KPIs exist yet for networking and business development, so growth still leans on the owner's relationships
  • A succession path to the associates is envisioned, but the timing and readiness are still open
The firm can only be sold or handed over when the next generation of attorneys can produce the business, not just serve it.

Profit

  • Revenue has grown 15–20% a year, but payroll after the second attorney hire now takes roughly half of revenue
  • You told us the firm is doing okay but could be more profitable, and that profitability is the key to being able to sell or step away
  • Pricing has room to move — you see the firm as upper-middle of the market and open to a modest increase
A firm that is profitable without you in it is the one you can sell, hand over, or step back from on your own terms.
