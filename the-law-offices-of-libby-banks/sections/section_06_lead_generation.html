Lead Generation

The Law Office of Libby Banks already runs a broad marketing mix — referral partners, in-house seminars, local newspapers, SEO, pay-per-click, two social channels, and monthly video — yet the owner is not sure why that effort is not increasing volume more than it has. The opportunity is less about adding channels and more about a measured paid-media system that turns high-intent Scottsdale searches into consults, plus visibility in the AI answers where affluent clients now look.

Website Audit

"I think our internet SEO marketing is falling short of what it used to be."
The Problem
  • The site's security layer blocked our automated review tools, so this assessment relies on indexed page titles and the third-party SEO data shown on our discovery call rather than a live page-by-page walkthrough
  • PageSpeed scores: mobile and desktop scores were not obtained in this review, and we will run them live rather than estimate them
  • The third-party tool shown on our call put the site at roughly 430 visitors a month with a very high bounce rate, meaning many people who arrive leave without taking a next step
  • The same tool showed organic visibility falling in August 2021 and again in August 2025, and no presence in ChatGPT or Gemini results (tool data, not independently verified)
Blind Spot: A site can rank in Google and still be invisible to AI assistants. ChatGPT and Gemini pull from clear, FAQ-style answers more than from traditional blog posts, so the content that once earned rankings may not be the content that earns AI recommendations.
The Positive
  • City and service pages exist for Scottsdale, Phoenix, and North Phoenix, along with specific pages for topics like A-B trusts, irrevocable trusts, and probate
  • An attorney profile page is in place, and the firm already produces monthly video content with a YouTube channel behind it
The Gap
  • A site drawing roughly 430 visitors a month cannot carry a stronger paid-media program on its own — ads need pages built to turn clicks into consults
  • Not appearing when a prospect asks an AI assistant who the best estate planning attorney is means those prospects never reach your name
  • Dedicated pages for other nearby affluent cities were not seen in search results, and whether the site has FAQ-style answer content still needs to be confirmed
A website that reliably turns searchers into consults is part of how the firm fills its capacity and builds profit that does not depend on you.

The Gap
  • Google reviews are the first thing referred prospects, local searchers, and AI assistants all weigh, so asking for a review at every consult and signing with a direct link — a goal of 50 or more was discussed on our call — is the highest-leverage fix
  • Without a confirmed view of map-pack position across estate planning, probate, and business formation searches, the firm cannot yet see where it is losing searches it should win
Bivens & Associates, P.L.L.C. — shares your Shea Blvd corridor and presents a visibly strong review base in directory listings, the trust signal that local search and prospects weigh most.
Winning the local search and review race is how referral-quality trust reaches the clients who have never heard of you — without more of your time.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • You confirmed pay-per-click is running with a monthly spending limit, and that ad spend is modest compared with the overall marketing budget; where in the month the limit is reached is not currently known
  • Live ad headlines, descriptions, and keyword coverage for estate planning, probate, and business formation searches were not captured in this review and need a live check
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Bivens & Associates, John B. Even, and RJP Estate Planning — competitor ad messaging for Scottsdale estate planning searches was not observed in this review; these three firms are the first to check live
The Opportunity
  • Core searches such as "estate planning attorney Scottsdale," "living trust attorney Scottsdale," and "special needs trust attorney Scottsdale" capture people already ready to book a consult
  • Probate and trust administration searches reach families at the moment they need help, a practice area you already serve without taking contested matters
  • Neighboring affluent areas such as Paradise Valley, Fountain Hills, and Cave Creek extend your reach beyond the ten core zip codes without opening a new office
  • At your own numbers — an average plan of about $4,500 and roughly 70% of consults signing — each additional consult is worth about $3,150 in expected revenue, which is the yardstick every ad dollar should be measured against
Blind Spot: If a monthly spending limit is reached before month-end, ads switch off during the very days high-intent prospects are still searching. Checking when the limit is hit is a quick way to see whether budget, not demand, is the constraint.
