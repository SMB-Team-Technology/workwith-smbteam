Here Is Where The Law Office of Libby Banks Stands Today

The Law Office of Libby Banks is a Scottsdale estate planning firm — wills, trusts, special needs trusts, and non-contested probate and trust administration — serving higher-net-worth families across roughly ten core zip codes. With a 70% consult-to-signed rate, a strong referral-partner base, and a team of ten, the firm is running at about half of its capacity, and the owner's own words are that marketing and referral efforts are not growing volume the way the added attorney capacity was meant to.

Stage 6 is where the firm is profitable enough to run — and one day be handed over — without you at the center of it, on your own terms, and it is exactly where we are taking you.

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner

The gap between a firm at half capacity and a firm profitable enough to run without you is not about working harder — it is about turning the capacity you have already built into predictable, profitable growth, and that is exactly what this plan is for.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
The Law Office of Libby Banks
The Law Office of Libby Banks is operating at Stage 4 — a Small Business Manager with several lead sources, a ten-person team, and a strong close rate, but still dependent on the owner and her referral relationships. The most urgent gap is that the capacity added with two associate attorneys is only about half used, while search visibility, AI-search visibility, and source tracking are not keeping pace. Until marketing reliably fills that capacity, the profit needed to sell or step back without Libby stays out of reach.
