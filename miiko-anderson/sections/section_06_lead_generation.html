Lead Generation

Miiko Anderson Law has grown entirely on word of mouth, personal relationships, and a bar association referral service — with no marketing for the firm to date. The result is a practice with real credibility but no second source of clients when the referrals go quiet.

Website Audit

"I feel like it's used for somebody who's been referred to me to go and look me up to verify that I'm legit and then they call me."
The Problem
  • The website works as a credibility check for referrals, not as a source of new clients
  • The contact form sits on a separate Contact page — the homepage offers only a mailing-list sign-up and a consultation button
  • No city-specific pages were found for Clovis, Madera, or other surrounding communities
  • No blog or video content was observed, and an older home page still appears in the site structure with different copy
Blind Spot: You mentioned a separate consulting website that you believe outranks the law firm site when people Google your name. If that is true, someone who was referred to you may land on the wrong site first — and a prospect who cannot quickly find the law firm may simply move on to the next name.
The Positive
  • The homepage leads with strong trust signals — Certified Criminal Law Specialist, former senior prosecutor, and experienced trial attorney
  • A full attorney bio documents more than 13,000 courtroom appearances and years in the Fresno County District Attorney's Office
  • The site was built recently, with practice area pages for criminal defense, post-conviction relief, victim representation, and more
The Gap
  • Searchers who were not referred have little reason to stay, because the site is not built to capture and convert them
  • Without city-focused pages, the firm has little chance of appearing for searches in the communities around Fresno
  • Strong credentials are present, but your brand voice and what makes you different are not yet carrying the message
A website that brings in clients on its own — not just confirms the ones who already know you — is how the firm stops depending on you being everywhere.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Local visibility for searches like "criminal defense attorney Fresno," "Fresno DUI lawyer," and "domestic violence defense attorney Fresno" has not yet been benchmarked — Google Business Profile details such as category, photos, posts, and review volume should be confirmed as the first step
  • The website lists a toll-free (855) number — the firm's name, address, and phone should read identically on Google, directories, and the website so search engines trust the listing
  • The firm has no city-specific content to compete for "near me" and neighboring-community searches
The Positive
  • An active State Bar of California listing plus profiles on LinkedIn, the Aequitas Institute, and San Joaquin College of Law give the firm credibility signals that search engines and prospects can both see
The Gap
  • Fresno criminal defense searchers compare options by reviews and map listings before they ever visit a website
  • Without a steady, ongoing flow of client reviews and an optimized Google Business Profile, the firm is easy to overlook next to better-established names
Law Office of Jon K. Renge — appears prominently in Fresno criminal defense directory results with an established client-review profile, which is what puts a firm in front of local searchers first.
Showing up first when someone in Fresno needs a criminal defense attorney is how new, higher-value clients find you instead of the other way around.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Criminal defense: Miiko told us she has not advertised for the law firm, so there is no existing campaign history to fix — a clean starting point
  • Victim representation and other abuse-related work: no Google search ads were observed for these services either, which means this demand is not yet being captured
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Competitor ad activity was not observed in this audit — it should be benchmarked live before launch
  • Established Fresno firms such as Law Office of Jon K. Renge, The Law Office of Gregory W. Fox, and Mitchell Law Group are the names a searcher will be comparing Miiko against
The Opportunity
  • Core criminal defense searches such as "criminal defense attorney Fresno," "Fresno DUI lawyer," and "domestic violence defense attorney Fresno"
  • Victim-focused searches such as "sexual abuse attorney Fresno" and "school abuse lawyer Fresno" for the second side of the practice
  • Surrounding-community searches in Clovis, Madera, and nearby cities that the current website does not target
  • Urgent, high-intent searches from people who have just been arrested or contacted by police and need a lawyer right away

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • The firm was not observed in Local Service Ads for Fresno criminal defense searches in this audit — an opportunity gap rather than a confirmed absence
  • Google Screened status was not observed, and it is the entry requirement for these placements
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Which Fresno firms currently hold Local Service Ad positions was not observed in this audit and should be checked live before launch
The Opportunity
  • Earning the Google Screened badge gives a solo attorney with strong credentials a visible trust signal at the very top of the page
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Early client reviews will determine how competitive the listing is, so building a steady review flow alongside LSAs matters

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • Miiko told us she has not run Facebook or Instagram ads for the law firm; the firm's presence is a handful of organic posts on Instagram, LinkedIn, and Facebook
  • No active law firm ads were observed in the Meta Ad Library in this audit
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Competitor Meta ad activity was not observed in this audit and should be reviewed live before launch
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Messaging built around your "defense and advocacy for the whole person" approach to set you apart from other Fresno defense firms
