Here Is Where Miiko Anderson Law Stands Today

Miiko Anderson is a Certified Criminal Law Specialist and former senior prosecutor who now runs a solo criminal defense and victim-advocacy practice in Fresno. Every client so far has come through word of mouth and personal relationships, with no marketing for the firm at all — a strong reputation, but no system that turns it into a steady flow of new cases.

Stage 6 is where the firm is "scaled to the level that I'm not needed in every case" — and for you, the road there starts with a predictable flow of the right clients.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • Word of mouth, personal relationships, and a bar association referral service are the only sources of new clients
  • You personally take the first conversation with every prospective client
  • Monthly revenue is uneven, which makes it hard to plan hires with confidence
  • Your website mainly confirms credibility for people who were already referred to you, and your brand voice is still being defined

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a practice that depends on you in every case and a firm with space and freedom built in is closeable — it starts with a lead system that does not depend on who you happen to know.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Miiko Anderson Law
