Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) is the only paid channel, and every ad feeds a single landing-page-to-webinar funnel
  • Registrations and consultations have dropped over the past year, and webinar attendance has slipped to around 40%
  • Google Ads were tried before and set aside because the leads felt cold, so no second paid channel is backing up Meta
A steadier lead engine is what lets you grow past one major client without carrying the pipeline yourself.

Intake

  • You are the lead closer on consultations, so conversion depends on your calendar
  • A second licensed practitioner is being trained, but consultation volume is not yet high enough to hand calls over
  • Every lead must register, attend and book through one funnel, so drop-off at any step reduces the consultations that reach you
Intake that does not depend on you is the biggest single step toward being out of the middle of the business.

Self-Managing Team

  • A 10-person team, including two automation engineers, already runs much of the firm's internal work through automation
  • Ad placement and ad creative are handled by separate remote contractors rather than one accountable partner
  • Closing consultations and monitoring ad results day to day still sit with you
Freedom comes when every step from lead to signed client has an owner other than you.

Profit

  • Revenue holds at about CAD 1 million a year, with most of it coming from one corporate client
  • A recent month saw ad spend significantly outrun results, and cost per lead cannot yet be stated with a figure
  • The goal is to grow revenue at least fivefold, which makes measured cost per signed client essential
Spreading revenue across many individual clients is how the firm becomes safer and more valuable, not just bigger.
