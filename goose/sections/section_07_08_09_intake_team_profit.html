Intake Assessment

At Goose Law, every lead moves through a single path of landing page, webinar registration and paid consultation, and you personally close the consultation. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: RED
"I feel confident closing leads, but I shouldn't be the guy. It shouldn't be dependent on me."
The Problem
  • You are the lead closer on consultations, so conversion depends on your calendar
  • A second licensed practitioner is being trained to take consultations, but volume is not yet high enough to hand calls over
  • Every lead must register for the webinar, attend and then book, and attendance has slipped from the 50–60% range to around 40%
  • Fewer people are registering and fewer consultations are being booked than a year ago
Blind Spot: When attendance falls, a growing share of paid registrations never reach a conversation, so ad spend can look like lead flow while fewer prospects actually meet the person who can sign them.
The Positive
  • Historically you closed 10 to 15 percent of consultations, so the closing skill is clearly there
  • Much of the firm's internal work is already automated, a strong base for a repeatable intake process
The Gap
  • Every consultation you personally take is time not spent running or growing the firm
  • With individual permanent-residency files valued around CAD 10,000 each, every consultation that does not reach a decision is meaningful revenue left on the table
  • Falling registrations and attendance mean fewer consultations to convert, no matter how strong the closing is
An intake process that does not need you on every call is what turns a lead flow into a firm that runs without you.

Team Assessment

Goose Law has a 10-person team and strong internal automation, but the steps that bring in and convert clients still run through you. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: AMBER
"I don't want all of that to be dependent on me because I don't, I won't always have the ability to be monitoring this stuff on a day-to-day basis"
The Problem
  • Closing consultations sits with you, and the second practitioner is not yet taking them
  • Ad placement and ad creative are handled by separate remote contractors, with no single partner accountable for results
  • You are still the one monitoring ad performance day to day
Blind Spot: With this much automation already in place, the missing piece is not more tools. It is a defined owner for each step from lead to signed client.
The Positive
  • Two automation engineers on the team mean much of what the firm does internally is already automated
  • You have said the firm has the capacity to handle more work, so added leads would not overwhelm delivery
The Gap
  • Because you sit at the center of closing and ad oversight, you cannot step back without growth stalling
  • A trained second practitioner is capacity the firm is not using yet, because lead volume is too low
  • Every additional lead lands on the same calendar until closing is shared
A team where each step has an owner other than you is how the firm grows without depending on you.

Profit Plan Assessment

Goose Law brings in about CAD 1 million a year, with most of it tied to one corporate client, a position you describe as good but very risky. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"We have one major client, a corporate client, from whom 80% of our revenue comes, right? It's a good position, but it's also very risky."
The Problem
  • Most of the firm's revenue depends on a single corporate client
  • A recent month saw ad spend significantly outrun the results it produced
  • Cost per lead and cost per signed client cannot yet be stated with a figure, so spend decisions rely on feel
  • The goal of growing at least fivefold needs a clear view of what each signed client costs to acquire
Blind Spot: A cheaper cost per registration is not the same as a cheaper cost per signed client, so only tracking the first can hide where money is really being made or lost.
The Positive
  • Revenue is steady at about CAD 1 million, which you describe as never falling below that level
  • Individual permanent-residency files carry strong value, around CAD 10,000 per application
The Gap
  • Without a measured cost per consultation and per client, you cannot say when it is safe to spend more
  • Revenue concentrated in one client limits how freely the firm can invest and plan
  • Building a base of many individual clients is what turns the same revenue into a safer, more valuable firm
Knowing what each client costs, and spreading revenue across many, is how growth becomes wealth you can count on.
