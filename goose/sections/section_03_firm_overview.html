Here Is Where Goose Law Stands Today

Goose Law is a Mississauga-based Canadian immigration firm led by Donovan Francis, serving businesses on work-permit and corporate immigration matters and, increasingly, individuals seeking permanent residency, with a 10-person team. Annual revenue is about CAD 1 million, most of it tied to a single corporate client, while the Meta-ad-to-webinar funnel that once drove strong growth in individual files is now producing fewer registrations and consultations.

Stage 6 is where Goose Law grows without depending on any single client, or on you being the one who converts every lead — and it is exactly where we are taking you.

Where You Are Today

Stage 4: Small Business Manager
  • Meta ads feeding a landing page, a monthly WebinarJam event and paid consultations are the firm's one scalable lead source
  • Registrations, webinar attendance and consultations have all fallen from where they were a year ago
  • You are still the person who closes consultations, with a second practitioner in training but not yet taking calls
  • Most revenue comes from one corporate client, a position you describe as good but very risky

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap is not as wide as it feels: you already have a team, automation and a funnel that has worked before. What is missing is a steadier lead engine and a way to convert leads without you at the center.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Goose Law
Goose Law is operating at Stage 4, a small business with a 10-person team, one proven but shrinking lead source and an owner who is still the closer. Closing that gap is what stands between today's roughly CAD 1 million and the fivefold growth you want, and between you and a firm that does not depend on you to convert every lead.
