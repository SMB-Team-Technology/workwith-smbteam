Lead Generation

Goose Law's lead generation runs through one path: Meta ads to a landing page that registers prospects into a monthly webinar, then on to a paid consultation. That path has produced strong results before, but registrations and consultations have fallen and there is no second paid channel behind it.

Website Audit

"in terms of, like, building websites and so on, we don't, you know, that stuff, I can get that done pretty cheaply."
The Problem
  • Homepage layout, calls to action and contact-form placement could not be assessed in this review because the site's bot protection blocked our automated check; these will be confirmed in a live walkthrough
  • PageSpeed scores: not captured for this report, and no figures are shown rather than estimating. Mobile and desktop scores will be run and shared in the live review
  • Websites and landing pages are built with low-cost resources, so speed, trust signals and conversion elements are worth testing rather than assuming
Blind Spot: A landing page whose only job is to register people for a webinar can quietly lose visitors who were ready to book a consultation straight away.
The Positive
  • The funnel already connects a landing page to WebinarJam and to Twilio text follow-up, which shows the technical groundwork is in place
  • Internal automation is strong, so improvements to the site and funnel can be implemented quickly
The Gap
  • Without current speed and conversion data, there is no way to know how many paid clicks are lost before they register
  • The site and landing page are the destination for every Meta ad, so any weakness affects every paid lead
  • A verified baseline is needed before ad spend is increased, so added budget is not poured into a leaky page
A site that converts paid clicks reliably is the foundation for growing without you personally watching every number.

The Opportunity
  • Individual permanent residency: "permanent residence lawyer Mississauga", "Express Entry lawyer Mississauga" and "Canadian immigration lawyer", the searches closest to your growth focus
  • Business immigration: "work permit lawyer Mississauga" and "LMIA lawyer Mississauga" to capture employers looking for counsel
  • Neighboring cities such as Brampton, Oakville and Toronto, where prospects searching for an immigration lawyer may not stop at the Mississauga border
  • Urgency terms such as refused applications, expiring work permits and renewal deadlines, which signal a prospect ready to book a consultation now
Blind Spot: Google leads that felt cold in the past may reflect how the campaign was set up, including keywords, landing page and speed of follow-up, more than the channel itself.

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • Meta is the firm's only paid channel, and ads send traffic to a landing page that registers people into a monthly WebinarJam event
  • Many current ads are AI-generated video ads using your own likeness, which is an early adopter's edge. Results have faded over time, and a recent month saw spend significantly outrun returns
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Competitor Meta activity — the Meta Ad Library could not be reviewed in this research, so competitor ad counts, formats and run times are not shown. These will be pulled live before the call
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — registrants who did not attend and visitors who did not book a consultation should keep seeing follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — you are already doing this, and a managed testing program can show which hooks and formats bring the most qualified consultations
  • Educational content ads — capture people earlier in the permanent-residency decision process
  • Measure cost per consultation booked and per client signed, not just cost per registration, so a month like the recent one is caught early
