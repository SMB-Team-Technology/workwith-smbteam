Here Is Where Goose Law Stands Today

Goose Law is a Mississauga-based Canadian immigration firm led by Donovan Francis, serving businesses on work-permit and corporate immigration matters and, increasingly, individuals seeking permanent residency, with a 10-person team. Annual revenue is about CAD 1 million, most of it tied to a single corporate client, while the Meta-ad-to-webinar funnel that once drove strong growth in individual files is now producing fewer registrations and consultations.

Stage 6 is where Goose Law grows without depending on any single client, or on you being the one who converts every lead — and it is exactly where we are taking you.

Where You Are Today

Stage 4: Small Business Manager
  • Meta ads feeding a landing page, a monthly WebinarJam event and paid consultations are the firm's one scalable lead source
  • Registrations, webinar attendance and consultations have all fallen from where they were a year ago
  • You are still the person who closes consultations, with a second practitioner in training but not yet taking calls
  • Most revenue comes from one corporate client, a position you describe as good but very risky

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap is not as wide as it feels: you already have a team, automation and a funnel that has worked before. What is missing is a steadier lead engine and a way to convert leads without you at the center.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Intake Assessment

At Goose Law, every lead moves through a single path of landing page, webinar registration and paid consultation, and you personally close the consultation. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: RED
"I feel confident closing leads, but I shouldn't be the guy. It shouldn't be dependent on me."
The Problem
  • You are the lead closer on consultations, so conversion depends on your calendar
  • A second licensed practitioner is being trained to take consultations, but volume is not yet high enough to hand calls over
  • Every lead must register for the webinar, attend and then book, and attendance has slipped from the 50–60% range to around 40%
  • Fewer people are registering and fewer consultations are being booked than a year ago
Blind Spot: When attendance falls, a growing share of paid registrations never reach a conversation, so ad spend can look like lead flow while fewer prospects actually meet the person who can sign them.
The Positive
  • Historically you closed 10 to 15 percent of consultations, so the closing skill is clearly there
  • Much of the firm's internal work is already automated, a strong base for a repeatable intake process
The Gap
  • Every consultation you personally take is time not spent running or growing the firm
  • With individual permanent-residency files valued around CAD 10,000 each, every consultation that does not reach a decision is meaningful revenue left on the table
  • Falling registrations and attendance mean fewer consultations to convert, no matter how strong the closing is
An intake process that does not need you on every call is what turns a lead flow into a firm that runs without you.
