Lead Generation

Prendamano Tax Resolution & State Tax Advisory is spending $4,200/month across two outside marketing vendors — a Facebook Ads agency and a separate SEO agency — that together have produced one signed client since the firm launched. Meanwhile the firm has zero presence on Google Search, Local Service Ads, or a claimed Google Business Profile, the exact channels where someone facing an IRS problem right now goes looking for help.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The FAQ section displays a Washington-state area code phone number ((253) 235-4038) that does not match the firm's real Long Island number anywhere else on the site — likely leftover template content that was never removed
  • PageSpeed scores could not be pulled live in this research pass (tooling quota limitation) — mobile and desktop scores should be confirmed on the proposal call before finalizing website scope
  • The homepage CTA links out to a separate contact page rather than surfacing a form above the fold — the request is buried one click away
  • No live chat and no video content — visitors get no interactive way to engage or hear from Jennifer directly before calling
Blind Spot: One CTA sentence on the site itself reads "(631) 886-5530" — a transposed-digit version of the correct (631) 886-5630 number used everywhere else. A prospect calling that number while in crisis over an IRS notice gets nothing, and most will not try twice.
The Positive
  • Jennifer has a dedicated attorney bio with photo, NY State Bar credentials, CTRC certification, and a clear "26+ years of experience" narrative — strong trust-building content once visitors reach it
  • The blog is active and current, with a new post roughly every week through August 2026 — a real, ongoing content asset most solo firms never maintain
The Gap
  • Two conflicting phone numbers on the same site cost real calls — anyone who dials the wrong one before finding the right one may not call back
  • A buried contact form means visitors who are ready to act have to work for it, and urgency-driven prospects rarely do
  • None of the site's genuine credibility (26+ years, CTRC certification, active blog) is currently being amplified by any paid channel
Fixing these trust and conversion leaks costs nothing in ad spend — it simply stops the site from quietly turning away the very clients who are already trying to reach you.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • No claimed Google Business Profile, Yelp, Avvo, or BBB listing could be found for Prendamano Tax Resolution — the firm currently has no local review footprint to build trust with
  • The firm does not appear in local search results for either core practice area — IRS tax resolution or state tax advisory — anywhere in the Long Island market
  • No directory presence on Avvo, Justia, or FindLaw was found, leaving several standard local SEO trust signals completely unclaimed
Blind Spot: The website already states the firm serves clients "in any state" and operates 100% virtually — but there are no state-specific or city-specific landing pages to capture that nationwide search demand. This is a built-in SEO opportunity for a virtual practice that most local competitors, tied to one office, cannot use.
The Gap
  • Every local search for "IRS tax resolution attorney Long Island" or "offer in compromise attorney Suffolk County" is currently going to competitors instead
  • A firm with zero reviews and no GBP has to win on ads and content first — reviews take active client volume to build, which is exactly what is missing today
Tenenbaum Law, P.C. — based in the same city, Melville, with far more reviews and 25+ years of local positioning in the same tax-resolution niche, giving them the trust signals a brand-new firm has not yet built.
A claimed, optimized Google Business Profile is one of the fastest, lowest-cost ways to start closing the credibility gap with Tenenbaum Law and the other established firms already ranking in your own market.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Prendamano Tax Resolution does not appear to be running Google Search ads for IRS tax resolution or back taxes — consistent with the transcript, where all current paid spend is on Facebook, not Google
  • The firm does not appear to be running Google Search ads for state tax advisory either — this practice area has no paid presence anywhere today
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Tenenbaum Law, P.C. and J. David Tax Law — both have the review volume and multi-market presence typical of firms actively bidding on IRS tax debt keywords; live ad placements should be confirmed before the proposal call
  • Goldburd McCone LLP and Meltzer Lippe — the most visible organic competitors for state tax advisory searches, and plausible ad spenders in that niche as well
The Opportunity
  • Obvious terms: "IRS tax resolution attorney Long Island," "back taxes help Melville NY," "offer in compromise attorney Suffolk County"
  • Obvious terms: "NY state tax attorney audit representation," "state and local tax attorney Long Island"
  • Non-obvious: competitor-conquesting campaigns against Tenenbaum Law and J. David Tax Law for prospects comparing options
  • Non-obvious: leveraging the firm's own stated virtual, nationwide service model to capture "IRS tax resolution attorney" searches outside Long Island, where local competitors cannot compete on convenience
Blind Spot: Someone who just received an IRS notice is one of the highest-intent searchers in legal marketing — they are actively looking for help right now, not researching for later. Every day without a Google Ads presence hands that exact moment to a competitor.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Prendamano Tax Resolution does not appear in Local Service Ads and is not Google Screened today
  • With no GBP and no reviews yet, the firm currently has no foothold in this placement at all
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Live LSA placements for named competitors could not be confirmed in this research pass and should be checked before the proposal call
  • Given the emotionally urgent nature of "IRS problem" searches, LSAs are a strong structural fit for this niche regardless of which specific competitors currently hold spots
The Opportunity
  • The Google Screened badge gives a brand-new firm with zero reviews an instant, Google-verified trust signal — something Tenenbaum Law and Long Island Tax Resolution Services had to earn over years
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Pay-per-lead pricing fits a firm still building its budget discipline better than pure pay-per-click spend
Blind Spot: Because the firm has no reviews yet, LSA's Google Screened badge may do more to build instant credibility here than review count alone would for a firm this new.

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • The firm is actively running Facebook Ads through the Closers.io agency at $3,000/month ($100/day) — the campaign's live creative could not be pulled from the Ad Library in this research pass and should be reviewed manually
  • Per the call, this campaign has produced a low volume of unqualified leads and only one signed client in its first month — a targeting or message-match problem more than a channel problem
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Tenenbaum Law, P.C. and Long Island Tax Resolution Services — plausible Meta advertisers given their established market presence; live ad activity could not be confirmed due to a tooling failure in this research pass and should be checked manually
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Jennifer's 26+ years of experience and CTRC certification are strong on-camera credibility assets that the current Facebook campaign does not appear to be using — a message-match fix, paired with a shift toward higher-intent Google traffic, is the fastest path to turning $3,000/month from unqualified leads into signed clients
