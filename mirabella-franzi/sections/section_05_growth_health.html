Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • New clients come strictly from word-of-mouth referral — no advertising has ever been run
  • Estate planning and probate are never marketed; they arrive only from other transactions
  • No ongoing SEO, and the estate planning page on the website is one short page with no articles behind it
You cannot become a leader in estate planning while the only way clients find that practice is by being sent there from a real estate closing.

Intake

  • Intake has been built around referred clients who already trust the firm — it has never handled a stream of cold inquiries
  • The website offers a phone number above the fold and a contact form only on a separate page
  • Staff can handle initial inputting on probate files, which is a workable base to build on
Predictable growth only counts if every new inquiry is answered and converted fast enough to protect the reputation you have built.

Self-Managing Team

  • Three attorneys and six support staff give the firm real capacity — roughly four additional files a month would be easy to absorb
  • No one at the firm owns marketing or lead generation today, because it has never been part of the operation
  • Growth plans are paced deliberately, with hiring and office expansion to follow demand
A team that can grow with the firm is already in place — it needs a steady flow of work and an owner for the systems that create it.

Profit

  • Revenue has stayed at about $1 million for several years, with the COVID-era refinance volume gone
  • Real estate fees per file are much smaller, so profit depends on high volume
  • No specific revenue or profit targets have been set against the 25% growth goal
Shifting the mix toward higher-fee estate and probate work is how growth can add profit without adding proportionally more volume.
