Lead Generation

Mirabella & Franzi has grown to roughly $1 million a year entirely on word-of-mouth referral, with no advertising and no ongoing SEO since the firm started. Estate planning and probate — the practice areas the firm wants to grow first — are not marketed at all and reach the firm only when a real estate transaction happens to send them over.

Website Audit

"We're not super tech savvy, which is why we're kind of in the place that we're currently in."
The Problem
  • The estate planning page is a single short page of about four bullets — no FAQs, no process explanation, no fee guidance and no content specific to Garden City or Nassau County
  • Lighthouse performance scores: Mobile 65/100 — Desktop 89/100. The mobile experience is noticeably slower than desktop, and most local searches happen on phones
  • The only call to action above the fold is a phone number; the contact form lives on a separate Contact page and does not appear on the homepage, estate planning page or personal injury page
  • There is no blog or article content and no video, and no attorney or legal-service structured data was observed, which limits what search engines and AI answer tools can pull from the site
Blind Spot: You told us you want to explore advertising on AI search. AI answer tools quote sites that answer real questions in depth — such as how probate works in Nassau County or when a trust makes sense — and a four-bullet estate planning page gives them very little to cite.
The Positive
  • The site was rebuilt within the last year, so the firm is not starting from an outdated design
  • It is mobile responsive, and both attorneys have their own bio pages that build credibility
The Gap
  • A prospect searching for a wills, trusts or probate lawyer lands on a page with almost nothing to answer their questions, so they leave for a firm that does
  • Visitors who are ready to reach out have to find the Contact page first, and every extra step loses inquiries
  • With no articles or FAQs, the site gives Google and AI tools no reason to recommend the firm for estate planning
A website that earns trust and answers questions is the foundation of becoming the recognized estate planning leader in Garden City.
