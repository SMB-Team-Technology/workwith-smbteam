Intake Assessment

Mirabella & Franzi's intake has grown up around referred clients who already trust the firm, which is very different from answering strangers who found you through a search or an ad. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • Estate planning and probate inquiries have only ever come from existing relationships, so the firm has never had to respond quickly to cold inquiries from people comparing several lawyers
  • The homepage offers a phone number but no form or button above the fold, so anyone who prefers not to call has no immediate way to reach out
  • The contact form sits on its own Contact page and asks for a free-form subject and message, which is more effort than a short request-a-consultation form
  • No form appears on the estate planning page or the personal injury page, the two places a prospect decides whether to reach out
Blind Spot: When growth comes from advertising, the people who inquire are comparing firms and often contact several at once. The first firm to respond usually wins the file, which is a speed standard a referral-based intake has never needed to meet.
The Positive
  • Staff can do the initial inputting on probate files with attorney review, which gives the firm a ready-made process to scale
  • You told us the team could very easily take on about four more files a month, so capacity is not the constraint
The Gap
  • Every inquiry that arrives with no easy way to submit a request, or sits unanswered, is a file that goes to the next firm on the list
  • Without a defined response process for new estate planning inquiries, results from any new marketing will depend on who happens to answer the phone
  • The firm cannot tell which marketing source produced which inquiry until tracking is built into the intake path
Becoming a leader in the industry means every new family that reaches out is answered quickly, professionally and consistently.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • The only contact form is on the separate Contact page, and it asks for name, phone, email, subject and message
  • The header offers click-to-call, but there is no form, button or consultation request on the homepage, estate planning page or personal injury page
  • No live chat or other real-time inquiry option was found, so visitors browsing outside office hours have no quick way to leave a request

These findings are also referenced in the Website Audit section.

Team Assessment

Mirabella & Franzi has three attorneys and six support staff, which is a solid base for a firm of this size and plenty of room to grow into. The question is who will own the systems that bring new work in, because today growth is not anyone's job. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: AMBER
"We could very easily pick up four files a month."
The Problem
  • No one at the firm owns marketing or lead generation, because the firm has never marketed — new work simply arrives or it does not
  • Probate work needs attorney review even when staff do the initial inputting, so attorney time is the pinch point as estate files increase
  • Growth decisions rest on the two partners agreeing together, with no separate role to run a marketing program day to day
  • The firm describes itself as not very tech savvy, so website, tracking and online-listing work has no internal owner and depends on outside providers
Blind Spot: Your plan to hire and expand office space as work grows is sound, but it only works if demand is predictable. Hiring ahead of an unpredictable referral flow is risky, while hiring behind a steady one is simple — the system has to come first.
The Positive
  • The firm already carries a high volume of real estate work with its current team, which shows the staff can handle sustained transaction flow
  • Staff are already trusted with initial work on probate files, so the team has experience in the practice area you want to grow
The Gap
  • Without a defined owner for lead generation, attorney time stays tied up in referral relationships instead of a system that works while you serve clients
  • A firm that grows by chance cannot plan hiring or office space, which is the next step you described
  • Any growth in estate and probate work will land on the same attorneys unless review and follow-up are shared across the team
Becoming an industry leader at a pace you control means the team has clear roles for bringing work in and for delivering it well.

Profit Plan Assessment

Mirabella & Franzi has held revenue at about $1 million for several years and has set its sights on at least 25% more, roughly another $250,000. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"The PI practice is a smaller portion of our practice, although profitability-wise tends to be pretty large, whereas by volume, we do a significant amount of real estate, but the legal fees on those transactions are much smaller."
The Problem
  • The firm's largest source of volume, real estate, carries smaller fees per file, so reaching a higher profit takes a lot more files
  • The COVID-era refinance volume that once added to the practice no longer exists at current interest rates
  • You have not set specific revenue or profit targets, so the 25% goal has no number attached to each practice area or each month
  • You told us any investment depends on a return, but with no marketing history there is no baseline cost per client to measure a return against
Blind Spot: If nothing changes, you said profitability will probably stay about the same. That means the real cost of waiting is not a lost opportunity someday — it is another year at the same $1 million.
The Positive
  • Revenue has held at or slightly above $1 million for several years, which shows a stable, trusted practice
  • A simple will probate runs about $4,500 per file, a meaningful fee on its own, in a practice where you told us real estate fees per file are much smaller
The Gap
  • Reaching the 25% goal through real estate alone would require a very large increase in volume, which the market is not offering right now
  • Without a target per practice area, there is no way to tell which new files move profit the most
  • Not knowing what a new estate planning client costs to acquire makes every marketing decision a guess instead of a calculation
Becoming a leader in the industry should add profit as well as workload, and that starts with knowing which files move the numbers.
