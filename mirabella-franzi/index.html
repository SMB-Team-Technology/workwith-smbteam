Here Is Where Mirabella & Franzi Stands Today

Mirabella & Franzi is a Garden City, NY firm led by partners Rosanna Franzi and David Mirabella, with one associate attorney and six support staff. Real estate drives the firm's volume, while estate planning, probate and personal injury make up a smaller share of the work. The firm has built roughly $1 million a year entirely on word-of-mouth referral and has never advertised, so the growth Rosanna wants in estate planning has no system behind it yet.

Stage 6 is the stage that lets you become a leader in your industry on your own timeline — with growth that is predictable and sustainable instead of left to chance — and it is exactly where we are taking you.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • Every new client arrives by word of mouth — the firm has never advertised or run ongoing SEO
  • Estate planning and probate come only as a by-product of real estate work, and the firm does not solicit that business
  • Revenue has held at about $1 million for several years, with no stated revenue or profit targets
  • The website is under a year old but gives prospects one short estate planning page and no form above the fold

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a referral-dependent firm and an industry leader is not as wide as it feels — it starts with one practice area, estate planning, and a system that brings in new files at a pace you control.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Website Audit

"We're not super tech savvy, which is why we're kind of in the place that we're currently in."
The Problem
  • The estate planning page is a single short page of about four bullets — no FAQs, no process explanation, no fee guidance and no content specific to Garden City or Nassau County
  • Lighthouse performance scores: Mobile 65/100 — Desktop 89/100. The mobile experience is noticeably slower than desktop, and most local searches happen on phones
  • The only call to action above the fold is a phone number; the contact form lives on a separate Contact page and does not appear on the homepage, estate planning page or personal injury page
  • There is no blog or article content and no video, and no attorney or legal-service structured data was observed, which limits what search engines and AI answer tools can pull from the site
Blind Spot: You told us you want to explore advertising on AI search. AI answer tools quote sites that answer real questions in depth — such as how probate works in Nassau County or when a trust makes sense — and a four-bullet estate planning page gives them very little to cite.
The Positive
  • The site was rebuilt within the last year, so the firm is not starting from an outdated design
  • It is mobile responsive, and both attorneys have their own bio pages that build credibility
The Gap
  • A prospect searching for a wills, trusts or probate lawyer lands on a page with almost nothing to answer their questions, so they leave for a firm that does
  • Visitors who are ready to reach out have to find the Contact page first, and every extra step loses inquiries
  • With no articles or FAQs, the site gives Google and AI tools no reason to recommend the firm for estate planning
A website that earns trust and answers questions is the foundation of becoming the recognized estate planning leader in Garden City.

Intake Assessment

Mirabella & Franzi's intake has grown up around referred clients who already trust the firm, which is very different from answering strangers who found you through a search or an ad. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • Estate planning and probate inquiries have only ever come from existing relationships, so the firm has never had to respond quickly to cold inquiries from people comparing several lawyers
  • The homepage offers a phone number but no form or button above the fold, so anyone who prefers not to call has no immediate way to reach out
  • The contact form sits on its own Contact page and asks for a free-form subject and message, which is more effort than a short request-a-consultation form
  • No form appears on the estate planning page or the personal injury page, the two places a prospect decides whether to reach out
Blind Spot: When growth comes from advertising, the people who inquire are comparing firms and often contact several at once. The first firm to respond usually wins the file, which is a speed standard a referral-based intake has never needed to meet.
The Positive
  • Staff can do the initial inputting on probate files with attorney review, which gives the firm a ready-made process to scale
  • You told us the team could very easily take on about four more files a month, so capacity is not the constraint
The Gap
  • Every inquiry that arrives with no easy way to submit a request, or sits unanswered, is a file that goes to the next firm on the list
  • Without a defined response process for new estate planning inquiries, results from any new marketing will depend on who happens to answer the phone
  • The firm cannot tell which marketing source produced which inquiry until tracking is built into the intake path
Becoming a leader in the industry means every new family that reaches out is answered quickly, professionally and consistently.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • The only contact form is on the separate Contact page, and it asks for name, phone, email, subject and message
  • The header offers click-to-call, but there is no form, button or consultation request on the homepage, estate planning page or personal injury page

Profit Plan Assessment

Mirabella & Franzi has held revenue at about $1 million for several years and has set its sights on at least 25% more, roughly another $250,000. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"The PI practice is a smaller portion of our practice, although profitability-wise tends to be pretty large, whereas by volume, we do a significant amount of real estate, but the legal fees on those transactions are much smaller."
The Problem
  • The firm's largest source of volume, real estate, carries smaller fees per file, so reaching a higher profit takes a lot more files
  • The COVID-era refinance volume that once added to the practice no longer exists at current interest rates
  • You have not set specific revenue or profit targets, so the 25% goal has no number attached to each practice area or each month
  • You told us any investment depends on a return, but with no marketing history there is no baseline cost per client to measure a return against
Blind Spot: If nothing changes, you said profitability will probably stay about the same. That means the real cost of waiting is not a lost opportunity someday — it is another year at the same $1 million.
The Positive
  • Revenue has held at or slightly above $1 million for several years, which shows a stable, trusted practice
  • A simple will probate runs about $4,500 per file, a meaningful fee on its own, in a practice where you told us real estate fees per file are much smaller
The Gap
  • Reaching the 25% goal through real estate alone would require a very large increase in volume, which the market is not offering right now
  • Without a target per practice area, there is no way to tell which new files move profit the most
  • Not knowing what a new estate planning client costs to acquire makes every marketing decision a guess instead of a calculation
Becoming a leader in the industry should add profit as well as workload, and that starts with knowing which files move the numbers.
