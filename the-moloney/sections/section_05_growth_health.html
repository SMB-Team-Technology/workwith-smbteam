Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Referrals and organic Google deliver most new business, and Bradley says lead flow is not a pressing problem today
  • Paid advertising is limited to a small Microsoft campaign Bradley runs himself; Google, Local Service, and Meta ads were not observed in this review
  • An SEO vendor agreement runs through roughly April 2027, and Bradley does not plan to renew it
A steady, referral-led pipeline gives Bradley room to build his systems first — which is the order he wants.

Intake

  • Bradley personally screens every new lead by phone to decide whether to take the case
  • Fee agreements and first retainer payments run through Lawmatics, a platform he finds large for a solo practice
  • The back-and-forth with new leads is manual and time-consuming
Every intake conversation Bradley handles himself is time not spent building a firm that runs without staff.

Self-Managing Team

  • Solo by design: Bradley is the only person doing intake, court appearances, and filings
  • Phone answering, bookkeeping, accounting, and SEO are outsourced, but he coordinates all of it
  • Daily admin such as downloading and organizing discovery is still done by hand
A firm with no staff depends on automation doing the work an employee would — and that layer is still being built.

Profit

  • Revenue is roughly $250,000 with a goal near $350,000 next year; profit and margin figures were not discussed
  • Software is the largest expense, spread across overlapping tools
  • A bookkeeper and accountant are in place, giving the firm a base for clear financial reporting
A firm that holds value on its own needs numbers a buyer can trust — and tool costs are the first margin lever.
