Lead Generation

The Moloney Law Firm has grown in under two years on referrals and organic Google, and Bradley reports that lead flow is steady enough that he is not feeling pressure to add volume yet. That makes this section less about fixing a problem and more about protecting and extending the visibility the firm already has.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • No embedded video was found on the homepage, so visitors cannot see or hear Bradley before they call
  • The homepage has no contact form of its own — the “Get A Free Case Evaluation Now!” button sends visitors to a separate contact page
The Positive
  • A modern WordPress site built in early 2025, with a clear case-evaluation button and a phone number in the header
  • A strong attorney bio — 16+ years of experience, 12+ years in prosecution, 100+ trials, and an adjunct role at DU Sturm College of Law — plus an active blog with 107 posts
The Gap
  • Trial and prosecution experience is the strongest trust signal in criminal defense, but today it reaches only visitors who read the bio
  • Visitors who are not ready to call must take an extra step to reach a form
A website that earns trust and captures inquiries on its own is one more part of the firm that works without you.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The firm appears to have a smaller review base than several of the best-known Denver criminal defense firms, which are listed in third-party roundups with large review histories
  • Map-pack placement for Denver criminal defense, DUI, and domestic violence searches is the first thing to confirm live
  • Listings exist on Birdeye, Yahoo Local, Yelp, and Facebook, but a targeted search did not surface profiles on Avvo, Justia, or FindLaw
Blind Spot: Your SEO vendor agreement ends around April 2027 and you plan to take SEO over yourself. The rankings, content, and reporting built so far should be documented before then so nothing is lost in the handoff.
The Positive
  • Name, address, and phone match across the listings reviewed, the firm is already collecting Google reviews, and fresh content is published steadily — the latest posts are from late September 2026
The Gap
  • Review volume and recency drive map-pack placement, so firms with longer review histories get the first look from searchers
  • Missing legal-directory profiles mean fewer trusted references pointing back to the firm
Colorado Legal Defense Group — frequently listed among Denver’s leading criminal defense firms in third-party roundups, with a review base that appears far larger than most firms in this market. Its live local-pack position is worth verifying.
Local visibility that keeps working in the background is the kind of system that runs without you — and the kind that adds to the value of the firm.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • The firm does not appear to be running Google search ads for Denver criminal defense, DUI, or domestic violence terms — it may not be set up to show for these searches, or may be getting outbid. This was not confirmed directly.
  • You mentioned running limited ads on Microsoft; budget and results were not discussed on the call.
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Colorado Legal Defense Group, Robinson & Henry, and Law Office of Jacob E. Martinez — the firms most often listed in Denver defense roundups. Their live ad messaging has not been captured and is worth reviewing.
The Opportunity
  • High-intent searches that match your core practice pages: “Denver criminal defense lawyer,” “Denver DUI lawyer,” and “Denver domestic violence attorney”
  • Case-type searches that match pages already on your site: assault, drug possession, sexual assault, white collar, and pre-charge investigation
  • Boulder and Denver-metro variations — your site already has pages written for those searches
  • Urgency searches such as “arrested in Denver,” “bail,” and “charged with,” where the person searching needs a lawyer that day

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence

Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Competitor LSA listings were not captured in this review — which Denver firms hold the criminal and DUI defense LSA spots, and with what review history, is worth checking live.
The Opportunity
  • Appearing in LSA would place the firm at the very top of Google for Denver criminal defense and DUI searches.
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Lead-based pricing ties cost to real calls rather than clicks, which suits a firm where the owner personally screens every lead.
