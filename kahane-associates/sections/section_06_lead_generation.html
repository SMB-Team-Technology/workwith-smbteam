Lead Generation

Kahane & Associates serves banks, lenders and servicers, and on the call the IT team described new business as largely a matter of networking and relationships, which means consumer-style search advertising is not the main way this firm wins work. That makes the firm's website and online listings a credibility check for institutional decision-makers rather than a lead funnel, and that is where the observable gaps are.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The homepage has no explicit call to action above the fold, so a visitor is not told what to do next
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 36/100 — Desktop 94/100 (lab test run October 20, 2026 with Lighthouse, the same engine PageSpeed uses)
  • The contact form lives only on a separate Contact page, not on the homepage
  • The most recent Industry News post we found is dated April 7, 2023, so the news feed reads as inactive
Blind Spot: You may not realize that the people who decide which firms to trust with a portfolio often check the firm's website before they ever call. A slow mobile experience and a news feed that stopped in 2023 can quietly signal a firm that is less current than it really is.
The Positive
  • The desktop score of 94/100 shows the site is fundamentally sound and loads quickly on a computer
  • The homepage positions the firm clearly as "The Pinnacle Default Servicing Law Firm" and lists foreclosure, bankruptcy, litigation, eviction, title and REO, and collections
The Gap
  • A mobile score of 36/100 means anyone checking the firm from a phone gets a noticeably slower experience
  • With no clear next step on the homepage, interested visitors have to hunt for a way to reach you
  • An inactive news feed misses a free chance to show lenders that the firm follows regulatory changes
A website that builds credibility on its own means growth depends less on any one person's relationships and less on adding people to carry them.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The firm's website and Contact page list the Florida corporate office at 1619 NW 136th Avenue, Suite D-220, Sunrise, while Avvo, Justia, FindLaw, Yelp, Yellow Pages and the BBB list 8201 Peters Road in Plantation, with differing suite numbers
  • The site is organized by service rather than by location, and we observed no pages aimed at Florida, Georgia or Tennessee markets even though the firm serves all three
  • We could not render live Google map results in this audit, so we have not made any claims about the firm's local pack position or review profile
Blind Spot: Conflicting addresses can be left over from a previous office, and they quietly undermine trust for anyone verifying the firm, from a new client's vendor-management team to a search engine deciding which listing to believe.
The Positive
  • The firm's phone number, (954) 382-3486, is consistent across every directory we reviewed, and the firm is listed on a wide range of legal directories
The Gap
  • Inconsistent address data splits the firm's online identity across two locations
  • Consumer-style local map rankings are not the main route to institutional clients, so the practical value here is accurate, consistent information for anyone vetting the firm
Cleaning up how the firm appears online is a small, low-effort fix that removes friction from every vetting conversation.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • We could not render live Google ad results in this audit, so we make no claim about whether the firm is running search ads for foreclosure, bankruptcy, litigation, eviction, title and REO, or collections terms
  • The IT team did not discuss advertising on the call and said they do not follow the firm's marketing
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Storey Law Group, Lender Legal PLLC and Gilbert Garcia Group — creditor-side firms serving the same lender and servicer audience in Florida; we did not observe their paid search activity
The Opportunity
  • If leadership ever wants growth beyond existing relationships, searches by institutional audiences for creditor-side foreclosure, bankruptcy, eviction and collections counsel in Florida, Georgia and Tennessee are a place to test
  • Title and REO closing services are a distinct audience from default servicing and could be tested separately
  • Searches that include the names of major servicers and lenders are a non-obvious opportunity to reach the people who assign work
  • For this engagement, paid search is a lower priority than the operational work that follows
Blind Spot: Many of the most visible foreclosure-related search results belong to homeowner-side firms representing the opposite side of your clients. Buying clicks on those broad terms would mostly attract the wrong audience.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • We could not render live Google Local Service Ads in this audit, so we have not observed whether the firm appears or is Google Screened
  • Local Service Ads are built for consumers searching for a lawyer, which is a limited fit for a firm whose clients are financial institutions
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Creditor-side peers — no competitor Local Service Ads were observed in this audit
The Opportunity
  • For any consumer-facing practice the firm might add in the future, Local Service Ads are worth evaluating
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • For the firm's current institutional client base, this channel is a low priority

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • The Meta Ad Library could not be reached in this audit, so we have not observed whether the firm is running Facebook or Instagram ads
  • Consumer social advertising is not how the firm described winning business on the call
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Creditor-side peers — no competitor Meta ad activity was observed in this audit
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who looked at the firm and did not reach out can be re-engaged instead of leaving with no follow-up
  • Video featuring the firm's attorneys — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content — regulatory and servicing updates capture decision-makers earlier in their process
  • For an institutional audience, professional-network channels are likely a better fit than consumer social feeds
