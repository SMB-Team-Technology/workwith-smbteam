Intake Quick Win
Why It Matters
A dedicated person routes every court notice by hand, and that role has to grow as volume grows, so more assignments mean more headcount.
The Opportunity
Route notices from the Exchange mailbox into the case management system and out to the assigned attorney automatically, with pending-status reporting, so the same team can handle a larger volume.
Automate the Fees-and-Costs Report
Profit Quick Win
Why It Matters
One person spends almost full-time on this report, and covering a day off is nearly impossible, which puts a time-sensitive billing input at risk.
The Opportunity

Turn Unstructured Reports into Importable Data
Intake Quick Win
Why It Matters
Reports that arrive as Word documents cannot be fed straight into the firm's workflow, so useful data such as addresses sits trapped in text.
The Opportunity
Extract structured, importable data from those documents so they flow into the existing case workflow without retyping, while keeping personally identifiable information out of AI's reach.

Make Directory Addresses Consistent
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
The firm's website lists the Sunrise office, while Avvo, Justia, FindLaw, Yelp, Yellow Pages and the BBB list a Plantation address, which splits the firm's online identity.

Update each directory to the current corporate address so anyone verifying the firm sees one consistent location and phone number.

Automate Final-Billing Reconciliation
Profit Long-Term Play
