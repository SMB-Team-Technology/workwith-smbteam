Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Work comes largely through networking and relationships with institutional clients, which fits a B2B default servicing firm
  • The website scores 36/100 on mobile speed and offers no clear call to action above the fold
  • Directory listings show a different office address than the firm's own website
A stronger online presence lets the firm's reputation do some of the vetting for you, so growth depends less on any one person's relationships.

Intake

  • Court notices are routed by a dedicated person from a shared mailbox into the case management system
  • That role has to grow as notice volume grows
  • Reports that arrive as unstructured documents cannot feed the firm's workflows directly
Automating the front door of the firm's workflow is how it takes on more assignments without taking on more headcount.

Self-Managing Team

  • Fees-and-costs reporting is an almost full-time job for one person with no practical backup
  • The push for AI has fallen on the IT department without a roadmap
  • Management expectations for AI still need to be aligned with what is realistic
A team that does not depend on any single person to keep the firm moving is a team that can grow without constant oversight.

Profit

  • The firm advances costs on behalf of its clients, so timely and accurate fees-and-costs reporting matters
  • Final-billing reconciliation across unbilled tasks, time entries and hearings is a report the team wants automated
  • Profit planning and margin targets were not part of the call
Faster, cleaner billing data is what turns higher volume into higher profit rather than just more work.
