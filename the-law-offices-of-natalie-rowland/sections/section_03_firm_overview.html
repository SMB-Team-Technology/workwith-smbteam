Here Is Where The Law Offices of Natalie Rowland Stands Today

Natalie Rowland is an 18-year family law attorney who recently restarted her solo practice after a firm partnership ended, and is now repositioning entirely around high-asset family law under the incoming trade name “Rowland Family Law.” She has built her entire caseload — 15 to 20 active matters generating $22,000–$30,000 a month — on referrals alone, with a virtual assistant hired just three weeks ago as her only support. The single most important finding: her Google Business Profile carries zero reviews and her website is currently a single-page stub mid-rebuild, while three named local competitors already own the exact “high-asset divorce Atlanta” search terms she is trying to claim.

Stage 6 is the version of this firm where the systems run themselves and Natalie gets to choose exactly which high-asset clients and cases she takes — without ever becoming the “cookie-cutter” firm she has explicitly said she does not want to build.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • Every client relationship today comes through referrals — there is no other lead source in place
  • Natalie personally handles intake, casework, and client communication, with only a 3-week-old virtual assistant for support
  • Her Google Business Profile has zero reviews and her website is a single-page stub mid-rebuild, with no visibility in any of the searches her ideal high-asset clients are running
  • Revenue today is unpredictable month to month, and the $1M/yr goal requires a meaningfully higher average case value than her current caseload

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
Closing that gap starts with building the systems and online credibility to support the high-asset positioning Natalie is stepping into — not by taking on more casework, but by making the practice she already has run without demanding more of her time.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
The Law Offices of Natalie Rowland
Natalie is operating at Stage 3 — a Solo Practitioner whose 18 years of experience and reputation have never needed to be backed by a system, because referrals were always enough. The single most urgent gap is that her firm has no online presence to match the high-asset rebrand she is pursuing: zero Google reviews, no ranking website, and three named competitors already dominating the exact keywords she needs to own. Until that foundation exists, launching paid marketing would mean sending high-intent prospects to a page that cannot compete — which is exactly why the plan ahead starts with systems and credibility, not ad spend.
