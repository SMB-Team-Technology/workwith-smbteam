Here Is Where Kommer Bave & Ciccone LLP Stands Today

Kommer Bave & Ciccone LLP is a general-practice firm that has served White Plains and Rockland County since 1979, and managing partner Rachel Ciccone is focused on growing its family-law work — divorce, custody, and post-judgment enforcement — while cautiously adding personal injury. The firm sits at Stage 3: new clients arrive mostly through word of mouth and rabbinical-court referrals, while its website and Google presence do not reflect a 40-plus-year reputation.

Stage 6 is where your marketing works while you are in court, and where your online presence finally matches the reputation you have spent decades earning — and it is exactly where we are taking you.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • Word of mouth and rabbinical-court referrals are the main source of new family-law clients
  • Rachel is in court every day and handles consultations and briefs herself, leaving little time to build marketing
  • Google Ads and the website are handled by one vendor at about $2,900 a month, but the ads lean toward personal injury and wills and trusts rather than divorce, custody, and post-judgment enforcement
  • A personal injury hire and a paralegal are needed now, while the firm is still getting its house in order

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a respected firm and one that does not need Rachel in every room is not as wide as it feels — it takes one centralized system that brings in the right family-law cases, and a team set up to handle them.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Website Audit

"I feel like I'm losing a lot of the market share or clients because of my less than stellar web page."
The Problem
  • The homepage headline, "Legal Insight. Trusted Advice.", names no practice area, city, or differentiator — a divorce client searching for help sees nothing about family law
  • PageSpeed scores (Lighthouse testing, the engine behind PageSpeed Insights): Mobile 44–67/100 across two runs — Desktop 71/100. Mobile load time (LCP) measured roughly 10 to 18 seconds
  • Family law is one of seven practice areas on a general-practice site, with no per-city or per-office pages for White Plains or New City, and post-judgment enforcement is not listed by name
  • Team photos and the attorney list are out of date, and the blog was last updated in March 2026 with elder-law news rather than family-law content
Blind Spot: The roughly 83 pages already on your site carry search value you have built over the years. A rebuild should reorganize that content by practice area and location rather than start from scratch.
The Positive
  • The site is mobile responsive and lists bios for all four attorneys
  • "Serving Clients Since 1979" and a clear call-to-action phone number sit above the fold, and the Family Law page already includes a free consultation form
The Gap
  • Visitors arriving from a divorce search see a generic general-practice homepage and no clear family-law-first path to a consultation
  • Slow mobile load times send away people searching from their phones, often at a stressful moment
  • Without location pages for White Plains and New City, Google has little reason to show the firm for Rockland searches
A website that matches the firm's real-world reputation is the first step toward marketing that works without taking more of Rachel's time.

Intake Assessment

Rachel takes consultations herself between court appearances, and her close rate is strong once a prospect reaches her — the opportunity is getting more prospects that far and following up with those who do not sign right away. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
"I just need them to get one… Once get through the door, I'm good."
The Problem
  • You personally take consultations between court appearances, so intake competes with courtroom time
  • The homepage offers a phone number only — the consultation form sits on the Family Law page
  • The Family Law form notes that "a consultation fee may apply," which may give prospects comparing firms a reason to call someone else first
  • No structured follow-up, review request, or nurture process was described for prospects who do not sign right away
Blind Spot: Divorce prospects often reach out to several firms at once, so a slow or missed reply can hand the consultation to a competitor before you ever know it happened.
The Positive
  • You close at a high rate once a prospect gets through the door
  • Referral relationships, including rabbinical courts, send clients who already arrive trusting the firm
The Gap
  • Every consultation you take personally is time not spent in court or on briefs
  • Prospects who do not sign right away are not being nurtured, so leads the firm has already earned can slip away
  • Without a clear online path to book, some prospects who would have called never do
A consistent intake path means more of the clients you earn the chance to serve — without taking more of your time.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • The homepage call to action is a phone number, (914) 633-7400; there is no contact form on the homepage
  • The Family Law page includes a "Free Consultation" form (name, email, phone, situation, contact preference), with a note that a consultation fee may apply when meeting with the attorney
  • No live chat was detected on the site, so there is no way to capture a visitor outside of a phone call or the Family Law page form
