Here Is Where Oakwood Legal Group Stands Today

Oakwood Legal Group is a Los Angeles personal injury firm built on a well-established reputation — auto, truck, motorcycle, bicycle, pedestrian, and wrongful death cases — that recently launched a new Employment Law department already gaining traction. But the firm's digital footprint has not kept pace with its legal reputation: it does not surface in Google's local pack for any of the personal injury or employment law searches tested, its Google Business Profile carries compliance gaps that were only partially addressed live on this call, and it has never run a paid lead generation campaign. The single most important finding is this — Oakwood is invisible in the exact searches where its competitors are winning the click.

Stage 6 is where Oakwood stops chasing referrals and starts dominating the category — the exact ambition Mike described when he talked about wanting to own the local market for personal injury and employment law alike.

  • The firm does not appear in Google's local 3-pack for any personal injury or employment law search tested in this market
  • Google Business Profile carries compliance gaps — a keyword-stuffed business name with no registered DBA, and until this call, no Employment Attorney category at all

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.
