   $180,000 from HubSpot. This is incorrect — the research notes STEP 9 explicitly state
   revenue: "~$150k-$200k/year" current pace, with monthly revenue "<$15K" (trigger record).
   Corrected effective revenue used throughout this audit: $180,000/year (~$15,000/month) —
   consistent with the stated range, and noted here as a correction (stated, not inferred).

2. 35% SPEND CAP VIOLATION (hard rule, not optional): The general eligibility rule caps
   total SMB spend (management fees + ad spend) at 35% of monthly revenue. At ~$15,000/month
   revenue, that cap is ~$5,250/month. package_decision.json recommended Full Service
   Marketing Starter ($4,997/mo bundled) + Elite Coach ($2,600/mo bundled) = $7,597/mo in
   management fees ALONE — before any ad spend — which already blows past the $5,250 cap by
   over $2,300/month. Even Starter alone ($4,997/mo = ~33% of revenue) leaves no room for the
   mandatory $3,000/mo minimum ad spend floor. Essentials (the cheaper marketing tier,
   $3,497/mo) is hidden anyway because the firm has multiple detected practice areas (family
   immigration + business immigration), and Essentials requires a single practice area.
   CONCLUSION: no marketing package tier fits this firm's revenue within the 35% cap.

3. BUDGET-REALITY OVERRIDE (transcript-stated need override #3) independently confirms this:
   Research notes STEP 9 state "Rahim is described as 'open to SMB Team's $2,500/mo minimum
   package (12-month commitment)'" — this matches Elite Coach's $2,600/mo bundled price
   almost exactly, and is far below the $7,597/mo the revenue table implied.

4. LAW (Legal AI Workforce) EXCLUDED despite real transcript interest in "AI Workforce Pro"
   (explicitly discussed on the call per STEP 9) — LAW eligibility requires revenue $500K+
   AND at least 1-2 support staff; this firm is under $500K revenue AND solo (Rahim is the
   only attorney, no support staff beyond an outsourced 24/7 answering service). LAW is not
   recommended this round. INTERNAL FLAG: sales should revisit LAW / AI Workforce Pro once
