  • PageSpeed scores could not be pulled this session (rate-limited); no CDN or caching signals were detected in the page's overall weight, consistent with a site not yet optimized for speed

  • No dedicated attorney bio page linked from primary navigation, despite Rahim's 25+ years of experience being a genuine credibility asset
Blind Spot: You mentioned worrying that AI search (AI Overviews and answer engines) is making traditional websites obsolete. That concern is legitimate — but the answer isn't to give up on the website, it's to build content and structure that AI answer engines can actually cite. A site with no SEO foundation today is also invisible to AI-driven search tomorrow; fixing one problem addresses both.
The Positive

  • Two embedded video testimonials are already in place — a trust-building asset most solo firms don't have
The Gap
  • Every visitor who reaches the site but can't find the contact form quickly is a lead lost to a competitor with a clearer path to conversion
  • A blog that has gone quiet since April 2026 signals lower ongoing SEO investment than competitors actively publishing content
  • Without SEO foundations, the site cannot support paid campaigns or organic growth once lead generation does launch
A website that converts on its own is one less thing that requires Rahim's personal attention — a small but real piece of the systematized firm he's building toward.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Google Business Profile shows a 4.7-star rating from 46 reviews — solid, but well behind the review volume of every named local competitor
  • Firm does not surface as a top organic result for "best immigration lawyer atlanta," "green card attorney atlanta," or "H-1B visa lawyer atlanta" — competitors dominate all three searches
  • NAP inconsistency found: Avvo lists the firm's ZIP code as 30329, while the firm's own website and other directories list 30309, for the same Peachtree Street suite — actively hurting local search rankings
  • No geo-targeted landing pages exist for surrounding metro cities like Marietta, Decatur, or Sandy Springs, where immigration clients are also searching
Blind Spot: A NAP (name/address/phone) mismatch like the ZIP code discrepancy between Avvo and your own site is one of the most common — and most fixable — things quietly suppressing local rankings. It costs nothing to fix and can be corrected within days.
The Positive
  • A 4.7-star rating from 46 reviews is a genuine trust asset — the foundation is good, it just needs volume behind it
The Gap
  • Every immigration searcher who never sees Dhanani Law in the local pack goes straight to a competitor instead
  • Review volume gap alone is likely costing organic visibility in a market where review count is a known ranking signal
Quijano Law — 132 reviews at 4.9 stars, nearly 3x Dhanani Law's review count, giving them stronger local pack visibility across the same green card and business immigration searches.
Fixing the NAP error and building review volume is foundational, low-cost work — exactly the kind of "no ad spend needed yet" progress that builds toward the bigger systematized firm Rahim wants.
