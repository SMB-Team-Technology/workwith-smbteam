Here Is Where Law Office of Walter Reaves, PC Stands Today

Walter Reaves has practiced criminal defense law in Waco for over 45 years, board-certified in both Criminal Law and Criminal Appellate Law, with a reputation (Avvo Superb rating, Martindale-Hubbell recognition) that most attorneys in the region cannot match. A failed merger cut the firm's annual revenue roughly in half — from about $600,000 to about $250,000 — and left Walter and one part-time legal assistant trying to personally field every incoming call, with Local Service Ads paused and no structured follow-up process in place.

Stage 6 is where the practice runs on structure, not on you personally answering every call — and it starts with rebuilding the predictable revenue this merger took away.

Where You Are Today

STAGE 3: SOLO PRACTITIONER
  • Revenue dropped from about $600,000 to about $250,000 a year following a failed merger
  • Walter personally handles nearly every incoming call, alongside one part-time legal assistant
  • Local Service Ads were paused post-merger — the firm has no active paid lead generation channel right now
  • The blog and content marketing have gone stale since mid-2024, even as competitors run active, SEO-built campaigns for the same search terms

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between where the practice is today and where it needs to be is not about talent or reputation — Walter has both in abundance — it is about rebuilding the structure a failed merger stripped away, one deliberate step at a time.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.
