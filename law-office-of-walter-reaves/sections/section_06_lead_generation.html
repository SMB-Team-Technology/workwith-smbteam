Lead Generation

The Law Office of Walter Reaves, PC has spent 45 years building organic credibility in Waco — but a failed merger paused the firm's Local Service Ads and left it with no active paid lead generation channel of any kind. Every month that gap stays open, established, marketing-forward competitors like Dunham & Jones and Dunnam & Dunnam capture searchers this firm's reputation should be winning.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The site is mature and content-rich, but has not been meaningfully modernized in years — blog posts date back through 2022–2024 with no visible redesign in that window
  • PageSpeed mobile and desktop scores could not be obtained live this session (PageSpeed API returned HTTP 429 on every attempt) — a manual live check is recommended before quoting exact numbers to Walter
  • The blog's most recent post is dated June 2024 — over two years stale — even as the firm maintains an active YouTube presence
Blind Spot: Because the blog has been static since 2024, every month it stays that way is a month Dunnam & Dunnam adds another dedicated county-level landing page to a search field this firm used to be able to compete in on content alone.
The Positive
  • Attorney bio is extensive and credibility-rich — 35+ years of practice, Board Certification in Criminal Law and Criminal Appellate Law, Academy of Forensic Sciences membership
  • A strong above-the-fold CTA (free Criminal Case Map) paired with a lead form, repeated twice more down the page — the contact opportunity is not buried
The Gap
  • A stale blog and unverified mobile performance are turning away exactly the visitors the site's strong bio and CTA are meant to convert
  • Video content (attorney-featured YouTube videos) and the existing Spanish FAQ library are built assets sitting mostly idle as lead-gen tools
  • Every visitor who leaves without converting today is a visitor the firm cannot afford to lose while rebuilding post-merger revenue
A stronger, more current website compounds every other fix in this report — but it will not, by itself, replace the revenue this merger cost you.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • GBP star rating and review count could not be verified live this session — Avvo shows 4.8/5.0 across 5 reviews and a 10.0 "Superb" rating, but these are not Google figures and should be confirmed manually before the proposal call
  • The firm appears in organic results for "Waco criminal defense attorney" and "best criminal defense lawyer Waco Texas" but ranks alongside, not above, Dunnam & Dunnam, Dunham & Jones, and Greening Law Group
  • The firm's strongest organic showing is its dedicated drug crimes page, which ranks at or near the top for "Waco drug crime lawyer" — proof the firm can win rankings when it builds dedicated content, which it has not done for DWI or general criminal defense
Blind Spot: The site displays a "Top DUI Lawyer in Temple" award badge and runs a second, separately branded phone line — both signs the firm already draws clients from outside Waco — but there is no dedicated Temple, Killeen, or Bell County landing page built to capture that existing demand.
The Positive
  • Directory NAP consistency is clean across Avvo, FindLaw, Lawyers.com, and BBB (A+ rating, in business since 1980) — a credible footprint to build new SEO investment on top of
The Gap
  • Every month without a local pack presence, nearby searchers with no prior relationship to Walter default to competitors with more active local SEO
  • The Temple/Killeen/Bell County demand the firm already earns through reputation and its Temple DUI award goes uncaptured by any dedicated page
Dunnam & Dunnam — maintains dedicated McLennan County landing pages for both DWI and drug possession, built exactly where this firm's SEO investment has been thinnest.
Local SEO is the lowest-cost path back to the referral-level trust this firm built over 45 years — it just needs to be pointed at every practice area, not just one.

Blind Spot: Pausing Local Service Ads did not just pause LSAs — it left the entire paid search landscape open for Dunham & Jones and Dunnam & Dunnam to capture without needing to outbid this firm at all.
