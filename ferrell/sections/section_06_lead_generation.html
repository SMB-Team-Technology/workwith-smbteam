Lead Generation

Ferrell Law Firm has built its entire client base on referrals — church connections and word-of-mouth — with no paid advertising, no SEO strategy, and no Google Business Profile ever put in place. That referral network produces what Andrew describes as "feast or famine" lead flow, with no predictable system behind it.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • Site is a brand-new, still-in-progress $2.5k Wix build with unfinished placeholder ("Lorem ipsum") copy in the "Our Philosophy" section and generic placeholder testimonials
  • PageSpeed scores could not be captured live this session (daily API quota exhausted) — a live re-check is recommended before or during the proposal call, though an unoptimized mobile score would be typical for a site still mid-build
  • No dedicated call-to-action button above the fold — top navigation offers only plain links to Practice Areas, Attorneys, and Contact
  • Contact form is buried at the bottom of the homepage under a "Contact Us" section — not visible without scrolling past the full page
The Positive
  • Site is mobile responsive with Wix's adaptive layout already in place
  • Attorney bio is present with headshot, contact information, and a personal story foundation to build on
The Gap
  • Placeholder copy and no clear CTA above the fold means visitors who do arrive are likely to leave without contacting the firm
  • No blog or published "News & Resources" content means no SEO foundation is being built as the site launches
  • Every day the site remains unfinished with no SEO plan is a day it cannot support paid traffic without wasting ad spend
A website built to convert — not just to exist — is what turns the traffic you eventually pay for into the case volume that lets you step back and hire the help you need.

  • Firm does not appear in the local 3-pack for "personal injury lawyer Kansas City MO," "car accident lawyer Kansas City MO near me," or "personal injury attorney free consultation Kansas City Missouri"

The Gap
  • Every local search for personal injury help in Kansas City currently routes around Ferrell Law Firm entirely
  • Zero reviews means zero social proof for prospects comparing options before they call
Dickerson Oxton, LLC — established since 2010 with a dedicated SEO-built site and directory presence across FindLaw and Yelp, actively targeting "Kansas City car accident lawyer" and related terms Ferrell Law Firm does not rank for.
Closing the local SEO gap is what lets the firm's reputation finally show up where prospects are already searching — without you having to personally chase every referral.
