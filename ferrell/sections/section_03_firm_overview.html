Here Is Where Ferrell Law Firm Stands Today

Andrew Ferrell runs a solo personal injury practice in Kansas City, MO, handling the claim side of PI cases exclusively while co-counseling all litigation with another firm. Every case that comes through the door today arrives through referrals — church connections and word-of-mouth — with no paid marketing, no online reviews, and no Google Business Profile to be found. He is currently capped at 7 cases while working only 10-15 hours a week, because the administrative and intake work he handles personally leaves little room for anything else.

Stage 6 is the stage where the firm no longer depends on your personal hours to function — and it is exactly what makes the time for family, investments, and ministry you are working toward actually possible.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner

  • Andrew personally handles every case, every intake conversation, and all administrative work — no other staff exists
  • No paid advertising, no SEO, and no Google Business Profile have ever been put in place

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a solo practice capped at 7 cases and a firm that runs without consuming every hour of your week is a matter of building the systems you have not needed until now — and closing it is what turns your stated goal of more time for family, investments, and ministry into reality.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Ferrell Law Firm
Andrew is operating at Stage 3 — a Solo Practitioner whose referral network has kept the practice alive but has produced revenue that has swung from $100K to $700K year to year with no predictability. The single biggest thing holding him back is the total absence of any lead generation system outside of referrals, in a Kansas City market where established PI firms like Foster Wallace and McShane & Brady carry hundreds of reviews and actively-marketed websites. Until that changes, the consistent $250K-$350K net profit Andrew is aiming for — and the time back for family, investments, and ministry that goal is meant to buy him — will stay out of reach.
