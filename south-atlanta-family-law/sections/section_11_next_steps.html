Why It Matters

The Opportunity

Write a Simple AI-Use Policy for Client Data
Team Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Connecting AI to the case management system has been on hold over concerns about client information, which keeps AI use limited to drafting by one person.
The Opportunity
