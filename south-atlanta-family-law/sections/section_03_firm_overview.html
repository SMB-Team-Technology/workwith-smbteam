Here Is Where South Atlanta Family Law Stands Today

South Atlanta Family Law is a Stockbridge, Georgia firm led by Janice Alfred, built mostly on divorce work with custody modifications and legitimations behind it. The firm already has a team of about nine, a broad website, and active Meta and Google advertising — but it has not reached its $1.5 million revenue goal, and more cases would currently mean building another attorney and paralegal team.

Stage 6 is the stage where the firm reaches its revenue goal without you having to build an entire second attorney and paralegal team — and it is exactly where we are taking you.

Where You Are Today

Stage 4: Small Business Manager
  • Leads come from several channels — in-house Meta ads, Google Ads run by your website provider, and a short ChatGPT ads test — but the $1.5 million goal has not been met
  • After-hours calls go to an answering service that takes messages but does not book consultations
  • Taking on more cases would mean hiring and setting up another attorney and paralegal team
  • Workflows in your case management system are still manual, and past attempts to automate them in-house have not stuck

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between where you are and where you want to be is not about working harder — it is about letting systems and AI handle the repeatable work so more cases do not require a whole new team.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
South Atlanta Family Law
South Atlanta Family Law is a Stage 4 Small Business Manager: it has real lead sources, an intake person, and a working team, but the owner is still close to the day-to-day. The most urgent limiting area is capacity — the firm needs more cases to hit its goal, yet more cases would force it to build another attorney and paralegal team rather than getting more from the people it has. Closing that gap with automation and AI-driven workflows is the most direct path to the $1.5 million goal.
