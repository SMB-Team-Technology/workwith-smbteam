Lead Generation

South Atlanta Family Law generates leads through in-house Meta ads, Google Ads run by its website provider, and a short ChatGPT ads test — yet the firm has not reached its revenue goal and needs more cases. This section shows where each channel stands and where live Google and Meta data still needs to be confirmed.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • Mobile speed is the site's weakest point — most visitors from social and search arrive on a phone
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 29/100 — Desktop 81/100 (local Lighthouse test)
  • Location pages cover McDonough, Henry County, and Atlanta, but no pages were found for other surrounding cities such as Locust Grove, Fayetteville, Griffin, Conyers, or Hampton
Blind Spot: Facebook and Instagram clicks happen overwhelmingly on phones, so a 29/100 mobile score hits the traffic the firm is paying for hardest.
The Positive
  • The site is mobile responsive, actively maintained, and carries a consultation request form above the fold, in the navigation, and in the footer
  • It has 17+ practice area pages, an attorney bio for the owner, video content, and a blog with posts through August 2026
The Gap
  • Slow mobile loads cost consultation requests from both paid and organic visitors before the page finishes loading
  • Searchers in nearby cities are not matched to a page built for their town, leaving those searches to competitors
  • A strong set of practice area pages cannot convert visitors who leave before they appear
A faster site means the cases you are already paying to attract actually arrive — without adding headcount.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Local map-pack position for divorce, custody, and legitimation searches in Stockbridge, McDonough, and Henry County was not observed in this review and has not yet been benchmarked
  • Google Business Profile details — categories, photos, posts, and review recency — were not observed in this review
  • The firm name appears two ways across Yelp, Facebook, and attorney directories ("South Atlanta Family Law" and "South Atlanta Family Law Firm")
The Positive
  • Address and phone number are consistent across every source reviewed
  • The firm is listed on Yelp, Facebook, the Henry County Chamber of Commerce, and major legal directories, and public summaries suggest a healthy base of Google reviews
The Gap
  • Without a measured baseline, the firm cannot tell whether competitors hold the map-pack spots for its highest-value searches
  • Review volume and freshness are among the strongest local ranking signals, and they only stay strong with a steady stream of new client reviews
Allen Bodiford and Michelle R. Clark — both Stockbridge divorce attorneys with established directory profiles for the firm's core search. Their map-pack standing should be benchmarked alongside the firm's.
Showing up first when a Stockbridge divorce client searches is the cheapest way to fill the calendar without adding to the team.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Divorce: per the call, the firm's website provider runs its Google Ads. Live ad copy, keywords, and activity were not observed in this review, and budgets and results were not shared.
  • Custody, legitimation, and modification: whether ads are running for these searches was not observed in this review, and it is worth confirming that they are covered alongside divorce.
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Allen Bodiford and Michelle R. Clark — Stockbridge divorce competitors; their ad messaging was not observed in this review.
  • Atlanta Family Law Group — publishes a dedicated Henry County family law page, so it is competing for the same county-level searches.
The Opportunity
  • Divorce: "divorce lawyer Stockbridge GA," "divorce attorney McDonough GA," and "Henry County divorce lawyer"
  • Custody and legitimation: "child custody lawyer Stockbridge GA," "legitimation attorney Henry County GA," and "custody modification attorney McDonough GA"
  • Neighboring cities such as Locust Grove, Fayetteville, Griffin, Conyers, and Hampton, paired with the firm's strongest case types
  • Urgency-driven searches such as protective orders, contempt, and fathers' custody rights, plus competitor firm names
Blind Spot: The ChatGPT ads test produced clicks but no tracked conversions. The same risk applies to any channel measured by clicks instead of booked consultations.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Whether the firm appears in Local Service Ads, and its Google Screened status, was not observed in this review.
  • Local Service Ads were not among the channels discussed on the call, which makes this an open opportunity gap to confirm.
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Competitor LSA listings and review counts were not observed in this review and should be checked for family law in Stockbridge and McDonough.
The Opportunity
  • Family law LSA eligibility for the Stockbridge and McDonough service area should be confirmed before launch.
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • LSAs reward responsiveness, so the after-hours booking gap would need to be closed for them to perform at their best.

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • Meta campaigns are run in-house — the call confirmed they are active, and Meta is the one channel managed directly by the firm.
  • Ad count, formats, and run time were not observed because the Meta Ad Library was not reviewed for this report.
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Competitor Meta activity — ad counts, formats, and run time — was not observed in this review.
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Because Meta is already managed in-house, tracking booked consultations — not just clicks — and feeding that back into optimization is the quickest lever
