Intake Assessment

For Blackheath, "intake" means how an interested advisor becomes a conversation and then a working relationship. Juniors handle smaller relationships, but the larger ones still route through Matt, and the website gives interested advisors no clear way to start. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
"people will accept to work with my juniors… They won't accept, you know, my referrals that come to me."
The Problem
  • Larger advisor relationships need you on the final call, so your calendar sets the pace for the most valuable conversations
  • The homepage and Contact page have no form or book-a-call option, only a "Learn More" button, a phone number and an email address
  • There is no formal referral system for advisors who send other advisors, even though referrals are your strongest source
  • No conversion or response-time baseline was discussed, so there is no way yet to measure how well new inquiries become conversations
Blind Spot: More advertising will bring more inquiries, but if every serious one still waits for your calendar, the extra volume lands on the same bottleneck instead of growing the firm.
The Positive
  • A broker-dealer's head of insurance sends every new firm that joins straight to you
  • You are already receiving roughly four to five practice referrals a week with no marketing behind them
The Gap
  • A strong advisor who cannot easily start a conversation, or has to wait for one, can drift to a competing brokerage
  • Without tracking, inquiries from future campaigns could not be tied back to the ad that produced them
  • A defined first conversation led by the team would free your time for the relationships that need you most
Giving your salespeople a clear process for first conversations is how your reputation starts working through the team instead of only through you.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • There is no contact form on the homepage or the Contact page, only a phone number and an email address
  • The homepage button reads "Learn More" and leads to the advisor solutions page rather than to a way to book a call or reach the team
  • With no Meta pixel or analytics on the site, form submissions and calls from ads could not currently be tracked back to their source

These findings are also referenced in the Website Audit section.

Team Assessment

Blackheath has four field salespeople, two internal team members and certified financial planners on the advisory side, with regional directors in three markets. Even so, the firm's momentum still depends heavily on you personally. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: AMBER
"I need my salespeople carrying me because people come to me because they want to work with me."
The Problem
  • Advisors who come in through your reputation want to work with you, so the team cannot yet carry those relationships on their own
  • The regional offices you are building have no shared marketing engine feeding them new advisors
  • Marketing and branding rely on one branding resource plus content you record yourself
  • No events, webinars or formal referral program are running today to extend the team's reach
Blind Spot: Because people come to you personally, the brand is you. Each new regional director inherits your referrals but not your reputation, unless Blackheath's own brand is doing the introducing.
The Positive
  • Your advisory team is made up of certified financial planners who are fully registered with securities
  • You have regional directors in Nashville, Overland Park and Orange County, and you can already record content that your team can edit
The Gap
  • As long as larger conversations depend on you, adding offices adds demands on your time instead of relieving it
  • Each regional office needs a steady stream of the right advisors to grow without you opening every door
  • A known brand makes your salespeople's conversations warmer before they begin
The more Blackheath's brand opens doors for your team, the more of the franchise vision you can build without being the bottleneck.

Profit Plan Assessment

Revenue, margin and cost-per-advisor figures were not part of our call, so this section looks at how much visibility Blackheath has into what it costs to win a new advisor, rather than at results. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"it was a lot of like lower tiered insurance people, not financial advisors."
The Problem
  • The earlier outside campaign generated calls, but from the wrong kind of lead, so the spend did not translate into the advisors you want
  • No one has measured what it costs to win a high-net-worth advisor through marketing
  • With no pixel or analytics, a new campaign could not be tied to the relationships it produces
  • Your strongest growth source today, referrals, depends on your personal time rather than a repeatable system
Blind Spot: Strong results without marketing can make marketing look optional. The better question is what it would cost to win advisors who have never heard of you, and only a measured test can answer that.
The Gap
  • Without a cost per qualified advisor, it is hard to decide how much to spend or which channel deserves more budget
  • Repeating a broad funnel risks paying again for volume that does not fit your premium sizes
  • You have said you only want to work with high-net-worth clients, so every dollar needs to be aimed at that audience
A measured proof of concept lets you scale into the market with numbers behind every decision instead of hoping the next campaign works.
