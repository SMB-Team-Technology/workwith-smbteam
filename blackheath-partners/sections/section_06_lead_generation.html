Lead Generation

Blackheath Partners has grown on Matt's referrals, his reputation and a broker-dealer contact who sends new firms his way, with very little coming from marketing. The goal is new high-net-worth wealth advisors, and nothing is currently in place to reach them at scale or to measure what reaching them costs.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The only call to action above the fold is "Learn More," which leads to the advisor solutions page rather than a book-a-call or contact action
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 80/100 — Desktop 97/100 (lab test run October 28, 2026; mobile total blocking time of 760 ms is the main drag)
  • No contact form on the homepage or the Contact page, only a phone number and an email address
  • No Meta pixel, Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager was found in the homepage code, and there is no blog or video on the homepage
Blind Spot: Without a pixel or analytics, every advisor who clicks an ad or a video and visits the site leaves no trace, so they cannot be retargeted and no one can say which channel brought them. Spending on ads before this is fixed means paying for traffic you cannot learn from.
The Positive
  • The site loads quickly, with a strong desktop speed score and a respectable mobile score
  • A team page introduces ten people, including regional directors in Nashville, Overland Park and Orange County, which gives advisors real faces and credibility
The Gap
  • Advisors who are interested after seeing an ad or a video have no obvious next step on the site
  • No tracking means visitors who do not reach out cannot be followed up with
  • With no blog or video content on the homepage, there is little to show advisors why Blackheath is different from other brokerage firms
A website that captures and tracks interested advisors turns your reputation into a system that keeps working while you are in other conversations.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • We could not confirm a Google Business Profile for the Dorado address during research, so the firm's Google listing needs to be checked directly
  • The site lists a Dorado, Puerto Rico street address, a North Carolina PO Box and a 704 North Carolina phone number, which sends mixed location signals
  • No regional landing pages were found, so the offices in Nashville, Overland Park and Orange County appear only on the team page
Blind Spot: You are building regional offices that you call franchises, but online each one is a single line on a team page. An advisor in Nashville or Orange County searching for a local brokerage partner has no page that tells them Blackheath is there.
The Positive
  • Regional directors in three U.S. markets already give Blackheath a real local presence to build on
The Gap
  • Because advisors are recruited nationally, Google reviews matter less here than clear, credible pages for each market and each audience
  • The regional offices have no search presence of their own to support their growth
Every regional office with its own findable presence is one more place where the brand, not just Matt, introduces Blackheath to advisors.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Google search ads for Blackheath or its advisor-focused services were not observed in our research, though this does not mean none are running
  • The channels discussed on our call were Meta, LinkedIn, YouTube and Google Display rather than Google Search
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • No specific competing brokerage firms or their Google ads could be verified from our research, so this section focuses on opportunity rather than named threats
The Opportunity
  • Advisor-intent searches such as an independent marketing organization for financial advisors, or life insurance and premium financing support for advisors
  • Searches for Blackheath by name, so advisors who hear about the firm from a referral can find it easily
  • Google Display and YouTube placements that put short educational content in front of wealth advisors rather than consumers
  • Search terms tied to the high-net-worth planning topics your content already covers, such as estate tax exposure and premium financing

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Google Local Service Ads are built for local service providers, and there is no category for a national insurance brokerage recruiting advisors
  • Because of this, Blackheath would not be expected to appear there, and this is not a gap
The Opportunity
  • The better use of budget is on channels where wealth advisors spend time, such as LinkedIn, YouTube and Meta
  • Skipping a channel that does not fit keeps the proof of concept focused on the audience that matters

Facebook & Instagram Ads

"you don't have a call to action."
Ad Presence
  • At least one live Facebook video ad was shown during our call, a short educational piece on where to establish a dynasty trust, and you noted there are not a lot of them
  • The Facebook Ad Library could not be loaded in our research, so the number of ads, how long they have run and their spend are not confirmed
  • The website has no Meta pixel, so these ads cannot build retargeting audiences from the people who watch them
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • No competing brokerage firms' Meta ads could be verified from our research
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — advisors who watch a video or visit the site and do not reach out are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring you — you can record short videos yourself and your team can edit them, so the missing piece is distribution
  • Educational content ads — FAQ and myth-busting topics reach advisors earlier in their decision process
  • A clear call to action on every video and ad, since people have told you your content does not have one
  • Custom and lookalike audiences built from advisor lists you can buy or already own, aimed at wealth advisors rather than lower-tier agents
