Here Is Where Blackheath Partners Stands Today

Blackheath Partners is a national insurance brokerage general agency, founded by Matt Eller roughly two and a half years ago, that helps wealth advisors place life insurance, annuities, long-term care, disability and premium financing for high-net-worth clients. Growth so far has come almost entirely from Matt's referrals and reputation, and the single most important finding is that the firm has no marketing engine of its own to bring in the high-net-worth advisors it wants to work with.

Stage 6 is where Blackheath's brand, not Matt's personal calendar, brings the right advisors in the door, which is exactly the foundation you need to build out the regional offices and own your niche.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • New advisor relationships come from Matt's referrals, his reputation and word of mouth, with "hardly anything off of marketing"
  • The last outside marketing effort produced calls, but from lower-tier insurance agents rather than the financial advisors you want
  • Larger relationships still need Matt on the final call, even though juniors handle smaller ones
  • The website has no Meta pixel or analytics, no contact form and no clear book-a-call action, so interest cannot be tracked or followed up

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a referral-driven firm and a brand that advisors seek out is not wide, because the reputation and the team are already there, but closing it takes a measured marketing plan, and that is what this report lays out.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The only call to action above the fold is "Learn More," which leads to the advisor solutions page rather than a book-a-call or contact action
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 80/100 — Desktop 97/100 (lab test run October 28, 2026; mobile total blocking time of 760 ms is the main drag)
  • No contact form on the homepage or the Contact page, only a phone number and an email address
  • No Meta pixel, Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager was found in the homepage code, and there is no blog or video on the homepage
Blind Spot: Without a pixel or analytics, every advisor who clicks an ad or a video and visits the site leaves no trace, so they cannot be retargeted and no one can say which channel brought them. Spending on ads before this is fixed means paying for traffic you cannot learn from.
The Positive
  • The site loads quickly, with a strong desktop speed score and a respectable mobile score
  • A team page introduces ten people, including regional directors in Nashville, Overland Park and Orange County, which gives advisors real faces and credibility
The Gap
  • Advisors who are interested after seeing an ad or a video have no obvious next step on the site
  • No tracking means visitors who do not reach out cannot be followed up with
  • With no blog or video content on the homepage, there is little to show advisors why Blackheath is different from other brokerage firms
A website that captures and tracks interested advisors turns your reputation into a system that keeps working while you are in other conversations.
