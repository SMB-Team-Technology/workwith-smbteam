   planning/probate/wills as a stated practice area and an active,
   professionally maintained web presence.

Dynamic sentence: "A score of 4 means competitors have meaningful
advantages in one or more areas and are pulling ahead. You are not
invisible yet — but the window to close the gap is narrowing. Every
month without action is a month they extend their lead."

Rationale: No single competitor is currently dominating paid channels —
this session found no confirmed active Google Ads, LSA, or Meta ad
campaigns for Brumbaugh Law or any of its three named local competitors,
and the local market appears relatively quiet on paid media. The
meaningful advantage in play is structural rather than a specific rival
out-spending Brumbaugh: Brumbaugh Law's own lead generation is fragmented
across three uncoordinated vendors with attribution problems, its social
channel has already hit a saturation ceiling in a geographically
constrained "half-circle" market, and a large pool of warm seminar
leads is going un-monetized — all while at least two direct-niche
competitors (Van Tine on elder law, Kaufman & Kaufman on probate) sit
inside the same small addressable market. The urgency here is
self-inflicted inefficiency compounding a hard geographic ceiling, not
an external competitor blitz — which still argues for prompt action,
just via consolidation and the untapped-database play rather than an
arms race on ad spend.
