Quick Wins for Riggs Law Firm

Ask every signed client for a Google review
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
A new firm earns trust slowly in a market where people compare several bankruptcy firms before they call. Reviews from signed clients are one of the fastest ways to build that trust.
Who Is Doing It Better
Fair Fee Legal Services — directory listings point to a very large volume of client reviews, which gives Las Vegas searchers an easy reason to choose them.
The Opportunity
A simple, consistent request to each client at the end of their case, built into the team's routine, starts building the social proof that supports both search visibility and consult bookings.
Follow up with every consult that does not sign right away
Intake Quick Win
Why It Matters
Today, follow-up usually stops within a day or two of a consult. By Dan's own estimate, the firm may be losing up to 20% of potential clients, an estimate he has not yet measured.
The Opportunity
A short, consistent follow-up routine for the days after a consult gives undecided prospects a reason to come back instead of calling the next firm.
Give lead follow-up a single owner
Team Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Intake is handled by whoever is available, so follow-up depends on how busy the day happens to be.
The Opportunity
Naming one person to own every open lead, and training the team on one routine, lets Dan step out of daily follow-up and spend that time on legal work.
Put a dollar target and a cost per signed case on each lead source
Profit Quick Win
Why It Matters
The growth goal today is a case count, and the split between referrals, Google Ads and the two lead vendors is unclear, so it is hard to tell which source earns its cost.
The Opportunity
A simple monthly view of what each source costs per signed case shows where the next dollar should go, and a dollar target turns 50 cases a month into a plan.
Answer the "$0 down" offers competitors are advertising
Lead Gen Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
People facing bankruptcy are often worried about the up-front cost, and competitors are speaking directly to that worry.
Who Is Doing It Better
Fair Fee Legal Services — its Facebook page promotes "$0 down" bankruptcy filings, and Dan says they mostly run social media ads.
The Opportunity
Clear messaging on the site and in ads about what the first step costs, a free financial assessment, helps cost-conscious prospects choose a consult with Dan.
Capture website visitors who are not ready to book
Intake Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
The homepage's main calls to action are a scheduler and a phone number, with no contact form on the page, so a visitor who is not ready to book has no easy next step.
The Opportunity
A short form on the homepage, followed by a prompt reply, adds another path to a consult from the traffic that Dan's blogging and ads already bring to the site.
