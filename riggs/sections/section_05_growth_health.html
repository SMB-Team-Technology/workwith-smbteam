Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Inquiries come from several sources at once: trustee-side referrals, Google Ads, blogging and social media, and two paid lead vendors
  • The website is active, with frequent blog posts and separate pages for Chapter 7, Chapter 13 and Henderson, Summerlin and North Las Vegas
  • The firm is only about seven months old and competes against established firms that file far more cases each month
Building the leading consumer bankruptcy firm in the valley starts with turning the lead sources Dan already has into a steady, predictable flow.

Intake

  • Intake is shared informally between Dan, his paralegal and a receptionist who just started, with no defined process
  • Follow-up stops within a day or two if a lead does not sign at the consult, and Dan sees leads slipping through the cracks
  • Practice Panther is not yet being used to track leads or trigger follow-up, and the website relies on a scheduler link with no sign of automated follow-up for people who do not book
Every lead that slips away is a client Dan could have helped — and a step further from the 50-case-a-month firm he wants to build.

Self-Managing Team

  • Dan is the sole attorney and the only person who can handle legal work, while a paralegal and a newly hired receptionist support the firm
  • Dan says he is so busy that he struggles to find the time to build the processes the team needs
  • The team is just forming, so roles, hand-offs and accountability for lead follow-up are not yet defined
A team that follows a clear process lets Dan spend his time on the legal work he missed doing instead of chasing every lead himself.

Profit

  • Chapter 7 is a flat fee where the firm absorbs the filing and credit-report costs, so each case needs to convert reliably to pay off
  • Dan is paying per lead to two vendors and running Google Ads, so lead cost matters more when leads go unconverted
  • The firm is new and has not yet set a dollar revenue goal, only a case-volume target of 50 a month
Knowing what each signed case is worth turns growth from a hopeful goal into a plan for building the firm Dan wants.
