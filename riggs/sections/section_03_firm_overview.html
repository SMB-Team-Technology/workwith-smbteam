Here Is Where Riggs Law Firm Stands Today

Dan Riggs opened Riggs Law Firm in Las Vegas in April 2026 after nearly two decades representing bankruptcy trustees, and today he files mostly Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 cases with a team of three. Referrals from his trustee-side network, Google Ads, his own blogging and two paid lead vendors are already bringing in inquiries, but there is no system to follow up with the people who do not sign on the first visit. In Dan's words, leads are "slipping through the cracks."

Stage 6 is where the firm runs on a repeatable process instead of your personal follow-up — and it is the foundation for becoming the firm in the Las Vegas valley that Dan set out to build.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • Dan is the only attorney, and intake is handled ad hoc by Dan, his paralegal and a receptionist who started in October 2026
  • Leads come from referrals, Google Ads, blogging and two paid lead vendors, with no follow-up process once someone leaves a consult without signing
  • Practice Panther is fully set up for clients after they retain, but is not yet used to track or follow up with leads
  • The goal is a 50-case-a-month firm within a year, with filings now running at roughly 20 to 25 a month

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a firm that files 25 cases a month and the leading firm in the valley is mostly process, not demand — and Dan is right that the time to build it is now, while the firm is still small.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Riggs Law Firm
Riggs Law Firm sits at Stage 3: Solo Practitioner. Dan is the only attorney, the firm is only months old, and intake and follow-up are shared informally across a team of three. The most urgent limiting area is intake and follow-up, because leads the firm has already paid to generate are not converting into signed clients, and that stands directly between the firm and the 50-case-a-month goal Dan described.
