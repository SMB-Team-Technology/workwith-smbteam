Lead Generation

Riggs Law Firm already draws inquiries from trustee-side referrals, Google Ads, blogging and social media, and two paid lead vendors, which gives Dan more demand than his team can currently convert. The opportunity is to make those sources work harder, in a Las Vegas market where Fair Fee Legal Services files about 200 cases a month.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • No contact form appears on the homepage, so the only paths to the firm are a scheduler link and a phone number
  • No testimonials, review badges or trust badges are visible on the homepage
  • The attorney bio on the homepage is thin, just a photo and one positioning line, with the fuller story on a separate Firm Overview page
  • No video appears on the site, so visitors cannot see or hear Dan before they decide to call
Blind Spot: The main call to action is a free assessment scheduler. A visitor who is not ready to book that day has no easy way to leave their details, and with no automated follow-up, that person is gone.
The Positive
  • The site is active, with a new blog post nearly every day in early October 2026 covering topics like the means test, foreclosure and wage garnishment
  • Dedicated Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 pages, plus pages for Henderson, Summerlin and North Las Vegas, give search engines clear signals about what the firm does and where
The Gap
  • Visitors who want proof before calling find no reviews or testimonials on the page where they land
  • Without video or a fuller bio up front, Dan's most valuable asset, almost two decades on the trustee side of bankruptcy, is easy to miss
  • Visitors who are not ready to book right away have no way to stay connected to the firm
A website that builds trust and captures every visitor is the first step toward becoming the firm in this valley that people choose.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Riggs Law Firm opened in April 2026, while competitors like Peters & Associates have been building their online reputations since 2008
  • No Yelp listing was found for the firm, and no FindLaw listing was found either
  • The homepage does not display Google reviews or directory ratings, so any reviews the firm earns are not working for it on the site
Blind Spot: Search results surfaced a similarly named Las Vegas firm, Riggi Law Firm. Prospects searching for Dan by name can land on the wrong firm if the listings are not clearly distinguished.
The Positive
  • The firm's name, address and phone number match across its website, Justia and the Avvo and Martindale listings, and it has local pages for Henderson, Summerlin and North Las Vegas
The Gap
  • A new firm competing for "bankruptcy attorney near me" searches needs a steady stream of client reviews to earn the trust that established firms already have
  • Every signed client is a chance for a review, and without a process to ask, those chances pass
Fair Fee Legal Services — directory listings point to a very large volume of client reviews, which is the kind of social proof that helps a firm stand out in local results.
Showing up first and being trusted on sight is how Riggs Law Firm becomes the firm people in the valley think of first.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Dan confirmed on the call that Riggs Law Firm runs Google Ads, and he compares how his ads convert against the firms around him
  • Ad spend, ad copy and who manages the campaigns were not part of this review, so this section focuses on where the opportunity sits for both Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 cases
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Fair Fee Legal Services — according to Dan, they spend heavily on Google Ads, and he sees how his own conversion compares against them and other firms around him
The Opportunity
  • Chapter 7: searches like "las vegas chapter 7 bankruptcy attorney" and "bankruptcy lawyer las vegas", which match about 70% of the firm's cases
  • Chapter 13: searches like "las vegas chapter 13 bankruptcy attorney" and "stop foreclosure las vegas chapter 13", which match the roughly 30% of cases with the larger fee
  • Urgency searches such as emergency bankruptcy, stopping wage garnishment and stopping a foreclosure, from people who need help now and tend to act fast
  • Location searches for Henderson, Summerlin and North Las Vegas, which the website already has pages ready to receive
Blind Spot: Dan said he moves on from leads within a day or two if they do not sign at the consult. Every ad dollar that brings in a lead who then goes unfollowed is spent without producing a case, so better follow-up makes the same ad budget go further.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • A Local Services Ads listing for Riggs Law Firm was not confirmed in this review, so it is treated here as an opportunity to evaluate rather than a confirmed gap
  • LSAs require the firm to become Google Screened, which means passing Google's verification for the firm and its attorney
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Fair Fee Legal Services, Peters & Associates and Freedom Law Firm are the established names Las Vegas searchers will compare against any listing the firm earns
  • Competitor LSA listings and review counts were not confirmed in this review
The Opportunity
  • Dan already pays two lead vendors per lead, so a pay-per-lead channel that Google itself sends is a familiar model for the firm
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Reviews from signed clients carry extra weight in LSAs, so building a steady review habit now helps a new firm compete for these spots

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • Dan said he has been building out his social media, and whether the firm runs paid Facebook or Instagram ads was not confirmed in this review
  • Where paid social is not yet in use, it is an opportunity to reach people who are not actively searching but are dealing with debt
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Fair Fee Legal Services — according to Dan, they mostly run social media ads, and their Facebook page promotes a "$0 down" bankruptcy filing. Their active ad count and formats were not confirmed in this review
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Affordability and payment-plan messaging to answer the "$0 down" offers competitors use, so a prospect who is price-sensitive still comes to Dan's consult
