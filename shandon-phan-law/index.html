Here Is Where Shandon Phan Law Stands Today

The most important finding: his clients have always trusted him personally, but he has no system that turns that trust into a steady flow of new clients without him personally handling every conversation.

Stage 6 is where the firm carries institutional value beyond your personal name and runs on systems instead of your own low-level work — and for you, the road there starts with a dependable intake process and one streamlined set of tools.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Intake Assessment

Shandon Phan Law converts well when Shandon personally engages a lead and noticeably less well when staff or vendors handle it, which means the firm's biggest asset is also its biggest bottleneck. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating. This is where the biggest immediate ROI lives.

Status: RED
"So, when I directly engage, we have high conversion, but when we let the staff handle that, then, of course, it's lower …"
The Problem
  • There is no strong intake process, so the quality of every lead conversation depends on who happens to pick it up
  • Few staff speak both English and Vietnamese, and the part-time VA who covers Vietnamese-speaking leads is based overseas
  • Initial engagement is handed to live receptionists through your ad vendor's portal, a separate system from the rest of your tools
  • The automation and follow-up you want have not been built yet, and after-hours AI voice coverage is still only being evaluated
Blind Spot: Because you close well in person, intake can look healthy on your best days. The real cost shows up when you are busy, because every lead you cannot personally engage converts at the lower rate, and that is exactly when more marketing would add the most leads.

The Gap
  • Every lead handled below your own conversion rate is a plan the firm never signs, at fees that start at $1,500 and reach $4,000 and higher
  • Extra ad spend or restarted radio cannot pay back until the people handling those leads convert closer to the rate you do

Profit Plan Assessment

Shandon Phan Law is rebuilding from a leaner base, with a clear four-level fee ladder and a current pace that sits well below the firm's earlier peak months. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"Because we already charge lower than our competitors, and then we spend way more on the overhead just to serve or just to overcome the weakness of our clients."
The Problem
  • Each plan takes at least 10 to 15 hours of work, which puts a ceiling on how many plans the firm can serve
  • You told us fees are lower than competitors' while overhead goes up to serve each client
  • Hours are not tracked against each level of the fee ladder, so it is unclear which plans earn the most for the time they take
  • Several overlapping tools and vendor commitments add to monthly costs without a single view of what they return
Blind Spot: A Level 1 plan and a Level 4 plan can take a similar amount of your attention. Without knowing hours by level, you may be working hardest on the plans that earn the least per hour.

  • The firm reached much higher monthly revenue in earlier years with only a small team, which shows what the model can do when leads and intake line up
The Gap
  • Without profit per plan, every new client may add work faster than it adds wealth
  • Pricing below competitors leaves little room to hire the help that would free your time
  • Consolidating tools and vendors is the quickest way to bring overhead back in line with a leaner firm
Knowing what each plan really earns is how the firm builds lasting value that does not depend on your hours.
