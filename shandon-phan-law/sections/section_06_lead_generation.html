Lead Generation

Shandon Phan Law has grown on referrals and the personal authority Shandon built through his weekly Vietnamese radio show, but the show is paused and the Google Ads campaign is being wound down, so no repeatable system is currently feeding the firm. The brand-new website is the first piece of a foundation that was not there a year ago, and it is the right moment to decide what gets built on top of it.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • Two spots on the live site, the homepage and the FAQ page, still show unfinished placeholder text in brackets in place of the "Is the first consultation free?" answer, which is the question a prospect is most likely to ask before booking
  • The meta tags and other SEO fundamentals have not been set up yet, so the new pages are not yet telling Google what they are about
  • The newest blog post is from August 2025, and the three most recent posts all went up the same morning, so the site shows no recent content
  • The site names Houston, Katy, and Sugar Land in its copy, but there are no dedicated city pages, and no video appears on the homepage even though your radio authority is your strongest trust-builder
Blind Spot: The live site sits on heartlandlawtx.com, your email still runs on shandonphanlaw.com, directories list an older Bellaire Boulevard office, and a new "Heartland" name is on the way. Search engines and AI tools see several versions of the firm at once, and each version weakens the others until the name, domain, and address match everywhere.
The Positive
  • The new site is mobile responsive, with a clear headline and a "Get Started Today" button and phone number above the fold
  • A dedicated FAQ page with FAQ schema, a Shandon Phan bio page, nine practice area pages, and a free workshop promotion are all already live
  • The consultation form lets a visitor choose English or Vietnamese, which speaks directly to the community you serve
The Gap
  • A visitor who reads a bracketed note instead of a real answer about the consultation fee loses confidence before ever calling
  • Pages without finished SEO tags and without city-specific pages are harder for a family in Katy or Sugar Land to find
  • Without recent content or video, the site does little to show the authority that drives your conversion on the radio
A site that says who you are on every page is the first step toward a firm that carries your authority beyond your personal name.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The website footer shows 11211 Katy Freeway, while Justia, Yelp, MapQuest, Lawyer.com, and the BBB list 11205 Bellaire Boulevard, and the firm appears under at least four name variations
  • Local map-pack placement for estate planning, elder law, probate, business law, and immigration searches in Houston, Sugar Land, and Katy should be verified once the new site is indexed, since the site is only days old
  • No geo-targeted landing pages exist for the surrounding cities the firm says it serves
The Positive
  • Your Google profile carries a strong positive rating, and clients' words on other review sites praise your responsiveness and the quality of your trust work
The Gap
  • Mismatched addresses and names make Google less sure which listing is the real firm, which weakens your chance of showing up when families search for an estate attorney nearby
  • Without a steady review-request routine, a strong reputation stays flat while competitors keep adding new reviews every month
Your Legacy Legal Care — operates from several Houston-area offices including Katy and Sugar Land, which gives it a presence in more of the local searches you want to win as you grow beyond your current base.
Consistent listings and steady reviews turn the trust you have already earned into a signal Google can read without you.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • You confirmed Google Ads are managed by an outside vendor and are being wound down by the end of this month or next so the firm can work with fewer vendors
  • The campaign produced a handful of leads a month, and you identified intake, not traffic, as the reason conversion stayed low
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Capstone Legal Strategies, PLLC — an estate planning and asset protection firm serving Houston, Katy, and Sugar Land, the same cities you want to reach; live ad copy was not captured in this review
  • Heights Law Group — a Houston estate planning firm competing for the same estate planning searches; live ad copy was not captured in this review
The Opportunity
  • Estate planning — high-intent searches such as "living trust lawyer Houston" and "estate planning attorney Katy or Sugar Land," your core service and the one you want to grow to 90 percent of the practice
  • Elder law and Medicaid planning — "Medicaid planning lawyer Houston" and "asset protection attorney Houston," where your new site is leading with Level 4 plans
  • Probate — "probate lawyer Houston" and "probate attorney Sugar Land," a natural follow-on for families who already trust you with their plan
  • Business law — "business succession planning attorney Houston" and "small business attorney Sugar Land," connecting to your Level 3 business entity work
  • Immigration — "Vietnamese immigration lawyer Houston," a limited side area that is worth capturing only where it feeds your estate planning focus
  • Non-obvious: Vietnamese-language searches for wills and trusts, competitor firm names, and "free estate planning workshop Houston" to fill your seminars
Blind Spot: Two walk-ins told you they found you through ChatGPT. Whether or not that holds up, it signals that families are using AI tools to find an estate attorney, and a site with finished pages, consistent listings, and clear answers is what those tools read.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • We did not confirm whether Shandon Phan Law currently appears in Local Service Ads or holds the Google Screened badge, so this should be treated as an open opportunity
  • Estate planning is generally an eligible LSA category, and the firm's positive Google rating gives it a foundation to build on
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Your Legacy Legal Care — a multi-office Houston-area firm with a strong public Google rating, which is the kind of profile Google rewards in LSA placement; LSA positions were not captured in this review
The Opportunity
  • Earn Google Screened status and put your reviews to work in the one ad format that rewards trust above all else
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Because LSAs send calls directly to your team, they only pay off if intake is ready to answer in English and in Vietnamese, which is why intake comes first
Blind Spot: LSA leads call you directly, so a missed call is a lost lead and a paid one. Without after-hours and bilingual coverage, LSA spend can reward competitors who answer faster.
