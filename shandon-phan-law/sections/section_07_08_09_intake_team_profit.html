Intake Assessment

Shandon Phan Law converts well when Shandon personally engages a lead and noticeably less well when staff or vendors handle it, which means the firm's biggest asset is also its biggest bottleneck. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating. This is where the biggest immediate ROI lives.

Status: RED
"So, when I directly engage, we have high conversion, but when we let the staff handle that, then, of course, it's lower …"
The Problem
  • There is no strong intake process, so the quality of every lead conversation depends on who happens to pick it up
  • Few staff speak both English and Vietnamese, and the part-time VA who covers Vietnamese-speaking leads is based overseas
  • Initial engagement is handed to live receptionists through your ad vendor's portal, a separate system from the rest of your tools
  • The automation and follow-up you want have not been built yet, and after-hours AI voice coverage is still only being evaluated
Blind Spot: Because you close well in person, intake can look healthy on your best days. The real cost shows up when you are busy, because every lead you cannot personally engage converts at the lower rate, and that is exactly when more marketing would add the most leads.
The Positive
  • Your own conversion is high, which proves the offer, the pricing ladder, and your authority with the Vietnamese community all work
  • You are already focused on tuning intake before increasing your ad budget, which is the right order to do it in
The Gap
  • Every lead handled below your own conversion rate is a plan the firm never signs, at fees that start at $1,500 and reach $4,000 and higher
  • Extra ad spend or restarted radio cannot pay back until the people handling those leads convert closer to the rate you do
  • While you are the only one who converts well, the firm can only grow as fast as your calendar allows
Intake that works without you is how the firm stops depending on your personal name and starts building the system that lasts.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • The homepage has a "Get Started Today" button and phone number above the fold, but the consultation form sits lower on the page
  • The form asks for a preferred language, English or Vietnamese, which helps route leads but only works if someone is ready to follow up in that language
  • The answers about the consultation fee, length, and format are unfinished placeholders, so a visitor cannot tell what happens after they submit the form
  • A chat plugin loads on the homepage, and we did not test how it responds after hours or to Vietnamese-speaking visitors

These findings are also referenced in the Website Audit section.

Team Assessment

Shandon is the only attorney, supported by a small team that includes a part-time VA in Vietnam and a newly added right-hand person finishing an MBA, while the firm still relies on outside vendors for much of the rest. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: AMBER
"anyone I hire, I have to invest at least six months to two years to train them."
The Problem
  • As the only attorney, every legal decision and every client conversation that matters still comes back to you
  • Vietnamese-speaking paralegals are scarce, and training any new hire takes six months to two years
  • There are no written procedures yet for the VAs you plan to hire, so new people would learn by asking you
  • The tools you rely on are spread across several vendors and have not been connected into a single command center
Blind Spot: The most time-consuming part of your work, helping immigrant families inventory the properties and accounts they own, is also the most repeatable. It is the first task that trained helpers or AI can take off your plate, and you may be spending it on yourself by default.
The Positive
  • Henry is a capable right-hand person with a business intelligence focus who is already helping you build systems
  • A part-time VA already handles Vietnamese-speaking leads and content and could grow into an engagement specialist role
  • You have already scaled the office down, and you describe wanting to spend your time on growing the business and building the system rather than on low-level tasks
The Gap
  • Each hire costs months of your attention before it saves any, which is why the firm keeps relying on you personally
  • Without procedures in writing, the VAs you plan to add will not make you less necessary
  • The hours you spend on repeatable tasks are hours you are not spending on growing the firm
A team that runs on written systems is how you step out of the low-level work and build a firm with value beyond your personal name.

Profit Plan Assessment

Shandon Phan Law is rebuilding from a leaner base, with a clear four-level fee ladder and a current pace that sits well below the firm's earlier peak months. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"Because we already charge lower than our competitors, and then we spend way more on the overhead just to serve or just to overcome the weakness of our clients."
The Problem
  • Each plan takes at least 10 to 15 hours of work, which puts a ceiling on how many plans the firm can serve
  • You told us fees are lower than competitors' while overhead goes up to serve each client
  • Hours are not tracked against each level of the fee ladder, so it is unclear which plans earn the most for the time they take
  • Several overlapping tools and vendor commitments add to monthly costs without a single view of what they return
Blind Spot: A Level 1 plan and a Level 4 plan can take a similar amount of your attention. Without knowing hours by level, you may be working hardest on the plans that earn the least per hour.
The Positive
  • Flat-fee levels with half collected at signing keep cash coming in before the work begins
  • The firm reached much higher monthly revenue in earlier years with only a small team, which shows what the model can do when leads and intake line up
The Gap
  • Without profit per plan, every new client may add work faster than it adds wealth
  • Pricing below competitors leaves little room to hire the help that would free your time
  • Consolidating tools and vendors is the quickest way to bring overhead back in line with a leaner firm
Knowing what each plan really earns is how the firm builds lasting value that does not depend on your hours.
