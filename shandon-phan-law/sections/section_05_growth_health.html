Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Referrals and the Vietnamese radio show built the client base, but the show has been paused, and the clients who still arrive from it are not a source you can measure or repeat
  • Google Ads through the current vendor have produced a handful of leads and are being wound down, which leaves no scalable system in place
  • The new website just launched, and the meta tags and SEO work have not been done yet
A dependable lead source is what lets you grow the firm instead of chasing the next client yourself.

Intake

  • Conversion is high when you engage the lead yourself and lower when staff handle it, with no strong intake process documented
  • Few bilingual staff are available to speak with Vietnamese-speaking leads, and after-hours coverage is still being evaluated
  • The automation you want for the firm is not yet functional, so follow-up depends on people
Until intake runs without you, you stay the bottleneck on every new client.

Self-Managing Team

  • You are the only attorney, working with a part-time VA in Vietnam and a newly added right-hand person who is still learning the law
  • Training a new hire takes six months to two years, and Vietnamese-speaking paralegals are scarce
  • Plans to add one or two VAs are only effective if SOPs exist for them to follow
Written systems and trained helpers are what let you spend your time growing the business instead of on every low-level task.

Profit

  • Revenue is recovering from a smaller base, with current monthly collections well below the firm's earlier peak months
  • Each plan takes 10 to 15 hours of work, and the firm charges less than competitors for it
  • The fee ladder is clear, but there is no view yet of profit per plan or per service level
Knowing what each plan really earns is how a firm built on your name becomes one that holds its value without you.
