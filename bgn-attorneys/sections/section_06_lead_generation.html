Lead Generation

BGN Attorneys has grown almost entirely through a high-touch referral network with medical providers and insurance agents rather than any paid or organic lead-generation system of its own. Per the September 2 call, marketing has intentionally been set aside until at least January 2027 while the firm focuses on coaching and capacity — the findings below are documented as context for that eventual conversation, not as an urgent call to action today.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • Per the transcript, the www subdomain does not redirect to the root domain and instead shows a spam-firewall interstitial page — a known issue the partners planned to send to their web developer
  • PageSpeed scores: Not obtained — the PageSpeed API was rate-limited on repeated attempts during research; recommend a manual pagespeed.web.dev check before the next client conversation
  • NAP (name/address/phone) is inconsistent across directories — FindLaw, Superlawyers, and one Yelp listing show a Spokane, WA address while the firm's active office is in Kent, WA
  • Live chat, above-the-fold CTA, and video presence could not be independently confirmed — automated access to the site was blocked by bot/firewall protection during this research pass
Blind Spot: The www version of your site is currently showing prospects a spam-firewall page instead of your homepage — anyone who types "www" instead of clicking a link may conclude the site is broken or the firm is no longer active.
The Positive
  • Dedicated attorney bio page and active blog are already in place — a foundation many firms at this stage have not yet built
  • Geo-targeted landing pages already exist for both Kent and Seattle
The Gap
  • A broken www redirect is a lost-trust moment for anyone reaching the site that way, with no way to know how many prospects hit it and left
  • Directory listings pointing to a Spokane address instead of Kent create confusion for prospects trying to verify or find the firm before calling
  • Geo-landing pages already built for Kent and Seattle are not yet paying off, since they aren't surfacing in the searches most likely to bring in local clients
Fixing the technical and directory issues costs nothing in ad spend and would be the first ground recovered once marketing becomes a priority again.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • GBP star rating and review count could not be independently verified via a live Google listing, but by every available source the firm's own review count sits far below every named competitor in the Kent market
  • The firm did not appear in the organic/indexed results for any of the five practice-area searches conducted in Kent, WA (car accidents, motorcycle, truck, slip and fall, wrongful death)
  • NAP inconsistency across legacy Spokane and Pasco directory listings is diluting local map-pack signals for the active Kent office
Blind Spot: Because the firm has never had to compete on local search visibility — referrals have carried it so far — this gap has had no visible cost yet. That changes the moment referral volume plateaus.
The Positive
  • Geo-targeted landing pages already exist for Kent and Seattle — most firms at this stage have not built this yet
The Gap
  • Every practice-area search conducted surfaced competitors, never BGN — prospects searching directly for these services in Kent are not finding this firm at all
  • A review count in the single digits next to competitors with hundreds of reviews sends a credibility signal working against the firm the moment referral trust isn't already established
Khan Injury Law — has dedicated, indexed landing pages for every practice area BGN handles in the same city, backed by an established review history dating back to 2007.
None of this needs fixing before January — but it is worth knowing what is quietly compounding against the firm while the coaching and capacity work takes priority.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • This firm does not appear to be running Google search ads for any of its practice areas in Kent, WA — they may not be configured to show for these terms, may be relying solely on Local Service Ads, or may simply not have surfaced during the searches conducted
  • No Google Ads activity was mentioned on the discovery call — the firm's paid presence discussed was limited to LSAs run through an outside agency
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Live paid-ad units could not be confirmed in this research pass (tooling limitation) — a manual live-SERP check would be needed to see which named competitors, such as Khan Injury Law and Brumley Law Firm, are running search ads today
The Opportunity
  • Obvious terms: "car accident lawyer Kent WA," "personal injury lawyer Kent WA," "truck accident attorney Kent Washington"
  • Obvious terms: motorcycle accident, slip and fall, and wrongful death variants for Kent and Seattle
  • Non-obvious: conquesting on named competitor terms (Khan Injury Law, Brumley Law Firm)
  • Non-obvious: neighboring-city terms (Auburn, Renton, Federal Way) where BGN has no current geo pages
Blind Spot: Because LSAs are already running, it is easy to assume search ads would be redundant — but LSAs and Google search ads occupy different real estate on the results page and typically compound rather than cannibalize each other.

Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Not observed in this pass (tooling limitation) — a manual check of the Kent, WA personal injury LSA unit would show which named competitors, such as Khan Injury Law and Brumley Law Firm, are also appearing there
The Opportunity
