Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Referral network with medical providers and insurance agents is strong but entirely relationship-dependent, with no digital lead-generation system of its own
  • LSAs run through an outside agency under review, with no Google Ads, Meta ads, or organic SEO visibility observed for any practice area
  • The firm's own Kent and Seattle geo-landing pages did not surface in any organic searches conducted, while several named competitors dominate those results
Until there is a lead-generation system that runs independent of any one relationship, case volume growth will always be capped by how many meetings the marketer can book.

Intake

  • A new intake specialist was recently hired, but a partner is still personally involved in intake and case administration day to day
  • No after-hours coverage or defined follow-up sequence was described for leads or referrals arriving outside business hours
  • An additional pre-lit paralegal is actively being hired, suggesting intake-to-case handoff is already stretched at roughly 30 cases/month
Every hour spent on intake and case administration is an hour not spent on the higher-value legal and business-development work the partners say they actually want to be doing.

Self-Managing Team

  • All three partners each personally own a core function — litigation, HR/hiring/IT, and intake/ops — with no manager layer beneath them
  • 100% employee retention to date is a genuine strength, but the team has not yet been tested at the 40–50 cases/month volume the firm is targeting
  • A new "pre-legal" role is planned for mid-September 2026, showing the team is growing reactively rather than against a defined capacity plan
A self-managing team is the only way the partners get back the freedom they left their W-2 jobs to build for themselves.

Profit

  • No annual revenue, revenue goal, or fee structure was tracked or discussed in a way that gives a clear financial picture of the firm today
  • The stated concern about over-hiring depressing owner take-home shows real awareness of the risk, but there is no profit plan turning that concern into a guardrail number
  • Personal comp targets ($350–400K in year two, $500K within three years) exist without a visible plan connecting current case volume and case value to those targets
Without a profit plan turning case volume into a target take-home number, hitting 40–50 cases/month will not automatically deliver the $500K personal comp the partners are working toward.
