Here Is Where Church, Page & Gailan Stands Today

Church, Page & Gailan, PLLC is a Yakima-based personal injury firm with four attorneys and offices in Yakima, Kennewick, Wenatchee, and Spokane, built on motor vehicle accident cases. The firm already runs Google Ads and Local Service Ads and has about 15 support staff today with plans to grow toward 22 or 23 — but the tools meant to take work off that team, starting with case management automation in Filevine, are not yet doing the job.

Stage 6 is where the firm runs on systems instead of manual effort, so a growing team can focus on clients and cases — and it is exactly where we want to take you.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Lead Generation

Church, Page & Gailan already runs both Google Ads and Local Service Ads, which puts the firm ahead of many personal injury practices on channel coverage. The weak links are the website those channels send people to and local search visibility across four offices.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The site is built on Wix, and the homepage is slow on phones
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 37/100 — Desktop 86/100
  • Technical SEO issues raised on the discovery call include title and H1 mismatches, multiple H1s on a page, practice area pages competing with each other, and confusing redirects
  • The contact form sits at the bottom of the homepage, and no video appears on the homepage; the blog appears to have last been updated around November 2025
Blind Spot: Paid and organic visitors land on the same site, so slow mobile loading and overlapping pages can quietly raise the cost of every click and lower the number of calls from the traffic the firm already pays for.
The Positive
  • A clear call to action above the fold — "Schedule A FREE Case Evaluation Today!" — plus the phone number (509) 638-1414
  • City-specific practice area and location pages, individual attorney bio pages, and bilingual English and Spanish consultations
The Gap
  • A mobile score of 37 means many phone visitors may leave before the case evaluation button ever loads
  • Practice area pages that compete with each other split ranking strength instead of building one strong page per case type
  • Technical issues like these make it harder for search engines to understand which page should rank for which search
A faster, cleaner site means the traffic the firm already pays for turns into cases without adding more work for the team.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Google Business Profile hours are not set to 24/7 for the offices, even though the website advertises a 24/7 phone line
  • Per the discovery call, Google Maps coverage in the firm's primary market is limited even during open hours, and likely worse after hours
  • The legacy "Church & Page" name still appears on FindLaw, Yelp, and other directories, while the current brand is Church, Page & Gailan
  • In a web search for Yakima motorcycle accident lawyers, the firm was not among the top organic results; Tamaki Law, Bernard Law Group, and LGP Law Firm were
Blind Spot: A website promising 24/7 availability while the Google profile shows closed hours sends a mixed signal — people searching at night may never see the firm as an option, and Google may treat it as less relevant for "open now" searches.
The Positive
  • The firm carries a strong review profile on third-party listings, an asset worth protecting and building on
The Gap
  • Inconsistent business names across directories weaken the trust signals Google uses to decide who appears on the map
  • The Kennewick, Wenatchee, and Spokane offices each need their own review of profile completeness, hours, and name consistency
Tamaki Law — shows up prominently in organic results for car, truck, and motorcycle accident searches in Yakima, with offices in both Yakima and Kennewick and a strong review profile in third-party listings.
