Intake Assessment

Intake was not the focus of the discovery call, but the firm advertises a 24/7 phone line and is considering 24/7 answering, which tells us the front door is on the radar. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • The website promises a 24/7 phone line, but 24/7 answering is still something the firm is considering
  • Google Business Profile hours are not 24/7 for the offices, which does not match what the website advertises
  • The homepage contact form sits at the bottom of the page, below the fold
  • Client status updates and scheduling were named on the call as processes still to be automated
Blind Spot: Injury clients often call more than one firm. When a call or form goes unanswered for even a short time, that case can sign with the next firm on the list.
The Positive
  • A clear free case evaluation button and phone number sit above the fold on the homepage
  • The firm offers consultations in English and Spanish
The Gap
  • Any call that reaches the firm after hours without a live answer is a paid lead at risk of going to a competitor
  • Manual follow-up and status updates take support staff time away from moving cases forward
  • With four offices, consistent intake coverage across locations depends on systems rather than on whoever happens to be available
An intake process that runs on automation and clear coverage lets the team focus on clients and keeps the firm moving without constant manual effort.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • The contact form is at the bottom of the homepage and collects name, email, phone, a description of the incident, and SMS consent
  • The free case evaluation button and phone number are above the fold, giving visitors a fast way to reach the firm
  • No live chat was detected on the site

These findings are also referenced in the Website Audit section.

Team Assessment

Church, Page & Gailan has four attorneys and about 15 support staff, with hiring planned toward roughly 22 to 23 support staff when fully staffed. The challenge is that much of the support team's day still runs on manual, tedious tasks, and the automation inside Filevine is not yet delivering what the firm needs. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • Support staff still carry manual work such as medical records requests, client status updates, and scheduling
  • The automation built into Filevine is not working as well as the firm needs, and the process for creating new automations is not well developed
  • Systems are not fully integrated across the platforms the team uses
  • The firm is still evaluating what AI can do before it defines which automations to build
Blind Spot: Hiring seven or eight more support staff solves a volume problem, but it also multiplies the manual work that has to be trained, managed, and checked. Automating first means each new hire does higher-value work from day one.
The Positive
  • The firm has already started an AI Workforce Pro trial and registered for the Law Firm AI Growth Summit, showing real momentum toward AI
  • Filevine's built-in AI tool has been in use for about a year to a year and a half with some benefits, so the team already has experience with AI in daily work
The Gap
  • Manual support tasks consume hours that could go toward moving cases forward and serving clients
  • Without a clear plan for which processes to automate first, the firm risks spending time on tools that do not connect to each other
  • A growing team without strong systems keeps the work, and the pressure, concentrated on a small group of leaders
A team that runs on well-built automation is a team that can grow without the firm's leaders having to carry every manual detail themselves.

Profit Plan Assessment

The discovery call centered on AI and operations, so revenue, profit, and cost metrics were not discussed and cannot be assessed here. What we can see is a team about to grow by roughly half, which makes the efficiency of every process more important. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • Profit, revenue, and finance tracking were not covered on the discovery call, so margin and cost-per-case visibility are unknown
  • Planned hiring toward roughly 22 to 23 support staff raises payroll, so how much work each person handles becomes a bigger factor in margin
  • Manual tasks that could be automated cost staff hours today and will cost more as the team grows
Blind Spot: Time spent on medical records requests, client updates, and scheduling is a cost that does not show up as its own line on a financial report, but it is paid for every month in staff hours.
The Gap
  • Without visibility into cost per case, it is hard to know how much automation could improve margin
  • Without a plan connecting hiring to automation, new headcount may be added to cover work that systems could handle
  • Without clear targets, it is difficult to measure whether AI investments are paying off
Putting efficiency first means the firm's growth shows up as profit and freedom — not just as a larger payroll and a longer to-do list.
