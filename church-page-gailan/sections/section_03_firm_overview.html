Here Is Where Church, Page & Gailan Stands Today

Church, Page & Gailan, PLLC is a Yakima-based personal injury firm with four attorneys and offices in Yakima, Kennewick, Wenatchee, and Spokane, built on motor vehicle accident cases. The firm already runs Google Ads and Local Service Ads and has about 15 support staff today with plans to grow toward 22 or 23 — but the tools meant to take work off that team, starting with case management automation in Filevine, are not yet doing the job.

Stage 6 is where the firm runs on systems instead of manual effort, so a growing team can focus on clients and cases — and it is exactly where we want to take you.

Where You Are Today

Stage 4: Small Business Manager
  • Two paid lead sources are active — Google Ads and Local Service Ads — alongside a website with city-specific practice area pages
  • Four attorneys and about 15 support staff today, with hiring planned toward roughly 22 to 23 support staff
  • Support staff still carry manual, tedious tasks, and the automation built into Filevine is not working as well as the firm needs
  • AI is being explored, with an AI Workforce Pro trial underway, before the firm defines which processes to automate

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a growing team doing manual work and a firm that runs on automation is a matter of defining the right processes and putting the right leadership behind them — and that is exactly what this plan is built to do.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Church, Page & Gailan
Church, Page & Gailan is operating at Stage 4 — a Small Business Manager with more than one active lead source, four offices, and a support team that is about to grow by roughly half. The most urgent gap is operational: manual support work and Filevine automation that is not yet reliable will only get more expensive as headcount rises. Closing that gap is what lets the firm grow its team without growing the manual work that comes with it.
