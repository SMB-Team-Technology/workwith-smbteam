Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Google Ads and Local Service Ads are both active — a real foundation most firms this size do not have
  • Mobile PageSpeed of 37/100 and technical SEO issues raised on the call slow down the site those channels depend on
  • Google Maps visibility is limited in the primary market even during business hours, and the Business Profile is not set to 24/7
Reliable lead flow only pays off if the systems behind it can handle every case that arrives without adding manual work.

Intake

  • The website advertises a 24/7 phone line and bilingual consultations, but 24/7 answering is still being considered
  • The homepage contact form sits at the bottom of the page, below the free case evaluation button
  • Client status updates and scheduling were named on the call as candidates for automation
Every call and form that is answered fast is a case the firm already paid to generate — and one less task for the team.

Self-Managing Team

  • Four attorneys and about 15 support staff today, with hiring toward roughly 22 to 23 support staff
  • Support staff still handle manual, tedious work such as medical records requests and client status updates
  • Filevine automation is not working as well as the firm needs, so growth in headcount means growth in manual effort
A larger team only creates freedom when routine work runs on systems instead of on people.

Profit

  • Financial metrics were not part of the discovery call, so margin and cost-per-case visibility are still unknown
  • Adding seven to eight support staff raises payroll, making efficiency gains more valuable as the team grows
  • Automating records requests, status updates, and scheduling is a direct way to protect margin as volume grows
The goal is a firm where growth in cases does not require an equal growth in cost.
