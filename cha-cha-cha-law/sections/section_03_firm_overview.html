Here Is Where Cha Cha Cha Law Stands Today

Cha Cha Cha Law is a downtown Los Angeles personal injury firm with Lemon Law on the side, led by attorney Alex Cha and run day to day by a 10-person team that includes two attorneys, two case managers, and six legal assistants. The firm is in growth mode with a 2026 goal of $3M and a 2027 goal of $3.5M — and the single most important finding is that the owner is still in production while the team lacks the systems to run without him.

Stage 6 is where the firm runs as a self-managing team and Alex is out of production and focused on the business — which is exactly where we want to take you.

Where You Are Today

Stage 4: Small Business Manager
  • A 10-person team: two attorneys, two case managers, and six legal assistants
  • The owner is still working cases hands-on rather than leading marketing and the business
  • Marketing is handled in-house, with a mix of paid and organic efforts
  • Intake, drafting, and document search are time-consuming manual work the team wants to automate with AI

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a firm that depends on its owner and a self-managing team is smaller than it looks — closing it starts with taking repetitive work off your people's plates.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Cha Cha Cha Law
Cha Cha Cha Law is operating at Stage 4 — a Small Business Manager with a real team and a strong, multilingual web presence, but with the owner still deeply involved in production. The most urgent gap is manual, time-consuming work in intake, drafting, and document search that keeps the team stretched and the owner in the cases. Until that work is systemized, the goal of a self-managing team — and the move from $3M toward $3.5M without adding headcount-driven complexity — will stay harder than it needs to be.
