Here Is Where Silverback Legal LLP Stands Today

Silverback Legal LLP is a personal injury and medical malpractice firm, a little under a year old, built by experienced trial attorneys and experienced marketers and working New York first and Florida second. Medical malpractice cases are already flowing through a physician-led network and a growing referral network, while motor vehicle accident cases have stalled since the Google campaign was shut down. The single most important finding is that the firm has proven it can buy demand at scale, but it does not yet have a consistent, trackable system that turns that demand into signed cases.

Stage 6 is where the firm runs on a proven model without you carrying it every day, and that is exactly the stage this roadmap is built to take Silverback toward.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • Medical malpractice comes through a physician-led network and referrals, not a repeatable marketing channel
  • Paid advertising is limited to a small Meta lead-gen budget, and the earlier Google campaign has been shut down
  • Motor vehicle accident cases have not been replenished since that campaign ended
  • The team has few hands-on doers, so execution leans on borrowed capacity and the owners themselves

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a firm that depends on its owners and a firm that follows a proven model is closed with a plan, quick measurable wins, and a team that executes it, and that is exactly what this report lays out.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Lead Generation

Silverback Legal LLP has already shown it can generate demand at scale, but today its lead flow rests on a small Meta budget and a medical malpractice network built on relationships. The Google campaign that once produced a large volume of motor vehicle accident leads is shut down, and the local search and AI visibility foundation has not been built yet.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The site is not yet built out for local maps or AI ranking: it needs dedicated practice-area pages and location work, as we saw when reviewing it live on the call
  • PageSpeed scores could not be captured in this review because the site blocked automated testing, so a live speed test is an early onboarding step
  • The Meta tracking pixel does not appear to be installed on the site, which limits retargeting and conversion tracking for the ads you are already running
  • Contact forms, calls to action and mobile layout were not observable in this review and will be checked hands-on
Blind Spot: Ranking in AI answers and in local maps draws on much of the same groundwork, including practice-area pages, a verified listing and reviews, so the visibility you want on ChatGPT starts with the same foundation.
The Positive
  • The brand presentation looks good, which gives the site a credible base to build on
  • The site already lists a broad set of injury practice areas, including motor vehicle accidents, medical malpractice, nursing home negligence and premises liability
The Gap
  • Without practice-area and location pages, searches for car accident and medical malpractice lawyers in New York and Florida have little on your site to match
  • Without the pixel, visitors who do not call on the first visit leave with no follow-up, and your Meta spend cannot learn from who converts
  • Any new ad traffic, including ChatGPT ads, needs strong landing pages to send people to, or the spend is wasted
A site built to rank and convert is the first quick win that gives your partners confidence and moves Silverback toward a firm that runs on a proven model.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The Google Business Profile exists, but it is set up as a service-area business that hides the firm's address, as we saw live on the call
  • The firm's address on file is a mailing address rather than a dedicated office in the New York or Florida markets it wants to win
  • The firm is under a year old, so its review base is still being built, and Google review counts could not be verified in this review
  • A live map-pack check for motor vehicle accident and medical malpractice searches in New York and Florida was not captured and is an early onboarding step
Blind Spot: A service-area listing that hides the address makes it harder to compete for map-pack placement in a specific city, and verified offices in the states you serve also strengthen eligibility for Local Service Ads.
The Positive
  • A Google listing already exists, so the foundation can be improved rather than started from scratch
The Gap
  • Injured people searching on their phones choose from the map pack first, and a listing that is not tuned for it hands those calls to firms that are
  • Every month without a steady review stream is a month the review gap with established firms can widen, so reviews should start now
Local visibility is a long-term asset that keeps producing cases without adding to the workload of a team that is already thin.

Intake Assessment

Silverback has already seen what happens when a large amount of call volume meets an intake process that was not built to screen it, and intake was not reviewed in detail on the call. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: RED
"People were just literally searching and then calling the first phone number they saw."
The Problem
  • At its peak, Google sent a very high number of calls each day, and many were irrelevant, including criminal and family law inquiries
  • Your intake process, response time and after-hours coverage were not walked through on the call, so they have not been assessed yet
  • With few hands-on doers on the team, a surge of calls competes with casework for the same people
The Positive
  • Medical malpractice cases are screened by an experienced attorney and a physician before the firm takes them, which is a strong quality filter
  • You are already exploring intake support partners, such as virtual assistants and 24/7 answering, which shows the right instinct
The Gap
  • Without a defined screening and follow-up flow, paid calls that could become signed cases are lost to noise
  • Without tracking from first call to signed case, it is hard to show your partners which source is actually producing results
  • As motor vehicle accident volume returns, an unprepared intake process risks repeating the earlier pattern of many calls and too few signed cases
Clean intake turns every marketing dollar into more signed cases, which is the proof that keeps your partners confident and the firm moving.

Profit Plan Assessment

Silverback is under a year old, and its model is built on back-end case value, so profit shows up long after the marketing dollars go out. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"I just got tired of selling leads or calls and the big money is on the back end."
The Problem
  • Revenue and margin figures were not shared on the call, so there is no baseline for cost per signed case by channel
  • Cases can take years to resolve, with medical malpractice running three to five years, so marketing spend is felt long before it pays back
  • The earlier Google program ran in the red up front and relied on cases settling a year later to pay back
  • No profit plan or financial dashboard was described that ties ad spend to settled-case value
Blind Spot: When payback takes a year or more, the number that matters is cost per signed case by channel, not clicks or calls, and without it a campaign can look busy while quietly losing money.
The Positive
  • Cases from the earlier Google campaign are now settling, which gives the firm real data on what that spend was worth
  • The model of keeping cases for back-end value, rather than selling leads, is where the larger economics sit
The Gap
  • Without a cost-per-case view, it is hard to decide how much to spend on motor vehicle accident cases versus building the medical malpractice side
  • Partners who are cautious about spend are easier to win over with a clear scorecard than with a hopeful pitch
  • Long payback periods make cash planning essential, or growth can strain the firm instead of funding it
Seeing which dollars come back as profit is how the back-end value you are building becomes a number you can plan around.
