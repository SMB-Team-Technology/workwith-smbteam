Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Motor vehicle accident case flow has stalled since the Google campaign was shut down
  • Paid social is limited to a small Meta lead-gen budget, with no other consistent channel running
  • The local search and AI visibility foundation has not been built yet, so the firm is hard to find organically
A predictable flow of cases is what turns a young firm into one that can follow a proven model instead of relying on the owners to chase every lead.

Intake

  • At its peak, Google produced a very high call volume with many irrelevant calls, which points to a screening gap
  • Medical malpractice cases are screened by an experienced attorney and a physician before they are taken
  • Intake support options, such as virtual assistants and after-hours answering, are on your radar but not yet in place
Every qualified call that signs is a case you did not have to buy twice, and that is how quick wins get built.

Self-Managing Team

  • The firm has few hands-on doers and leans on borrowed capacity from your other businesses
  • Campaign management and media buying have historically sat with the owners themselves
  • Strong specialists are in place, but there is no dedicated operations lead or documented playbook yet
A documented model that your team can execute is what lets you step back without the firm slowing down.

Profit

  • Revenue and margin figures were not shared, so there is no baseline for cost per signed case yet
  • Case payback runs long, so ad spend is felt immediately while returns arrive on the back end
  • The earlier Google program ran negative up front and relied on cases settling to pay back
Knowing which channel produces profitable cases is how the back-end money you are building toward becomes predictable.
