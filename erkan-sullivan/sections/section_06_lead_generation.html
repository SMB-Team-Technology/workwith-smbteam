Lead Generation

Erkan & Sullivan, P.C. runs one lead generation channel — SEO through Blue Shark — and it is underperforming badly against the firm's local competitors, with zero paid search, social, or Local Service Ads presence to fill the gap.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • Primary contact CTAs ("contact us today," the online contact form) sit mid-page and in the footer rather than immediately visible on load — the only above-the-fold action is a mobile call/text icon
  • PageSpeed scores: Not observed — Google's PageSpeed Insights API was rate-limited (HTTP 429) across four separate attempts and could not return real mobile or desktop scores
  • No live chat and no video content anywhere on the homepage — despite two named partners and four staff members with photos and bios already on the site
  • No visible build or copyright date, so the exact age of the current design is unconfirmed — though an active blog through June 2026 suggests it has been maintained
The Positive
  • Mobile-responsive design with a prominent call/text option in the header
  • Strong trust signals above and throughout the page — Super Lawyers badges for both partners, a Martindale-Hubbell AV rating, Avvo and Justia badges, and two highlighted case results
  • An active blog (1–2 posts/month through H1 2026) including a June 5, 2026 post tied directly to the statewide OUI breathalyzer ruling the firm was personally involved in
The Gap
  • Every visitor who lands on the site but has to hunt for a way to reach the firm is a visitor who may call Neyman or Gilman Law instead — both of whom are already easier to find in search
  • No live chat means after-hours visitors searching in a moment of urgency (a common pattern for criminal defense inquiries) have no way to engage before morning
  • The firm's single biggest content asset — the 27,000-case breathalyzer ruling — exists as one blog post, not a dedicated landing page or campaign built to capture the search demand it created
A website built to convert as hard as your trial record works is the difference between visitors reading about your OUI win and visitors calling you because of it.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Firm carries only 100 total organic keywords and 18 top-3 rankings — versus 868 keywords/210 top-3 rankings for Stephen Neyman and 450/131 for Urbelis Law
  • Across nine local-pack test searches spanning OUI/DUI, domestic violence, and drug crimes, the firm surfaced in only 3 — missing entirely for "OUI lawyer Andover MA," "DUI attorney Lawrence MA," and both domestic violence searches run
  • Geo-targeted landing pages exist (/lowell-dui-lawyer/, /andover-criminal/) but coverage is partial rather than a full city-by-city buildout across all practice areas
Blind Spot: The firm's 154 five-star Google reviews already beat two of its three biggest named competitors on trust signals — Neyman (96) and Urbelis (37). The problem isn't reputation. It's that Google isn't surfacing that reputation when the searches that matter are actually happening.
The Positive
  • 154 reviews at a 5.0 rating, with NAP (name, address, phone) matching exactly across every directory checked — Avvo, Justia, FindLaw, Yelp, Martindale-Hubbell, Lawyers.com, and Super Lawyers
The Gap
  • Every search where a competitor appears and Erkan & Sullivan does not is a prospect who never even sees the firm as an option
  • The firm's better review profile is being wasted on searches where it never shows up to be compared against
Gilman Law, P.C. — an OUI/DUI specialist holding a 470-review advantage (vs. 154) in the exact niche the firm wants to build its brand around.
Closing the visibility gap is what turns a firm with a better reputation than most of its competitors into the firm that actually gets chosen.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • This firm does not appear to be running Google search ads for OUI/DUI, domestic violence, or drug crimes terms in Andover, Lawrence, or Lowell — consistent with the transcript confirmation that Blue Shark handles website/SEO only, with no paid search program in place
  • No sponsored listings were surfaced in any of the nine search queries run across all three practice areas
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • No sponsored markers surfaced for named local competitors either (Panas Law, Stephen Neyman, Gilman Law) across any of the searches run — this appears to be an open channel across the entire competitive set, not one where the firm is behind a specific rival
The Opportunity
  • Obvious terms with zero paid competition observed: "OUI lawyer Andover MA," "DUI attorney Lawrence MA," "drug crimes attorney Lawrence MA"
  • Neighboring-city terms tied to the firm's court coverage: Lowell and Lawrence District Court searches
  • Non-obvious: campaigns built specifically around "OUI breathalyzer lawsuit Massachusetts" and "vacate OUI conviction Massachusetts" — search demand created by the exact case Murat was personally involved in, and currently uncaptured by any paid channel
  • Competitor-name bidding on Neyman and Gilman Law searches, since neither appears to be running ads themselves
Blind Spot: Because no competitor in this set is running search ads either, being first to launch — rather than reacting to a competitor's ads — is still possible here.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • The firm does not appear to hold an active LSA/Google Screened placement — framed as an opportunity gap, since the LSA unit itself was not directly renderable through available research tools
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • No named local competitor's LSA presence could be confirmed through available tools — this channel is unverified across the entire competitive set, not just for this firm
The Opportunity
  • With 154 reviews at 5.0 stars, the firm is already positioned to qualify for Google Screened status faster than most local competitors
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • At a $15,000+ average case value, even a modest LSA lead volume pays for the placement many times over each month
Blind Spot: LSA visibility does not depend on winning the organic ranking battle you are currently losing — it sits above it, which makes it the fastest available path to being seen first.

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • No active Meta ads were confirmed for the firm — framed as an opportunity gap, since the Meta Ads Library returned an access restriction (HTTP 403) to automated research tools and could not be independently verified
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Stephen Neyman Law Offices — active ad status not observed (same access limitation)
  • Gilman Law, P.C. — active ad status not observed (same access limitation)
The Opportunity

  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Content built specifically around the statewide OUI breathalyzer scandal could reach the 27,000 people directly affected by it — a uniquely defensible audience no competitor can target the same way
