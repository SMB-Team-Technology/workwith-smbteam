Intake

  • Primary contact CTAs sit mid-page and in the footer rather than immediately visible on load — the site's most prominent action is a phone/text option, not a form

Fixing intake is the fastest way to raise average case value without spending another marketing dollar — every case is a signed client instead of a missed call.

Self-Managing Team

  • Team of two partners plus four support staff (Shah, Victor, Sandra, Felicia Gordon) — but the firm's growth plan centers on hiring an associate specifically to relieve Murat and Ryan, not on delegating existing work
  • Partners are personally targeting high-margin casework (clerk's hearings at ~$2,000/hr) themselves rather than having that work distributed across a scaled team
  • No leadership or management layer beyond the two named partners was described in the discovery call
Until casework and intake are no longer running through the two of you personally, hiring an associate adds revenue potential — but not the freedom you're actually after.

Profit

  • Clear per-case profitability awareness exists for one case type ($4,000 fee for a 2-hour clerk's hearing = $2,000/hr) — but no firm-wide profit plan was described

  • No annual revenue figure, close rate, or acquisition cost was quoted on the call — decisions are being made without full financial visibility
A clear profit plan is what turns "we should be making $800/hr" into a number you actually see land in the bank every month.
