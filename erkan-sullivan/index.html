Here Is Where Erkan & Sullivan, P.C. Stands Today

Murat Erkan and Ryan Sullivan have built a respected criminal defense practice in Andover — 154 five-star Google reviews, Super Lawyers honors for both partners, and a landmark OUI breathalyzer case that entitled 27,000 people to new trials. But their current SEO vendor, Blue Shark, has failed to turn any of that credibility into visibility: the firm draws just 212 organic visitors a month against 1,300–1,900 for its closest competitors. The reputation is there. The system to convert it into a predictable flow of premium cases is not.

Stage 6 is the stage where the firm's brand finally commands the respect your trial record has already earned — where Murat and Ryan aren't the only two people capable of bringing in a premium case, and hiring an associate is a capacity upgrade instead of a gamble.

Where You Are Today

STAGE 4: Small Business Manager
  • One lead generation system in place (SEO/website through Blue Shark) — but it is badly underperforming against local competitors
  • No paid search, no Local Service Ads, and no social advertising running in any of the firm's three core practice areas
  • Murat and Ryan remain personally involved in the highest-value casework, with growth plans built around hiring an associate to create relief
  • A landmark, market-defining case (the statewide OUI breathalyzer ruling) went uncapitalized on by the firm's current marketing vendor

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap here is not reputation or trial skill — it is visibility and capacity. Closing it means finally putting a marketing system in place that matches the record you have already built, and a team structure that lets that record work for you instead of resting entirely on the two of you.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.
