Intake Assessment

Intake was not the focus of our conversation, so this rating is provisional — but the way information and requests reach you each day is part of the overhead you want to reduce. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • Roughly 300 emails a day need to be prioritized, and that work rests on one person
  • The website contact form lives on a separate page rather than on the homepage
  • No documented intake process, after-hours coverage or lead follow-up sequence came up on our call
Blind Spot: A single person doing a high-volume triage job well is a strength until that person is out for a week — a documented, AI-assisted triage workflow protects the firm from that single point of failure.
The Positive
  • The homepage carries a clear "Schedule a Strategy Session" call to action, twice, above the fold
  • A long-tenured assistant already knows how to prioritize what matters to you
The Gap
  • Manual triage costs hours every day that could go to billable or leadership work
  • Without a structured process, important client messages depend on one inbox review
  • A form that is not on the homepage adds a step for every inquiry
Smart triage gives you a firm that surfaces what needs you — and quietly handles the rest.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

Team Assessment

SOLOMON | ARIEH has a capable team of five attorneys and about five support staff, but you still carry roughly 20 hours a week of overhead oversight. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: AMBER
"If I can take overhead oversight from 20 hours a week to five hours a week because I can use efficiencies to AI, hell yes, I'm going to do that."
The Problem
  • Overhead oversight consumes about 20 hours of your week
  • KPI review, executive summaries and attendance monitoring are manual rather than automated
  • HR and onboarding tasks rely on people following steps by hand
  • AI use inside the firm is effectively off-limits today, so the team has no sanctioned way to work more efficiently
Blind Spot: You are deliberately keeping and redeploying your people rather than replacing them — the opportunity is freeing them from routine work so their time goes to higher-value tasks, not shrinking the team.
The Positive
  • A Director of Operations and Client Experience is already in place
  • You approach AI with a clear principle — to supplement, not supplant, human judgment
The Gap
  • Fifteen hours a week of oversight is the difference between today and the target you set
  • Daily visibility depends on gathering information rather than receiving it automatically
  • The team is not yet equipped or trained to use AI safely, so efficiency gains stay out of reach
A team supported by well-governed AI is how you lead from the front without being pulled into every detail.

Profit Plan Assessment

SOLOMON | ARIEH's financial reporting and reconciliations are handled by an outside accounting firm, and you want clearer, faster visibility into how each client and matter performs. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"I pay our accounting firm $6,800 a month, I think, for our financial statements, reconciliations."
The Problem
  • Financial statements and reconciliations are an ongoing outside vendor cost
  • Client-level margin monitoring is something you want but do not currently have
  • Hourly billing ties the firm's output directly to hours worked
Blind Spot: Vendor costs rarely get reviewed once they become routine — each recurring vendor is worth a fresh look at whether the work could be done faster or in-house with the right tools.
The Positive
  • You are already thinking in terms of cost per workflow and where efficiency could replace spend
The Gap
  • Without client-level margin data, it is hard to know which relationships and matters earn the most for the time invested
  • Delays in reporting mean decisions are made on older numbers
  • Savings from automated reporting are not yet captured because the workflows do not exist
Better numbers, faster, is how you leave behind a stronger firm and do well in the process.
