Quick Wins for Jason Breit Law

Fix Mobile Page Speed Before Scaling Ad Spend
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
The website scores 43/100 on mobile, with the main content taking about 7.4 seconds to appear. Many families search from a phone, and a slow page can lose them before they see the booking button.
The Opportunity
Faster mobile load times are typically one of the lowest-cost ways to raise the share of ad clicks that turn into consultation requests, and they make every future advertising dollar work harder.

Build a Steady Google Review Habit
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
Families comparing estate planning firms look at reviews first, and fresh Google reviews strengthen both map visibility and trust. The firm's testimonials are drawn from its Google profile, which gives it a base to build on.
Who Is Doing It Better
Davidov Law Group — promotes a large base of five-star Google reviews alongside its attorney credentials.
The Opportunity
A simple review request after each completed plan and each three-year review turns satisfied clients into a steady stream of recent Google reviews.
Add a Short Inquiry Form and After-Hours Capture
Intake Quick Win
Why It Matters
The homepage offers a booking button and a phone number, and posted office hours are weekdays 9am–5pm. Visitors who are not ready to book, or who look at night or on weekends, have no easy lower-commitment option.
The Opportunity
A short inquiry form with an immediate confirmation and next-business-day follow-up keeps those visitors from leaving for a competitor.
Build Town-Specific Landing Pages
Lead Gen Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
The firm says it serves Garden City, Huntington, Massapequa, and both counties, but no pages targeting those communities were observed, so Google has little to rank for searches outside Melville.
The Opportunity
Dedicated pages for each community and practice area give both organic search and paid campaigns a relevant place to send families.
Define Follow-Up for Consultations That Do Not Sign Right Away
Intake Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Estate planning decisions often take time, and without a defined follow-up sequence a family that needs a few weeks can quietly drift to another firm.
The Opportunity
A simple follow-up sequence after each consultation helps convert more of the leads the firm already earns.
Document Roles and Handoffs From Consultation to Signed Plan
Team Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
The firm's brand, bio, and consultation offer are built around Jason, so more consultations will land on him unless handoffs are written down.
The Opportunity
Clear roles and checklists let the team carry more of the work, which creates room for growth without adding to the founding attorney's workload.
Track Cost per Signed Client by Channel
Profit Quick Win
Why It Matters
Without knowing what it costs to sign a client from each source, the firm cannot tell whether marketing spend is paying for itself.
The Opportunity
Monthly reporting that ties leads and spend to signed plans turns marketing from a guess into a number Jason can plan around.
