Here Is Where Jason Breit Law Stands Today

Jason Breit Law is a single-office estate planning and elder law firm in Melville, New York, led by a founding attorney who has practiced since 2007 and serves families across Nassau and Suffolk Counties. The firm has real credibility signals on its website — published fixed fees, a free-consultation offer, attorney credentials, and local blog content — but the mobile site loads slowly and no paid advertising channel was observed to turn that credibility into a measurable flow of new consultations.

Stage 6 is where the firm runs and grows without depending on any one person's hours — and whatever growth means personally for Jason, that is the foundation it is built on.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner (preliminary)
  • A founding attorney leads a single Melville office, with a Client Services Director as the only other team member listed on the website
  • Estate planning, elder law, probate, special needs, and kids protection planning are offered on a published fixed-fee model
  • The website relies on a free-consultation booking button, a phone number, testimonials, and a local blog to attract clients
  • Mobile site speed is slow, and no paid search or social advertising was observed during our research

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between the firm's strong credibility and a predictable flow of new clients is smaller than it looks — closing it takes one measured, always-on marketing channel, and that is where this plan starts.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Jason Breit Law
Based on public information, Jason Breit Law is operating at Stage 3 — a credentialed founding attorney with a small team, a clear fixed-fee offer, and a website that already earns trust. The most urgent gap is the lack of a confirmed, measurable lead generation channel: paid advertising was not observed, and a mobile load time of more than seven seconds can cost the firm visitors before they ever see the booking button. These findings come from public research only and will be refined with Jason's own numbers and goals on the proposal call.
