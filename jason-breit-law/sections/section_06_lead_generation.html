Lead Generation

Jason Breit Law's website pairs a clear free-consultation offer with published fixed fees and regular local blog content — a solid base for attracting estate planning and elder law clients. What we could not confirm is a measurable paid channel built on top of it, in a Melville and Nassau County market where nearby firms openly promote their reviews and experience.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The homepage is slow on a phone: the main content takes about 7.4 seconds to appear and the page is blocked from responding for over a second
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 43/100 — Desktop 89/100 (Lighthouse performance score)
  • The homepage offers a booking button and phone number but no short inquiry form, and no video was detected
  • No city-specific pages were observed for Garden City, Huntington, Massapequa, or other towns the firm says it serves
Blind Spot: Search results also surface a second domain (longisland-estateplanning.com) and a separate "Nassau County Estate Planning Attorney" landing page that use the same address and phone number. If they carry overlapping content, they can split search authority away from jasonbreitlaw.com.
The Positive
  • The attorney bio is strong: admitted in New York since 2007, JD and MBA, RICP and CLTC designations, and Personal Family Lawyer network membership
  • A published pricing page with fixed fees, around fifteen client testimonials on the homepage, and a free consultation offered by Zoom or in person all lower the barrier to a first call
The Gap
  • Many families search from a phone, and a slow page can lose them before they see the booking button
  • Every future ad click will land on this same page, so slow load times raise the cost of every consultation
  • Without town-specific pages, Google has nothing to rank for searches outside Melville
A faster, more focused website means every marketing dollar Jason invests works harder.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The firm's reviews page states that its testimonials come from its Google Business Profile, but the profile's category, photos, posts, and most recent review date were not observed in this research
  • Local map results for estate planning, elder law, probate, and special needs searches in Melville and Nassau County were not observed, so the firm's position there is unconfirmed and should be checked live
  • No geo-targeted pages for surrounding communities were found, and the blog's regional titles use "Near You on Long Island" rather than specific towns
The Positive
  • The firm's address and phone number are consistent across its main website, its related site, and search listings, which supports local search trust
The Gap
  • Prospective clients choosing from map results compare review totals and recency first, and the firm's standing in those results is not yet measured
  • Without town-level pages and an actively managed profile, the firm competes for only a fraction of the searches in its stated service area
Davidov Law Group — pairs a husband-and-wife attorney team and a CFP credential with a large base of five-star Google reviews, exactly the kind of trust signals prospective clients weigh when choosing from local results in Nassau County.
Showing up with the right proof when families search is the first step toward a predictable stream of consultations.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Google search ads for estate planning, elder law, probate, kids protection, or special needs planning terms were not observed; Google results could not be viewed directly in this research, so this is not confirmation that none are running
  • A separate "Nassau County Estate Planning Attorney" landing page exists, and it is worth confirming whether it supports any campaign
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Ettinger Law Firm — a same-town Melville competitor offering free consultations across 12 New York locations; its ad messaging was not observed
  • Davidov Law Group — a Nassau County estate planning and elder law firm that leads with its review base and attorney credentials; its ad activity was not observed
The Opportunity
  • High-intent searches such as "estate planning attorney Melville," "wills and trusts attorney Long Island," and "estate planning lawyer Nassau County"
  • Elder law and Medicaid planning searches, plus probate and special needs trust searches across Nassau and Suffolk Counties
  • Neighboring-town searches such as Garden City, Huntington, and Massapequa, where no dedicated pages exist today
  • Trigger-event searches from adult children, such as probate after a parent's death or Medicaid planning for a parent, and competitor-name searches for firms like Ettinger and Davidov
Blind Spot: With a mobile score of 43/100, paid clicks will arrive at a page that is slow to load on a phone. Improving it raises the share of ad clicks that turn into consultations.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Local Service Ads results could not be viewed in this research, so the firm's LSA listing and Google Screened status were not observed
  • This is an opportunity gap to verify, not a confirmed absence
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • No named competitor LSA listings were observed in this research, and competitor review totals could not be confirmed on Google
The Opportunity
  • Where available for the firm's practice areas and service area, LSAs put a firm in front of ready-to-call prospects at the very top of Google
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • A steady flow of recent Google reviews and the Google Screened badge are what position a firm to compete in this channel

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • The Meta Ads Library could not be reached in this research, so active Facebook and Instagram ads were not observed
  • Treat this as an opportunity gap to verify with the firm rather than a confirmed absence
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Davidov Law Group — active ad count, formats, and duration not observed
  • Ettinger Law Firm — active ad count, formats, and duration not observed
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Educational ads built from the firm's existing blog topics, such as naming guardians for minor children and choosing a special needs planning attorney, to reach parents and adult children early in the planning decision
