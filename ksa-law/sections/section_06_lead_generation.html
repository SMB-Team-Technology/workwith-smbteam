Lead Generation

KSA Law has grown its client base entirely through referral relationships and direct business development in its first four months — no paid advertising, SEO strategy, or marketing channel of any kind has been evaluated or used. Because the firm's fractional general counsel and business transactions model targets growth-stage and VC-backed companies who typically source counsel through referrals and investor networks rather than local searches, several standard lead-generation channels below have limited natural fit here — that mismatch is called out at each step rather than papered over.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • ksagc.com could not be directly rendered this session — a bot-verification interstitial blocked every fetch attempt (homepage, /our-story/, /contact/)
  • PageSpeed scores: Not obtained — Google PageSpeed Insights API returned a quota-exceeded error on two separate attempts (mobile and desktop); recommend a manual run before the call
  • Homepage headline, CTA placement, contact form location, and mobile responsiveness could not be directly observed this session — all flagged for manual confirmation
  • No blog and no geo-targeting landing pages surfaced in indexed search results — consistent with a narrow, referral-driven B2B site rather than a local-SEO-built one
Blind Spot: Because ksagc.com is only about 4 months old, it likely has not yet been fully indexed or optimized — meaning a prospect who searches for Kelly or the firm by name after a referral conversation may find thin or incomplete results before that first call ever happens.
The Positive
  • Indexed search snippets show a clear, differentiated positioning built around Kelly personally leading every engagement ("no junior attorneys running matters while a partner's name appears on letterhead") — a message likely to resonate with growth-stage founders wary of being handed off to junior staff
The Gap
  • Without a rendered site to test, it is unknown whether the site converts a referral's "let me check them out" search into a booked call
  • If PageSpeed or mobile issues do exist, referred prospects doing a quick credibility check before their first call could bounce before ever making contact
  • A site with unconfirmed CTA and contact form placement risks losing warm referral traffic it should be capturing almost for free
Referrals are the one channel already working for Kelly — but until the website is confirmed to convert that goodwill into booked calls, some of the path to $5M ARR is being left on the table before a single marketing dollar is ever spent.

The Gap
  • No confirmed GBP presence means there is no simple, defensible online proof point to reinforce a referral once a prospect starts researching the firm
  • Established competitors already have more visible third-party credibility signals in this market
Cook Ellis LLC — a 4.6-star aggregator rating (TrustAnalytica) and a 15+ year track record positioned for the same growth-stage business clients KSA Law is targeting.
Every VC-backed founder who searches for KSA Law before a proposal call and finds a thinner online presence than MSB Law or Cook Ellis is a small crack in the credibility Kelly is trying to build toward becoming the definitive fractional-GC platform in this market.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • This firm does not appear to be running Google search ads for fractional general counsel or business law terms in Kansas City — no live paid-results block could be rendered this session, so this is not confirmed as a deliberate absence, only as not observed
  • Per the transcript, Google and Meta advertising have never been part of KSA Law's business development — the firm has built its client base entirely through referrals and direct outreach
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • MSB Law and Cook Ellis LLC are the most relevant named local competitors surfaced in this research; ad status for either firm was not confirmed live this session
The Opportunity
  • Structural note: fractional general counsel and business transactions for growth-stage/VC-backed companies is not a category founders typically search for on Google the way "personal injury lawyer near me" is — paid search likely plays a smaller role here than referral and investor-network channels
  • If a targeted paid search test is ever run, "outside general counsel Kansas City" and "fractional general counsel Kansas City" are the two terms where KSA Law and named competitors already show organic visibility — the same terms a cold-searching operator or CFO would most likely type
Blind Spot: The absence of Google Ads activity here is not a gap the way it would be for a consumer-facing practice — spending budget against a channel that doesn't match how growth-stage founders actually find counsel could waste money better spent on the firm's proposal process and financial visibility first.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence

  • No live Google Maps/LSA session was available this session to confirm either way
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • No competitor firm was confirmed to be running LSA campaigns for outside or fractional general counsel this session
The Opportunity
  • Structural note: Google Local Service Ads is built around high-volume, consumer-intent legal verticals (personal injury, family law, DUI, immigration) that Google has explicitly onboarded — "fractional general counsel" is not a confirmed LSA category
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly
Blind Spot: Even if a business-law LSA category exists, LSAs are built for prospects sourcing counsel the way they'd source a plumber — a single, urgent, high-intent search. Confirm with Google whether a fractional-GC-fitting category exists at all before treating this as a real channel for KSA Law.
