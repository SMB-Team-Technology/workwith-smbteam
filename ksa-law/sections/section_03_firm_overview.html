Here Is Where KSA Law Stands Today

KSA Law is a four-month-old boutique fractional general counsel and business transactions firm built by Kelly Sullivan Angles, who also leads Sandberg Phoenix's Kansas City office as Managing Shareholder. In that time the firm has grown from a solo practice to a 7-person team — including a new litigation practice added through a Senior Counsel hire — almost entirely on referral relationships with growth-stage and VC-backed businesses, with no formal system yet for generating new client relationships or improving proposal close rates. The single most important finding: KSA Law's growth has outpaced its systems — cash flow strain from 30–45 day client payment cycles is requiring Kelly to personally capitalize firm expenses, and the firm has not yet reached its own break-even target.

Stage 6 is where KSA Law stops running on Kelly's personal referral network and bandwidth alone — becoming the fractional-GC platform for growth-stage and VC-backed businesses that can reach $5M ARR without Kelly personally bankrolling the growth along the way.

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you

The gap between a four-month-old firm scaling on referrals and personal bandwidth alone, and a firm with the financial visibility and proposal systems to close $5M in VC-fund business, is closeable — but only with a plan, not more hours from Kelly.
