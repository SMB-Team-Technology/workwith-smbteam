Here Is Where KSA Law Stands Today

KSA Law is a four-month-old boutique fractional general counsel and business transactions firm built by Kelly Sullivan Angles, who also leads Sandberg Phoenix's Kansas City office as Managing Shareholder. In that time the firm has grown from a solo practice to a 7-person team — including a new litigation practice added through a Senior Counsel hire — almost entirely on referral relationships with growth-stage and VC-backed businesses, with no formal system yet for generating new client relationships or improving proposal close rates. The single most important finding: KSA Law's growth has outpaced its systems — cash flow strain from 30–45 day client payment cycles is requiring Kelly to personally capitalize firm expenses, and the firm has not yet reached its own break-even target.

Stage 6 is where KSA Law stops running on Kelly's personal referral network and bandwidth alone — becoming the fractional-GC platform for growth-stage and VC-backed businesses that can reach $5M ARR without Kelly personally bankrolling the growth along the way.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Lead Generation

KSA Law has grown its client base entirely through referral relationships and direct business development in its first four months — no paid advertising, SEO strategy, or marketing channel of any kind has been evaluated or used. Because the firm's fractional general counsel and business transactions model targets growth-stage and VC-backed companies who typically source counsel through referrals and investor networks rather than local searches, several standard lead-generation channels below have limited natural fit here — that mismatch is called out at each step rather than papered over.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • ksagc.com could not be directly rendered this session — a bot-verification interstitial blocked every fetch attempt (homepage, /our-story/, /contact/)
  • PageSpeed scores: Not obtained — Google PageSpeed Insights API returned a quota-exceeded error on two separate attempts (mobile and desktop); recommend a manual run before the call

The Positive

The Gap
