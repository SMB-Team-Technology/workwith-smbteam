Lead Generation

The Licatesi Law Group spends roughly $100k/month with third-party lead-gen agencies for the bulk of its case volume, while its own strong, modern website — licatesilaw.com — is not surfacing in top organic search results for the firm's own core local searches. A mid-rebrand digital footprint, split across a legacy brand and an unlaunched new domain, is compounding the gap between what the firm has built and what prospects actually find.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The URL provided for this engagement, tllgllp.com, is not the firm's real site — it is a bare "Launching Soon" placeholder built on GoDaddy Website Builder, with no practice areas, no attorney bios, and no blog
  • PageSpeed scores could not be obtained this session (the PageSpeed Insights API returned a quota-exceeded error on every attempt) — this should be run manually on the real operating site, licatesilaw.com, before the call
  • The firm's real operating site, licatesilaw.com, is modern and well-built, but it sits alongside a prior brand (Rubin & Licatesi, P.C.) still indexed and live on Facebook, creating a fragmented brand presence during the rebrand
  • The new domain given for this engagement (tllgllp.com) is not yet connected to the firm's real site, reviews, or brand equity
Blind Spot: Prospects who search the firm's new brand name directly may land on the unfinished "Launching Soon" placeholder instead of the fully built licatesilaw.com site — actively losing warm, brand-aware traffic during the rebrand window.
The Positive
  • licatesilaw.com is a modern, professionally designed site with 9 attorney bios, a testimonial video carousel, a case-results carousel, and dedicated geo-targeted landing pages for Corona/Queens, Brooklyn, Brooklyn Heights, Uniondale, and Nassau County
  • The site includes an active blog and clear above-the-fold calls to action ("Schedule Free Consultation," "Receive a call back") tied to a clickable phone number
The Gap
  • Running two live web properties under two different brand identities during a rebrand costs the firm consistency exactly when consistency matters most for search visibility
  • Without a completed PageSpeed check, it isn't yet known whether load speed is quietly costing the firm mobile visitors before they ever see the strong site behind it
  • An unlaunched new domain with no redirect strategy risks split link equity and confused prospects right as the new brand tries to establish itself
Consolidating one clean, fast, single-brand web presence is a foundational step toward the hands-off marketing engine Michael and Brianna are asking for.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The firm's own Google-sourced reviews page reports 4.8 stars across 291 reviews — solid, but genuinely behind at least two of its three most relevant named local competitors on volume
  • The firm does not surface in top organic results for "car accident lawyer Uniondale NY," "personal injury lawyer Uniondale NY," or "best personal injury lawyer Nassau County Long Island" — named competitors surface in its place
  • NAP inconsistency is real and measurable: at least three different phone numbers are in active circulation across directories (516-227-2662, 516-806-6384, 516-659-2058), plus a still-indexed legacy "Licatesi Law Group, LLC" listing at a different Garden City address
Blind Spot: Google weighs listing consistency heavily in local ranking — three different phone numbers across major directories may be actively suppressing the firm's own local pack visibility, not just confusing prospects who find them.
The Positive
  • A 4.8-star rating across 291 reviews is a genuinely strong trust signal — the foundation is there, it simply is not winning visibility against the market's top local pack results right now
The Gap
  • Every month the firm does not surface for its own core local searches is a month those searchers become another firm's client instead
  • Cleaning up NAP data and consolidating the rebrand is a one-time fix with a compounding, ongoing payoff in local visibility
Palermo Law, P.L.L.C. — surfaces prominently in Nassau County personal injury searches with 400+ reviews at a 5.0 rating and self-marketed "Long Island's Best Personal Injury Law Firm" positioning, directly occupying visibility Licatesi's own strong site and review base should be capturing.
Winning back local visibility means the firm's own reputation — not a rented vendor relationship — becomes the engine bringing in cases.
