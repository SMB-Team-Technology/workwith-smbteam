Here Is Where The Licatesi Law Group, LLP Stands Today

The Licatesi Law Group, LLP is a 40+ year, $100M+-recovered personal injury firm serving Nassau County and the wider Long Island / NYC metro area out of offices in Uniondale, Brooklyn, and Corona/Queens. The firm is mid-rebrand — from its prior name, Rubin & Licatesi, P.C., to its current name, and toward a not-yet-launched domain (tllgllp.com) — while its real operating site, licatesilaw.com, is modern and well-built with attorney bios, testimonials, and geo-targeted landing pages. The single most important finding: despite that strength, the firm does not surface in top organic search results for its own core local searches, and depends almost entirely on third-party lead-gen agencies (~$100k/month) rather than its own owned channels for new case volume.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

The Problem
  • The URL provided for this engagement, tllgllp.com, is not the firm's real site — it is a bare "Launching Soon" placeholder built on GoDaddy Website Builder, with no practice areas, no attorney bios, and no blog
  • PageSpeed scores could not be obtained this session (the PageSpeed Insights API returned a quota-exceeded error on every attempt) — this should be run manually on the real operating site, licatesilaw.com, before the call
  • The firm's real operating site, licatesilaw.com, is modern and well-built, but it sits alongside a prior brand (Rubin & Licatesi, P.C.) still indexed and live on Facebook, creating a fragmented brand presence during the rebrand
  • The new domain given for this engagement (tllgllp.com) is not yet connected to the firm's real site, reviews, or brand equity

The Positive

The Gap

Consolidating one clean, fast, single-brand web presence is a foundational step toward the hands-off marketing engine Michael and Brianna are asking for.
