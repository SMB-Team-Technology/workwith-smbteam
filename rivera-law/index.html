Here Is Where Rivera Law Stands Today

Jesus Rivera runs a bilingual Schaumburg, IL practice built on Family Law (55%), Immigration (35%), and Criminal Defense (20%), supported by an office manager, two paralegals, a legal assistant, and an IT contact. The firm is still recovering from a serious setback: an associate left in November and took clients with them, cutting the active file count from 900 to 688 and monthly revenue roughly in half — from about $100k to about $50k — and revenue has stayed volatile since. Rivera Law did not surface organically in any of nine local searches run across its three practice areas, while named local competitors in every one of them carry stronger review counts or reputational credentials.

Stage 6 is where the firm can absorb a shock like the one it just survived without the owner personally holding it together — and where the legacy Jesus wants to hand his daughter actually becomes durable.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • No contact form on the homepage — the site pushes phone and email only, with no low-friction way for a prospect to reach out after hours
  • PageSpeed scores could not be retrieved this pass (Google's public API returned a daily quota error on repeated attempts) — flagged to verify live before the proposal call rather than guessed at
  • No attorney bio, no video, and no blog on the site — nothing builds trust or credibility with a visitor beyond the homepage message
  • No geo-targeting pages exist for nearby cities like Hoffman Estates, Elgin, Palatine, or Arlington Heights, despite the firm serving that broader area
Blind Spot: Your homepage is entirely Spanish ("Bienvenido/a") with no English entry point — which is a real asset for your bilingual client base, but it also means any English-language searcher landing on your site sees no immediate confirmation you serve them too.
The Positive
  • The site is clean, professional, and clearly bilingual-first — a genuine reflection of the client base Rivera Law serves
  • A prominent above-the-fold phone number and free-consultation call to action make it easy for a ready-to-call visitor to take action
The Gap
  • Every visitor who isn't ready to call immediately — and doesn't want to fill out an email — has no way to convert, so those leads simply leave
  • Without attorney bios or credibility content, visitors comparing you against Francis Law Center or James G. Dimeas & Associates see far less reason to trust you before they ever pick up the phone
  • Missing geo pages mean the firm is invisible to searchers in surrounding towns who would otherwise become referral-quality clients
A website that converts around the clock is what lets you stop being the one who has to personally close every prospect who calls.
