Profit Plan Assessment

Rivera Law's monthly revenue has swung from roughly $100k down to $50k and back since losing an associate and a large share of its client base in November. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"remains volatile"
The Problem
  • Monthly revenue fell from roughly $100k to roughly $50k after the November associate departure, and has since had a "recent $100k month" but continues to swing rather than hold steady
  • The active client file count dropped from 900 to 688 in the same disruption — a direct hit to the base the firm's revenue is built on
  • The Access Legal referral channel converts at a strong 50% and is called the firm's "most profitable" source, but no comparable performance data exists for the firm's other channels
  • The stated near-term goal — restoring $1M in annual revenue — is a recovery target, not yet a confirmed current run rate
Blind Spot: Knowing that Access Legal is your "most profitable" source is a real insight — but without the same visibility into what Google Ads and word of mouth actually cost you per client, you can't yet tell which channel is truly worth scaling first.
The Gap
  • Without predictable revenue, Jesus cannot plan hiring, marketing investment, or his own income with any confidence
  • A firm that swings between $50k and $100k a month cannot reliably fund the systems needed to protect against another disruption like November's
  • The gap between today's volatile revenue and the $2M-$3M, $500k-personal-income vision is wide — and closing it requires more than recovering to $1M, it requires making that number predictable
Predictable profit — not just a good month here and there — is what actually lets you build the legacy you want to leave your daughter.
