Here Is Where Rivera Law Stands Today

Jesus Rivera runs a bilingual Schaumburg, IL practice built on Family Law (55%), Immigration (35%), and Criminal Defense (20%), supported by an office manager, two paralegals, a legal assistant, and an IT contact. The firm is still recovering from a serious setback: an associate left in November and took clients with them, cutting the active file count from 900 to 688 and monthly revenue roughly in half — from about $100k to about $50k — and revenue has stayed volatile since. Rivera Law did not surface organically in any of nine local searches run across its three practice areas, while named local competitors in every one of them carry stronger review counts or reputational credentials.

Stage 6 is where the firm can absorb a shock like the one it just survived without the owner personally holding it together — and where the legacy Jesus wants to hand his daughter actually becomes durable.

Where You Are Today

STAGE 4: SMALL BUSINESS MANAGER
  • Revenue is volatile — monthly income has swung from roughly $100k to $50k and back since a former associate left with a large share of the client base
  • Google Ads runs at just $125/week and is described as "less effective," while the Access Legal referral program remains the firm's most profitable lead source
  • No system exists to convert the firm's strong 4.7-star reputation into visibility — Rivera Law does not surface organically for family law, immigration, or criminal defense searches in its own market
  • Jesus is still the "Chief Everything Officer," personally the bottleneck on every consultation despite having a five-person staff in place

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
Rivera Law already has the staff and the reputation most firms spend years building — what's missing is a system that keeps revenue predictable and keeps Jesus out of the bottleneck seat, and that is exactly the gap this plan closes.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Rivera Law
Rivera Law is operating at Stage 4 — a Small Business Manager with a real team and a modest paid lead-gen system in place, but no reliable engine protecting the firm from the kind of shock it just experienced. The single biggest gap holding the firm back is visibility: with three named, well-reviewed competitors actively out-positioning Rivera Law in family law, immigration, and criminal defense, and a firm-wide organic absence across nine local searches, growth is being left almost entirely to referrals and a single $125-per-week ad budget. Until that changes, the near-term goal of restoring $1M in annual revenue — and the longer-term vision of a $2M-$3M firm Jesus can eventually hand to his daughter — will stay out of reach.
