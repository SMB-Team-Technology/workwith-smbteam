Here Is Where The Woods Law Firm Stands Today

Freddy Woods leads a statewide criminal defense practice in South Carolina — mainly DUI, domestic violence, and drug cases — with three staffed offices in Greer, Columbia, and Beaufort and, by his own estimate, about $2 million a year in criminal work. His close rate is strong, but lead flow is the constraint: his own ad tests showed that spending more did not bring in more cases, and on a DUI map centered on his Greer office the firm shows in only about 9% of the area versus 43–65% for the three top competitors.

Stage 6 is where the firm's growth is funded by its own cash flow — the "house money" you described — and it starts with getting more of the right people through the door right now.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • Lead flow is the stated bottleneck — Google LSA and PPC are self-managed at roughly $3,000 a month, and earlier tests at higher spend showed no lift
  • Referrals and co-counsel work still fill much of the pipeline, and personal injury and workers' comp come entirely from referrals
  • The website is a 2019 build with mostly statewide pages and no pages for Spartanburg, York, or Myrtle Beach — markets you named as the ones that matter most
  • As the lead trial attorney you are always in court, while the firm adds staff and expects more volume

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between chasing cash and playing with house money is a steady flow of the right cases — and building that flow is exactly what this plan is for.
