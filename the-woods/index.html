Here Is Where The Woods Law Firm Stands Today

Freddy Woods leads a statewide criminal defense practice in South Carolina — mainly DUI, domestic violence, and drug cases — with three staffed offices in Greer, Columbia, and Beaufort and, by his own estimate, about $2 million a year in criminal work. His close rate is strong, but lead flow is the constraint: his own ad tests showed that spending more did not bring in more cases, and on a DUI map centered on his Greer office the firm shows in only about 9% of the area versus 43–65% for the three top competitors.

Stage 6 is where the firm's growth is funded by its own cash flow — the "house money" you described — and it starts with getting more of the right people through the door right now.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • Lead flow is the stated bottleneck — Google LSA and PPC are self-managed at roughly $3,000 a month, and earlier tests at higher spend showed no lift
  • Referrals and co-counsel work still fill much of the pipeline, and personal injury and workers' comp come entirely from referrals
  • The website is a 2019 build with mostly statewide pages and no pages for Spartanburg, York, or Myrtle Beach — markets you named as the ones that matter most
  • As the lead trial attorney you are always in court, while the firm adds staff and expects more volume

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between chasing cash and playing with house money is a steady flow of the right cases — and building that flow is exactly what this plan is for.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Website Audit

"My site is trash from 2019, man."
The Problem
  • The live site is a 2019 build — the footer reads © 2019 and the newest blog post dates to September 2019
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 50/100 — Desktop 88/100
  • Most pages target all of South Carolina; the only city pages are Greenville, Columbia, and Beaufort — there are none for Spartanburg, York, or Myrtle Beach
  • The contact form sits lower on the homepage, the page title repeats the firm name twice, and there is no video
Blind Spot: A second domain (woodslaw-firm.com) appears in search results with similar "Why Choose the Woods Law Firm" content. It is unclear whether it is an old duplicate or another business — worth checking before the new site launches, because duplicate content can dilute rankings.
The Positive
  • A click-to-call button sits above the fold, so a mobile visitor can reach you in one tap
  • Strong trust signals are already in place — NACDL and ABA logos, an Expertise.com badge, and media logos — plus a base of about 31 pages to build on
The Gap
  • A mobile score of 50 means paid clicks land on a slower experience, which raises the cost of every signed case
  • With no Spartanburg, York, or Myrtle Beach pages, ad clicks in those counties land on statewide pages instead of a page that speaks to that market
  • A stale blog and no video give searchers and Google little evidence of a currently active firm
A site built to convert the clicks you already pay for is the fastest way to turn existing ad spend into signed cases.

Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

The Gap
  • At about $5,000 per case, every additional ad lead that goes unanswered or unsigned is a meaningful amount of lost fees
  • Without source-level tracking, the firm cannot tell which county or campaign is producing signed cases
  • Adding roughly 60 cases in flow puts more pressure on response time and follow-up than the firm has needed so far
