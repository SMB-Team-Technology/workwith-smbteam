Here Is Where Mason Law Stands Today

Mason Law is an estate planning firm — wills, trusts, probate and estate administration — based in Monument, Colorado, with a Castle Rock office that grew into a million-dollar branch in 15 months and a third office lease now signed in Woodland Park. With 855 five-star Google reviews on its main profile, marketing run in-house, and a stated goal of building a brand rather than a personal practice, the firm is strong in every market where it has an office — and absent from the Colorado Springs map results, where it does not yet have an address.

Stage 6 is where the firm is the brand and opens office after office without anything pointing back to Paul — exactly the firm he has said he wants to build.

Where You Are Today

Stage 4: Small Business Manager
  • Marketing runs in-house — Google Business Profiles, Local Services Ads, search ads and Meta seminar ads — led by a Director of Marketing who is building out a team
  • Two offices operate today, a third lease is signed in Woodland Park, and a fourth location is under consideration
  • Major decisions are still shared between Paul and one other leader rather than spread across a leadership layer
  • No after-hours call answering today, even though the firm's ads run around the clock

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a strong founder-led firm and a brand that runs without its founder is a leadership and systems gap, not a demand gap — and that is the gap this plan is built to close.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Mason Law
Mason Law is operating at Stage 4 — a Small Business Manager whose lead generation is already strong where it has offices, with in-house marketing, a client care team and a growing network of locations, but with key decisions still concentrated around Paul. The most urgent gap is the leadership and operating layer that lets every new office run the same way without depending on him, along with coverage for the calls the firm's around-the-clock ads already generate. That gap sits directly between where the firm is today and Paul's goal of building a brand — "I want to brand us" — across as many as ten locations.
