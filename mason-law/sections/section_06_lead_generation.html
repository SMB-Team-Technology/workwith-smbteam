Lead Generation

Mason Law already generates leads through Google Business Profiles, Local Services Ads, search ads, Meta seminar ads, a blog and in-person dinner seminars, all run in-house — and Paul describes the leads that come from Google and ads as the ones that close best. The research shows a firm that owns the local results wherever it has an office and nearly disappears the moment a search moves one city south.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • None of the 855 five-star Google reviews, and no client testimonials, appear on the pages we reviewed — the strongest trust signal the firm owns is invisible on its own website
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 26–31/100 — Desktop 79/100 (two mobile lab runs; load times measured far above Google's recommended range, though a lab runner is slower than a typical phone)
  • The contact form sits lower on the homepage under "Contact Mason Law to Start Your Estate Plan," not in the top section
  • No video appears on the homepage; the only embedded media is a Google Map
Blind Spot: Your ads run at all times, which means paid visitors arrive on a mobile page that is slow to load and shows none of your reviews. The ad click is paid for before the visitor ever sees the proof that makes Mason Law the obvious choice.
The Positive
  • The site is mobile responsive, with a clear "Schedule A Consultation" button and a tap-to-call phone number at the top of the page
  • Dedicated Monument, Castle Rock and Colorado Springs pages exist, each with will, trust and probate pages
  • The blog is active — the latest post is dated October 20, 2026 — and its posts also feed the firm's Google Business Profile updates
  • Four free dinner seminars are listed on the site, from October 28 through December 3, 2026
The Gap
  • Visitors from search and ads on a phone may leave before the page finishes loading, so spend and rankings never turn into consultations
  • Without reviews on the page, a visitor has to leave the site to find the reason to trust Mason Law
  • No page yet exists for the Woodland Park office, where the lease is already signed
A fast website that shows proof on every page carries the Mason Law brand on its own, without needing anyone's face on it.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The firm appears in 0 of 4 Colorado Springs searches tested (estate planning, living trust, wills and probate), even though its 855 reviews exceed every pack competitor, whose counts range from 60 to 303
  • In Castle Rock the firm reached the local pack for estate planning and trust searches (2 of 4) but not for wills or probate; the Castle Rock profile has about 100 reviews
  • The Castle Rock office lists (303) 656-2148 on Google but the Monument phone number, 719-428-4495, on its FindLaw listing
  • The FindLaw listing shows only one practice area and has no reviews
Blind Spot: The Colorado Springs gap appears to be about distance, not reputation. Google ranks local results by how close the searcher is to a business address, and the firm has no Colorado Springs address — so reviews alone will not close it.
The Positive
  • The main Monument profile holds 855 reviews at 5.0 stars and ranks #1 in all four Monument local-pack searches tested
  • Google Business Profile posts are active — updates appeared 2 hours, 2 days and 5 days before our check — and photos are in place
  • Name, address, phone and hours for the Monument office are consistent across the website, Google, FindLaw and Avvo
The Gap
  • Colorado Springs searchers for wills, trusts and probate are being shown Wiley Reynolds, Kevin R. Hancock and Bridget Rachel Grace instead of Mason Law
  • Castle Rock wills and probate searchers see Shannon J. Marino, Walters Law and Gubbels Law Office
  • Every new office starts with zero reviews on its own profile, so each opening needs its own local-search playbook
Wiley Reynolds PLLC — only 60 reviews at 5.0 stars, but it appears in the Colorado Springs local pack for all four searches tested and shows "Open 24 hours," because it has an address where searchers are.
Each new office is a new local market to win, and a repeatable local-search playbook is what lets the brand — not one person — open the next door.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Estate planning: a text ad appeared on "estate planning attorney monument co" with the headline "Mason Law & Planning Group - Monument Will Lawyer Services" and the description "Honest Pricing. No Hidden Fees. Wills & Trusts. Probate." It is location-specific and the pricing promise is strong, though the headline leads with wills rather than the full plan.
  • Trusts, wills and probate: the firm appeared through Local Services Ads only in our check; a text ad was not observed for these searches in this single-point-in-time render. The team confirmed the ads run around the clock, not just during open hours.
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Kevin R. Hancock, Drexler Law, Hammond Law Group and The Harris Law Firm — all hold Local Services Ads positions across the estate planning, trust and wills searches checked; no text ads from named local estate-planning competitors were observed.
  • Robinson & Henry, Peakstone Law Group and Solem Woodward & McKinley — hold probate Local Services Ads positions, competing for the same estate-administration searches.
The Opportunity
  • Colorado Springs and Castle Rock location pages already exist; text ads for trust and probate searches routed to them would reach searchers the map pack does not
  • Urgency terms such as "probate attorney after a death" capture people searching at the moment of need, including evenings and weekends
  • Neighboring communities — Woodmoor, Cañon City and Woodland Park — match your seminar towns and your newest office
  • Searches that include competitor names such as Hancock or The Harris Law Firm reach people already comparing firms

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • The firm appeared in every Local Services Ads block we saw (10 of 10), in positions #1 to #3; two Colorado Springs searches (estate planning and wills) did not show an LSA block at all
  • The Monument profile shows 855 reviews at 5.0 stars and the Castle Rock profile shows 97 at 5.0; Google Screened status could not be confirmed from the results page
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • The Law Office of Kevin R. Hancock — 303 reviews, 4.9 stars, in every block observed
  • The Harris Law Firm — 496 reviews, 4.4 stars, the #1 Castle Rock estate planning and wills slot
  • Hammond Law Group PC — 162 reviews, 4.8 stars; Drexler Law — 151 reviews, 4.5 stars, #1 in Colorado Springs for trusts
  • Evans Case — 125 reviews, 4.6 stars; Wetterow Law — 57 reviews, 5.0 stars
The Opportunity
  • The Monument profile's 855 five-star reviews put it ahead of every competitor seen in LSA, which is why the firm already holds top positions there
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • LSAs already put the firm at #2 for Colorado Springs probate searches where the map pack does not show the firm at all, making them the quickest way into a market where there is no local address yet
Blind Spot: If Local Services Ads are set to run outside office hours, calls placed at night reach no live person today, so the firm can pay for a lead it never gets to answer.

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • Two active static-image ads were found, both free dinner seminar promotions: one for the Old Post Office in Cañon City running since October 17, 2026, and one for an estate planning seminar ("Avoid probate / Protect your family / Preserve your assets / Gain peace of mind") running since October 19, 2026
  • Both ads are only days old, so the seminar-driven Meta funnel is just getting started; the count may miss ads published under a different Page name
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • The Law Office of Kevin R. Hancock — 2 active ads, both running since June 15, 2026 (about four months)
  • Peakstone Law Group — 12 active ads running since late June 2026, though the creative seen is criminal defense rather than estate planning
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Branded video ads — build trust before a prospect ever calls, with the firm's brand rather than any one attorney at the center
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Retargeting seminar registrants and attendees across all four October–December seminar dates keeps a warm audience in front of the firm until they book
