Lead Generation

Law Offices of Jason L. Abelove has built a strong reputation over nearly three decades in employment law — but with no paid advertising, no SEO strategy, and no predictable system for attracting new clients. That gap is now most visible in criminal defense, a brand-new practice area with no digital presence at all.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • No criminal defense page, practice area, or mention anywhere on the site — despite hiring an attorney specifically for this practice area
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile Not obtainable — Desktop Not obtainable (Google PageSpeed Insights returned a quota error in this research pass, retried twice; not fabricated)
  • Blog is stale — most recent posts dated 05.13.21 and 04.22.21, over five years old
  • Contact form sits mid-page under a "Set Up a Free Meeting" section, not visible above the fold
Blind Spot: The site has no geo-targeting landing pages for surrounding towns like Mineola, Hempstead, or Hicksville — meaning searches from residents just outside Garden City proper never find the firm at all.
The Positive
  • Three case results displayed on the homepage, ranging from $620,000 to $6,000,000 — a real credibility asset most firms would envy
  • A prominent attorney bio emphasizes nearly three decades of experience and personal accessibility
The Gap
  • Those seven-figure case results sit on the homepage doing nothing — no video, no dedicated page, no use in ads or social proof anywhere else
  • Every criminal defense search that reaches the site finds nothing to convert on, sending that demand straight to competitors
  • A stale, unoptimized site under-converts the traffic it does get from referrals and organic search
A modern site that actually sells both practice areas is the foundation of the sellable firm you are trying to build — right now it is quietly costing you cases.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Google Business Profile shows 26 total reviews — a number both you and Jonathan flagged as an immediate win to grow
  • For "criminal defense attorney Garden City NY," "criminal defense lawyer Nassau County," and "DWI defense attorney Garden City," the firm does not appear at all
  • A duplicate Yelp listing (two separate business pages for the same firm) is splitting review volume and local-SEO signal across two pages instead of one
Blind Spot: Your #3 ChatGPT ranking for "employment lawyer in Garden City" is being driven largely by a single 5-star Yelp review — a fragile, unmanaged asset rather than a deliberate strategy, and one the duplicate-listing issue puts at further risk.
The Positive
  • The firm does appear organically for employment law searches ("employment lawyer Garden City NY," "workplace discrimination attorney Garden City," "wrongful termination lawyer Nassau County") and every third-party review platform found shows uniformly positive client language
The Gap
  • Competitors in both practice areas are outpacing the firm's review volume and local visibility
  • Criminal defense generates zero local search visibility right now, while a major competitor sits one floor away
Barket Epstein Kearon Aldea & LoTurco, LLP — 9 Super Lawyers/Rising Stars attorneys and a "Distinguished" Martindale rating, operating out of the same building at 666 Old Country Rd, dominating the exact criminal defense searches your new hire needs to win.
Consolidating and growing your review presence is the fastest, lowest-cost way to protect the organic wins you already have while you build the system behind them.
