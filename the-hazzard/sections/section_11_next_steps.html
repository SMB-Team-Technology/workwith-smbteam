Speed Up the Mobile Experience
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
The site scores 99/100 on desktop but 67/100 on mobile, with the largest content taking 5.6 seconds to appear. People searching for a lawyer from their phone are the least patient visitors you have.
The Opportunity
Closing the gap between desktop and mobile speed means more of the visitors who already find the site stay long enough to call or submit a form.
Align Your Google Profile and Directory Listings
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
Yelp categorizes the firm under Divorce & Family Law while the website leads with criminal defense, and two different phone numbers are published. Mixed signals make it harder for Google to know which searches the firm should win.
Who Is Doing It Better
Revill Law Firm — named in Birmingham criminal defense "best of" roundups and promotes its Google reviews on its own website.
The Opportunity
A consistent name, category and phone number across every listing strengthens local rankings for the practice area the firm most wants to grow.
Put the Contact Form Where Visitors Look
Intake Quick Win
Why It Matters
The contact form lives in the footer, and two phone numbers are published. A visitor who is ready to reach out has to hunt for the easiest way to do it.
The Opportunity
A form above the fold and one tracked phone number make every inquiry easier to send, easier to answer and easier to measure.
Build a Steady Review Engine
Lead Gen Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Review volume and recency are among the strongest local ranking and trust signals, and they also drive how well a firm performs in Local Service Ads.
Who Is Doing It Better
The Harris Firm LLC — frequently named in Birmingham family law roundups and promotes a substantial online review profile.
The Opportunity
A simple, repeatable process for asking satisfied clients for reviews builds trust before a prospect ever picks up the phone.
Install Tracking and Retargeting
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
No Meta pixel appears in the homepage code. Unless it is loaded elsewhere, every visitor who leaves without calling disappears with no follow-up.
The Opportunity
Retargeting keeps the firm in front of people who already visited the site — especially valuable in family law and personal injury, where decisions take days or weeks.
Know Your Cost Per Lead and Per Case
Profit Quick Win
Why It Matters
No call-tracking tool was found on the site, so calls cannot be tied back to the marketing that produced them. Without that link, ad spend gets judged on activity instead of profit.
The Opportunity
Call tracking plus a simple cost-per-lead and cost-per-signed-case baseline shows which practice areas deserve the next marketing dollar.
Launch Paid Search and Local Service Ads
Lead Gen Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Paid search and Local Service Ads presence was not observed for criminal defense, family law or personal injury. These channels capture people who are searching for a lawyer right now.
The Opportunity
Targeted campaigns for high-intent Birmingham searches put the firm in front of ready-to-hire prospects in all three practice areas.
Measure Speed-to-Lead and After-Hours Coverage
Intake Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Response speed, after-hours coverage and follow-up were not discussed, so how many inquiries are lost before they become consultations is unknown.
The Opportunity
Measuring how fast and how consistently every inquiry is answered turns intake into the cheapest source of new clients the firm has.
Define Roles and Accountability
Team Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
In a firm built around a founding attorney's reputation, growth tends to pull more work toward the owner unless intake, consults and sales each have a clear owner.
The Opportunity
Written roles and a simple management rhythm let the team absorb more cases without more of your time.
