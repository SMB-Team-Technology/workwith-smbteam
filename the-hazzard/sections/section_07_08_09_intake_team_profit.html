Intake Assessment

Intake was not covered in detail on this call, so this assessment reflects what the firm's website shows a prospect on first contact. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • The contact form sits in the footer only, so beyond the phone number the next step is not obvious to a visitor ready to reach out
  • Two different phone numbers are published on the site, which can split calls, tracking and directory listings
  • No call-tracking tool was found on the site, so calls cannot be tied back to the marketing that produced them
  • Response speed, after-hours coverage and follow-up were not discussed, so how many inquiries are lost before they become consultations is unknown
Blind Spot: You may not realize how many people call after hours or while attorneys are in court, reach voicemail, and move on to the next firm on their list.
The Positive
  • A clear "Schedule Your Consultation" call to action with the phone number sits above the fold, so the path to a first call is easy to find
The Gap
  • When intake is not measured, leads lost between the first call and the signed agreement are invisible — and so is the revenue they represent
  • Without call tracking, it is impossible to tell which marketing sources produce signed cases and which only produce calls
  • Every marketing dollar added on top of an unmeasured intake process carries the same leak
Fixing intake means the marketing you pay for turns into signed cases instead of missed calls — so growth finally feels like progress.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • A "Schedule Your Consultation" button and phone number are visible above the fold on the homepage

Team Assessment

How the team is structured beneath the founding attorney was not covered in detail on this call, so this assessment reflects what the website shows. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • Who handles intake, consultations and sales day to day, and who manages them, was not covered — so the firm's ability to run without constant owner involvement cannot be confirmed yet
  • In a firm built around a founding attorney's name and reputation, growth tends to pull more work toward the owner unless roles and accountability are clearly defined
  • Serving three practice areas adds handoffs between attorneys and staff, which makes written processes and clear ownership more important as volume grows
The Positive
  • Three attorneys are profiled on the website, which gives the firm depth beyond its founder to build delegation on
The Gap
  • Without defined roles and a management layer, every new case and every decision lands back on the owner's desk
  • More leads without a team that can absorb them means more of the owner's time, not more of her freedom
  • Accountability that does not depend on the owner being in the room is what lets a firm grow without the owner working harder
A team that runs on defined roles and clear accountability is what gives you back control of your time.

Profit Plan Assessment

No revenue, margin or cost-per-case figures were shared on this call, so the profit picture is still a blank page. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • Current margins and the cost of winning a new client were not discussed, so there is no baseline to measure growth against
  • Without cost per lead and cost per signed case tracked by practice area, it is hard to know where the next marketing dollar earns the most
  • No call-tracking tool was found on the website, so marketing results cannot yet be tied to revenue
The Gap
  • Without a baseline, ad spend gets judged on activity — clicks and calls — instead of profit
  • A firm with three practice areas needs to know which ones deserve the next dollar before spending it
  • A simple profit plan turns marketing from an expense you hope works into an investment you can forecast
Knowing your numbers is how revenue becomes wealth you can plan around instead of hope for.
