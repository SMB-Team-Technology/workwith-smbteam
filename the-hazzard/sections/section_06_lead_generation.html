Lead Generation

The Hazzard Law Firm's website is fast on desktop, mobile-ready and credible, with attorney profiles and a clear consultation call to action. What this research could not confirm is where new cases come from today — paid search, Local Service Ads, Meta ads and map-pack visibility were all not observed for criminal defense, family law and personal injury.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 67/100 — Desktop 99/100. On mobile, the largest content takes 5.6 seconds to appear and the first content takes 4.6 seconds
  • The contact form sits in the footer — no form is visible above the fold, so a visitor ready to act has to scroll or call
  • The most recent blog post found is dated April 25, 2025 — roughly 18 months without fresh content
  • No video was detected on the homepage to put a face and a story behind the firm
The Positive
  • The site is mobile-responsive and loads in under a second on desktop
  • Dedicated profile pages for Wakisha Hazzard, Messiah Williams-Cole, Sr. and Phil Fikes build credibility with prospects researching the firm
  • A clear "Schedule Your Consultation" call to action with the phone number sits above the fold
  • The firm's blog highlights its appearance on A&E's acclaimed series — strong authority content to build on
The Gap
  • Google's benchmark for a good mobile load is 2.5 seconds — a 5.6-second load gives phone visitors time to go back and call the next firm
  • Hiding the contact form in the footer adds a step for the highest-intent visitors on the page
  • Eighteen months without new content tells both Google and prospects the site is not actively maintained
A website that loads fast, shows fresh authority and makes the next step obvious is the foundation for every other dollar you invest in growth.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The Google Business Profile could not be reviewed directly in this research, so its primary category, photos, posts and review recency still need to be confirmed
  • Local map-pack position was not observed for any of the searches run across criminal defense, family law and personal injury, so the firm's visibility on those searches is unconfirmed
  • Directory listings send mixed signals: Yelp categorizes the firm under Divorce & Family Law while the website leads with criminal defense, and two different phone numbers are published
Blind Spot: Google uses the consistency of your name, address, phone number and category across the web to decide who to trust for local searches. Two phone numbers and a mismatched category on a directory listing can quietly weaken rankings for the practice area you most want to grow.
The Positive
  • The firm is listed on Justia, FindLaw, Yelp and other directories, and its Birmingham office address is consistent across the sources reviewed
The Gap
  • Review volume and recency are among the strongest local ranking and trust signals, and several Birmingham competitors actively promote large review profiles
  • With three practice areas, each needs its own search visibility — a single profile category cannot carry criminal defense, family law and personal injury at once
Revill Law Firm — named in Birmingham criminal defense "best of" roundups and promotes its Google reviews on its own website, building trust before a prospect ever calls.
Showing up first when someone in Birmingham needs a lawyer is how the firm's reputation turns into the growth you are working toward.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Paid search ads for the firm were not observed on criminal defense, family law or personal injury searches in Birmingham — this is a limit of this research rather than a finding, and the firm's Google Ads account should be reviewed to confirm what is running
  • The site uses Google Tag Manager, but whether Google Ads conversion tracking is configured inside it was not checked
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Revill Law Firm (criminal defense) — ad copy was not captured; its homepage uses conversion-focused calls to action, which suggests a firm built to turn clicks into calls
  • The Harris Firm LLC (family law) — ad copy was not captured; frequently named in Birmingham family law roundups
  • Farris, Riley & Pitt, LLP (personal injury) — ad copy was not captured; personal injury is among the most heavily advertised categories in local search
The Opportunity
  • Criminal defense: "criminal defense lawyer Birmingham AL," "criminal defense attorney Birmingham" and "DUI lawyer Birmingham AL" — high-intent searches for the practice area your website leads with
  • Family law and personal injury: "divorce lawyer Birmingham AL," "child custody lawyer Birmingham," "personal injury lawyer Birmingham AL," "car accident lawyer Birmingham" and "truck accident lawyer Birmingham"
  • Urgency terms such as "arrested in Birmingham" or "criminal lawyer open now" reach people at the moment of crisis, when the first firm to answer usually wins the case
  • Surrounding-city searches across the Birmingham metro and searches on competitor firm names capture prospects who never type "Birmingham" or are comparing options

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Local Service Ads placement and Google Screened status were not observed for the firm in this research
  • Treat this as an opportunity gap to confirm — Local Service Ads are available for criminal defense, family law and personal injury, the firm's three practice areas
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Revill Law Firm — LSA placement and review count were not observed; worth checking live for criminal defense searches
  • The Harris Firm LLC — LSA placement and review count were not observed; worth checking live for family law searches
The Opportunity
  • The firm's Google reviews largely determine how it ranks among Local Service Ads, which makes steady review growth the fuel for this channel
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • With three practice areas, the firm can test LSAs in the area where each lead is most valuable and expand from there

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • Active Facebook and Instagram ads for the firm were not observed — the Meta Ad Library could not be queried in this research, so this is unconfirmed rather than a finding
  • No Meta pixel appears in the homepage code; unless it is loaded through Google Tag Manager (not checked), website visitors cannot be retargeted
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Revill Law Firm — Meta ad activity was not observed in this research
  • The Harris Firm LLC — Meta ad activity was not observed in this research
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Family law and personal injury decisions are considered over days or weeks, which makes retargeting and educational content on Facebook and Instagram a natural fit for those practice areas
