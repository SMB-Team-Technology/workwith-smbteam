Overall Growth Stage Assessment
The Hazzard Law Firm, LLC
The Hazzard Law Firm is placed at Stage 3 for now — the lower of the two stages the available evidence supports — and that placement will be refined as we learn more about lead sources, intake and team structure. The most visible gap is between a firm with real credibility (three attorney profiles and an A&E series feature highlighted on its own blog) and a digital presence that loads slowly on mobile, has not published a new blog post since April 2025, and shows no Meta pixel on its homepage. Closing that gap is the clearest path from a respected local firm toward one that grows predictably.
