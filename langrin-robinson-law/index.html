Here Is Where Langrin-Robertson Law Stands Today

Langrin-Robertson Law is an Atlanta-area personal injury firm led by attorney Hazel Langrin-Robertson, with an office in Snellville and a four-person team supported by a trusted operations helper. The firm has the credentials and a website already working for it, but the day-to-day runs on a small group still building its systems, with no standard procedures yet and a hiring search for a key case management role. The single most important finding is that the firm's time, not its demand, is the constraint, and the AI and team structure that could return that time have not been set up yet.

Stage 6 is the stage where the firm runs on trained people and clear systems instead of on your own six-day week, and it is exactly where we are taking you.

  • A small team of four, with a trusted helper stepping in on marketing and operations until the right staff are in place

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Website Audit

"Half the stuff they're putting out is wrong." — on a previous marketing vendor, from our discovery call
The Problem
  • No contact form appears above the fold; visitors have only the phone number and the free case evaluation button
  • There is no video on the homepage, so a prospect cannot see or hear the attorney before calling
  • The most recent blog post we found is dated February 21, 2025, about 20 months ago
  • PageSpeed scores are not reported here: the site's bot protection blocked our automated speed test, so this is an item to verify rather than a finding
The Positive
  • The site is live and appears to be mobile-friendly, with a clear "Free case evaluation" button and a prominent phone number
  • Attorney Hazel Langrin-Robertson's bio, her years of practice, and her Super Lawyers and National Trial Lawyers honors are on the site
  • City landing pages for Atlanta, Snellville, Lawrenceville, Loganville, Gwinnett County, and many more nearby cities are already in place
The Gap
  • Without a form or video above the fold, a visitor who is not ready to call has no easy next step
  • A blog that has gone quiet tells Google and prospects that the firm may be less active than it really is
  • The website is only as strong as the content and follow-up behind it, and that behind-the-scenes work is currently done by hand
The website should keep working for the firm while the team is busy with clients, not wait on someone to find spare hours.
