Intake Assessment

Intake at Langrin-Robertson Law runs through a small team and a lot of email and manual follow-up, with no written process described on our call. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
"If I can do some of that in her email so that she's not even having to look through email hunting and pecking for stuff, that's an hour, potentially two." — from our discovery call
The Problem
  • Email triage and admin can take an hour, potentially two, out of a day
  • A previous marketing vendor was cut off in part because calls reportedly were not getting through to the firm
  • No standard process for new-inquiry follow-up was described on the call
  • The team is described as not yet comfortable with AI, so tools that could speed up follow-up are not being used
Blind Spot: The website advertises free consultations around the clock, so a call or message that waits is a promise not kept, and the prospect is likely calling another firm in the meantime.
The Positive
  • The website puts a "Free case evaluation" button and the phone number at the top of the page, so prospects can find the way in quickly
  • A trusted helper is already working to set up AI tools for follow-up, calendar, and email triage before the team takes them over
The Gap
  • Follow-up that depends on one person's inbox can slip when that person is in court or out of the office
  • Without a written intake process, each new team member has to learn it from scratch
  • Time spent hunting through email is time not spent on cases
A defined intake process with AI-assisted follow-up means fewer calls and emails depend on you personally.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • No contact form was observed above the fold; the options are the "Free case evaluation" button and the phone number
  • No live chat was detected on the site
  • A "Free Consultation 24/7" banner is shown in the header, which sets an expectation of fast response at any hour

These findings are also referenced in the Website Audit section.

Team Assessment

Langrin-Robertson Law runs with four people, and a trusted helper is stepping in on marketing and operations until the right staff are in place. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: RED
"I'll tell you right now, she works six days a week." — from our discovery call
The Problem
  • The owner's schedule is heavy, and the firm leans on an interim helper rather than on a settled team structure
  • There are no standard operating procedures yet, so each new hire risks rebuilding the process "three, four, or five different times"
  • Finding and keeping the right people, especially for the case manager role, has been difficult, and the owner is cautious about hiring full-time
  • The staff are described as not a technical group and not yet familiar with AI
The Positive
  • Someone close to the firm is investing time to put systems in place before the next hires arrive
  • The firm has already started an AI Workforce Pro trial and registered for the AI Growth Summit, so the first step is already taken
The Gap
  • Without written procedures, a hire's success depends on who trains them rather than on the firm's own system
  • Without role clarity, accountability, and a hiring plan, the owner remains the person everything routes back to
  • Interim help is a bridge, not a structure, and it will not hold once the helper steps back
The goal is a team, human and AI, that carries the day-to-day so you can run a firm that does not depend on your six-day week.

Profit Plan Assessment

Revenue, margins, and profit targets did not come up on our call, so there is no baseline to plan against yet. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"I need to know how many different things that I need to get her out of that this can replace." — from our discovery call
The Problem
  • No profit target, margin goal, or cost-per-case figure was described, so growth is not yet tied to a number
  • Project management, social scheduling, email triage, and SOPs were all discussed as separate jobs, and the tools used for each today were not mapped on the call
  • Hiring is the largest cost decision ahead, and it is made harder by turnover and the lack of written procedures
The Positive
  • The firm cut off a $2,000-per-month marketing vendor that was not delivering, which shows attention to where money goes
  • The question of how many tools one platform can replace is already being asked, which is the right starting point for a cost plan
The Gap
  • Without a profit plan, it is hard to know which tools, hires, and services are worth paying for
  • Piecemeal tools can add cost without adding capacity
  • Time spent on repeatable admin is time not spent on casework that earns revenue
The aim is for added capacity to show up in your take-home and your calendar, not in added overhead.
