Lead Generation

Langrin-Robertson Law has a live website with a free case evaluation button, a bio built on more than 22 years of combined legal experience, and landing pages for dozens of Atlanta-area cities. Where new clients come from today was not covered on our call, and the blog has not been updated since early 2025, so it is not yet clear what is feeding the pipeline behind the website.

Website Audit

"Half the stuff they're putting out is wrong." — on a previous marketing vendor, from our discovery call
The Problem
  • No contact form appears above the fold; visitors have only the phone number and the free case evaluation button
  • There is no video on the homepage, so a prospect cannot see or hear the attorney before calling
  • The most recent blog post we found is dated February 21, 2025, about 20 months ago
  • PageSpeed scores are not reported here: the site's bot protection blocked our automated speed test, so this is an item to verify rather than a finding
The Positive
  • The site is live and appears to be mobile-friendly, with a clear "Free case evaluation" button and a prominent phone number
  • Attorney Hazel Langrin-Robertson's bio, her years of practice, and her Super Lawyers and National Trial Lawyers honors are on the site
  • City landing pages for Atlanta, Snellville, Lawrenceville, Loganville, Gwinnett County, and many more nearby cities are already in place
The Gap
  • Without a form or video above the fold, a visitor who is not ready to call has no easy next step
  • A blog that has gone quiet tells Google and prospects that the firm may be less active than it really is
  • The website is only as strong as the content and follow-up behind it, and that behind-the-scenes work is currently done by hand
The website should keep working for the firm while the team is busy with clients, not wait on someone to find spare hours.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Directory listings disagree with each other: one lists the firm in Johns Creek while the website, Avvo, and Yelp say Snellville, and the street address appears in different formats
  • Experience claims differ across sources: the website says over 22 years of combined legal experience, while one directory says 9 years in practice
  • Google review volume could not be confirmed directly, but ranking articles credit several metro Atlanta personal injury firms with far more reviews than this firm appears to have
  • Google map results for searches such as "Atlanta personal injury attorney" and "Snellville car accident lawyer" could not be loaded in this audit, so no claim is made about where the firm appears
Blind Spot: When a prospect, or an AI search tool, compares your site with a directory and sees two different experience claims and two different cities, it can quietly lower trust before they ever call.
The Positive
  • The firm has profiles on Avvo, Yelp, Facebook, and several legal directories, plus a Google Business Profile linked from the website
  • Dozens of local landing pages give Google many places to match the firm to nearby searches
The Gap
  • Inconsistent location and experience details make it harder for Google to trust which listing is the real firm
  • Fewer reviews than the firms near the top of local rankings gives prospects less proof to compare when choosing between attorneys
Griffin Law Firm, P.C. — a Snellville firm that runs dedicated Snellville injury pages and cites "Best of Gwinnett" recognition from 2021 and 2022, competing for the same local searches as your office.
A consistent local presence helps the firm get found without anyone on the team having to chase it.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Google ads for personal injury searches such as "Atlanta personal injury attorney," "Snellville car accident lawyer," and "Gwinnett County truck accident lawyer" could not be checked in this audit, so none were observed for the firm
  • Ads did not come up on the discovery call, and the firm's focus there was AI and operations rather than marketing
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Monge & Associates (Atlanta) — ad activity was not observed in this audit
  • Georgia Injury Advocates (Snellville / Gwinnett) and Griffin Law Firm, P.C. (Snellville) — ad activity was also not observed
The Opportunity
  • Paid search is not part of the plan recommended in this report, so this is a future option rather than a current priority
  • High-intent searches by case type, such as car, truck, motorcycle, and rideshare accidents, reach people who are looking for a lawyer right now
  • Local terms for Snellville, Lawrenceville, and Gwinnett County match the office's home market

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Local Service Ads for the firm were not observed, and no Google Screened badge was confirmed
  • Because Google results could not be loaded in this audit, this is a gap to evaluate rather than a confirmed absence
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Monge & Associates, Georgia Injury Advocates, and Griffin Law Firm, P.C. — LSA placement and review counts were not observed in this audit
  • No competitor LSA review counts are cited because none could be verified
The Opportunity
  • Where Google offers LSAs for the firm's practice area, a Google Screened badge adds a trust signal in the most prominent spot on the results page
  • LSAs are priced per lead, so you pay when a real prospect contacts you directly
  • Building Google reviews first would position the firm well for LSA, since review volume and rating influence placement
