Lead Generation

The Law Office of Matthew K. Tannenbaum has a working marketing engine behind a selective landlord-tenant and real estate practice, and the owner says he is not short of leads. The opportunity is not more volume — it is protecting what is working, deepening visibility for the matters he wants most, and keeping the calls that reach him high quality.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The homepage headline reads "Real Estate Attorney in Nassau County," and the page title in search results reads "Garden City Real Estate Attorney" — yet the owner describes landlord-tenant work as the bread and butter of the practice
  • The site lists seven practice areas, including probate, estate planning and traffic violations, which spreads the message across matters the owner is not focused on growing
  • No video was found on the homepage, so prospects have no way to see or hear the attorney before they call
  • No city-specific landing pages were found for surrounding communities; Nassau County, Suffolk County and Long Island are covered only in general copy
Blind Spot: A landlord searching for an eviction attorney may skim past a site that leads with "real estate" and never see that landlord-tenant is where this firm is strongest.
The Positive
  • The site is mobile responsive and carries a clear "Schedule Your free Consultation" button along with a consultation form on the homepage
  • An attorney bio is in place and the blog is active, with a recent post on how a landlord can legally screen tenants in New York
The Gap
  • Landlord-tenant, the firm's core, is not the first thing a visitor or a search engine sees
  • Without location pages, the firm is not built to rank for searches from nearby towns across Nassau and Suffolk
  • Without video, the high-service, boutique positioning is described in words rather than shown
A website that speaks directly to landlords brings in the right matters and keeps the wrong ones away — so your time goes to the work you actually want.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Map-pack positions for "landlord tenant attorney Garden City NY," "eviction lawyer Nassau County" and "real estate attorney Garden City NY" were not measured in this review; a separate Maps visibility audit is being sent to show where the firm appears
  • The Google Business Profile could not be inspected directly, so category selection, photo activity and post activity are not confirmed and should be checked
  • Nearby firms such as Gregory T. Levy, PC and Weidenbaum & Harari are active in the same searches and keep building their own review profiles
The Positive
  • The firm's review profile looks strong next to the named local competitors reviewed, and listings are in place on Avvo, Martindale, Lawyers.com and Yelp
The Gap
  • A strong review base only pays off if it keeps growing, because competitors in this market are adding reviews too
  • Without location pages and a verified, fully optimized profile, the firm may be leaving map visibility on the table for the landlord-tenant searches that matter most
Gregory T. Levy, PC — a Garden City real estate firm with strong directory ratings and its own review profile, positioned directly against this firm for real estate searches in the same town.
Staying visible on Google Maps keeps the right calls coming in on their own, without adding a single hour to your week.
