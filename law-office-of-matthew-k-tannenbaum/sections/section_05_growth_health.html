Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • The owner reports the marketing is working and that he is not short of leads
  • The firm's review profile looks strong next to the named local competitors reviewed
  • Lead flow appears to depend on one working channel, so adding depth over time protects it against seasonal slow stretches
Steady lead flow is what lets you stay selective about the cases you take instead of chasing volume.

Intake

  • An answering service screens calls so most unwanted matters never reach the owner
  • The website offers a free consultation, but the handling of qualified leads after the first screen was not discussed and appears to rest with the owner
  • With no team described beyond the answering service, tenant-side inquiries are turned away rather than converted
Every call handled well without you is time you do not have to spend at your desk.

Self-Managing Team

  • Solo practice with no described team, so drafting, research and case work all land on the owner
  • The owner worked almost every Saturday until this year and has said there is not the manpower to take on more work
  • AI is already taking on drafting, which is the first step toward a practice that carries more of the workload on its own
A practice that carries the workload is what turns your Saturdays back into your own time.

Profit

  • The firm is positioned as high service at a premium, not high volume, which supports strong revenue per matter
  • Revenue, margins and profit targets were not discussed, so the numbers behind the model are not yet visible in this review
  • Capacity, not demand, is the ceiling on revenue today, so freeing owner hours is the most direct lever on profit
The goal is a boutique practice where profit is planned and the owner is not the bottleneck.
