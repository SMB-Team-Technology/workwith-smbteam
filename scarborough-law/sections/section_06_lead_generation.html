Lead Generation

Scarborough Law has grown in its first year through networking lunches, LinkedIn outreach, and strong referral partners, with Google Ads and Local Service Ads producing early clients before going quiet. What the firm does not yet have is a measured, always-on channel that brings in high-net-worth estate planning and probate clients without depending on Xavier's calendar.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The site was rebuilt very recently with ChatGPT, so it is still new and unproven — no client testimonials, reviews, or schema markup were identified on it
  • The domain, ScarboroughFamilyLawyer.com, reads as family law while the practice is estate planning and probate
  • No dedicated special needs trust page, and no pages aimed at Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands, or St. Louis
  • No video of the attorney and no live chat identified — the first impression is text only
Blind Spot: A name and domain that suggest family law can pull in the wrong searches and the wrong expectations — and the contact email uses a different domain (scarboroughpllc.com) than the website, which splits the firm's identity in two places.
The Positive
  • A clear attorney bio with Georgetown Law, U.S. Army service, and Texas and Missouri licenses builds credibility with high-net-worth families
  • A clear call to action (“Tell us how we can help”) and a dedicated intake page, plus a blog with Texas-specific trust and probate topics
The Gap
  • Paid traffic that lands on a site without trust signals or matching pages converts less of the money spent on ads
  • Special needs trusts are one of Xavier's services, yet searchers for that topic have no page to land on
  • Suburban Houston families searching close to home have no location-specific page to find
A site that matches the high-net-worth families you want to serve is what lets Google Ads turn clicks into the six to eight clients a month you are aiming for.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Xavier told us people have left reviews on his Google profile, but the review count and rating were not verified for this audit
  • Local map pack placement for “estate planning attorney Houston,” “living trust attorney Houston,” and “special needs trust attorney Houston” was not confirmed
  • The same applies to probate: “probate attorney Houston” and “probate lawyer Houston TX” placement was not confirmed, and no Avvo, Justia, or FindLaw profile was found for the firm
The Positive
  • The State Bar of Texas lists Xavier under Wills-Trusts-Probate, Elder Law, and Taxation, giving the firm a verified authoritative listing to build on
The Gap
  • Established Houston firms show visible review bases on public profiles; a newer firm without one loses side-by-side comparisons before a call is ever made
  • No geographic landing pages means no organic entry point for Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands, or Missouri searches
Holmes Law, PLLC — appears to have built a deep base of client reviews on public profiles, which is the social proof Houston families see first when comparing estate planning and probate firms.
Local visibility is what lets the firm grow from referrals into a name families search for — the foundation of building a high-revenue firm fast.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Xavier told us he started with Google Ads, that early efforts brought in “really good clients,” and that he later moved to Local Service Ads — he now wants to refocus on Google Ads as the highest return on his time
  • Live search ads for estate planning and probate in Houston were not observed during this audit, and no conversion tracking from ads to signed clients is in place — this firm may not be configured to show for these terms or may be getting outbid
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Holmes Law, PLLC and Heights Law Group — established Houston estate planning and probate firms most likely competing for the same searches; their live ad messaging was not observed in this audit
  • Mike Massey Law — a Houston estate planning practice to monitor for ad activity on trust and will searches
The Opportunity
  • Estate planning: “estate planning attorney Houston” and “living trust attorney Houston” — high-intent searches from families ready to hire
  • Probate: “probate attorney Houston” and “probate lawyer Houston TX” — searches driven by the emotional pressure Xavier himself described as the better clientele for ads
  • “Special needs trust attorney Houston” and educational searches such as “how to probate a will in Texas” — lower-competition entry points that need matching pages
  • Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands, and Missouri searches — neighboring markets where Xavier is licensed and where no firm-specific page exists yet
Blind Spot: Without tracking each call and form back to the campaign that produced it, it is impossible to tell whether an ad channel is failing or its leads are simply not being counted — and Xavier told us he has not been tracking conversion.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Xavier told us his Local Service Ads are set at an average weekly budget of $444 with a $2,000 monthly max, and that the dashboard showed zero leads in the most recent seven days
  • He described the early weeks as producing leads (“it just tailed off”) and said he cannot figure out why; Google Screened status and LSA review count were not verified in this audit
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Holmes Law, PLLC — LSA positions for estate planning and probate in Houston were not observed; review volume is one of the factors Google weighs when ranking LSA listings, and this firm appears to carry a deep review base
  • Heights Law Group — another Houston firm with a visible review presence to watch in the LSA results
The Opportunity
  • An LSA account review can pinpoint whether the drop-off comes from service-area setup, profile strength, budget pacing, or how calls and messages are answered
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Building review volume and Google Screened status on the profile strengthens both LSA ranking and the local map pack at the same time

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • Xavier did not mention running Facebook or Instagram ads, and active Meta ads for the firm were not observed in this audit
  • The firm has marketed through a Wine and Wills event and postcards instead — the event produced two clients on roughly $1,500 of marketing
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Holmes Law, PLLC and Heights Law Group — Meta ad activity for these Houston firms was not observed in this audit
  • Mike Massey Law — ad activity was not observed; an open channel to monitor
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Promote events like Wine and Wills to retargeted site visitors — Xavier said the event is reproducible, and ads can fill the room instead of postcards
