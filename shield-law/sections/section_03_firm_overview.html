Here Is Where Shield Law Stands Today

Shield Law is a Quincy, Massachusetts firm founded by attorney Nick Yousif, built on bankruptcy and consumer protection casework generated almost entirely through organic search tied to the firm's former Brockton office. An April 2026 move from Brockton to Quincy severed that pipeline overnight — organic clicks fell roughly 91% and new client signings were cut in half — leaving Nick working seven-day weeks plus a second job in real estate just to keep the firm's income steady while the pipeline is rebuilt.

Stage 6 is the version of Shield Law where the phone rings without a marketing crisis causing it, and Nick works because he wants to — not because a second job is the only thing keeping the lights on.

Where You Are Today

STAGE 4: SMALL BUSINESS MANAGER
  • Organic search once delivered ~124 clicks and roughly 30 new clients a month — until the April 2026 move from Brockton to Quincy severed that pipeline overnight
  • Every review, directory listing, and phone number tied to those results is still anchored to the old Brockton address — none of it has followed the firm to Quincy
  • The firm's only active paid channel is a $1,500/mo Google Ads campaign through an outside agency — a budget too small to replace what was lost
  • Nick personally handles every intake call, follow-up, and client communication himself, with no delegation in place even as new client volume has been cut in half

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose

Closing that gap starts with rebuilding the lead flow Shield Law lost — not by chance this time, but by design — so Nick can stop trading a second job for the income his firm should already be producing.
