Lead Generation

Quesada Law Firm has built a strong 20-year reputation entirely on referrals, with no paid advertising, no SEO strategy, and no predictable system for attracting new clients beyond word of mouth. That reliance has held revenue flat at roughly $700,000/year for three straight years while named competitors with far larger review bases dominate the exact searches this firm needs to win.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The firm's blog has not been updated since a single post in September 2019 — nearly 7 years of dormant content signals to both visitors and search engines that the site is not actively maintained
  • PageSpeed scores were not able to be measured during this research pass and should be tested directly — page speed materially affects both search ranking and how many visitors stay long enough to convert
  • The contact form is buried near the bottom of the homepage rather than prominent above the fold, adding friction for a visitor ready to reach out
  • The firm's secondary domain, criminaldefensemiamilawyer.com, is currently dead — anyone clicking an old link or directory listing pointing to it hits a dead page instead of the firm
Blind Spot: The dead criminaldefensemiamilawyer.com domain may still be referenced in old directory listings or backlinks — every one of those is a potential client hitting a wall instead of reaching the firm.
The Positive
  • The site includes a clear "About Attorney Quesada" bio highlighting 20+ years of criminal defense experience, and a strong above-the-fold headline and free consultation call to action with the phone number prominently displayed
The Gap
  • A dormant blog and unverified page speed mean the site is likely losing visitors before they ever see the strong attorney credibility it does have
  • A buried contact form means visitors who are ready to act have to work harder than they should to reach out
  • The dead secondary domain represents lost inquiries that never reach the firm at all
A website that works around the clock to capture and qualify leads is a foundational piece of the "well-oiled machine" Marco described wanting to build.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Google Business Profile shows approximately 36 reviews at 5.0 stars — far below the 306 and 203 reviews held by the firm's two closest named competitors
  • The firm does not appear in top organic results for "criminal defense lawyer Miami," "felony defense attorney Miami," or "criminal defense attorney Miami-Dade"
  • The firm also does not surface for "DUI lawyer Miami," "DUI attorney Miami-Dade," or "drunk driving defense lawyer Miami" — the core searches for its second practice area
Blind Spot: The site's meta title references "Broward County Drug Crime Attorney," implying service across greater South Florida, but there is no dedicated Broward or Fort Lauderdale landing page to back that claim — a missed opportunity to actually rank for that broader service area.
The Positive
  • The firm's 5.0-star rating is higher than every named competitor identified, and NAP (name, address, phone) is consistent across all six directories checked — a clean foundation to build visibility on
The Gap
  • Every month spent invisible in these searches is a month of prospective clients finding Mirer, Hernandez, or Wichman first
  • Without location-specific landing pages, the firm cannot capture the broader South Florida service area it already claims to serve
Law Office of Michael Mirer, P.A. — 306 reviews and a former Miami-Dade prosecutor positioning that consistently surfaces at the top of organic results where Quesada Law Firm does not appear at all.
A firm with a perfect 5.0 rating deserves to be found — closing this visibility gap is what turns Marco's two decades of reputation into a steady stream of new clients rather than a well-kept secret.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Quesada Law Firm does not appear to be running Google Search ads for criminal defense or DUI defense terms in Miami — consistent with the transcript, which confirms the firm has never run any paid marketing
  • This means every single lead the firm generates today comes through referrals alone — a fully untapped channel sits open
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Law Office of Michael Mirer, P.A. — established market presence and review volume that positions the firm well for aggressive paid search bidding, though specific live ad copy could not be confirmed with the tools available in this research pass
  • Law Offices of Robert S. Reiff, P.A. — Best Lawyers "DUI/DWI" recognition gives this competitor strong ad messaging potential in the DUI defense space specifically
The Opportunity
  • "criminal defense lawyer Miami" and "DUI lawyer Miami" — the two highest-intent, highest-volume keywords the firm is currently invisible for
  • "felony defense attorney Miami" and "Miami-Dade DUI attorney" — case-type-specific searches matching the firm's exact practice focus
  • Competitor name bidding — capturing prospects actively searching for Mirer, Hernandez, or Reiff by name
  • Spanish-language campaigns — Marco is bilingual and the website content reflects this, representing an audience segment not yet targeted with paid search

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Quesada Law Firm does not appear to be running Local Service Ads or carrying a Google Screened badge at this time
  • This is an open opportunity gap rather than a confirmed permanent absence — LSA and Google Screened badges are common among the well-established competitors in this market
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Law Office of Michael Mirer, P.A. — 306 reviews at 4.9 stars, a review and credential profile well suited to strong LSA placement
  • Law Office of Armando J. Hernandez, P.A. — 203 reviews at 4.9 stars with National Trial Lawyers Top 100 recognition, another strong LSA-ready profile
The Opportunity
  • With a 5.0-star rating already higher than every named competitor, Quesada Law Firm is well positioned to qualify for LSA once enrolled
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Pay-per-lead pricing fits naturally alongside the firm's current no-paid-marketing-history starting point, since it ties spend directly to actual inbound calls rather than clicks

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence

  • Direct verification of the Meta Ads Library was not accessible with the tools available in this research pass, so this should be treated as an open opportunity gap
Competitor Ad Landscape

The Opportunity

  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
