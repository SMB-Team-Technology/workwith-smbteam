Here Is Where Quesada Law Firm Stands Today

Marco Quesada has built Quesada Law Firm in Miami on 20+ years of criminal defense experience, handling high-value felonies and quick-turnaround DUI cases out of a lean three-person practice — himself, one assistant, and one investigator. The firm has held steady at roughly $700,000 a year for three years, built entirely on referrals, with zero paid marketing and zero visibility in the organic searches where three named competitors with 3 to 10 times its review volume dominate the market.

Stage 6 is the stage where the "well-oiled machine" Marco described becomes real — a firm that operates without his daily involvement, freeing him to spend more time outside the practice and, eventually, to transition out of law on his own terms.

Where You Are Today

STAGE 3: SOLO PRACTITIONER
  • 100% of new clients come from referrals — no scalable lead generation system exists
  • Marco personally handles intake alongside casework, with no delegation in place
  • Revenue has plateaued at approximately $700,000/year for three straight years
  • No paid advertising has ever been run, despite three named competitors actively out-ranking the firm online

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
Closing the gap between a referral-only solo practice and a firm that runs itself starts with building the capacity to delegate — and that is exactly the plan Marco has already begun laying out.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Quesada Law Firm
Marco is operating at Stage 3 — a Solo Practitioner whose 20 years of courtroom credibility have never been matched by a system to generate new clients beyond word of mouth. The single most urgent gap is that the firm has zero scalable lead-generation channel: it is entirely dependent on referrals in a market where Law Office of Michael Mirer (306 reviews) and Law Office of Armando J. Hernandez (203 reviews) dominate the exact searches where Quesada Law Firm does not appear at all. Until intake is delegated away from Marco and a predictable lead system exists, the firm's stated goal of breaking $1,000,000 in revenue — and Marco's deeper goal of stepping back from daily involvement — will remain out of reach.
