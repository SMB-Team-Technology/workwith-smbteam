Intake Assessment

Marco personally handles intake for every incoming referral alongside his own casework, with no delegation currently in place. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating. This is where the biggest immediate ROI lives.

Status: RED
The Problem
  • You personally handle every intake call yourself, alongside a full caseload of high-value felonies and DUI defense work
  • No delegation currently exists — the firm's own stated first priority is to build the capacity to delegate this away from you
  • Your contact form is buried near the bottom of the homepage rather than visible immediately when a prospect lands on the site
  • With only one assistant and one investigator, there is no dedicated intake role to absorb this work today
Blind Spot: Because referrals already trust you before they call, it may feel like intake is not costing you anything — but every minute you personally spend on intake is a minute not spent on casework or, more importantly, not spent stepping back from the firm.
The Positive
  • Referral clients typically arrive pre-qualified and already trusting the firm, which means the intake conversations themselves tend to be higher-quality even without a formal process
  • You have already identified intake delegation as the necessary first step before adding marketing or automation — a clear-eyed, correctly sequenced plan
The Gap
  • Every hour you spend personally on intake is an hour not spent on higher-value casework or building the systems that would let the firm run without you
  • As soon as lead volume increases through marketing, personally handling intake will not scale — the bottleneck will only get worse
  • Without a defined intake process, there is no way to measure how many referrals actually convert into signed cases
Delegating intake is the single clearest first step toward the "well-oiled machine" you described — a firm that does not depend on you personally to turn an inquiry into a client.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • The homepage contact form is located near the bottom of the page in a "Get Strong Representation Today" section rather than immediately visible above the fold
  • A prospect must scroll past the full homepage or navigate to a separate "Contact Us" page to reach the form
  • The phone number is prominently displayed above the fold, which likely accounts for most current inbound contact rather than the form itself

Team Assessment

Quesada Law Firm runs on a lean team of three — Marco, one assistant, and one investigator — with Marco still directly involved in nearly every part of the operation. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: AMBER
"Build a 'well-oiled machine' that can operate without the owner's daily involvement, enabling him to eventually transition out of law."
The Problem
  • The team consists of only three people — you, one assistant, and one investigator — with no additional management or delegation layer
  • You have stated a clear preference to scale through AI automation and agentic workflows rather than by growing headcount significantly
  • No documented process currently exists for intake, case follow-up, or handoff beyond what you manage directly
  • You are already personally building your own automation connecting tools like Dropbox and Manus — valuable initiative, but it is one more thing currently running through you alone
Blind Spot: Because you are already comfortable being hands-on with both casework and the technology side, it is easy to underestimate how much of the firm's daily function still depends entirely on you being available.
The Positive
  • You have explicitly asked for a Fractional CTO to lead your AI automation effort rather than trying to build and maintain it entirely solo indefinitely — a sign you are ready to bring in outside leadership rather than carry everything yourself
  • A three-person team is lean enough that new systems and delegation can be introduced quickly, without untangling years of bureaucracy first
The Gap
  • Every day the firm depends on your direct involvement is a day further from the personal time and presence outside the firm you said you want
  • Without a self-managing structure, growth in revenue will simply mean more hours for you, not more freedom
  • Your eventual goal of transitioning out of law entirely is not possible while the firm's daily operations still run through you
A firm that can operate without your daily involvement is not just a nice-to-have — it is the specific outcome you described wanting, and it is only possible once delegation and automation replace your personal presence in day-to-day operations.

Profit Plan Assessment

Revenue has held at approximately $700,000/year for three consecutive years, with a clearly stated goal of breaking the $1,000,000 mark. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"a plateau maintained for the last three years"
The Problem
  • Revenue has been stuck at roughly $700,000/year for three straight years, with no new system introduced to change that trajectory
  • No profitability claims were made on the call, so per-case or per-practice-area margins are currently unknown
  • A referral-only client acquisition model creates inherent revenue unpredictability from month to month
  • There is a clearly stated revenue goal ($1,000,000) but no current plan connecting today's operations to that number
Blind Spot: A three-year plateau usually is not a sign that growth is impossible — it is a sign that the current model has reached the ceiling of what referrals alone can produce, and a different lever is needed to move past it.
The Positive
  • A three-year revenue plateau at $700,000 shows real stability and a proven ability to sustain a caseload — the firm is not struggling, it is simply capped by its current lead source
The Gap
  • Without a new lead source, the firm is likely to remain at the same $700,000 plateau indefinitely, regardless of effort
  • Ad-driven revenue alone is unlikely to fully close the gap to $1,000,000 since referrals will remain part of the mix — reaching the full goal will likely require growth across multiple channels together
  • Without financial visibility into which case types are most profitable, the firm cannot yet target its growth efforts efficiently
Breaking through a three-year plateau is what turns "the firm makes money" into "the firm makes money without needing more of my time" — the financial half of the freedom Marco is building toward.
