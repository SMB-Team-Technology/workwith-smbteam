Intake Assessment

Dorcey Law Firm's attorneys close a high share of the inquiries that reach them, and Josh confirmed the firm has room to handle more leads, intakes and consultations right now. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
"our typical close rate of our attorneys in our office range between 70, 90%."
The Problem
  • Josh said there is "no way to ever identify exactly where those people come from," so intake cannot report which channels produce signed clients
  • Leads generated cold through social media close at a lower rate than referrals, so growing online lead volume will test intake harder than referral volume does
  • Because attorneys close consultations themselves, intake results depend on attorney availability during the January–May peak
  • No live chat was detected on the homepage or contact page, so a visitor who wants an answer outside office hours has only a form and a wait
Blind Spot: A 70–90% close rate is largely a warm-referral story, with financial-advisor and CPA referrals closing closer to 90%. Online leads arrive colder and will convert lower, so intake should be measured separately by lead source from the first day of any new campaign.
The Positive
  • Attorney close rates of 70–90% mean the firm turns most qualified conversations into clients
  • The firm already follows up with a cultivation video, and Josh confirmed the team can take on more leads, intakes, consultations and casework today
The Gap
  • Without lead-source tracking, the firm cannot tell which intake conversations came from direct mail, referrals, Google or social, so it cannot put more money behind what works
  • Capacity that goes unused in the slower months is revenue the firm is already paying staff to be ready for but not earning
  • If online leads are not followed up differently from referrals, the colder leads the firm pays for may convert below their potential
Counting, following up with and converting every new lead is how Dorcey Law Firm grows its share of the market without adding a single FTE.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • A "Schedule A Consultation" button sits above the fold on the homepage and links to the contact page, and a contact form appears on the homepage as well
  • The contact page form collects first and last name, phone, email, client status (new potential client, current client or neither) and a message, with SMS consent language included — the client-status field helps keep existing clients out of the new-lead queue
  • No live chat was detected on the homepage or contact page, so there is no instant way for a visitor to ask a question outside office hours

These findings are also referenced in the Website Audit section.

Team Assessment

Dorcey Law Firm's 28 domestic FTEs and 8 global team members are sized for a January–May peak, which leaves capacity underused for much of the rest of the year. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: AMBER
"we have to staff for our high peak season, even though seven months out of the year, I don't need all the staff members I have."
The Problem
  • The team is sized for the peak season, so payroll built for January–May has to be carried through months when fewer clients are coming in
  • The 2027 goal of 25–30% growth comes with a no-new-FTE constraint, so growth has to come from more output per person
  • Revenue per FTE is about $300,000 today against a goal of $350,000–$400,000
  • Four offices and a global team of eight mean the firm's systems, not just its people, have to carry the coordination
Blind Spot: Opening the Marco Island, LaBelle and Sarasota offices extended the firm's season, which proves the model works. But seasonality is still set by when clients are in town, so the next lever is demand generated in the months when they are not.
The Positive
  • The firm already combines a domestic team with global team members, and Josh is comfortable using more of them to grow without adding domestic FTEs
  • Seven attorneys, a firm administrator and an executive assistant give the firm a leadership and support structure to build on
The Gap
  • Underused capacity in the slower months is a cost the firm carries every year without earning from it
  • Growth without new FTEs only works if demand is steady enough to keep the existing team busy all year
  • Until demand is steadier, staffing decisions stay tied to the peak season instead of the firm's growth plan
A steadier year-round workload is what lets Dorcey Law Firm raise revenue per FTE and own its market without adding headcount.

Profit Plan Assessment

Dorcey Law Firm's revenue is between $8 million and $9 million this year, and Josh watches revenue per FTE closely, but no profit or margin figures came up on the call. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"I'd like to get us up to the $350,000 to $400,000 per FTE."
The Problem
  • Revenue came in below the $10 million Josh believes the firm should have reached, with the real estate market slowing that department
  • No profit, margin or acquisition-cost figures were shared, so the firm cannot yet see what each new client costs to win
  • Because the firm cannot identify where many clients come from, it cannot compare what direct mail, workshops, referrals, Google and social each cost per signed client
  • A payroll built for the peak season is a fixed cost carried through the months when fewer clients arrive
Blind Spot: Workshops, direct mail and community sponsorships cost real money, but without source tracking the firm cannot say which of them earn it back. The channels that feel most important are not always the ones that pay best.
The Positive
  • About 40% of revenue comes from existing or previous clients returning for new work, and another 10% comes from client-care renewals and referral fees, a durable base most firms would envy
  • Josh already tracks revenue per FTE, a strong efficiency measure to build a profit plan around
The Gap
  • Without acquisition cost by channel, the firm cannot tell which marketing dollars deserve more investment
  • Reaching $350,000–$400,000 per FTE means earning more from the same team, which depends on a steadier flow of new clients through the slower months
  • Without a profit plan, a 25–30% revenue goal says little about how much of that growth the firm actually keeps
Knowing what each client costs to win and what each person produces is how growth at Dorcey Law Firm becomes wealth instead of workload.
