Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Financial-advisor and CPA referrals, direct-mail workshops and local nonprofit involvement are established, reliable sources of new clients
  • Google and social media produced about $600,000 this year, and the firm cannot tell exactly where many new clients come from
  • The website is a real asset, but the newest blog post we could find is dated November 2025
A trackable online engine is how Dorcey Law Firm becomes the firm nobody in town hires without considering first.

Intake

  • Attorneys close an estimated 70–90% of the inquiries they handle, with referral sources closer to 90%
  • Leads generated cold through social media close at a lower rate than referrals, so online inquiries need their own follow-up standard
  • The firm can handle more leads, intakes, consults and casework right now, so lead flow is the constraint, not intake capacity
Converting more of every inquiry is how the firm adds revenue without adding a single FTE.

Self-Managing Team

  • 28 domestic FTEs plus 8 global team members support seven attorneys across four offices
  • The firm staffs for its January–May peak, even though it does not need the full team for roughly seven months of the year
  • The 2027 goal is 25–30% growth without adding FTEs, which makes team productivity the central lever
A team that is fully used all year is how Dorcey Law Firm grows without growing headcount.

Profit

  • Revenue is between $8 million and $9 million this year, with Josh believing the firm should have reached $10 million before real estate slowed
  • Revenue per FTE, about $300,000 today, is the efficiency measure the firm is watching, with a goal of $350,000–$400,000
  • No profit or margin figures were discussed, so margin visibility is still an open question
Higher revenue per FTE is how growth turns into wealth instead of just more workload.
