Lead Generation

Dorcey Law Firm has built strong offline lead sources — financial-advisor and CPA referrals, direct-mail workshops and seminars, a publication and more than a decade of nonprofit involvement — but Google and social media produced only about $600,000 this year. With the firm's team underused for much of the year, the online channel is where the largest and most measurable upside sits.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • Lighthouse mobile performance score: 72/100 from a single valid run (scores vary a few points run to run), with a Total Blocking Time of about 1,640 ms that makes pages slow to respond on phones even though the largest content paints in about 2.3 seconds
  • The newest blog post we could find is dated November 3, 2025, about eleven months ago, which gives search engines and prospects little sign of fresh content
  • The homepage practice list covers wills, trusts, probate, estate tax, business planning, elder law, Medicaid planning, asset protection, disaster protection and charitable planning, but does not mention the real estate and title work Josh described as a core department
  • The testimonials page carries roughly 33 undated written testimonials with no Google or Yelp review feed, star rating or count, so visitors cannot see recent, verifiable proof
Blind Spot: The site already loads Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, a Facebook pixel and a programmatic display tag, yet the firm still cannot say where its clients come from. Tracking tools alone do not answer that question — the leads, calls and consultations have to be connected to the campaign that produced them.
The Positive
  • Seven attorneys are listed with their own profile pages, a strong trust signal for estate planning clients
  • A multi-generational family legacy video on the homepage and a clear "Schedule A Consultation" button give visitors an emotional hook and an obvious next step
  • The site is mobile responsive and the homepage headline, "Put Your Legacy on Cruise Control," reinforces the firm's recurring-planning positioning
The Gap
  • Sluggish mobile page response and an aging blog make it harder to turn the traffic the firm's direct mail and referrals already send to the site into booked consultations
  • Real estate and title services, along with the Sarasota office Josh mentioned, are missing from the pages we reviewed, so people searching for those services may never find them
  • Without dated, verifiable review proof, a visitor comparing Dorcey Law Firm with a competitor has less to go on than the firm's reputation deserves
A website that proves your reputation online and shows where every inquiry came from is the foundation for being the first firm your town considers.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Review figures for the firm vary widely between Google, Yelp and aggregator listings, so the firm's online reputation reads inconsistently depending on where a prospect looks
  • Live Google local-pack results for estate planning, probate and trust administration, asset protection and real estate closing searches could not be captured in this review, so the firm's map visibility is unconfirmed and worth reviewing together on our call
  • At least one directory result showed a different phone number from the one on the website, and the firm name appears three ways ("Dorcey Law Firm," "The Dorcey Law Firm, PLC" and "DORCEY LAW")
  • The Marco Island and LaBelle offices are not reflected on any directory listing we saw, and the Sarasota office does not appear on the website
Blind Spot: A firm with four offices needs four consistent local footprints. Each office that is missing or inconsistent online is a market where families searching "near me" for an estate planning attorney may not see Dorcey Law Firm at all.
The Positive
  • The firm is listed on the major legal directories, including Martindale-Hubbell, FindLaw, Justia and Super Lawyers, and the main address is consistent everywhere we saw it
The Gap
  • Inconsistent names, phone numbers and missing branch-office listings dilute the signals Google uses to rank a firm locally
  • Without a clear, consistent review presence and fresh local content, Dorcey Law Firm gives up ground in markets like Marco Island and Sarasota where it has built physical offices
The Dellutri Law Group, P.A. — appears to have built a sizeable review footprint across Google and several directories, giving Fort Myers estate planning searchers more visible social proof to weigh before they pick up the phone.
Consistent local listings and a visible, growing review presence are how a firm known to its referral partners becomes known to everyone searching in town.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Live Google search results could not be reviewed for this audit, so we are making no claim about which ads, if any, Dorcey Law Firm is running for estate planning, probate and trust administration, asset protection, elder law or real estate closing searches
  • Josh credited Google and social media with about $600,000 this year but did not say how much of that came from paid advertising, so the first step is understanding what spend, if any, sits behind it
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • The Dellutri Law Group, P.A., Safe Harbor Law Firm and Powell, Jackman, Stevens & Ricciardi, P.A. are the Fort Myers estate planning firms whose ad copy and keyword coverage we would benchmark in a live search review; their ad activity was not captured in this audit
The Opportunity
  • High-intent estate planning searches such as "estate planning attorney Fort Myers," "trust attorney Fort Myers" and "wills and trusts lawyer Fort Myers"
  • Probate and trust administration searches such as "probate attorney Fort Myers" and "trust administration lawyer Lee County," which line up with the roughly $1–1.2 million probate and trust administration line Josh expects to grow from about 10,000 existing estate-plan clients
  • Office-city searches for Marco Island, LaBelle and Sarasota, so each of the firm's four locations has its own search presence
  • Off-season scheduling: because demand peaks January through May, steady campaigns in the slower months are a direct way to put underused team capacity to work

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Live Google results could not be reviewed, so Google Screened status and any Local Service Ads presence for Dorcey Law Firm were not observed in this audit
  • Local Service Ads availability for estate planning attorneys varies by market, and eligibility for Lee County would need to be confirmed before any budget is planned
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • No competitor Local Service Ads placements were captured in this audit; where the category is available, the same three Fort Myers firms would be the first to benchmark
The Opportunity
  • If Local Service Ads are available for Dorcey Law Firm's practice areas in Lee County, Google Screened status and a strong review base are the entry ticket
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • A steady stream of new Google reviews, which a review-request program can build across all four offices, strengthens the firm's position for LSA and the map pack at the same time

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • The Meta Ads Library could not be loaded for this audit, so we are not making a claim about whether Dorcey Law Firm currently has active Facebook or Instagram ads
  • A Facebook pixel is installed on the website, which means the groundwork for retargeting website visitors is already in place
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Competitor Meta ad activity was not captured in this audit; The Dellutri Law Group, P.A. and Safe Harbor Law Firm are the first Fort Myers estate planning firms to benchmark once the library can be reviewed
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Workshop and seminar promotion: the same audiences who respond to direct-mail invitations can be reached online with the firm's family legacy video and event registrations
