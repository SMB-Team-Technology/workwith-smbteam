Here Is Where Dorcey Law Firm Stands Today

Dorcey Law Firm is a Fort Myers, Florida estate planning firm led by Managing Partner Joshua Dorcey, with seven attorneys, four offices and a team of 28 domestic employees plus 8 global team members serving estate planning, business planning, asset protection, real estate and probate clients. New business comes largely from financial-advisor and CPA referrals, direct-mail workshops and a decade and a half of local nonprofit involvement, while Google and social media produced about $600,000 this year and, in Josh's words, there is "no way to ever identify exactly where those people come from." The single most important finding is that the firm has real capacity to take on more clients right now but no measurable online system to bring them in.

Stage 6 is a firm where nobody in your town hires another law firm without at least considering yours, and where 25–30% growth comes from smarter systems instead of more headcount — and it is exactly where we want to take Dorcey Law Firm.

Where You Are Today

Stage 4: Small Business Manager
  • Financial-advisor and CPA referrals, direct-mail workshops, a coffee-table publication and nonprofit involvement drive new business, but the firm cannot trace many clients back to the source that produced them
  • Google and social media produced about $600,000 this year, a modest slice of a firm in the $8–9 million range
  • The team is staffed for the January–May peak season, which leaves capacity underused for much of the other seven months
  • The 2027 goal is 25–30% growth without adding any FTEs, while lifting revenue per FTE from about $300,000 toward $350,000–$400,000

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The distance between a firm every referral partner trusts and a firm every Fort Myers family considers first is a measurable, scalable online presence — and that is exactly what this plan is built to create.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • Lighthouse mobile performance score: 72/100 from a single valid run (scores vary a few points run to run), with a Total Blocking Time of about 1,640 ms that makes pages slow to respond on phones even though the largest content paints in about 2.3 seconds
  • The newest blog post we could find is dated November 3, 2025, about eleven months ago, which gives search engines and prospects little sign of fresh content
  • The homepage practice list covers wills, trusts, probate, estate tax, business planning, elder law, Medicaid planning, asset protection, disaster protection and charitable planning, but does not mention the real estate and title work Josh described as a core department
  • The testimonials page carries roughly 33 undated written testimonials with no Google or Yelp review feed, star rating or count, so visitors cannot see recent, verifiable proof
Blind Spot: The site already loads Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, a Facebook pixel and a programmatic display tag, yet the firm still cannot say where its clients come from. Tracking tools alone do not answer that question — the leads, calls and consultations have to be connected to the campaign that produced them.
The Positive
  • Seven attorneys are listed with their own profile pages, a strong trust signal for estate planning clients
  • A multi-generational family legacy video on the homepage and a clear "Schedule A Consultation" button give visitors an emotional hook and an obvious next step
  • The site is mobile responsive and the homepage headline, "Put Your Legacy on Cruise Control," reinforces the firm's recurring-planning positioning
The Gap
  • Sluggish mobile page response and an aging blog make it harder to turn the traffic the firm's direct mail and referrals already send to the site into booked consultations
  • Real estate and title services, along with the Sarasota office Josh mentioned, are missing from the pages we reviewed, so people searching for those services may never find them
  • Without dated, verifiable review proof, a visitor comparing Dorcey Law Firm with a competitor has less to go on than the firm's reputation deserves
A website that proves your reputation online and shows where every inquiry came from is the foundation for being the first firm your town considers.

Intake Assessment

Dorcey Law Firm's attorneys close a high share of the inquiries that reach them, and Josh confirmed the firm has room to handle more leads, intakes and consultations right now. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
"our typical close rate of our attorneys in our office range between 70, 90%."
The Problem
  • Josh said there is "no way to ever identify exactly where those people come from," so intake cannot report which channels produce signed clients
  • Leads generated cold through social media close at a lower rate than referrals, so growing online lead volume will test intake harder than referral volume does
  • Because attorneys close consultations themselves, intake results depend on attorney availability during the January–May peak
  • No live chat was detected on the homepage or contact page, so a visitor who wants an answer outside office hours has only a form and a wait
Blind Spot: A 70–90% close rate is largely a warm-referral story, with financial-advisor and CPA referrals closing closer to 90%. Online leads arrive colder and will convert lower, so intake should be measured separately by lead source from the first day of any new campaign.
The Positive
  • Attorney close rates of 70–90% mean the firm turns most qualified conversations into clients
  • The firm already follows up with a cultivation video, and Josh confirmed the team can take on more leads, intakes, consultations and casework today
The Gap
  • Without lead-source tracking, the firm cannot tell which intake conversations came from direct mail, referrals, Google or social, so it cannot put more money behind what works
  • Capacity that goes unused in the slower months is revenue the firm is already paying staff to be ready for but not earning
  • If online leads are not followed up differently from referrals, the colder leads the firm pays for may convert below their potential
Counting, following up with and converting every new lead is how Dorcey Law Firm grows its share of the market without adding a single FTE.
