Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Kinect Law
Kinect Law is operating at Stage 3: the firm has deep IP expertise and a strong partner bench, but its client flow still relies on word-of-mouth and referrals rather than a repeatable system. The most urgent limiting area is lead generation: the Google Business Profile is outdated, the website has no location-specific content to reach Manhattan, Brooklyn, Nassau, or Westchester, and there are no reviews to build trust with first-time searchers. Until that changes, the predictable, high-value B2B pipeline the partners want will stay out of reach.
