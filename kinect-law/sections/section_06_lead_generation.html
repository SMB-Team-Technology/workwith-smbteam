Lead Generation

Kinect Law's trademark, patent, and IP litigation work reaches new clients mostly through word-of-mouth and referrals, which makes the flow of new matters inconsistent. The firm's online presence is not yet doing the work of attracting high-value B2B clients: the Google Business Profile is outdated and the website has no pages built for the markets the partners want to serve.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The homepage headline speaks to protecting intellectual property, but its only button, "Learn More," leads to a services page instead of inviting a call or consultation
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 61/100 — Desktop 94/100. The mobile largest-contentful-paint is 6.7 seconds, well past the point where visitors start to leave
  • There are no location-specific "Areas We Serve" pages, which limits visibility to Whitestone even though the firm wants Manhattan, Brooklyn, Nassau County, and Westchester clients
  • No blog, video, or client testimonials were observed, so a first-time visitor has little beyond the attorney bios to build confidence
The Positive
  • All four partners are listed with photos and titles, giving visitors a clear view of the team behind the work
  • Desktop performance is strong at 94/100, so the foundation is sound and mobile speed is the main fix
The Gap
  • A business owner who finds the site but sees no clear next step has no easy way to start a conversation
  • With no pages written for the surrounding markets, searches from Manhattan, Brooklyn, Nassau, and Westchester have nothing to land on
  • Slow mobile loading costs visitors before they ever read about the firm's patent and trademark experience
A website that turns search visitors into inquiries is the first step toward a pipeline that no longer depends on referrals.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The Google Business Profile still shows the outdated name "Albert Chan" rather than Kinect Law, and is missing service categories
  • The profile has no reviews or photos, so it offers little proof of the firm's work when someone compares options on Google
  • Directory listings use different names ("Albert Chan," "Law Offices of Albert Wai-Kit Chan PLLC," "Kinect Law") and different suite numbers (Suite 604 on some, Suite 408 on the website), which weakens trust signals for search engines
  • On FindLaw's "Trademarks Lawyers in Whitestone" page, Kinect Law did not appear among the top results
Blind Spot: The firm has changed its name and suite over time, but older listings still carry the previous details. Search engines treat conflicting business information as a reason to trust a listing less, so cleaning it up can lift visibility before any new content is written.
The Positive
  • Listings already exist on FindLaw, Avvo, and several business directories, so the work is correcting and building on them rather than starting from zero
The Gap
  • Searches for trademark and patent attorneys in Queens, Manhattan, Brooklyn, Nassau, and Westchester give Google little reason to surface Kinect Law
  • Local map-pack results for these searches were not captured in this research; a live check is the first step of the SEO work
Sisun Law — appears in the FindLaw Whitestone trademark results where Kinect Law does not, and carries a visible client-review presence in legal directories.
Fixing the firm's local presence means the right B2B clients find Kinect Law on their own, not only through a referral.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Trademarks (Whitestone / Queens): Google search results for these terms could not be reviewed in this research, so Kinect Law ad presence is unconfirmed
  • Patents (biotech and pharma) and IP litigation: ad presence was likewise not observed; confirming whether the firm is configured to show for these terms is a first step
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Sisun Law — Manhattan trademark competitor; ad messaging was not observed in this research
  • De Rose & Surico and Pollak & Slepian LLP — Bayside, Queens trademark competitors; ad activity was not observed in this research
The Opportunity
  • Trademark searches such as "trademark attorney Queens" and "trademark lawyer Manhattan"
  • Patent searches such as "patent attorney New York biotech," which match the biotech and pharma startups the firm wants
  • Infringement-driven searches such as "trademark infringement attorney Nassau County" and "patent infringement lawyer Brooklyn," where the need for counsel is urgent
  • Neighboring markets (Westchester, Nassau, Brooklyn, Manhattan) that today have no pages or presence tied to Kinect Law

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Kinect Law Local Service Ads placement and Google Screened status were not observed in this research
  • With no reviews on the Google Business Profile today, the firm is not yet positioned to compete for these placements
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Named competitor Local Service Ads placements and review counts were not observed in this research
The Opportunity
  • Building a base of Google reviews now is the groundwork for any future Local Service Ads placement
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Availability for IP-related categories varies by market and should be confirmed before any LSA budget is committed

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • The Meta Ad Library could not be reviewed in this research, so Kinect Law Facebook and Instagram ad presence is unconfirmed and treated as an opportunity gap
  • No video or client-facing social content was observed on the website to retarget visitors with
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Named competitor Meta ad activity (Sisun Law, De Rose & Surico, Pollak & Slepian LLP) was not observed in this research
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Educational content aimed at biotech and pharma founders and established brand owners, such as how to protect a portfolio before launch, to reach the high-value B2B clients the firm wants
