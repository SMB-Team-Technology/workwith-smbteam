Website Audit

The Problem
  • The primary lead-capture form sits near the page footer rather than above the fold — only the phone number is visible without scrolling
  • PageSpeed scores could not be verified this session — the PageSpeed Insights API was rate-limited on repeated attempts. Recommend a live retest before this is presented as a confirmed number
  • No live chat is present, narrowing the paths an after-hours or passive visitor has to start a conversation
  • A "Family Law Expert Blog" section exists, but the most recent post date could not be confirmed — unclear how active it actually is
Blind Spot: The phone number in your own website header (949-749-3617) does not match the number multiple third-party directories list for your firm (949-363-5551) — anyone who calls the number they found elsewhere may believe they've reached a disconnected or incorrect business.
The Positive
  • The homepage headline — "Protecting Parent's Rights in High Conflict Divorces" — speaks directly to a specific, high-intent buyer instead of generic messaging
  • An embedded video of John discussing celebrity divorce cases alongside his "26+ Years of Success" and State Bar Certified Family Law Specialist credentials builds real credibility before a prospect ever calls
The Gap
  • A buried contact form means visitors who don't pick up the phone are far more likely to leave without converting at all
  • Without live chat, a visitor searching late at night or on a weekend — often when family law urgency peaks — has no way to start a conversation while their motivation is highest
  • Unconfirmed site performance could quietly be costing mobile visitors before they ever see John's credentials or the consultation offer
Every visitor who leaves without converting is a client John has already paid to reach — fixing the capture path costs nothing extra, unlike replacing that lead with new ad spend.
