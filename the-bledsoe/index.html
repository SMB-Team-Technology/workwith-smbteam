Here Is Where The Bledsoe Firm Stands Today

John Bledsoe has spent 26+ years building a State Bar Certified family law practice in Lake Forest, handling high-conflict divorce, child custody, and father's rights cases across Orange County. The firm has already tripled in size and now spends roughly $36,000 a month across four separate marketing vendors — but with only a 17% lead-to-consultation close rate and no single point of accountability for that spend, John cannot yet step back from the business the way he wants to while opening new offices in San Diego and LA.

Stage 6 is where John opens San Diego and LA without carrying the marketing and intake load himself — with Kate and a trained team running the firm's growth engine day to day.

Where You Are Today

Stage 4: Small Business Manager
  • Currently investing roughly $36,000/month across four separate vendors — Noble, a direct mail vendor, Vertical 10, and Google Ads — with no single point of accountability for performance
  • Lead-to-consultation close rate sits at 17%, even with a $200 paid-consultation filter already in place to screen prospects
  • John is actively delegating marketing and intake ownership to Kate so he can focus on strategy, but that transition is new and unproven at scale
  • Planning new offices in San Diego and LA while aiming to double an already-tripled client base, without yet having the systems in place to support that growth

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between where John is and Stage 6 is not a lack of investment — it is a lack of consolidation and conversion, and closing it is exactly what makes opening San Diego and LA possible without adding to John's plate.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.
