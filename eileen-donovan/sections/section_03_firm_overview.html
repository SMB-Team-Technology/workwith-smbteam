Here Is Where Eileen Donovan Law Stands Today

Eileen Donovan Law is a Scarsdale estate planning practice serving Westchester County families with wills, trusts, powers of attorney, probate, and senior planning. Eileen closes nearly every consultation that reaches her — her own words are "shy of 100%" — but every one of those consultations arrives as a referral, at roughly six to eight new matters a month, with no search-driven or paid source of new clients behind it.

Stage 6 is where the firm gives you your time back and carries your income without depending on every hour you work — and it is exactly where this plan is taking you.

Where You Are Today

STAGE 3: SOLO PRACTITIONER
  • Referrals are the only source of new clients — about six to eight matters a month — and no paid advertising has been tried yet
  • As discussed on our call, there is no live Google Business Profile, and the website shows no address or phone number
  • You lead a team of two plus a drafter, yet you are still the one absorbing the work the team cannot
  • There is no dedicated receptionist, so phones and client flow rest on one client services director

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a referral-powered practice and a firm that gives you your time back is a repeatable way for new families to find you and a team structure that carries the day-to-day — and both are within reach.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Eileen Donovan Law
Eileen is operating at Stage 3 — a Solo Practitioner with a small team, an exceptional close rate, and a lead flow that depends entirely on referrals. The most urgent limiting area is that families searching online have no way to find her: no live Google listing, a website with no address or phone number, and no paid channel. Until that changes, the growth Eileen described — more matters each month and the freedom to "scale and operate differently" — stays tied to how many referrals happen to arrive.
