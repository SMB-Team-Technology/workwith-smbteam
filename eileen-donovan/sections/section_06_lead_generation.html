Lead Generation

Eileen Donovan Law's new clients arrive almost entirely by referral — roughly six to eight a month — with no paid advertising and, as discussed on our call, no live Google Business Profile. That is a strong word-of-mouth foundation, but it leaves the firm out of sight for the Westchester families who start their search on Google.

Website Audit

"It's, you know, it's not a total mess, but it's, it's not what it needs to be."
The Problem
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 63/100 — Desktop 88/100 (Lighthouse performance score)
  • Mobile is the weak spot: the main content on the homepage takes about 8 seconds to appear on a phone
  • No address or phone number appears on the homepage, and the contact form lives on a separate Contact page rather than where visitors land
  • The browser tab title reads "Keeping you out of court and out of conflict" — it names no city and no practice area, so it gives Google and searchers little to match on
  • At the time of our review, four of the five association and credential logos on the homepage load from a web host that no longer responds, so they appear as empty boxes
  • On our call we reviewed the estate planning page together: the copy is thin and its title simply says "estate planning"
Blind Spot: Association logos are among the quickest trust signals on a law firm homepage, and right now most of yours are not displaying. A family comparing three attorneys sees empty boxes where the credentials should be.
The Positive
  • A clear "Schedule an Appointment" button sits above the fold, with an attorney bio ("Meet Eileen") and a testimonials page
  • Memberships in NAELA, the NYS Bar Association, the Westchester County Bar Association, and WealthCounsel are credentials worth showing — and the desktop score is a solid 88/100
The Gap
  • Families searching on a phone face a slow page with no phone number to tap — many will move to the next result
  • With no city or practice area in the title and thin practice pages, the site gives Google little reason to show it for searches like "estate planning attorney White Plains"
  • Competitors such as Cavallo & Cavallo publish a dedicated Westchester page for each service, capturing searches this site is not built to answer
A website that works as a 24/7 front door is the first step toward a flow of new clients that no longer depends on who you happen to meet.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • As discussed on our call, there is no Google Business Profile live today, and the estate planning search we ran together from White Plains did not surface the firm — we recommend confirming this together before work begins
  • Without a live profile there are no Google reviews for prospects to see, which is the first thing most families compare
  • Directory listings show two different street addresses for the Scarsdale office, and the website shows none, so the firm's name, address, and phone number do not match across the web
  • There are no pages aimed at White Plains, Scarsdale, or the surrounding Westchester towns
Blind Spot: Google cross-checks the name, address, and phone number it finds across the web. Conflicting addresses can quietly hold back a new Google listing even after it is created, so cleaning them up has to come first.
The Positive
  • The firm already has an established footprint on Avvo, Justia, Nextdoor, the Westchester County Bar Association directory, and the Scarsdale chamber directory — the raw material for consistent citations is there
The Gap
  • Families searching "trust attorney Scarsdale" or "estate planning attorney White Plains" are choosing among firms that have visible listings and reviews — and this firm is not among them
  • Every month without a listing is a month of local search demand going to someone else
Cavallo & Cavallo — displays a Google review badge on its homepage and publishes dedicated "Westchester ... Attorney" pages for each service, giving it local search visibility this firm does not yet have.
Being found in your own backyard is how a firm that runs on referrals gets a second engine — and your time back.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • On our call you confirmed you have not yet used paid channels, and our review did not observe Google search ads for estate planning, probate, or senior and elder law planning in Westchester
  • This reads as an opportunity gap: those searches are being answered by other firms, with no ad from Eileen Donovan Law alongside them
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Competitor ad copy could not be captured in this review; the paid results we looked at together on our call included large multi-location White Plains firms
  • Ettinger Law Firm — by its own description the largest elder law estate planning firm in New York, with a dedicated White Plains page and the scale to compete for these searches
The Opportunity
  • Core estate planning searches: "estate planning attorney White Plains," "trust attorney Scarsdale," and "wills and trusts lawyer Westchester County"
  • Senior and elder law searches such as "medicaid planning attorney Westchester" and "elder law attorney Scarsdale"
  • Neighboring towns beyond Scarsdale and White Plains, such as Yonkers and New Rochelle, where families also search for a Westchester estate planning attorney
  • Probate and trust administration searches, which reach families at the moment they realize they need a plan of their own
Blind Spot: Your concern is paying for "tire kickers." Lead quality is largely decided by how campaigns and landing pages are built — clear language about who the firm serves and a focused consultation offer filter inquiries before the phone ever rings.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • We did not observe Eileen Donovan Law in Local Service Ads for estate planning in this review, and Google Screened status could not be confirmed
  • This is best read as an open opportunity rather than a confirmed absence, and it connects directly to the Google Business Profile gap above
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Competitor LSA review counts could not be read in this review
  • On our call, the Local Service Ads block for estate planning in White Plains showed established firms already holding the top spots
The Opportunity
  • A verified Google presence and a steady stream of reviews position the firm to compete for those top-of-page spots in Westchester
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Because LSA leads arrive as phone calls, they play to your strength: a strong closer talking to a prospect who has already decided to call
Blind Spot: Local Service Ads rank partly on how reliably calls are answered. With no dedicated receptionist today, phone coverage needs to be settled before launch so paid calls do not go to voicemail.
