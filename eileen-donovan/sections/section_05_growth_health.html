Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Referrals are the only source of new matters — roughly six to eight a month
  • No paid advertising to date, and as discussed on our call, no live Google Business Profile
  • The website shows no address or phone number and loads slowly on mobile (63/100)
Without a source of clients you control, the growth you want depends on who happens to refer you next.

Intake

  • Phones and client flow run through one client services director, with no dedicated receptionist
  • You personally filter out poor-fit callers rather than relying on a defined screening process
  • A near-total close rate on referrals has not yet been tested on cold leads from search or ads
Moving first contact off you and your team is how your time starts coming back.

Self-Managing Team

  • A lean team of two plus a drafter, anchored by a client services director you trust
  • You are still absorbing the overflow work the team cannot carry
  • A remote hire for phones and admin is under consideration but not yet in place
You have already invested to get your time back — the next step is a structure that actually returns it.

Profit

  • Owner pay has been set deliberately low, and you have said that business model needs to change
  • A new fee structure takes effect January 1, with a near-total close rate suggesting room to price
  • With every client a referral, there is no baseline yet for what it costs to win a new matter
Profit that is planned, not discovered, is what lets you scale and operate differently.
