Lead Generation

Kamaluddin Law has built a genuinely strong reputation handling immigration cases — including green card wins — and a growing personal injury practice, almost entirely on referrals. With no paid advertising running in either practice area and zero organic visibility across six local searches, that reputation is not yet reaching the clients actively searching for this firm online.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The site is built on a modern Next.js framework (estimated redesign within the last 12-18 months), but PageSpeed mobile and desktop scores could not be independently verified this research cycle — Google's tool was rate-limited across repeated attempts and should be measured directly rather than assumed
  • The case evaluation form sits mid-page rather than above the fold, adding friction for a prospect ready to reach out immediately
  • Attorney bio is limited to a brief mention inside the immigration section — no dedicated bio page showcasing Nahla's credentials as an award-winning attorney and former professor
  • No geo-targeted landing pages exist for surrounding service areas (Marietta, Cobb County, Vinings) despite competing in a hyper-local search market
Blind Spot: Many prospects search by city or county, not just practice area — competitors with dedicated Marietta or Cobb County pages can capture searches this site was never built to answer.
The Positive
  • A modern design foundation and an active blog with recent posts covering immigration, personal injury, and even cryptocurrency law — showing genuine subject-matter range
  • A clear phone number and consultation call-to-action are visible above the fold
The Gap
  • Every visitor who doesn't scroll to the mid-page form has no clear next action — a likely source of lost leads
  • No dedicated bio page limits the trust-building this firm's credentials should be earning it
  • Absent geo-targeted pages mean this site cannot compete for hyper-local searches at all right now
Fixing these gaps turns the website into an asset that works while you focus on leading the firm you are building — not one more thing you have to personally manage.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The Google Business Profile category is listed generically as "Law Firm" rather than "Immigration Law Firm" — actively hurting how the listing surfaces for immigration-specific searches
  • A live NAP inconsistency was found: a major directory listing shows the firm's correct street address under the wrong city and zip code, in direct conflict with the confirmed Smyrna, GA address
  • The firm did not appear organically in any of six local searches run across both immigration and personal injury in its own market — from "immigration lawyer near me Smyrna GA" to "car accident lawyer Marietta GA"
Blind Spot: A pending video verification issue for the new Smyrna office is currently blocking Nahla's own access to manage her Google Business Profile — she cannot update the category, respond to reviews, or add photos herself until it clears.
The Positive
  • The firm's review profile compares favorably to every other local firm named in this research — the gap here is visibility and verification, not reputation
The Gap
  • Without the correct category and a resolved verification issue, the firm cannot show up for the searches most likely to convert
  • The NAP inconsistency actively works against local ranking signals every day it remains uncorrected
Weinstock Immigration Lawyers — holds strong organic visibility for the exact "best immigration attorney Atlanta" search category Kamaluddin Law needs to win.
Every uncorrected local SEO issue is a client who found a competitor instead of the legacy firm you are building.
