Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • New cases come mostly by word of mouth, and there is no way to tell where they come from
  • Social media posting is occasional, and Facebook ads have only been tried once or twice
  • The website has no pages aimed at Jefferson Parish, Gretna, Metairie or other nearby areas
Without a reliable source of new cases, the constant flow needed to hire help cannot start.

Intake

  • A solo practice with no staff means every call, consultation and follow-up depends on one person
  • A phone-answering service was tried and dropped because case volume could not justify the cost
  • The only button above the fold on the website leads to the About page, not to a call or consultation
When intake depends on one person, every new case competes with everything else he is doing.

Self-Managing Team

  • Jonathan works alone, with no paralegal or attorney, and bookkeeping is referred out
  • Hiring depends on a constant flow of cases that the practice does not have yet
  • Every case, client and decision runs through the owner
A firm that does not rely on him to do everything starts with case flow steady enough to justify a first hire.

Profit

  • With no tracking of where cases come from, there is no way to know what any marketing dollar would return
  • No revenue goal or marketing budget has been set yet
  • Jonathan describes his bookkeeper as not the best, which limits financial visibility
Knowing which cases come from where turns the better bottom line he wants into a plan instead of a hope.
