Here Is Where Law Office of Jonathan M. Williams Stands Today

Jonathan Williams has practiced as a solo attorney in Harvey, Louisiana for about seven or eight years, handling personal injury, workers' compensation, estate planning, civil litigation and environmental claims, with personal injury where most of his income comes from. His new cases come almost entirely by word of mouth, and he has no way to see where they come from or to count on more of them.

Stage 6 is where a steady, predictable flow of cases replaces word-of-mouth guesswork, so you can hire help and stop carrying every case alone.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • Word of mouth is the main source of new cases, and there is no way to track where cases come from
  • Runs the practice alone, with no paralegal or other staff to share the work
  • Facebook ads have been tried once or twice and produced “more views,” and social posting is occasional and untracked
  • The website is new this year, but it was built by a third party and has no content program or pages for surrounding parishes

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a practice that depends on who already knows you and a firm with a constant flow of cases is closable, and it starts with one reliable source of new cases beyond referrals.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Law Office of Jonathan M. Williams
Jonathan is operating at Stage 3, a solo practitioner whose caseload depends on people who already know him. The most urgent gap is the lack of any predictable, trackable source of new personal injury cases, in a market where he says other attorneys are advertising through billboards and Google search. Until that changes, the steady flow of cases he wants, enough to eventually hire help, will stay out of reach.
