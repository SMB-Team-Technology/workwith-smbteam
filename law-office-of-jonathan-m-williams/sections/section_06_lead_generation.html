Lead Generation

Jonathan Williams's practice runs on word of mouth and returning clients, with only occasional social posts and a Facebook ad or two to date. In a Jefferson Parish market where, by his account, other injury firms advertise on billboards and Google search, that leaves a brand-new website with no content program and no way to measure where cases come from.

Website Audit

“They just made it, and that was it.”
The Problem
  • The site is new this year but was built once by a third party; no blog, video or ongoing content has been added since
  • PageSpeed scores (Lighthouse test of the live site): Mobile 65/100 — Desktop 97/100
  • No pages for Jefferson Parish, Orleans Parish, Gretna, Metairie, Marrero or other nearby communities
  • The only call to action above the fold is a “Learn More” button to the About page; the consultation form sits mid-page
Blind Spot: Paying a vendor to host a website is not the same as someone growing it. Search engines reward sites that keep adding pages and content for the areas and case types people search, and a site that was built once and left alone gives them little new to show.
The Positive
  • A clean, modern 2026 design with an attorney headshot and bio covering personal injury, wrongful death and workers' compensation
  • Strong desktop speed (97/100) and a responsive build give a solid base to grow from
The Gap
  • Without pages for each parish and city, search engines have no reason to show the site to people searching for an injury lawyer in those communities
  • With no blog, video or guides, there is little for search engines to rank beyond the firm's name, and little to build trust before a prospect calls
  • A mobile score of 65 leaves room to speed up the experience for visitors on their phones, where many injury searches happen
A website that brings in cases instead of acting as a digital business card is the first step toward the constant flow of cases that makes hiring help possible.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Google Business Profile status, review count and local map-pack position could not be verified in our research, so none is assumed and each should be confirmed live
  • One directory listing appears to show a Marrero address rather than Harvey, and another appears to be filed under a bankruptcy category, which is not a practice area of the firm
  • The website has no location pages, so local search visibility depends almost entirely on the Google Business Profile
Blind Spot: Mismatched addresses and categories across directories can confuse Google about which business is which, and quietly hold back map rankings even when the firm is doing good work.
The Positive
  • Profiles exist on LawInfo, Avvo, Martindale-Hubbell and Facebook, and the LawInfo listing matches the website's name, address and phone number
The Gap
  • A positive Facebook recommendation rate is a start, but it needs to be matched on Google, where most local searchers compare firms before calling
  • Local competitors such as Redmann and Palazzo have built visible review profiles across directory sites, the kind of trust signal injury clients compare
Ron Austin Law — also based in Harvey, with a dedicated Jefferson Parish personal injury page on its website, giving Google a clear local page to show for exactly the searches this firm wants to win.
Showing up when injury clients in Jefferson Parish search is how word-of-mouth guesswork becomes a steady flow of new cases.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Personal injury: Google Ads activity for the firm could not be verified in this review, and the owner has not mentioned any Google Ads spend; his paid advertising to date has been Facebook
  • Estate planning, workers' compensation, civil litigation and environmental claims: ad activity could not be verified in this review for any of these, so none is assumed
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Local personal injury firms — the owner says they “put a lot of dollars into marketing,” including billboards and Google search
  • Redmann, Ron Austin Law and Palazzo — specific ad copy and keywords could not be observed in this review
The Opportunity
  • Personal injury terms tied to the firm's home market, such as “personal injury lawyer Harvey LA” and “car accident lawyer Jefferson Parish”
  • Workers' compensation and estate planning searches in the same area give additional entry points beyond personal injury
  • Neighboring communities that clients search from, including Gretna, Marrero, Metairie and Orleans Parish
  • The owner found SMB Team through a Google search, which is exactly how injury clients look for a lawyer; before any ad budget is committed, a tracked website with location pages means every dollar can be tied to a signed case
Blind Spot: Ads only amplify what happens after the click. With one low-visibility call to action and no way to track cases back to their source, paid traffic would leak, which is why the foundation comes first.
