Here Is Where O'Brien Law Group PC Stands Today

Kerry O'Brien has practiced law for 25 years, handling personal injury cases (mostly motor vehicle accidents) along with premises liability and some workers' compensation from a single office in Los Alamitos. His firm grew on referrals and relationships, and now that existing cases are settling, the caseload has dropped to about 60 and new calls have slowed. The most important finding: a respected practice and a capable five-person team with no marketing system to bring in the next wave of clients, a website last updated in 2023, and a Google listing still tied to another business.

Stage 6 is a firm where new cases arrive through a system rather than luck: the steady, predictable pipeline you told us you want heading into 2027.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • New cases have come mostly through referrals and relationships, and calls have slowed as the firm has faded from view
  • The caseload is down to about 60 as existing cases settle, with no marketing channel in place to refill it
  • A trained team of five handles intake and screening, with after-hours and weekend coverage already in place
  • No paid advertising has been run, and the website, Google listing, and firm name still reflect the old brand

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between waiting on referrals and having a steady pipeline is a system, not more effort, and with your reputation and team already in place it is closer than it feels.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
O'Brien Law Group PC
O'Brien Law Group PC is at Stage 3, a Solo Practitioner whose reputation and team are well ahead of the marketing behind them. The most urgent gap is the absence of any system that generates new cases, which matters most now that the caseload is shrinking as cases settle. Until that changes, the steady flow of cases you want heading into 2027 will depend on chance rather than a plan.
