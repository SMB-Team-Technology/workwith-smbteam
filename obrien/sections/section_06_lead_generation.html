Lead Generation

O'Brien Law Group has been built over 25 years on referrals and relationships, but the phones are ringing less than they used to and no digital system exists to replace that flow. You have never run paid ads, and the website and Google listing still reflect the old Law Office of Kerry O'Brien name just as the firm moves to O'Brien Law Group PC.

Website Audit

"I do have to revamp my website and everything else to transition over."
The Problem
  • The site footer reads © 2023, and there have been no visible updates since then
  • The site still carries the old "Law Office Of Kerry P. O'Brien" brand while the firm is transitioning to O'Brien Law Group PC
  • Lighthouse performance test (a lab test, not an official PageSpeed Insights report): Mobile 65/100 — Desktop 92/100, with a mobile largest-content load time of 7.5 seconds
  • The only contact form we found sits in the footer rather than near the top of the page, and no Spanish-language pages or video were observed
Blind Spot: We found a Google tag on the site but no Meta (Facebook and Instagram) tracking pixel. Once social campaigns begin, every visitor who does not call on the first visit would leave with no way to follow up unless the pixel is installed first.
The Positive
  • A "Free Case Evaluation" button and a click-to-call phone number are visible at the top of the homepage
  • Your attorney bio and headshot give visitors a face and a track record, and a blog and legal definitions section already exist
  • The site is built on a mobile-responsive template, and desktop performance is strong
The Gap
  • A 7.5-second mobile load is slow for visitors who are often searching on a phone in the days after an accident, which puts calls at risk before they happen
  • An outdated look and the old firm name make it harder for a new visitor to see the firm you have become
  • Your Spanish-speaking staff cannot help site visitors who search in Spanish, because there is no Spanish content to find
A fast website that reflects the new O'Brien Law Group brand turns 25 years of reputation into the steady flow of calls you want in 2027.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Your Google Business Profile is attached to the location of a tax consulting business, which you have tried for years to get corrected; it was fixed once and then reverted
  • The listing still uses the old "Law Office of Kerry O'Brien" name and a generic "Attorney" category rather than "Personal Injury Attorney," so searchers may read it as a tax or business attorney
  • Your firm name appears four different ways across the web, and the address and phone number differ between the website, directory listings, and Google
  • Your review base appears modest compared with several personal injury firms in your market, which limits how often and how prominently you can earn a spot in the map results
Blind Spot: When our team searched live on your call, Google's AI overview described your business correctly. AI search tools draw on the same listing and directory information as the map, so a consistent, correct listing is what keeps you accurate in ChatGPT and Google AI answers as more clients ask those tools instead of scrolling a list.
The Positive
  • The phone number on your Google listing is yours, and dedicated Seal Beach and service area pages already exist on your website
  • You have a State Bar profile, an Avvo profile, and a Yelp listing that was updated in August 2026 with photos
The Gap
  • A listing that points to a tax consulting office sends the wrong signal to both Google and the person deciding whether to call
  • Inconsistent name, address, and phone details across directories make it harder for Google to trust that the listings all belong to one firm
Omega Law Group, PC — a Seal Beach area firm that surfaced for car accident searches across your market and appears to have a far deeper review history, which is exactly the kind of credibility signal that local map rankings reward.
Fixing the listing and rebuilding review momentum puts O'Brien Law Group in front of the people who are already searching for a firm like yours, so your pipeline does not depend on the phone happening to ring.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Personal injury and motor vehicle accidents: you told us you have never run paid ads and are nervous about them, so there is no Google Ads history to build on or repair
  • Premises liability, slip and fall, and dog bites: the same applies, with no paid search currently supporting these case types
  • Workers' compensation: you told us the time and effort are not worth it for the attorney fee, so we do not recommend spending ad dollars here
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Los Angeles-wide searches such as "car accident attorney LA" are crowded with large regional personal injury brands, as we saw live on your call, which is why nearby cities are the smarter place to start
  • Which local firms are bidding on your target searches and what they are paying should be confirmed from live auction data before any budget is committed
The Opportunity
  • Car accident lawyer and attorney searches for Long Beach, Los Alamitos, Seal Beach, and nearby cities, plus "motor vehicle accident attorney near me"
  • Premises liability, slip and fall, and dog bite searches, which you told us you want to take on
  • Lower-cost nearby cities such as Anaheim and Buena Park, where you can aim to show up number one instead of number 50 in Los Angeles, a strategy you told us you want to pursue
  • Spanish-language search campaigns supported by your Spanish-speaking staff, along with searches for the old and new firm names to protect your brand through the transition
Blind Spot: Past clients and referral partners know you as the Law Office of Kerry O'Brien. As the firm moves to O'Brien Law Group, people searching for you by the old name need to find the new one, so both names should be covered by your brand searches.
