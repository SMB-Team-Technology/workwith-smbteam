Lead Generation

O'Brien Law Group PC has built 25 years of practice on referrals and relationships, with no paid advertising, a website last updated in 2023, and a Google listing attached to the wrong address. With the caseload down to about 60 and fewer calls coming in, there is no system yet in place to bring in the next wave of clients.

Website Audit

"We're going to try and get away from a law office, Kerry O'Brien, and just to O'Brien Law Group."
The Problem
  • The site footer still shows 2023, and the site still carries the old "Law Office Of Kerry P. O'Brien" name rather than the new O'Brien Law Group PC
  • Lighthouse performance test scores: 65/100 mobile, 92/100 desktop, with the main content taking about 7.5 seconds to appear on mobile
  • The contact form sits in the footer rather than near the top of the page
  • No video or Spanish-language content was found on the site, even though the firm has Spanish-speaking staff
Blind Spot: The site's tracking shows Google's tag but no Meta pixel and no call-tracking that we could find, so there is no way to see which visitors turn into phone calls or to follow up with those who leave.
The Positive
  • Kerry's bio and headshot are on the site, with 20+ years of personal injury and workers' compensation experience
  • A Free Case Evaluation button and a click-to-call phone number are above the fold, and the desktop experience loads quickly
The Gap
  • A slow mobile load costs visitors who are searching on their phones in the moment they need a lawyer
  • Visitors see an outdated brand that no longer matches the new firm name, which weakens trust at the moment they decide whether to call
  • Without tracking or retargeting, every visitor who leaves without calling is gone for good
A fast website under your new name that turns visitors into phone calls is the first step toward the steady flow of cases you want heading into 2027.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The Google Business Profile shows the firm at another business's address, a tax consulting office, and Kerry has tried for years to get it fixed; it reverted once after being corrected
  • The profile still uses the old "Law Office of Kerry O'Brien" name and a generic "Attorney" category instead of "Personal Injury Attorney," so searchers can mistake the firm for a tax or business attorney
  • The firm's name, address, and phone number are inconsistent across the web, appearing as Law Office of Kerry P. O'Brien, Law Offices of Kerry P. O'Brien, Inc., O'Brien Law Group, and with a second Katella Avenue address on one directory
Blind Spot: Google's AI overview described the firm correctly when searched live on the call, but inconsistent names and addresses across directories can put that accuracy at risk as AI search relies on the same data.
The Positive
  • The phone number on the profile is Kerry's own, and the firm has a listing and a presence on Avvo, Yelp, and the State Bar of California
The Gap
  • The firm appears to have far fewer Google reviews than several nearby competitors, and review count is one of the first things an accident victim compares
  • Local map-pack placement for car accident, premises liability, and workers' compensation searches in Long Beach, Los Alamitos, Seal Beach, and Anaheim still needs to be confirmed live
Omega Law Group, PC — a Seal Beach–area personal injury firm that appears in search results for car accident queries across your service area and appears to carry a far larger base of online reviews.
Fixing your Google listing means the people who have forgotten about you can find you again, and new clients find you before a competitor — the foundation of a steady pipeline.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Kerry confirmed on the call that the firm has not run paid ads and that he is nervous about them
  • The firm does not appear to be running Google search ads today, and past lead-buying has been limited to first-come, first-served pay-per-case services like LegalMatch rather than visibility the firm owns
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Omega Law Group, PC, Pacific Attorney Group PLC, and Nordean Law APC — the nearby firms most visible for car accident searches in your service area; whether they run Google Ads was not confirmed in this audit
  • Large Los Angeles firms — dominate the results for Los Angeles itself, which is why a smarter entry point is the nearby cities where the firm can compete for the top spot
The Opportunity
  • Car accident lawyer and personal injury attorney searches in Long Beach, Los Alamitos, Seal Beach, Anaheim, and Buena Park, where Kerry would rather be number one than number 50 in Los Angeles
  • Case-specific searches for premises liability, slip and fall, and dog bite cases, which Kerry wants to pursue alongside motor vehicle accidents
  • Spanish-language search campaigns, which Kerry is open to and the team's Spanish-speaking staff are ready to support
  • Workers' compensation is not a recommended ad target, since Kerry told us the time it takes isn't worth the return for the firm
Blind Spot: Past clients, referral sources, and people who have forgotten about the firm often search by name, and with the name changing to O'Brien Law Group PC, a small branded campaign makes sure they land on your site and not a competitor's.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • No Local Services Ads presence or Google Screened badge for the firm was confirmed in this audit, so this is an open opportunity
  • LSAs lean heavily on reviews and verified business details, so the Google listing clean-up described above comes first
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Omega Law Group, PC, Pacific Attorney Group PLC, and Nordean Law APC — LSA positions and review profiles for these nearby firms were not confirmed in this audit
  • The Los Angeles market — expensive and crowded, which is why LSAs targeted at Orange County and the Long Beach area are the more practical starting point
The Opportunity
  • Because you pay per lead rather than per click, LSAs make cost easier to see, which suits an owner who wants to know the spend is working
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • A strong review profile is what makes LSAs convert, so the review-building and listing work in Local SEO feeds directly into this channel

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • Kerry describes the firm's social media presence as light, and no Meta pixel was found on the website
  • The firm does not appear to be running Facebook or Instagram ads today
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Nearby personal injury firms — Facebook and Instagram ad activity for Omega Law Group, Pacific Attorney Group, and Nordean Law was not confirmed in this audit
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • A professional social presence under the new O'Brien Law Group name, built around Kerry's 25 years of experience, gives a firm that people have forgotten about a way to be seen again, including to Spanish-speaking audiences
