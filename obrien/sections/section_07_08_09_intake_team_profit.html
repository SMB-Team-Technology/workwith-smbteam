Intake Assessment

Kerry's team handles intake and screening on its own, and he says the challenge is not converting calls but getting fewer calls than the firm used to. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: RED
"they know how to do the intake, they know how to screen. I guess that's not a problem for us here."
The Problem
  • Your team has little to work with while call volume is down, so a strong intake process is not yet being put to full use
  • There is no call tracking that we could find, so you cannot see which marketing source a new call came from
  • Your Spanish-speaking staff are an advantage that no marketing is putting in front of Spanish-speaking callers yet
Blind Spot: Strong intake can hide a weak top of the funnel. When a team converts the calls it gets, the real limit on new cases is the number of calls coming in.
The Positive
  • Trained staff handle intake and screening, so you are not answering every call yourself
  • After-hours and weekend coverage is already in place
The Gap
  • Every call that does not come in is a case your team is ready to convert but never gets the chance to
  • Without call-level tracking, you cannot tell which channel produces signed cases, which matters when you want to know any investment is working
  • Spanish-speaking prospects who might call a firm that speaks their language are not being reached
A team that already knows how to convert calls means every new lead you generate is more likely to become the steady caseload you want.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • The Free Case Evaluation button and click-to-call number are above the fold, which makes it easy for a visitor to reach the firm by phone
  • The contact form sits in the footer and asks for a name, email, and message but no phone number or type of case
  • No live chat was found on the site, and with no call tracking or Meta pixel, there is no way to see which visitors reached out or to follow up with those who did not

Team Assessment

Kerry is the only attorney, supported by five staff who run intake and day-to-day case work, so capacity is not the constraint but every case, decision, and growth task still runs through him. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • Kerry is the only attorney, so every case and client relationship runs through you
  • No one on the team owns marketing, and for the last couple of years the firm has focused on working its existing caseload instead
  • The move to O'Brien Law Group PC means a new website, Google listing, and directory updates, and that work falls to you on top of casework
Blind Spot: A team that is built for a caseload of 200 to 300 and is working about 60 today has capacity that is going unused.
The Positive
  • A five-person team that includes an office manager, case managers, a legal assistant, and a secretary
  • Several team members speak Spanish, and intake is already delegated so you are not the bottleneck on new calls
The Gap
  • Without more cases, the capacity of the team you have built goes unused
  • Marketing and rebranding tasks sit with you on top of casework, with no one else responsible for them
  • With one attorney responsible for both casework and growth, the firm has little cushion when new cases slow, as they have now
A steady stream of new cases puts your team's capacity to work and gives you the peace of mind of knowing next year is already filled.

Profit Plan Assessment

Kerry reports the firm is doing well financially, with roughly $1.8 to $2 million in revenue projected this year, but contingency-fee income follows the caseload, and the caseload is shrinking as cases settle. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"…we do great where we are financially."
The Problem
  • Contingency fees mean income follows the caseload, so as existing cases settle and fewer new ones arrive, revenue follows them down
  • With no marketing spend to measure yet, there is no baseline for what it costs to bring in a case or what that case returns
  • Case values range widely, from settlements as low as $10,000 to a policy limit of a million dollars this year, which makes income hard to predict without a steady flow of cases
Blind Spot: On contingency work, the cases you sign this month turn into income months from now. The dip you feel today began when signings slowed, so signing new cases now protects income later.
The Positive
  • Kerry reports the firm is financially comfortable and doing very well
  • The firm has produced strong results, including a million-dollar policy limit this year
The Gap
  • Without cost-per-case tracking, a marketing investment would be judged by feel rather than by numbers, and you have said you want to know it will work out
  • A goal of at least 10 new cases a month gives a clear number to plan spending and returns around
  • Revenue will keep following the caseload down until new cases arrive in a predictable flow
Knowing what each new case costs and returns lets you invest in growth with confidence and gives you the security of a year that is planned, not hoped for.
