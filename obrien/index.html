The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Website Audit

"We're going to try and get away from a law office, Kerry O'Brien, and just to O'Brien Law Group."
The Problem
  • The site footer still shows 2023, and the site still carries the old "Law Office Of Kerry P. O'Brien" name rather than the new O'Brien Law Group PC
  • Lighthouse performance test scores: 65/100 mobile, 92/100 desktop, with the main content taking about 7.5 seconds to appear on mobile
  • The contact form sits in the footer rather than near the top of the page
  • No video or Spanish-language content was found on the site, even though the firm has Spanish-speaking staff
Blind Spot: The site's tracking shows Google's tag but no Meta pixel and no call-tracking that we could find, so there is no way to see which visitors turn into phone calls or to follow up with those who leave.
The Positive
  • Kerry's bio and headshot are on the site, with 20+ years of personal injury and workers' compensation experience
  • A Free Case Evaluation button and a click-to-call phone number are above the fold, and the desktop experience loads quickly
The Gap
  • A slow mobile load costs visitors who are searching on their phones in the moment they need a lawyer
  • Visitors see an outdated brand that no longer matches the new firm name, which weakens trust at the moment they decide whether to call
  • Without tracking or retargeting, every visitor who leaves without calling is gone for good
A fast website under your new name that turns visitors into phone calls is the first step toward the steady flow of cases you want heading into 2027.

Profit Plan Assessment

Kerry reports the firm is doing well financially, with roughly $1.8 to $2 million in revenue projected this year, but contingency-fee income follows the caseload, and the caseload is shrinking as cases settle. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"…we do great where we are financially."
The Problem
  • Contingency fees mean income follows the caseload, so as existing cases settle and fewer new ones arrive, revenue follows them down
  • With no marketing spend to measure yet, there is no baseline for what it costs to bring in a case or what that case returns
  • Case values range widely, from settlements as low as $10,000 to a policy limit of a million dollars this year, which makes income hard to predict without a steady flow of cases
Blind Spot: On contingency work, the cases you sign this month turn into income months from now. The dip you feel today began when signings slowed, so signing new cases now protects income later.
The Positive
  • Kerry reports the firm is financially comfortable and doing very well
  • The firm has produced strong results, including a million-dollar policy limit this year
The Gap
  • Without cost-per-case tracking, a marketing investment would be judged by feel rather than by numbers, and you have said you want to know it will work out
  • A goal of at least 10 new cases a month gives a clear number to plan spending and returns around
  • Revenue will keep following the caseload down until new cases arrive in a predictable flow
