Here Is Where O'Brien Law Group PC Stands Today

Kerry O'Brien has practiced personal injury law for 25 years, focused primarily on motor vehicle accidents, and recently incorporated his Los Alamitos firm as O'Brien Law Group PC. His caseload has come down to about 60 cases as existing matters settle, and with no marketing system in place to replace them, the gap between today's firm and the steady pipeline he wants is the single most important finding in this audit.

Reaching the next stage means never again wondering where the next clients are coming from. A steady, predictable flow of cases is the foundation every higher stage is built on, and it is exactly where we are taking you.

  • Your website and Google listing still carry the old Law Office of Kerry O'Brien name, and Google ties your listing to another business's address

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Website Audit

"I do have to revamp my website and everything else to transition over."
The Problem
  • The site footer reads © 2023, and there have been no visible updates since then
  • The site still carries the old "Law Office Of Kerry P. O'Brien" brand while the firm is transitioning to O'Brien Law Group PC
  • Lighthouse performance test (a lab test, not an official PageSpeed Insights report): Mobile 65/100 — Desktop 92/100, with a mobile largest-content load time of 7.5 seconds
  • The only contact form we found sits in the footer rather than near the top of the page, and no Spanish-language pages or video were observed
Blind Spot: We found a Google tag on the site but no Meta (Facebook and Instagram) tracking pixel. Once social campaigns begin, every visitor who does not call on the first visit would leave with no way to follow up unless the pixel is installed first.
The Positive
  • A "Free Case Evaluation" button and a click-to-call phone number are visible at the top of the homepage
  • Your attorney bio and headshot give visitors a face and a track record, and a blog and legal definitions section already exist
  • The site is built on a mobile-responsive template, and desktop performance is strong
The Gap
  • A 7.5-second mobile load is slow for visitors who are often searching on a phone in the days after an accident, which puts calls at risk before they happen
  • An outdated look and the old firm name make it harder for a new visitor to see the firm you have become
  • Your Spanish-speaking staff cannot help site visitors who search in Spanish, because there is no Spanish content to find
A fast website that reflects the new O'Brien Law Group brand turns 25 years of reputation into the steady flow of calls you want in 2027.
