Intake Assessment

Vijay Law’s intake path starts with a phone number and consultation button, but the public record shows one attorney, no listed staff, and a form at the bottom of the page. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • The site lists one attorney and no support staff, so inquiries likely depend on Vijay being available between court dates
  • The consultation form is at the bottom of the homepage, so visitors who do not want to call right away have to hunt for it
  • No after-hours coverage, response-time commitment or scheduling tool was observed on the site
  • A few third-party client reviews mention slow responsiveness or communication
The Positive
  • A phone number and “Request a Consultation” button appear above the fold, so the primary conversion path is easy to find
  • The overwhelming majority of Avvo reviews are five-star (96% of 70), showing that clients who do engage are very happy with the experience
The Gap
  • Criminal defense and DUI prospects are often in crisis and call several firms — the first to answer is frequently the one that gets the case
  • Each slow response is a small share of reviews today, but it is the kind of impression that costs future signed clients
  • Without a defined first-response process, extra marketing would simply send more calls to the same bottleneck
A reliable intake process means the cases you work to earn actually come through the door — without everything depending on you answering every call.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • The contact form sits at the very bottom of the homepage under “Request A Consultation,” and is also reachable through the navigation
  • No live chat was observed, so visitors browsing outside business hours have only a phone number or a form to reach the firm
  • Whether the form sends an immediate confirmation or a same-day follow-up was not confirmed and should be tested live

These findings are also referenced in the Website Audit section.

Team Assessment

Vijay Law presents publicly as a one-attorney firm, so every hearing, consultation and case decision runs through one person. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: RED
The Problem
  • Only Vijay Dinakar is shown on the website — no other attorneys, paralegals or staff are listed
  • Nine practice areas, from DUI and domestic violence defense to family law and immigration consequences, all rest on one attorney’s time
  • Trial work pulls an attorney out of the office for long stretches, leaving little room for business development or management
  • Roles, accountability and a team structure were not visible in any public source
Blind Spot: Some third-party reviews refer to “he and his team,” which suggests support exists — but if it is not visible or clearly structured, prospects and Google both see a one-person firm.
The Positive
  • More than 18 years of experience, including trial work, is a strong foundation for training and leading a team
  • Client reviews suggest some support is already in place — a starting point for building defined roles
The Gap
  • When one person is the firm, a week away from work means a week of lost momentum
  • Without defined roles, new cases add to Vijay’s workload instead of to the firm’s capacity
  • Growth without a team structure turns into longer hours, not more freedom
A firm that runs on a clear structure is what gives an owner back his calendar.

Profit Plan Assessment

Public research cannot show Vijay Law’s revenue, margins or cost to acquire a client, which makes these numbers the first thing to establish before any growth investment. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
The Problem
  • Financial visibility could not be assessed from public sources and should be confirmed with the owner
  • Cost per signed case across criminal defense, DUI and family law is unlikely to be tracked unless a system exists for it
  • With one attorney, income is tied directly to the hours Vijay personally works
  • Without a profit target, it is hard to know how much to invest in marketing or in a team
The Gap
  • Decisions about hiring, marketing and pricing are made on instinct instead of on numbers
  • Spending without a target makes it hard to tell which investments are actually paying back
  • Growth that is not planned for profit can simply add work without adding take-home income
Knowing your numbers is what lets growth turn into income you keep.
