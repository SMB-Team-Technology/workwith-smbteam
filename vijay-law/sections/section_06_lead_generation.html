Lead Generation

Vijay Law’s reputation is stronger than its reach: a 4.9 Avvo rating and a domestic violence restraining order page that ranks well show real credibility, yet the firm was not seen in most of the broader San Jose criminal defense, DUI and family law searches where clients are looking. The sections below show where the visibility gaps are and where the opportunity is largest.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The consultation form is at the very bottom of the homepage rather than where a worried visitor first looks — the phone number and consultation button above the fold carry the full conversion load
  • Page speed: mobile and desktop PageSpeed scores were not available at the time of this review and will be tested live so we can show you exact numbers
  • Nine practice areas are listed, but city-specific pages for the surrounding Bay Area communities were not observed in the navigation or footer
  • No video was evident on the homepage, and the “Library” blog shows no post dates, so it is unclear whether content is being kept fresh
Blind Spot: Public bios indicate Vijay speaks Hindi, Kannada, Telugu and Tamil, yet the website is English only. If that is accurate, a large community of prospective clients is not being spoken to in their own language.
The Positive
  • A clear personal brand — “VIJAY = VICTORY” — with an attorney bio highlighting more than 18 years of trial experience
  • A prominent phone number and “Request a Consultation” button above the fold, plus a dedicated domestic violence restraining order page that ranks in search
The Gap
  • Visitors who are ready to act but do not want to call right away have to scroll to the bottom of the page to find a form
  • Without service-and-city pages, the site gives Google little reason to show it for the broader searches competitors are winning
  • With nine practice areas and few dedicated landing pages, visitors are asked to work out for themselves which service fits their situation
A website that works for you around the clock is what lets cases arrive without Vijay having to be the one chasing them.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Organic search testing showed Vijay Law for the domestic violence/criminal defense searches in 2 of 3 queries, but only 1 of 3 DUI queries, 0 of 2 family law queries and 0 of 1 child abuse defense query
  • Google review volume appears to trail the largest local competitor, based on third-party data — the Google Business Profile will be reviewed live to confirm exact counts
  • The firm name is shown inconsistently across the web — “Vijay Law,” “Vijay Dinakar Law Firm” and “Dinakar Law Firm” — which weakens the consistency Google looks for
Blind Spot: Yelp lists the firm under DUI Law, while the website leads with criminal and family law. Mixed category signals can make it harder for Google to know which searches the firm should show up for.
The Positive
  • Vijay Dinakar holds a 4.9 rating across 70 client reviews on Avvo (96% five-star), with Client’s Choice awards in 2019, 2020 and 2022 — a genuine reputation asset
The Gap
  • That strong third-party reputation is not yet matched by the same level of visibility in the broader searches where new clients start
  • Competitors with multiple offices and dedicated pages for DUI, domestic violence and child abuse defense are showing up where Vijay Law is not
Ahmed & Sukaram — appears across nearly every criminal defense and DUI search we ran, with offices in San Jose and Redwood City, dedicated pages for each service, and a review base that appears materially larger than Vijay Law’s on third-party sites.
Being found for more of the searches that matter is how a respected solo practice builds a steadier pipeline of cases.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Google search ads for Vijay Law were not observed for San Jose criminal defense, DUI, family law or child abuse defense searches in this review
  • Search ads could not be rendered during the research session, so this is a gap to confirm live rather than a conclusion that none are running
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Ahmed & Sukaram — appears in most criminal defense and DUI searches organically and is the firm most likely to be bidding on the same terms; their live ad copy will be pulled before we meet
  • Magdalena Law Group — shows up across criminal defense, DUI, restraining order and custody searches, giving them multiple chances to capture the same clients
The Opportunity
  • “San Jose DUI lawyer” and “DUI attorney San Jose” — high-intent searches where the firm was not seen organically
  • “San Jose domestic violence attorney” and “San Jose criminal defense attorney” — Vijay’s signature niche, where paid placement can reinforce the organic ranking he already has
  • Urgency searches such as “arrested for DUI San Jose what now” and neighboring-city terms across Santa Clara and Alameda Counties, which the site mentions but does not target with city pages
  • Cross-border and international family law — a narrow niche on the site that few local competitors appear to emphasize, and one that fits a multilingual attorney
