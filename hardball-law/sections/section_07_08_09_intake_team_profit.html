Intake Assessment

As a solo practitioner, Susan personally handles case intake and selection for every inquiry that comes in — with no dedicated intake team, no defined follow-up process, and no confirmed after-hours coverage. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating. This is where the biggest immediate ROI lives.

Status: RED
The Problem
  • You personally handle every intake call and case-selection decision — no delegation is in place
  • No after-hours coverage is confirmed — PI emergencies happen at all hours, and an unanswered after-hours call often goes to the next firm on the list
  • No CRM or intake tracking system is described — leads and follow-ups depend entirely on your personal bandwidth
  • Live chat is not present, and the contact form's above-the-fold visibility on the homepage was not confirmed
Blind Spot: You may not realize how many people visit your website after hours, cannot get an immediate response, and call the next firm on their list before morning.
The Positive
  • Every prospective client who reaches you gets direct access to a 40-year attorney rather than a junior associate or call center — a genuine differentiator in a market where volume firms often route calls elsewhere
The Gap
  • Every after-hours call that goes unanswered is a case that may never call back — direct lost revenue
  • Personally handling every intake call caps how many new client conversations you can realistically have on top of running active cases
  • Without a tracked or defined process, there is no way to know how many leads are dropped or delayed before they ever get a decision
Fixing intake means you get to choose the cases that come through — instead of hoping the right ones happen to catch you at the right moment.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • A contact form exists on the site (confirmed on the /contact-us/ and attorney-profile pages), but its exact above-the-fold placement on the homepage was not independently confirmed
  • No live chat is present on the site — visitors who aren't ready to call have no other way to reach the firm
  • 24/7 phone contact is prominently emphasized above the fold, which puts the entire real-time intake burden on someone answering the phone in the moment

These findings are also referenced in the Website Audit section.

Team Assessment

As a true solo practice, there is no one else to hand off client intake, marketing, or day-to-day operations to — everything currently runs through Susan personally. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: RED
"Solo practitioner who partners with larger firms on complex cases to avoid high overhead."
The Problem
  • No dedicated staff — you are solo, partnering with outside firms only when a case is too complex to run alone
  • No one internally to delegate marketing, lead follow-up, or case intake to
  • Every firm-level decision and day-to-day operational task runs through one person
  • The high-overhead model you're explicitly trying to avoid still requires your personal time on every case that comes through the door
Blind Spot: Partnering with larger firms on complex cases works today, but it may be masking how much your own personal capacity is the actual ceiling on growth — there is no team standing by to absorb more volume even if better leads started coming in tomorrow.
The Positive
  • Deliberately avoiding a high-overhead staffing model has kept the practice lean and let you stay selective about the cases you take on personally — a foundation a self-managing team can be built on top of, rather than replacing
The Gap
  • Every hour spent on intake, marketing decisions, or admin work is an hour not spent on casework or with clients — what owner dependency costs in time and freedom
  • There is no path to taking real time away from the practice without work stalling
  • Complex-case referrals to outside firms are a release valve, not a scalable team structure
A self-managing team is what turns "selective by necessity" into selective by choice — the exact freedom you're building toward.

Profit Plan Assessment

Neither revenue nor a fee structure was disclosed on the discovery call, meaning there is currently no documented financial visibility into the practice at all. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: RED
The Problem
  • No revenue figure — gross or net — was stated on the call
  • No fee structure was confirmed (contingency is standard for PI practice generally, but this was not explicitly stated)
  • No case-value or cost-per-case tracking was described
  • A revenue goal cannot be set using the standard default of 2x current revenue, because current revenue itself is unknown
Blind Spot: Without tracking which case types are actually most profitable once your time and referral costs are factored in, some of the complex cases you refer out could be more valuable to keep than they appear — and vice versa.
The Gap
  • Without a clear revenue baseline, it's impossible to know whether new marketing spend is actually paying for itself — what lack of financial visibility costs
  • No visibility into per-case profitability means growth could mean more work without more take-home pay
  • A revenue goal — the anchor for the entire ad-spend and ROI plan — cannot be set until this is established
Freedom starts with knowing your numbers — you can't plan the practice you want without first seeing clearly the one you have.
