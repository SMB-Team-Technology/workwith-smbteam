Here Is Where Hardball Law Stands Today

Susan R. Green, Esq. has practiced law in Maryland for over 40 years, building "Hardball Law" (The Law Offices of Susan R. Green, P.C.) as a solo personal injury practice in Towson focused on wrongful death, medical malpractice, and serious injury cases, partnering with larger firms on complex matters rather than staffing up. The single most important finding: despite four decades of earned reputation, no paid digital advertising has ever been run, her current SEO/radio spend produces zero ROI and attracts low-value cases, and a live NAP/brand inconsistency across directories is actively splitting her personal name recognition from the newer "Hardball Law" brand.

Stage 6 is where you get to be selective about the cases you take — not because you have to grind through volume to cover overhead, but because a reliable system fills your pipeline with the right cases.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • New cases still start with a referral or 40+ years of personal reputation — the current "city page" SEO and radio spend produce zero ROI and attract low-value cases
  • Susan handles her own case selection personally, partnering out to larger firms only when a case is too complex to run solo
  • No paid digital advertising (Google Ads, LSA, or Meta) has ever been run, even on the exact case types — wrongful death, med-mal, motorcycle — where named local competitors are outspending and outranking her
  • A live NAP/brand split has the firm listed under two different addresses and two different names ("Hardball Law" vs. "Law Offices of Susan R. Green, P.C.") across active directories

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
Closing that gap starts with turning four decades of earned trust into a predictable system for filling the pipeline — so Susan keeps choosing her cases instead of chasing them.
