Lead Generation

Eddington Law Firm is still signing about 10 cases a month with marketing paused, which says the firm's reputation and referrals are doing real work — but a new website and brand launching in the next month or two means the next few weeks decide how well the firm captures demand when marketing restarts. This section covers what we could confirm about the current presence, and where the relaunch can be sharper.

Website Audit

"We've kind of stopped our marketing while our new marketing people redo our website."
The Problem
  • The current site is the pre-rebrand version, and the new site is still a month or two from launch, so prospects today see a site the firm is already planning to replace
  • Site speed scores (mobile and desktop) could not be measured in this review, so no scores are shown here — we will run them live with you so the numbers are confirmed
  • The contact form sits near the bottom of the page instead of above the fold, so a visitor has to scroll to find it
  • The reviews page shows 10 undated testimonials with no overall rating and no link to Google, which limits how much trust they build
  • The site describes a four-state footprint and a start date that did not match how the firm described itself on the call — worth confirming and correcting in the rebrand
Blind Spot: When a site is rebuilt, changing page addresses without proper redirects can erase search rankings the firm has already earned. The launch is the moment to confirm that is handled with your new marketing partner.
The Positive
  • A clear "Call for a Free Consultation" phone link sits above the fold, so a visitor who wants to call can do it immediately
  • Attorney bios and featured case results are already on the site, giving the rebuild real credibility to build on
The Gap
  • Visitors who want to submit a form instead of calling have to hunt for it, and some will leave
  • No blog or video content was detected in our review, so the site has little to show a prospect who is still deciding
  • Without a verified speed baseline, there is no way to tell whether the rebuild improves on the site it replaces
A website that converts visitors without anyone chasing them is growth that does not add to the owners' daily workload.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Google Business Profile details and local map-pack positions for Denver car, truck, motorcycle, and wrongful death searches were not verified in this review — we will confirm them live rather than guess
  • We could not confirm the firm's Google review total in this review; what we saw suggests a much smaller base than the leading Denver personal injury firms
  • Directory listings appear to show at least three different Denver addresses, and one directory shows a non-Colorado phone number
Blind Spot: Inconsistent name, address, and phone details across directories are easy to overlook, but search engines use them to decide how much to trust a business listing. A rebrand launch is the cheapest moment to clean them up.
The Positive
  • The firm has a presence on Google, Yelp, Avvo, Justia, and FindLaw, and third-party snippets suggest clients rate the firm highly
The Gap
  • Competitors with far more reviews are favored when Google decides who to show and when a prospect decides who to call
  • Stale listings with old addresses send mixed signals to Google and to prospects at the moment they are choosing a firm
Bachus & Schanker — carries one of the largest review bases among Denver personal injury firms, which strengthens both local map visibility and the trust a prospect feels before calling.
A strong local profile brings in cases on its own, so the owners are not the ones pulling every new client in.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • On the call, the firm shared that marketing is paused while the new site is built, so a gap in search ads right now is expected
  • We could not review live Google results in this report, so which ads, if any, are currently showing for Denver personal injury searches was not confirmed
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Zaner Law, Anderson Hemmat, and Dan Caplis Law — all show up in organic results for Denver truck accident searches; whether they also run paid ads was not verified in this review
  • Frickey Law Firm and Boesen Law — also visible organically for the same searches, and worth checking live for paid presence during the call
The Opportunity
  • Core terms across the firm's practice areas: Denver car accident, truck accident, motorcycle accident, and wrongful death lawyer
  • Case types that are narrower and often less crowded: rideshare accident, premises liability, and product liability searches
  • Aviation accident and insurance litigation searches, where a specialty page can win cases the broader firms are not targeting
  • Competitor-name and neighboring-city terms, plus urgent searches like "hit by a semi truck Denver," so campaigns are ready to turn on the day the new site launches
Blind Spot: While your own campaigns are paused, competitors can bid on the firm's name and capture people who search for you directly.
