Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Cases are still coming in at about 10 a month, even with marketing paused during the website rebuild
  • The new site and brand are not live yet, so the current site is still the one prospects see
  • Several Denver competitors hold a far larger review base than Eddington appears to
A relaunch that is ready to capture demand matters only if the operation behind it can absorb new cases without pulling the owners deeper into the day-to-day.

Intake

  • An in-office intake person and a remote Spanish-speaking intake person are being trained, so intake is already delegated
  • A phone-routing problem trapped callers in a transfer loop for roughly four months
  • The intake platform is changing at the same time, which raises the risk of gaps during the switch
Intake that runs on a documented process is intake that does not need an attorney to step in.

Self-Managing Team

  • Both owners are involved in every case and every day brings something to fix
  • Tech problems and staff questions land on the owners instead of a single owner of operations
  • Hiring, onboarding, and SOPs are about to scale with more case managers and two associates planned
The goal is for the firm to keep running if one owner, or both, are away — and today it does not.

Profit

  • Profit planning and financial reporting were not discussed on the call, so there is no visibility to build on yet
  • Planned hires and technology changes add cost before any profit plan exists to measure them against
  • The firm has capital ready to deploy for marketing after the relaunch, which makes a clear spend plan important
Growth only pays off personally when it is planned to leave more profit behind, not just more work.
