Quick Wins for Eddington Law Firm

Give daily operations and tech issues one owner
Team Quick Win
Why It Matters
Both owners are involved in every case, and every day brings something else to fix. Each hour spent on a tech problem or staff question is an hour not spent growing the firm and signing cases.
The Opportunity
Put one accountable person in charge of the daily operation, so questions and problems go to them instead of to Wes and Adam.
Test every phone path before the new site launches
Intake Quick Win
Why It Matters
A transfer loop trapped callers for roughly four months, and two different phone numbers appear on the current site. A caller who cannot reach a person calls the next firm.
The Opportunity
Place test calls through both numbers and every routing path, including after hours, and confirm a live person or a clear next step answers each one before marketing restarts.
Put the contact form above the fold on the new site
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
On the current site, the contact form sits near the bottom of the page. Visitors who prefer to submit a form instead of calling have to scroll to find it.
The Opportunity
Make sure the relaunched site shows the form and the phone link in the first screen, and that the copy about offices and firm history matches the firm as it operates today.
Build a steady stream of Google reviews
Lead Gen Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Reviews influence both who Google shows and who a prospect decides to call, and the leading Denver firms carry far larger review bases.
Who Is Doing It Better
Bachus & Schanker — carries one of the largest review bases among Denver personal injury firms.
The Opportunity
Ask every signed client for a Google review as part of closing out the case, and track the count monthly so the gap narrows over time.
Clean up directory listings before the rebrand goes live
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
Directory listings appear to show at least three different Denver addresses and a non-Colorado phone number. Mixed details lower trust with Google and with prospects.
The Opportunity
A one-time cleanup so the new brand, address, and phone number match on every major directory the day the new site launches.
Build hiring, onboarding, and SOPs before more people start
Team Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Another case manager is starting soon and two associates are planned for next year. Without documented onboarding, each new person learns from whoever is nearest, which is usually an owner.
The Opportunity
Build the onboarding path and SOPs with your office manager so every new hire follows the same steps and the owners are not the training program.
Set a simple profit plan and spend budget
Profit Quick Win
Why It Matters
New hires, a technology switch, and a marketing relaunch are all coming, and no profit plan was described to guide how much to spend and when.
The Opportunity
Set a revenue and profit target and a simple monthly view of results, so each spend decision can be checked against what the firm keeps.
Write the intake and follow-up routine for the new platform
Intake Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Two intake team members are still in training while the platform is changing. A new tool without a defined routine leaves each person to figure out the process alone.
The Opportunity
Document the first response and the follow-up steps for every new lead, so the process lives in the firm and not in an owner's head.
