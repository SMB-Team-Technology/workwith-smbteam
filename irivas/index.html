Here Is Where Rivas Law Firm, PLLC Stands Today

Israel Rivas built Rivas Law Firm, PLLC into a $1.5–2M-a-year personal injury practice out of Pharr, Texas, handling car accident, 18-wheeler, and wrongful death cases with the help of contract attorneys who take on overflow casework. That growth has come almost entirely from word-of-mouth referrals — the firm has never run a single digital ad, has only 15 Google reviews against named local competitors with hundreds, and its own website lists a different address than every directory that lists the firm.

Stage 6 is the stage where doubling your revenue means more time with your family, not less — and it is exactly where we are taking you.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The live site (rivaslawrgv.com) consists of only a homepage and a contact page — pages the firm's own search listings reference, like /personal-injury and /meet-the-team, currently return 404 errors
  • PageSpeed mobile and desktop scores could not be measured this session (Google's API returned repeated quota errors on four attempts) — a manual check is recommended, but a two-page site with no live practice-area pages is itself a strong sign of an unmanaged web presence
  • The site is almost entirely in English despite a client base that is 95% Spanish-speaking — only one line on the homepage mentions bilingual service
  • No blog, no video, and no dedicated attorney bio page — only a brief "About Our Firm" section on the homepage
Blind Spot: Every prospect who clicks through to /personal-injury or /meet-the-team from a search result right now hits a dead page — those are exactly the pages Google is already trying to send people to.
The Positive
  • Clear calls to action above the fold — "Schedule Consultation" and "Call Now"
  • A genuine attorney bio already exists, with a headshot and settlement track record — the raw material for a stronger site is there, it just needs to be built out
The Gap
  • Every visitor who lands on a 404'd practice-area page is a lead lost before intake ever happens
  • A site with no Spanish-language practice-area content cannot convert the 95% of the client base that speaks Spanish
  • Without geo-targeted pages for McAllen, Edinburg, Mission, or San Juan, the firm is invisible to searches happening just outside Pharr
A website that actually converts is what turns the reputation you have already built into the digital case flow that funds real delegation — not more hours.
