Lead Generation

Rivas Law Firm has built a $1.5–2M-a-year personal injury practice almost entirely on word-of-mouth referrals, with two billboards as its only paid marketing. The firm has never run a single dollar of digital advertising, in a Rio Grande Valley market where three named competitors already dominate organic search, paid ads, and reviews.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The live site (rivaslawrgv.com) consists of only a homepage and a contact page — pages the firm's own search listings reference, like /personal-injury and /meet-the-team, currently return 404 errors
  • PageSpeed mobile and desktop scores could not be measured this session (Google's API returned repeated quota errors on four attempts) — a manual check is recommended, but a two-page site with no live practice-area pages is itself a strong sign of an unmanaged web presence
  • The site is almost entirely in English despite a client base that is 95% Spanish-speaking — only one line on the homepage mentions bilingual service
  • No blog, no video, and no dedicated attorney bio page — only a brief "About Our Firm" section on the homepage
Blind Spot: Every prospect who clicks through to /personal-injury or /meet-the-team from a search result right now hits a dead page — those are exactly the pages Google is already trying to send people to.
The Positive
  • Clear calls to action above the fold — "Schedule Consultation" and "Call Now"
  • A genuine attorney bio already exists, with a headshot and settlement track record — the raw material for a stronger site is there, it just needs to be built out
The Gap
  • Every visitor who lands on a 404'd practice-area page is a lead lost before intake ever happens
  • A site with no Spanish-language practice-area content cannot convert the 95% of the client base that speaks Spanish
  • Without geo-targeted pages for McAllen, Edinburg, Mission, or San Juan, the firm is invisible to searches happening just outside Pharr
A website that actually converts is what turns the reputation you have already built into the digital case flow that funds real delegation — not more hours.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Rivas Law Firm's directory-sourced review count is 15 at 4.7/5 — far below Leah Wise (782), Tijerina Legal Group (906), Ruy Mireles (115), and even Herrman & Herrman (72)
  • The firm did not appear at all across three separate searches for "car accident lawyer Pharr TX," "personal injury lawyer McAllen TX," or "18-wheeler accident lawyer Rio Grande Valley Texas" — while Tijerina Legal Group appeared in all three
  • The firm's own website lists "125 E. Caffery Ave, Pharr, TX" as its address, while every third-party directory checked (Avvo, Lawyers.com, Martindale, Yelp, AllUsInjuryLawyers) lists "3920 S Jackson Rd, Pharr, TX" — a NAP mismatch that can directly suppress map-pack ranking
Blind Spot: Google treats a mismatched address between your own website and your directory listings as a trust-signal problem — it can quietly suppress your map-pack ranking even if everything else about your listing looks fine.
The Positive
  • The reviews that do exist are strong — a 4.7/5 rating shows there is no reputation problem here, only a volume problem
The Gap
  • 15 reviews against competitors with hundreds means prospects comparing options online see the competition as the safer, more proven choice
  • Zero visibility in local search for the firm's own core practice area means every one of those searches is going straight to a competitor instead
Leah Wise Law Firm, PLLC — 782 reviews at 5.0/5, recognized for Texas's largest PI settlement in 2020, and an estimated 2,000 organic visitors/month — more than 100x what Rivas Law Firm is currently getting.
Closing the review and visibility gap with these competitors is what makes the digital lead flow you need to double revenue actually possible.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • This firm does not appear to be running Google search ads for personal-injury or accident-related keywords in the Pharr/McAllen market — consistent with a marketing history built entirely on billboards and referrals
  • None of the firm's $60,000/year in paid marketing spend has ever gone toward search — every dollar has gone to two billboards instead
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Ruy Mireles Law Firm, PLLC — per the transcript, runs paid search ads specifically to capture Pharr-market demand from a McAllen base, despite having no physical office in Pharr
  • Leah Wise Law Firm, PLLC and Tijerina Legal Group, PC — both plausible paid-search spenders given their review volume and organic dominance, though specific ad copy was not captured this session
The Opportunity
  • "car accident lawyer Pharr TX" and "personal injury lawyer McAllen TX" — the two exact searches where the firm did not appear at all this session
  • "18-wheeler accident lawyer Rio Grande Valley" — a higher case-value keyword the firm is not competing for today
  • Bidding on competitor names (Ruy Mireles, Leah Wise, Tijerina Legal Group) to capture comparison-shopping searches
  • Spanish-language search campaigns — the single largest untapped opportunity given a 95% Spanish-speaking client base with zero Spanish paid presence today
Blind Spot: Ruy Mireles is already buying attention in your own market from an office in a different city — the market you think of as "yours" is already being contested with money, whether you compete or not.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • The firm does not appear to be running Local Service Ads — no Google Screened badge or LSA presence was identified this session
  • This is consistent with a firm that has never run any form of digital advertising to date
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Live LSA-specific competitor data was not confirmed this session (the LSA widget requires interactive rendering not available with current research tools) — a manual check is recommended before the proposal call
  • Given their existing review volume, Leah Wise Law Firm (782 reviews) and Tijerina Legal Group (906 reviews) would be strong candidates for LSA slots once verified live
The Opportunity
  • With a 4.7/5 rating and 15 reviews, Rivas Law Firm already qualifies for Google Screened / LSA eligibility in most markets — the review base just needs a modest boost first
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Being one of the first PI firms in the Pharr market to claim LSA presence is a low-cost way to appear above billboards and referrals combined
Blind Spot: LSAs are pay-per-lead, not pay-per-click — meaning the $60,000/year currently spent on billboards could instead fund months of guaranteed, verified phone calls no billboard has ever produced.

The Opportunity
