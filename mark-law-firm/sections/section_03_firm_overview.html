Here Is Where Mark Law Firm Stands Today

Michael Mark has practiced law in New Jersey for 41 years and is a board-certified trial attorney, handling personal injury, municipal court and DWI matters, and probate litigation as a true solo practice from a home office. With no marketing of any kind and only a part-time helper, nearly everything runs through him personally, including the phone calls that go unanswered when he is busy. The expertise is clearly there. What is missing is a system that brings in the cases he prefers and lifts the routine work off his desk.

Stage 6 is where your experience is paid at its true value and the $10-an-hour tasks are no longer yours to do, and it is exactly where we are taking you.

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you

  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between where you are and the $2 to $4 million a year you have said is reasonable is not about your skill. It is about building the systems and support that let your time go to the work that is worth $500 an hour.
