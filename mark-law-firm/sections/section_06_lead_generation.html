Lead Generation

Mark Law Firm has been built over 41 years of trial work with no marketing behind it: in Michael's words, “I don’t do any marketing, none.” That leaves the firm largely invisible in the Ringwood and Passaic County searches where injury, DWI and probate clients look for an attorney.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The only action above the fold is a “Request a Consultation” button, while the contact form itself sits mid-page below the fold
  • No video, no blog and no educational content, so the site gives a prospect little reason to stay or to trust the firm before calling
  • Old and new domains are both in use: marknjlaw.com redirects to michaelmarklawfirm.com, and the firm email still uses the old domain
  • The website lists a P.O. Box in Ringwood while directories show a Hawthorne street address, sending mixed signals about where the firm is located
Blind Spot: Older pages on the previous domain are still showing up in search results, which splits the firm’s credibility across two web addresses instead of building it in one place.
The Positive
  • A dedicated attorney bio page highlights over four decades of trial experience and New Jersey Certified Civil Trial Attorney status
  • The site is live and current, with a clear consultation call-to-action in the hero section
The Gap
  • Visitors who are ready to call have no simple one-click way to reach you without scrolling
  • No content for the municipal court or probate litigation work you most want to grow, so those searches have nothing to find
  • Without a conversion-focused page for each practice area, the site cannot turn the traffic it does get into signed cases
A website that works while you are in court is the first step toward earning what your experience is worth without doing every task yourself.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Reviews are thin and scattered: directory profiles show a small number of reviews split across two duplicate listings, and the most recent we could find looks dated
  • The firm did not appear in the non-branded searches we ran for personal injury, DWI and probate litigation in Ringwood, Wanaque and Passaic County; it surfaced only when we searched for the firm by name
  • Name, address and phone do not match across the web: a Ringwood P.O. Box on the website, a Hawthorne street address on Avvo and other directories, and several firm-name variants
Blind Spot: Since you closed your physical office, an address that no longer matches your real situation can quietly hurt your Google Maps visibility and make prospects doubt which listing is really you.
The Positive
  • Your name search turns up your website, Avvo, Birdeye and other profiles, so there is a foundation of listings to clean up and build on
The Gap
  • Competitors with large review footprints and city-specific pages are what Ringwood and Passaic County searchers see first
  • Praise for your trial results, including a client thanking you for reducing his charges, is not being turned into a steady stream of fresh Google reviews
Birkhold & Maider, LLC — maintains dedicated Wanaque NJ and Passaic County pages, so they show up for the exact local searches your firm is missing.
A clean, well-reviewed local presence puts the cases you want in front of you, so you grow without having to chase them.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Personal injury: we could not observe any Google search ads from the firm for Ringwood or Passaic County injury searches. By your own account there is no marketing in place, so this looks like an open opportunity.
  • Municipal court / DWI and probate litigation: no firm ads were observed for these either, so the firm does not appear to be showing up for these high-intent searches
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Corradino & Partners — a strong organic presence for Ringwood injury searches through a dedicated “Personal Injury Lawyer in Ringwood NJ” page. We could not view live paid results to confirm their ad activity.
  • Jonathan Mincis — ranks across Ringwood and Passaic County DWI directory and search results as a certified Municipal Court Trial Lawyer. Ad activity was not observed.
The Opportunity
  • “DWI lawyer Ringwood NJ” and “Passaic County DWI lawyer”: high-intent searches in the municipal court work you prefer, where a simple DWI case can bring in $5,000 to $6,000
  • “Will contest attorney Passaic County NJ” and “probate litigation attorney New Jersey”: a single will contest has billed $50,000 to $60,000 within 12 months
  • Problem searches such as “driving while suspended lawyer” and “loss of license NJ”, which match the traffic and license matters you already handle
  • Neighboring towns such as Wanaque, Hawthorne and surrounding Passaic County communities, where prospects search by town and few firms have a dedicated page
Blind Spot: Paid ads only pay off if the phone is answered. With six or seven calls in one week reaching voicemail with no message left, ads without an intake fix would send money to waste.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • We could not view live Local Service Ads results, so we did not observe the firm in the LSA listings for injury, DWI or probate searches
  • No Google Screened badge was observed for the firm; with no active marketing, this is an open opportunity rather than a confirmed gap
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Corradino & Partners — competes for Ringwood-area injury searches with a large review base; LSA review figures were not available to us
  • Birkhold & Maider, LLC — has local-area pages in Wanaque and Passaic County; LSA position was not observed
The Opportunity
  • Becoming Google Screened gives a trust badge right at the top of the page for prospects searching for injury and DWI help
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • A steady flow of new Google reviews from your trial wins would strengthen your position, since review volume is a major ranking factor in LSAs
Blind Spot: Because LSAs are pay-per-lead and driven by phone calls, every missed call is a paid lead lost, so answering fast matters more here than in any other channel.
