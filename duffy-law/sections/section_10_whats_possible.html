Here's What's Possible for Duffy Law

Kristen has built a practice people trust enough to book and rebook for years, and the demand is strong enough that she has to turn work away. The question is not whether the firm can grow — it is whether it can grow without taking everything she has. It can, once the structure exists for capable people to carry the work.

1
You take a real vacation — two weeks, phone and laptop left behind — and the firm keeps running while you are gone.
2
You do roughly 20 percent of the work, the part you choose, while a team you trust handles the other 80 percent.
3
Clients reach the firm through the front door instead of your personal phone, and a week offline is simply a normal week.

This is not a fantasy. SMB Team can make this a reality for Duffy Law.
