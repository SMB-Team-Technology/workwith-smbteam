Lead Generation

Duffy Law's work comes from website bookings and clients who have trusted Kristen for five years or more — and there is more of it than the firm can handle. Lead generation is not what is holding this firm back today, so this section looks at how the firm shows up online as a foundation for when capacity is in place.

Website Audit

"People are booking calls on my website, which is great because it's a nice lead."
The Problem
  • The homepage headline, "Legal Services & Solutions," names no practice area and no city
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 58/100 — Desktop 89/100 (Lighthouse lab test; scores may differ by a few points from pagespeed.web.dev). Mobile largest content paint is 5.0 seconds
  • Nine practice areas are listed, including several you refer out, which dilutes your four main areas: estate, real estate, business and tax
  • No blog, no video and no location-specific pages for surrounding areas were found
Blind Spot: Listing personal injury, wrongful death and family law on purpose to refer them out also means those callers book time with you first. Each of those calls costs you attention even when the matter leaves the firm.
The Positive
  • A clear "Schedule a consultation today" call to action and a Book Your Appointment button sit above the fold
  • Your attorney bio highlights the rare combination of attorney and CPA
  • A revamp is already planned with your own website developer by the end of the year
The Gap
  • A slow mobile experience can lose visitors before they book
  • A generic headline gives visitors no quick reason to believe you are the right estate, business or tax attorney
  • The team behind you is largely invisible on the site, which makes the firm look like one person
A website that pre-qualifies callers and shows a real team is one more way to get your phone, your evenings and your vacations back.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Your Avvo profile lists litigation, criminal, real estate, tax and land use — estate planning, your stated number-one practice area, is not among them
  • Your Yelp listing is categorized as Real Estate Law, not estate planning, business or tax
  • Whether the firm appears in the Google local map pack for estate planning, real estate closing, or business and tax searches in Chicago could not be observed in our research and should be checked live
Blind Spot: A strong review base is useful only if searchers find you for the work you want. If directory categories point toward matters you would rather refer out, the reputation you have built may be attracting the wrong calls.
The Positive
  • Your address and phone number are consistent across your website and the directories we reviewed
  • Avvo shows a Clients' Choice award and a top attorney rating, and third-party listings point to a deep base of client reviews (not independently confirmed)
The Gap
  • Estate planning, business and tax are the matters you want more of, yet your directory profiles lead with other practice areas
  • No location-specific landing pages were detected for Chicago neighborhoods or surrounding communities
Bradford Miller Law, P.C. — works the same Chicago real estate closing and estate planning niche, which makes its listings a useful benchmark when tightening your own categories and profile.
Getting the right matters to find you is how the firm stays busy with work you enjoy — without the work you do not.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Estate planning: Paid search ads for this firm were not observed for estate planning searches in Chicago, and ad history was not discussed on the call.
  • Real estate law: Ads were not observed for real estate closing searches in Chicago.
  • Business and tax law: Ads were not observed for business attorney or tax attorney searches in Chicago.
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Live paid-search results could not be reviewed in our research, so no competitor ad messaging is reported here and none should be assumed.
The Opportunity
  • Paid search is not a current priority — demand already exceeds capacity — and becomes relevant only after intake and team capacity are in place.
  • When it is, the obvious searches are "estate planning attorney Chicago," "wills and trusts lawyer Chicago," "Chicago real estate closing attorney," and "Chicago business attorney."
  • A less obvious angle is "tax attorney CPA Chicago," which speaks directly to your dual credential, and Loop and downtown-specific searches.
  • Capturing these searches later would mean choosing which matters to grow rather than taking whatever calls arrive.
Blind Spot: More paid leads would land on the same overloaded intake path. Fix who answers the phone first, then add volume.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Local Service Ads for this firm were not observed in our research, and the Google Screened status could not be confirmed.
  • This is an opportunity gap to evaluate, not a confirmed absence.
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Live LSA results could not be reviewed in our research, so no competitor LSA listings are reported here.
The Opportunity
  • Third-party listings point to a deep review base, which would be a strong starting point for LSAs once those reviews are verified on Google.
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Because LSA leads arrive as phone calls, they make sense only once someone other than you is answering them.
