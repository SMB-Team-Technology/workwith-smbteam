Quick Wins for Duffy Law

Align Directory Categories With Estate, Business and Tax
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
Your Avvo profile does not list estate planning, your stated number-one area, and your Yelp listing is categorized as Real Estate Law. Directories may be steering callers toward matters you would rather refer out.
The Opportunity
Update categories and descriptions so estate, business and tax work is what searchers see first, bringing in the matters you want more of and fewer calls you have to pass along.
Have Your Admin Screen Booked Calls First
Intake Quick Win
Why It Matters
Calls booked through your website land on you, and a single long call can leave you stuck on the phone while review work piles up. The team member who handles phones is underused.
The Opportunity
Give your admin a short screening script and clear rules for which calls need an attorney, so you take only the calls that need you and hand off the rest.
Define Who Can Finalize Which Work Without Your Review
Team Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Work from your paralegal and legal assistant goes through you before it leaves the firm, so the review queue sets the pace for the whole firm and the day ends late.
The Opportunity
Sort work into what is final without review, what needs a quick check and what needs your full attention, so your review time goes only where it matters.
Get Billing Current and Add a Monthly Profit Check
Profit Quick Win
Why It Matters
Billing is behind, and unbilled time is earned revenue that has not yet been invoiced. Without a regular view of profit, it is hard to know whether more work means more take-home.
The Opportunity
Set a billing cadence, catch up on open items, and review a simple monthly profit snapshot so hiring and growth decisions are made with real numbers.
Turn the Planned Website Revamp Into a Screening Tool
Lead Gen Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
The homepage headline is generic, mobile speed scored 58/100 in our lab test, and nine practice areas are listed, including several you refer out. Your own developer is already planning changes.
The Opportunity
Lead with estate, real estate, business and tax, improve mobile speed, and use the booking step to sort callers, which makes a consultation fee or intake questions easier to apply.
Move Client Communication Off Your Personal Phone
Intake Quick Win
Why It Matters
Long-time clients text your personal phone, so the work follows you into evenings and time off, and you are the only one who sees those messages.
The Opportunity
Set up a firm number or shared inbox that your team monitors and tell clients where to reach the firm, so your personal phone stops being the front door.
Write the Associate Role and Hiring Plan Before You Recruit
Team Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
You want to hire an attorney but are unsure how to trust the hire and worried about training someone who then leaves. Without a defined role, the cost of not hiring is paid in your own hours.
The Opportunity
Define the role, the work it takes over, how success is measured and how the firm name and career path are addressed, so the hire is a plan rather than a risk.
