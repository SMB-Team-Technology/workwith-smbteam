Here Is Where Duffy Law Stands Today

Kristen Duffy is an attorney and CPA who runs a downtown Chicago firm focused on estate, real estate, business and tax law, with a small team supporting her. Demand is not the problem — she is turning work away — but every work product, every booked call and every hiring decision still runs through her, and she has not had a true vacation since 2016.

Stage 6 is where Duffy Law runs without you at the center — the stage that lets you leave your phone and computer behind for a week and come back to a firm that kept running.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • Demand exceeds capacity — work is being referred out because you cannot keep up with it
  • Booked calls from your website land on you personally, and long-time clients text your personal phone
  • Paralegal and legal-assistant work all routes through you for review before it goes out
  • No true vacation since 2016, and no clear plan yet for the associate hire that would change that

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between where you are and the firm that lets you take two weeks away is not about finding more work — it is about building the structure that lets capable people carry it.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Duffy Law
Duffy Law sits at the Solo Practitioner stage: although there is a team in place, the firm's judgment, review and intake all still depend on Kristen personally. The most urgent limiting gap is not lead generation but delegation — without a way to hand off intake, review and hiring, additional demand only adds to her workload. That gap sits directly between the firm today and the goal she described: doing 20 percent of the work and delegating the other 80 percent to competent people.
