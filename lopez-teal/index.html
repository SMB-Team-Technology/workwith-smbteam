Here Is Where Lopez Teal Stands Today

Lopez Teal Real Estate Law & Title is a boutique Coral Gables firm built by Annette Lopez Teal around a high-value niche: luxury residential real estate closings in Coral Gables and Coconut Grove, where individual files run as high as $15,000 compared to $2,000 for standard-volume work. Revenue has grown steadily since 2019 to a projected ~$1.3M in 2026, but every dollar of it still runs through one channel — word-of-mouth referrals from realtors and brokers — with Annette personally carrying the entire client relationship and business-development load. The single most important finding is that the firm has zero owned demand generation and a legal-directory footprint still filed under Annette's prior name and address, meaning its enterprise value is tied almost entirely to one person's referral network rather than to a system that could run, and sell, without her.

Stage 6 is what makes a sellable asset possible — a firm valued for its systems and diversified revenue instead of for Annette's personal referral relationships, and it is exactly where we are taking you.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • 100% of new business comes from individual referrals from realtors and brokers — no marketing, SEO, or paid lead generation of any kind
  • Annette is the firm's sole rainmaker, working 50-60+ hours a week to keep the referral pipeline alive
  • Legal directory listings (Avvo, Yelp, Justia, FindLaw, Martindale) are still filed under Annette's prior legal name and a different office address, undermining visibility under the current "Lopez Teal" brand
  • No geo-targeted local SEO pages exist for the Coral Gables/Coconut Grove niche the firm serves, while at least one direct competitor already has one

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
Closing the gap between a referral-only practice and a firm that runs on its own systems is exactly what makes Lopez Teal sellable at a 4-5x revenue multiple instead of the 2-3x multiple referral-only firms typically command.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.
