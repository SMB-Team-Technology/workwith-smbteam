Here Is Where Lopez Teal Stands Today

Lopez Teal Real Estate Law & Title is a boutique Coral Gables firm built by Annette Lopez Teal around a high-value niche: luxury residential real estate closings in Coral Gables and Coconut Grove, where individual files run as high as $15,000 compared to $2,000 for standard-volume work.
