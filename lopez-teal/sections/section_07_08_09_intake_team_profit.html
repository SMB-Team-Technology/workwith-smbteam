Team Assessment

Lopez Teal has five staff and one part-time attorney, but every meaningful client relationship — and all of the business development that drives the firm — still runs through Annette alone. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: RED
"Annette is 'drowning'."
The Problem
  • Annette is the sole rainmaker, personally carrying every referral relationship and closing every file
  • 50-60+ hour work weeks with no structural relief in sight
  • No leadership layer exists between Annette and the five-person staff
  • No plan currently exists for who would carry client relationships if Annette stepped back
Blind Spot: The fact that referrals keep flowing to Annette personally can feel like success — but it also means the firm's entire value is tied to her continued, hands-on involvement, which is the opposite of a sellable asset.
The Positive
  • A five-person staff plus a part-time attorney already exists — real infrastructure to build delegation on rather than starting from zero
The Gap
  • Every hour Annette spends managing referral relationships personally is an hour she isn't spending building the diversified, institutional pipeline she says she wants
  • No one else in the firm is currently positioned to carry a client relationship end-to-end
  • A buyer will discount a firm heavily if it cannot survive the owner stepping back — right now, this one could not
Reducing Annette's personal dependence is not optional — it is the mechanism that turns Lopez Teal from Annette's referral book into a business someone else could run, and eventually buy.

Profit Plan Assessment

Revenue has grown steadily since 2019 to a projected ~$1.3M in 2026, and Annette already understands which files are most profitable — but that visibility hasn't yet been translated into a plan for building enterprise value. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"The firm's luxury niche (Coral Gables, Coconut Grove) provides high-profit files ($15k vs. $2k), reducing the need for high-volume work."
The Problem
  • No firm-wide margin or profit-plan visibility was discussed beyond file-level economics
  • No specific revenue goal was stated — the stated objective is a sale-multiple target (moving from a 2-3x to a 4-5x multiple), not a revenue number
  • No fee structure (contingency, hourly, or flat) was disclosed on the call
  • Referral-only firms typically trade at a 2-3x revenue multiple versus 4-5x for diversified, institutional-client firms — and that gap is a direct cost to enterprise value today
Blind Spot: Growing revenue can feel like progress, but revenue alone doesn't change what the firm is worth at sale — multiple, not top-line, is what matters when the goal is an exit.
The Positive
  • Annette already tracks profitability at the file level — knowing luxury niche files run $15k versus $2k for non-luxury work is real financial visibility most referral-only firms don't have
The Gap
  • File-level profitability insight hasn't yet been extended into a firm-wide profit plan or enterprise-value roadmap
  • Without a diversified revenue base, the firm is capped at a referral-only 2-3x sale multiple instead of the 4-5x multiple institutional-client firms command
  • No number has been attached to "sellable" yet — without one, progress toward the goal can't be measured
The gap between a 2-3x and a 4-5x multiple is not a marketing problem — it's a profit-and-enterprise-value plan, and building one is what turns Annette's five-year goal into a number she can actually track.
