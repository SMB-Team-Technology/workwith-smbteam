Quick Wins for Current Shift Legal

Automate the Path From Intake Form to Engagement Letter
Intake Quick Win
Why It Matters
Prospects pass through a form, a conflict check, and "several touch points" before they see a person, and scoping, engagement letters, and onboarding are all manual afterward. Every one of those steps is team time spent on admin.
The Opportunity
Automating this path could leave a human involved only on the 30-minute intake call — exactly the intake experience you described — and give the team those hours back.
Automate Scheduling and Rescheduling
Intake Quick Win
Why It Matters
Scheduling and rescheduling back-and-forth is handled by hand, adding touch points for prospects and clients and pulling the team's attention away from higher-value work.
The Opportunity
Self-service scheduling that books, confirms, and reschedules without staff involvement is one of the fastest ways to remove manual steps from the front of the process.
Replace Manual Dashboards With Automated Financial Reporting
Profit Quick Win
Why It Matters
Reporting is semi-manual — data is pulled from separate billing and accounting tools and plugged into an Excel report. That is recurring time spent assembling numbers instead of acting on them.
The Opportunity
A dashboard that refreshes itself from the systems you already use would give leadership a current picture of margin and billable time without anyone rebuilding the report by hand.
Build a Matter Follow-Up System So Nothing Goes Quiet
Team Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
There is no process to make sure attorneys follow up regularly on client matters, and with transactional work, deadlines are often self-imposed — so a quiet matter can simply drift.
The Opportunity
Matter-level tracking with automatic nudges would surface stagnant matters before clients have to ask, and takes status-checking out of individual attorneys' heads.
Make Billable Time and Fixed-Fee Profitability Visible
Profit Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Attorneys bill roughly 40% of their time while feeling stretched, and the firm is moving from hourly to fixed fees. Under fixed fees, every unbilled hour comes straight out of margin.
The Opportunity
Understanding where non-billable hours go lets the firm price fixed-fee work with confidence and raise billable utilization — adding profit without adding attorneys.
Turn Advice Sent by Email Into a Searchable Knowledge Base
Team Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Legal advice given by email is not consolidated anywhere, so junior attorneys lose the learning and the firm loses reusable know-how from work it has already done.
The Opportunity
A consolidated, searchable library of past advice could speed up drafting and training and let attorneys reuse their best thinking instead of rebuilding it.
Verify and Consolidate Online Profiles After the Rebrand
Lead Gen Quick Win
Why It Matters
The firm rebranded this fall and relaunched its website, but we could not confirm the state of its Google Business Profile or legal directory listings, and profiles under the prior name may still be live.
The Opportunity
A one-time clean-up so every profile and link points to Current Shift Legal protects the referral trust you have built and ensures referred prospects find a consistent firm.
Add Attorney Bio Pages and Recurring Thought-Leadership Content
Lead Gen Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
The new site shows the team with photos and titles but has no individual bio pages, and its most recent blog posts date from 2018–2019, even though senior attorneys regularly speak at conferences and podcasts.
The Opportunity
Turning talks and podcasts into bios, articles, and case studies would build visibility for the cooperative and employee-ownership niche without adding to attorneys' business-development load.
