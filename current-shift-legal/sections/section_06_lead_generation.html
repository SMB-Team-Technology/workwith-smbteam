Lead Generation

Current Shift Legal has built its practice on referrals — roughly 70–90% of new clients — plus conference and podcast visibility from its senior attorneys, and referrals are already arriving faster than the team is comfortable with. The findings below show what the firm's new website and online presence look like today, and where competitor activity could not be verified.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The site is brand new — pages were published between late September and early October 2026 after the rebrand — so it has had little time to build search visibility
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 51/100 — Desktop 83/100
  • The homepage shows the team with photos and titles, but no individual attorney bio pages were found, so referred prospects cannot see the credentials behind each name
  • There is no contact form on the homepage — prospects are sent to a separate intake form — and there is no active blog; the most recent posts date from 2018–2019
Blind Spot: After a rebrand, directory profiles and links under the prior firm name can keep pointing referrers and search engines to the old identity. Making sure they all lead to Current Shift Legal protects the referral trust the firm has spent 13 years building.
The Positive
  • The homepage message is clear and niche-specific — "Legal services for organizations building with purpose"
  • The site is mobile responsive, has a clear call to action above the fold, and carries structured data that helps search engines understand the firm
The Gap
  • A mobile score of 51 leaves room to make the site faster for prospects who check the firm out on a phone before reaching out
  • Without attorney bio pages, the expertise that earns referrals is not visible to the people those referrals send to the site
  • With no recent content, the firm's cooperative and employee-ownership expertise is not building visibility for founders searching on their own
A website that educates and pre-qualifies prospects means fewer low-fit conversations eating into the team's time.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • We could not confirm whether the firm has a Google Business Profile, and local search results for cooperative, B Corp, and employee-ownership searches could not be observed — this is a verify-first item, not a confirmed gap
  • Services are organized by service type rather than by location, so there are no city or region pages to capture local searches
  • We did not find the firm listed in major legal directories such as Avvo, Justia, or Martindale during our search
Blind Spot: Because the firm is fully remote with a mailing-address-only Boulder location, Google may not treat it as a traditional storefront. For a niche practice like yours, the stronger visibility play is likely topical authority on cooperatives, employee ownership, and B Corps across Colorado and beyond — not a classic local map listing.
The Positive
  • Public positioning as a Certified B Corp and women-owned business is a distinctive trust signal that most general business-law firms cannot claim
The Gap
  • Prospects who hear about the firm by referral and then search its name should find a consistent, complete profile — that has not yet been confirmed after the rebrand
  • Review volume against peer firms could not be verified, so it should be checked live before any decision is made about review generation
Pote Law Firm — positions itself on the same niche, Public Benefit Cooperatives, Limited Cooperative Associations, and B Corps for social entrepreneurs, so a founder searching those terms may find them alongside Current Shift Legal.
Being easy to find and easy to verify supports the referral stream without asking your attorneys to spend more time on business development.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Google search results could not be observed during our research, so we cannot confirm whether the firm is running ads for cooperative formation and conversion searches in Colorado — and paid advertising did not come up on the call
  • The same applies to mission-driven business, B Corp, and nonprofit general counsel searches in Boulder, and to ownership-transition searches such as selling a business to employees
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Pote Law Firm — ad activity not observed in our research; worth checking live for cooperative and B Corp searches
  • Packard and Dierking, LLC — ad activity not observed in our research; worth checking live for Boulder nonprofit searches
The Opportunity
  • Cooperative formation and conversion: searches like "cooperative lawyer Colorado" and "worker cooperative conversion attorney"
  • Mission-driven business counsel: searches like "B Corp attorney Boulder" and "social enterprise attorney Colorado"
  • Ownership transition: high-intent searches from business owners researching "sell business to employees attorney" or "employee ownership trust lawyer"
  • Competitor-name and neighboring-market searches, such as people comparing cooperative-focused counsel across Colorado and nearby states
Blind Spot: Referrals are already arriving a little faster than the team is comfortable with, so paid search is not the first lever for this firm. It becomes a deliberate choice, not a need, once intake and operations can absorb more work without adding people.

Competitor LSA Landscape

The Opportunity

  • Google Screened status and a base of client reviews are what earn trust in LSA, so confirming the firm's current standing is the first step
