Here Is Where Current Shift Legal Stands Today

Current Shift Legal is a fully remote Colorado public benefit law firm, more than 13 years in practice, providing transactional legal services — formation, ownership transition, and general counsel — to cooperatives, employee-owned, and other values-driven organizations. Roughly 70–90% of its clients arrive by referral, but the most important finding is inside the firm: attorneys bill about 40% of their time and still feel stretched to the limit, because intake, scheduling, reporting, and matter follow-up all run by hand.

Stage 6 is where Current Shift Legal grows its profit margin without growing its headcount — and where your team's time goes to creative, strategic work instead of admin — and it is exactly where we are taking you.

Where You Are Today

Stage 4: Small Business Manager
  • Referrals bring in roughly 70–90% of clients — a stream strong enough that it is getting "a little bit" out of hand
  • Attorneys bill about 40% of their time while feeling stretched to their limit
  • Intake, scheduling, financial dashboards, and matter follow-up are handled by hand across separate tools
  • Growth is meant to come without adding headcount — "I don't need to have 20 attorneys to grow"

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a firm that is busy and a firm that is lean, profitable, and free of admin is mostly a systems gap — and closing it is how you grow your margin without hiring your way there.
