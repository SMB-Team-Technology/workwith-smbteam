Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Roughly 70–90% of clients come through referral — a steady stream, but one the firm does not control
  • Business development leans on senior attorneys' time at conferences and podcasts
  • The website was relaunched after the rebrand this fall; mobile PageSpeed is 51/100 and there is no active blog
A referral base this strong is an asset worth protecting — the goal is to keep it flowing without it costing attorney time.

Intake

  • Prospects move through an online form, a conflict check, and "several touch points" before they speak with a person
  • Scoping, engagement letters, and onboarding after the 30-minute intake call are all manual
  • The stated goal is an intake process where no human is involved outside the intake call itself
Every manual touch point in intake is team time that could go toward billable or strategic work instead.

Self-Managing Team

  • One team member covers executive assistance, client experience, and marketing — work that could each be a full-time role
  • Attorneys feel stretched to their limit even though only about 40% of their time is billable
  • There is no process to make sure matters get regular follow-up, so quiet matters can go stagnant
A team that runs on systems instead of heroics is what frees your people's brains for creative work.

Profit

  • Attorneys bill roughly 39–45% of their time; the rest goes to non-billable and admin work
  • Financial dashboards are assembled by hand, copying data from billing and accounting tools into Excel
  • The firm is shifting from hourly to fixed fees and is still working out that process
Growing margin without adding attorneys starts with seeing where the time goes — and giving it back.
