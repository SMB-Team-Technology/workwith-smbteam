Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Direct mail is the only lead source, and it is working well enough that Katherine says the firm is getting clients
  • The firm has never done digital marketing, so there is no search, paid, or social channel behind the mail
  • A Lighthouse performance test of the website scored 41 on mobile and 75 on desktop, which would matter once digital marketing is added
Katherine has said AI is the priority over marketing right now — lead generation is a strength to protect, not the urgent gap.

Intake

  • An outsourced receptionist company answers every incoming call and sets consultations, with a same-day follow-up process for people who do not schedule
  • Katherine says the process is good and the firm is not losing people — a genuine strength
  • Onboarding still requires double entry, because information must be re-keyed into the case management system before a case can be opened
Removing the re-keying between intake and case opening gives your team hours back without adding a single new lead.

Self-Managing Team

  • About 15 people, but both owners handle the business side themselves — "the running the business pulls me away from it"
  • Email handling is manual: one person is hired to review three people's emails and move hearings onto the right attorney's calendar
  • Only 5 of the firm's 15 people have Claude seats, and there is no firm AI policy yet for staff to sign
Until routine work runs without your oversight, the time you want for scaling into new states keeps going to running the business.

Profit

  • Revenue was about $1.9 million in 2025 and is over $2 million so far in 2026, on flat monthly fees from more than 500 active clients
  • Katherine says the books are strong, but the firm does not track how much revenue comes from fee recovery, and some fees are written off
  • No profit target or revenue goal has been reset since the firm hit its original case-count goal
A clear goal and visible numbers turn the five-state expansion from an ambition into a plan.
