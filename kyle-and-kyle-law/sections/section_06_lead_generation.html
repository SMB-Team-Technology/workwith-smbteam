Lead Generation

Kyle and Kyle Law gets its clients exclusively through direct mail and has never done digital marketing — one channel doing the work, with every new client arriving through a phone call to the office. Katherine has said AI is the priority over marketing right now, so this section maps what is in place and what is open for when she is ready, rather than listing urgent fixes.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The homepage headline, "Everyone Deserves To Smile," does not say what the firm does or where — a homeowner has to read further to learn it is foreclosure defense
  • A Lighthouse performance test scored the site 41/100 on mobile and 75/100 on desktop, with the main content taking about 25.7 seconds to load on mobile
  • Placeholder-style filler text ("Nullam quis risus eget urna mollis ornare…") appears in the page markup beneath the Business Litigation, Bankruptcy, and Loan Modification blocks — worth confirming it is not visible to visitors
  • We did not find a contact form on the homepage itself, so visitors who prefer not to call may have to go looking for one
Blind Spot: Homeowners who receive your mailer often look the firm up online before they call. A slow or unclear site can quietly lose people your mail has already paid to reach.
The Positive
  • The site is phone-first, with click-to-call links throughout the page that make it easy to reach the office
  • Individual attorney pages and a blog give the site a solid foundation to build on
The Gap
  • A mobile load time this slow means many visitors can leave before the page appears, including homeowners checking you out on their phones
  • A headline that does not name foreclosure defense makes every visitor work harder to confirm they are in the right place
  • Each new state on your expansion list (Indiana, Ohio, and one more) will need the same clear, fast, state-specific page to support the mail going out
A fast, clear website makes every dollar of mail — and any future marketing — work harder, supporting your growth without adding to your workload.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Google Business Profile details such as review recency, photos, and posts were not reviewed in this audit
  • In the one Lee County foreclosure defense search we ran, seven firms surfaced and Kyle and Kyle Law was not among them; the same was true of our Illinois foreclosure defense search (a single check, not a full ranking study)
  • The firm's listed headquarters is a suite-style address in Fort Myers, and we did not verify that the firm's name, address, and phone number match across directories
Blind Spot: A directory page lists a "Kyle & Kyle Law" under Athens, Georgia. We could not confirm whether it is this firm, but if it is, inconsistent listings can weaken how Google trusts your local presence.
The Positive
  • The firm appears to carry a strong Google rating with a healthy base of client reviews, though we have not independently confirmed the current figures
The Gap
  • Right now, direct mail carries the whole load — homeowners who search for foreclosure help on their own have little chance to find you
  • Every new state will need a local presence built from scratch, so strong profiles and local pages are best started before the mailers go out
Belmont Law Group — appeared in the Lee County foreclosure defense results we reviewed and lists a Fort Myers office, where Kyle and Kyle Law did not appear in that same check.
A strong local presence lets new markets start working before you commit the time and attention an expansion normally demands.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Foreclosure defense (Florida and Illinois): Katherine confirmed the firm has never done digital marketing, and we could not view live Google results in this audit, so no firm ads were observed
  • Loan modification: no firm ads were observed here either, for the same reason
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Competitor ad activity for foreclosure defense and loan modification in Fort Myers and Chicago was not observed in this audit, so we are not drawing conclusions about who is bidding
The Opportunity
  • "Foreclosure defense attorney" paired with Fort Myers, Lee County, Chicago, and Cook County — homeowners searching these terms are ready to hire
  • "Loan modification help" and "stop foreclosure" searches in Florida and Illinois, matching the loan modification work your team already does
  • Urgency searches such as "served with foreclosure papers" or "facing a foreclosure lawsuit," which pair naturally with the foreclosure landing page that already exists on your site
  • Foreclosure surplus funds and probate-related searches, which your website lists as services but your mail does not reach

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • No Local Service Ads listing for the firm was observed in this audit
  • We could not view live Google results, so this is a gap in what we could see rather than a confirmed absence
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Competitor LSA listings in Fort Myers and Chicago were not observed in this audit
The Opportunity
  • Whether LSAs are available for foreclosure defense and loan modification in each of your markets would need to be confirmed first, but where they are, they are a pay-per-lead channel
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Google Screened status leans on verification and client reviews, so an established review base gives the firm a head start
