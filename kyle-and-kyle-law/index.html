Here Is Where Kyle and Kyle Law Stands Today

Kyle and Kyle Law is a husband-and-wife foreclosure defense and loan modification firm serving homeowners in Florida and Illinois, opened in 2021 and now past 500 active clients on flat monthly fees with a team of about 15. Revenue is over $2 million so far in 2026, and both owners have stepped out of casework to run the business. The most important finding is not a client-acquisition problem — it is that running the firm still consumes the time Katherine wants to spend scaling it, and AI is the clearest lever to give that time back.

Stage 6 is the stage where the firm runs without running the business pulling you away from growth — and it is exactly where we are taking you, so you can put your focus on scaling into new states.

Where You Are Today

Stage 4: Small Business Manager
  • Direct mail is the firm's only source of new clients, and every new client arrives through a phone call to the office
  • Both owners have stopped taking cases, but they still handle the day-to-day running of the business themselves
  • A team of about 15 plus outsourced reception and IT — but only 5 seats are using Claude, mostly for one-off tasks
  • Email review, calendaring, and time entries still depend on manual effort — one person is hired just to review three people's emails

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between where you are and where you want to be is a time gap, not a client gap — and closing it starts with putting AI to work on the repetitive tasks that keep pulling you back into the business.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The homepage headline, "Everyone Deserves To Smile," does not say what the firm does or where — a homeowner has to read further to learn it is foreclosure defense
  • A Lighthouse performance test scored the site 41/100 on mobile and 75/100 on desktop, with the main content taking about 25.7 seconds to load on mobile
  • Placeholder-style filler text ("Nullam quis risus eget urna mollis ornare…") appears in the page markup beneath the Business Litigation, Bankruptcy, and Loan Modification blocks — worth confirming it is not visible to visitors
  • We did not find a contact form on the homepage itself, so visitors who prefer not to call may have to go looking for one
Blind Spot: Homeowners who receive your mailer often look the firm up online before they call. A slow or unclear site can quietly lose people your mail has already paid to reach.
The Positive
  • The site is phone-first, with click-to-call links throughout the page that make it easy to reach the office
  • Individual attorney pages and a blog give the site a solid foundation to build on
The Gap
  • A mobile load time this slow means many visitors can leave before the page appears, including homeowners checking you out on their phones
  • A headline that does not name foreclosure defense makes every visitor work harder to confirm they are in the right place
  • Each new state on your expansion list (Indiana, Ohio, and one more) will need the same clear, fast, state-specific page to support the mail going out
A fast, clear website makes every dollar of mail — and any future marketing — work harder, supporting your growth without adding to your workload.

Intake Assessment

Kyle and Kyle Law has a defined intake process: an outsourced receptionist company answers every call and sets consultations, and a same-day follow-up covers people who do not schedule. The gap is the manual work between a consultation and an open case. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: RED
"it requires too much double work because then we still have to put it in our case management system before the case can be opened."
The Problem
  • Information has to be entered twice — once during onboarding and again in your case management system — before a case can be opened
  • Every new client arrives by phone, so intake depends on an outsourced receptionist company and one person following up with leads
  • Lead follow-up and rescheduling are still handled by hand, and automating them is on your own AI wish list
  • Loan modification applications contain client banking data, and there is no firm AI policy yet to govern how that data is handled
Blind Spot: A good process can hide a hidden cost: every manual step between a consultation and an open case is staff time spent on data entry instead of client work, and it multiplies with every new state.
The Positive
  • An outsourced receptionist company handles every incoming call and sets consultations, with a same-day follow-up process in place
  • Katherine says the process is working — "We're not losing people" — and dashboards already track consult-to-client percentages by attorney
The Gap
  • Double entry takes staff time on every new client, time that could go to serving the 500-plus clients the firm already has
  • Manual follow-up and rescheduling leave room for delays as volume grows with each new state
  • Without an AI policy, the firm cannot safely extend AI to the intake work that handles sensitive client data
Smoother intake means more of your team's time goes to clients instead of re-keying — and less of the business lands on your desk.
