Here Is Where Nuclei TM Stands Today

Nuclei TM is a St. Augustine, Florida professional services company led by Ashley Gomes, specializing in employment-based immigration support for individuals, businesses, and other law firms. It runs as a one-person operation with a single contractor co-writer, and the most important finding is that Ashley herself is the bottleneck: scanning, printing, invoicing, and social media all still run through her, so more clients would simply mean more work with no one to hand it to.

Stage 6 is where the business runs on systems instead of on you, and taking the administrative work off your plate is exactly where we start.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • Work comes mainly from two consistent law-firm clients, with no paid or systematic lead channel described on the call
  • Scanning, printing, and social media are all handled personally alongside the client work
  • Every invoice is created, attached to an email, and sent by hand because scheduling and invoicing are not connected
  • One contractor co-writer supports petition preparation, with plans to hire two more people

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a business that depends on you for every task and one that runs on its own closes when the repeatable administrative work comes off your plate first, and that is the plan in this report.
