Intake Assessment

Nuclei TM already has a scheduling tool, a case management tool for document collection, and HubSpot, but they are not connected, so the hand-offs between them still run through Ashley. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
"if I invest in leads now, I can take the consultation call, but then after that, who's going to be doing all the work?"
The Problem
  • You take consultation calls personally, and everything that follows also lands with you
  • Your scheduling tool is not connected to invoicing, so each booking creates a manual step later
  • Documents are still scanned and printed by hand even though a case management tool is used for document collection
  • No intake team member or defined hand-off process was described on the call
Blind Spot: The intake bottleneck is not lead volume. It is what happens after the consultation call, so if more clients arrive, the same person who books them also has to do the work.
The Positive
  • The core tools are already in place: scheduling, document collection, and HubSpot
  • You are already using AI for drafting, so the team is not starting from zero with technology
The Gap
  • Every hand-off that needs a person is time taken away from petition work
  • Disconnected tools mean information gets re-entered by hand, which invites errors
  • Any growth in client volume lands on the same person who is already at capacity
Automating the repeatable steps from first booking to final invoice is the fastest way to take the administrative duties off your plate.

Team Assessment

Nuclei TM is essentially a one-person operation, supported by one contractor co-writer on petition preparation, with plans to hire two more people. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: RED
"I'm still doing the scanning, the printing. I'm also kind of doing a lot of the social media marketing, all of that stuff on my own."
The Problem
  • You handle scanning, printing, invoicing, social media, and consultation calls on top of petition work
  • Only one contractor supports petition preparation, and no one else covers administrative work
  • Knowledge about how the work gets done lives with individuals, and you want to retain it so you are not left "handicapped" if someone leaves
  • You plan to hire two more people, but no onboarding process or documented workflows were described yet
Blind Spot: Hiring two more people before the repetitive work is automated can simply move the bottleneck, because each new hire will need training, supervision, and hand-offs from you.
The Positive
  • Your contractor co-writer is already trusted with a large part of petition preparation
  • You are thinking about knowledge retention and hiring before they become problems, not after
The Gap
  • Every task only you can do puts a ceiling on how many clients you can serve
  • Knowledge held by one contractor is a business risk, not just an inconvenience
  • Without documented processes, new hires will keep coming back to you for answers
A business that runs on documented systems and automated workflows is the one that finally gives you the escape you are looking for.

Profit Plan Assessment

Financial performance was not discussed in detail on the call, so this section focuses on how the way work is billed and tracked affects the owner's time and take-home. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"I have to still manually go create an invoice, attach the invoice to the email, and then send it."
The Problem
  • Invoices are created, attached to an email, and sent by hand, one at a time
  • Your scheduling tool is not connected to invoicing, so each billable booking needs a manual step before an invoice goes out
  • Profit targets and financial reporting were not discussed on the call
  • Investment decisions are held to what clearly fits current financial priorities
Blind Spot: Time is the hidden cost. Every hour spent creating invoices and scanning documents is an hour that is not spent on petition work.
The Positive
  • You are focused on the core work that pays the bills and weigh each investment carefully, which protects cash
The Gap
  • Manual billing can slow cash collection and takes time away from billable work
  • Without profit targets, adding clients can add work without adding take-home
  • Without a view of how much of your time each engagement takes, it is hard to know which work is most worthwhile
Reclaiming hours from billing and administration turns your time into the asset that grows the business instead of the cost that limits it.
