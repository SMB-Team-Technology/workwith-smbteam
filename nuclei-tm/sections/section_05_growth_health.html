Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Work comes mainly through two consistent law-firm clients, with no repeatable lead system described on the call
  • LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram posts are written by the owner and, in her words, are not curated for a particular audience
  • Nuclei TM did not surface organically for generic employment-based immigration searches in the Jacksonville and St. Augustine area; it appeared only when searched by name
Marketing can rightly wait until capacity is in place, but a dependable lead source will matter the moment the administrative load is off your plate.

Intake

  • A scheduling tool, a case management tool for document collection, and HubSpot are already in place
  • Those tools are not connected to each other, so scanning, printing, and invoicing are still done by hand
  • Consultation calls are taken personally by the owner
Connecting what you already use means fewer manual hand-offs and more of your day back.

Self-Managing Team

  • The owner describes the company as essentially a one-person show, supported by one contractor co-writer on petition preparation
  • Administrative work and social media still sit with the owner
  • The owner wants to retain the data and knowledge behind the work so the company is not left "handicapped" if a team member leaves
Until the work no longer depends on you, the time you are looking for stays just out of reach.

Profit

  • Financial performance was not discussed in detail on the call, so margins are not assessed in this report
  • Every invoice is created, attached, and emailed manually, which ties billing to the owner's available time
  • Investment decisions are being weighed carefully against financial priorities, so low-cost tools that reduce workload are the right first step
A business that bills and runs smoothly without your hands on every step is the foundation for the growth you want.
