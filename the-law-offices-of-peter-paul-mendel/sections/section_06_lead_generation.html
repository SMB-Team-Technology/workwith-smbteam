Lead Generation

The Law Offices of Peter Paul Mendel has built its entire mediation practice on personal attorney referrals, with no independent lead generation system in place for either the business/real estate mediation practice or the divorce/family mediation offering. Past paid lead-generation efforts produced roughly 90% unqualified "looky-loo" inquiries — a structural mismatch, since mediation requires buy-in from every party to a dispute, not just one.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • PageSpeed Insights returned repeated capacity errors (HTTP 429) across three separate attempts during this audit and could not return a mobile or desktop score — this should be tested directly before the proposal call rather than assumed either way
  • The contact form sits on a separate Contact Us page, reached via a "Book Discovery Call" button — not directly on the homepage itself
  • No attorney bio, credentials, or personal story appears on the homepage (a fuller bio exists on a separate /About-Us page referencing BYU/Fordham Law and prior general counsel work, but it is not surfaced where new visitors first land)
  • No video and no blog anywhere on the site — nothing actively builds trust or answers a prospect's questions before they ever call
Blind Spot: Avvo lists Peter's location as Irvine, CA, practicing Tax, Business, and Family law, and FindLaw lists him practicing workers' compensation defense, business organizations, and international law — with no mention of mediation at all. Anyone doing due diligence on a referral before calling may find a completely different attorney than the one described on the current site.
The Positive
  • A prominent "Schedule Consultation" CTA appears early on the homepage
  • The longbeachmediation.peterpaulmendel.com subdomain is a real, dedicated divorce-mediation landing page already in place — a genuine geo/practice-specific asset most solo mediators never build
The Gap
  • Without a confirmed PageSpeed baseline, mobile visitors — the majority of local searches — may be dropping off before ever reaching the CTA
  • Every referral partner who researches Peter online before calling encounters a fragmented, sometimes contradictory picture, at exactly the moment attorneys performing due diligence on a referral are most likely to look
  • No blog or video content means zero long-tail search visibility beyond the homepage itself
None of the fifty mediators in the long-term vision will need to be vetted twice by a skeptical referral partner — but until the online presence is consistent and complete, every one of today's referrals still is.
