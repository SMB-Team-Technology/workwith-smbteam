Overall Growth Stage Assessment
The Law Offices of Peter Paul Mendel
Peter is operating at Stage 3 — a Solo Practitioner who has made the difficult but correct call to walk away from a general practice model that nearly cost him everything. The single most urgent gap limiting his growth right now is not lead volume, it is the absence of the referral, intake-qualification, and billing/collections systems needed to turn a solo mediation practice into anything larger without repeating the exact financial exposure that ended the last one. Until those systems exist, the short-term goal of three mediations a week — let alone the long-term vision of fifty mediators — will stay out of reach no matter how much time Peter puts in personally.
