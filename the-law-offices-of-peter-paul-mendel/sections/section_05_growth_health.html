Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Every mediation engagement currently comes from personal attorney referrals — there is no independent, repeatable lead source
  • Past paid lead-generation efforts produced roughly 90% unqualified "looky-loo" inquiries, since mediation requires multi-party buy-in that standard one-sided marketing funnels are not built to screen for
  • NAP information is inconsistent across three directories, and two of them (Avvo, FindLaw) still describe the prior general practice instead of mediation
Every referral that finds a fragmented, inconsistent online presence is a referral that could quietly go to Carter or Eskridge instead — before the fifty-mediator vision can even get started.

Intake

  • Peter personally handles every intake conversation himself, with no process for screening single-sided prospects from viable multi-party mediation engagements
  • No dedicated staff exist to triage inquiries, so every "looky-loo" costs Peter time directly
  • No after-hours or overflow coverage of any kind
A qualification filter that catches non-viable inquiries before they reach Peter's calendar is the fastest way to buy back the hours the fifty-mediator vision will eventually demand.

Self-Managing Team

  • Solo practice — no other staff or associate attorneys currently on payroll
  • Every case file, billing task, and collections follow-up runs through Peter personally
  • No org structure exists to support even the short-term goal of three mediations a week, let alone fifty mediators long-term
There is no version of a fifty-mediator platform that runs through one person's calendar — the first hire is the first real step toward the firm Peter is describing.

Profit

  • A prior general practice accumulated $500,000 in uncollected accounts receivable — no billing/collections discipline was in place to prevent it
  • Current reported revenue (~$3,500/month) is legacy attorney work, not mediation revenue — there is no clean tracking yet of what the mediation practice itself is generating
  • No stated profit plan exists connecting the $40,000/month short-term goal to an actual case-by-case revenue and collections system
Requiring upfront payment was the right instinct — but it only protects Peter if it is backed by a real tracking and collections system, not just a policy.
