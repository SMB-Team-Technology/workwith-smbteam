Here Is Where Hernandez Law Stands Today

Tatiana Hernandez has spent over a decade building Hernandez Law into a trusted employment law practice for Los Angeles's Spanish-speaking workforce, handling wrongful termination, wage and hour, discrimination, sexual harassment, and retaliation cases from a single Beverly Hills office. She has a small team in place and a Spanish-language ad campaign that has run for years — but with referrals slowing and revenue pacing at roughly half of last year's level, the firm has no organic visibility, no unified bilingual web presence, and an owner who still personally absorbs every intake and litigation bottleneck.

Stage 6 is the stage where the firm generates and converts cases without every decision routing through you first — giving you back the bandwidth that discovery-heavy litigation weeks currently consume.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Lead Generation

Hernandez Law has relied on two lead sources for years — a referral network that is now slowing and a Spanish-language paid ad campaign with declining lead quality — while organic search, local SEO, and the English-language market remain completely untapped.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The English-language site (hernandezlawca.com) and the Spanish-language site (justicieralegal.com) are two entirely separate domains, not unified — splitting SEO authority and confusing bilingual prospects about which site to trust
  • PageSpeed mobile and desktop scores could not be captured this research cycle due to tool rate-limiting — an independent test should be run to confirm current load-time performance before the campaign kicks off
  • The "Schedule Your Free Consultation" form requires scrolling and is not visible above the fold
  • No dedicated city or geo-targeted landing pages exist despite the firm being licensed to serve clients broadly across California
Blind Spot: Running two unconnected domains for English and Spanish visitors means Google sees two smaller, weaker sites instead of one strong bilingual authority — actively working against the local visibility you need most.
The Gap
  • A split EN/ES web presence means neither site builds enough authority to rank locally, so both audiences find named competitors first
  • A buried contact form means visitors who are ready to reach out have to work to do it — and many will not
Consolidating your web presence into one strong, bilingual asset is foundational work that keeps generating cases long after any single ad campaign stops — reducing how much new business depends on you personally sourcing it.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Google Business Profile is set to "Law firm" instead of "Employment attorney," and still displays an old Los Angeles address instead of the firm's real Beverly Hills office at 315 South Beverly Drive, Suite 504 — a live NAP mismatch actively hurting local map visibility
  • Cross-platform checks show effectively 0 reviews (Facebook: "Not yet rated"; Avvo: 0.0/5.0, "Not yet reviewed"), while named competitors in the same market carry double-digit review counts
  • The firm does not appear in organic search results for "wrongful termination lawyer Beverly Hills," "employment lawyer Beverly Hills," or "employment lawyer Los Angeles Spanish speaking" — searches where Setareh Law Group, Akopyan Law Firm, Blady Workforce Law Group, and West Coast Employment Lawyers consistently show up
Blind Spot: The GBP category and address issue is not just a minor listing error — it is likely the single biggest reason the firm is invisible in local map results for the exact searches its ideal clients are running.
The Gap
  • Being absent from the local pack for core employment law searches means prospects find and call competitors before they ever see Hernandez Law
  • Zero reviews next to competitors' double-digit counts creates an instant credibility gap at the exact moment a prospect is comparing firms
Setareh Law Group — appears consistently in the same Beverly Hills and Los Angeles employment law searches where Hernandez Law does not, backed by 17 live Yelp reviews and a correctly configured, address-consistent local presence.
Fixing your GBP and building real local SEO authority is what lets new clients find you without a referral, an ad, or a phone call from you — the exact kind of system that runs without your daily involvement.
