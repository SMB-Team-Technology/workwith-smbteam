Lead Generation

Hernandez Law has relied on two lead sources for years — a referral network that is now slowing and a Spanish-language paid ad campaign with declining lead quality — while organic search, local SEO, and the English-language market remain completely untapped.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The English-language site (hernandezlawca.com) and the Spanish-language site (justicieralegal.com) are two entirely separate domains, not unified — splitting SEO authority and confusing bilingual prospects about which site to trust
  • PageSpeed mobile and desktop scores could not be captured this research cycle due to tool rate-limiting — an independent test should be run to confirm current load-time performance before the campaign kicks off
  • The "Schedule Your Free Consultation" form requires scrolling and is not visible above the fold
  • No dedicated city or geo-targeted landing pages exist despite the firm being licensed to serve clients broadly across California
Blind Spot: Running two unconnected domains for English and Spanish visitors means Google sees two smaller, weaker sites instead of one strong bilingual authority — actively working against the local visibility you need most.
The Gap
  • A split EN/ES web presence means neither site builds enough authority to rank locally, so both audiences find named competitors first
  • A buried contact form means visitors who are ready to reach out have to work to do it — and many will not
Consolidating your web presence into one strong, bilingual asset is foundational work that keeps generating cases long after any single ad campaign stops — reducing how much new business depends on you personally sourcing it.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Google Business Profile is set to "Law firm" instead of "Employment attorney," and still displays an old Los Angeles address instead of the firm's real Beverly Hills office at 315 South Beverly Drive, Suite 504 — a live NAP mismatch actively hurting local map visibility
  • Cross-platform checks show effectively 0 reviews (Facebook: "Not yet rated"; Avvo: 0.0/5.0, "Not yet reviewed"), while named competitors in the same market carry double-digit review counts
  • The firm does not appear in organic search results for "wrongful termination lawyer Beverly Hills," "employment lawyer Beverly Hills," or "employment lawyer Los Angeles Spanish speaking" — searches where Setareh Law Group, Akopyan Law Firm, Blady Workforce Law Group, and West Coast Employment Lawyers consistently show up
Blind Spot: The GBP category and address issue is not just a minor listing error — it is likely the single biggest reason the firm is invisible in local map results for the exact searches its ideal clients are running.
The Gap
  • Being absent from the local pack for core employment law searches means prospects find and call competitors before they ever see Hernandez Law
  • Zero reviews next to competitors' double-digit counts creates an instant credibility gap at the exact moment a prospect is comparing firms
Setareh Law Group — appears consistently in the same Beverly Hills and Los Angeles employment law searches where Hernandez Law does not, backed by 17 live Yelp reviews and a correctly configured, address-consistent local presence.
Fixing your GBP and building real local SEO authority is what lets new clients find you without a referral, an ad, or a phone call from you — the exact kind of system that runs without your daily involvement.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Spanish-language Google Ads have been running for 2–3 years at roughly $5,000/month in spend plus roughly $1,200/month in agency management fees, but lead quality has been declining, with more misqualified and non-employment inquiries
  • The campaign is Spanish-only, so the firm does not appear to be running English-language search ads for employment law terms — leaving the English-speaking side of this market entirely to competitors
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Setareh Law Group — a long-tenured brand with "$1B+ recovered" positioning typical of firms sustaining ongoing paid search spend
  • King & Siegel LLP — "$120M+ settlements" messaging consistent with an active, high-visibility paid acquisition strategy
  • Kingsley Szamet Employment Lawyers — advertises Spanish-language service, a direct positioning overlap with Hernandez Law's core client base
The Opportunity
  • Employment law + Beverly Hills / Los Angeles keyword targeting, in both English and Spanish, to capture searchers the current Spanish-only campaign misses entirely
  • Wrongful termination, wage and hour, and discrimination-specific keywords to capture prospects further along in their decision process
  • Competitor-name and neighboring-city (Santa Monica, Culver City, West Hollywood) targeting to intercept searches currently going to named competitors
  • Rebuilding campaign structure around qualification would directly address the declining lead-quality problem the outside agency has not solved
Blind Spot: Running Spanish-only ads means every English-language searcher for employment law in your market never sees you at all — a second, untapped audience is available at essentially the flip of a switch.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Hernandez Law does not appear to be running Local Service Ads or holding Google Screened status — this could not be directly confirmed via live tools this session and should be spot-checked, but represents an open opportunity either way
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Setareh Law Group — 17 live Yelp reviews and strong directory prominence make it a plausible active LSA participant in this market
  • Akopyan Law Firm — 22 reviews and consistent local visibility position it similarly as a likely LSA participant
The Opportunity
  • Google Screened status would add a trust signal Hernandez Law currently has no equivalent for, given its 0 reviews across checked platforms
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • Pay-per-lead pricing would reduce the risk of paying for the kind of misqualified clicks currently affecting the Spanish Google Ads campaign

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • Hernandez Law does not appear to be running Meta ads — Facebook Ads Library access was blocked this session, and the transcript confirms no Meta ad activity has been mentioned by the prospect
  • The firm's Facebook business page is live but shows 0 reviews, consistent with its broader lack of review presence
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • West Coast Employment Lawyers — a 46,000-follower Instagram presence with documented client-reported Facebook ad exposure, indicating an active paid social program
  • Setareh Law Group — Ads Library access was blocked this session, but the firm's scale and 25+ year tenure make an active paid social presence plausible
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Spanish-language social ads specifically, since the firm's client base is majority Spanish-speaking and this channel is entirely untested to date
