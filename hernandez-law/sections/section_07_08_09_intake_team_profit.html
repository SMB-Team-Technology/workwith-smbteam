Intake Assessment

Nearly every intake decision at Hernandez Law still routes through Tatiana personally, and during discovery-heavy litigation weeks, that bottleneck causes real delays and missed opportunities. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating. This is where the biggest immediate ROI lives.

Status: RED
"Everything routes through her, causing delays and missed opportunities."
The Problem
  • You personally handle most intake decisions, with no dedicated intake process to absorb volume during your busiest litigation weeks
  • The website's consultation form is buried below the fold, adding friction right at the moment a prospect is ready to reach out
  • No after-hours coverage exists, so calls or inquiries outside business hours have no defined path to a fast response
  • No documented follow-up sequence exists for leads who do not sign immediately, which compounds the impact of already-declining Google Ads lead quality
Blind Spot: The weeks when discovery demands the most from you are the exact weeks intake suffers most — meaning your busiest, most valuable case work is quietly costing you new business.
The Gap
  • Every lead that slips through during a busy week is a case worth $30,000–$50,000+ that never gets recovered
  • A buried contact form and no after-hours coverage compound the cost of every dollar already spent on Spanish Google Ads
  • Without a defined intake process, hitting the stated goal of 5+ qualified cases a month (up from 1–2) will be capped by capacity, not by lead volume
A dedicated intake system means new cases stop depending on you personally being available — the first real step toward the firm running without routing through you.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat

  • The "Schedule Your Free Consultation" form requires scrolling and is not visible above the fold on the homepage
  • No live chat is present on the site, removing a real-time option for prospects who want an immediate response
  • The site's click-to-call button is above the fold, which helps, but it is currently the only low-friction contact option visible immediately

Team Assessment

Hernandez Law has built a small but real team — 2 staff, a newly hired post-bar law clerk, and a contract attorney — yet Tatiana remains the person nearly every operational and intake decision still runs through. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: AMBER
"Everything routes through her, causing delays and missed opportunities."
The Problem
  • A small team exists, but there is no stated layer of management between Tatiana and day-to-day intake or operational decisions
  • Discovery-heavy litigation weeks strain her bandwidth specifically because the team cannot yet absorb those decisions independently
  • Overhead is rising with the recent hire, at roughly $25,000/month, without a corresponding increase in the team's independence
  • Tatiana is exploring AI tools to reduce the discovery burden herself — a sign the workload gap is being felt personally, not just operationally
Blind Spot: Growing the team's headcount without growing its decision-making authority just adds more people who still need you — not more capacity that runs without you.
The Gap
  • As long as intake and operational decisions require you, taking real time off remains functionally impossible
  • The newly hired post-bar law clerk represents real future capacity, but only once a defined process exists for what routes to them versus to you
  • A rising overhead with no matching rise in team independence means growth adds workload before it adds freedom
Building a team that can run intake and casework decisions without you is what finally gives you the bandwidth back that discovery weeks currently take away.

Profit Plan Assessment

Tatiana knows her overhead precisely at roughly $25,000/month, but revenue itself is currently declining — pacing toward roughly $500,000 in 2026 versus close to $1M the two prior years. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: AMBER
"Overhead is about $25,000 a month, and it's increasing with the new hire."
The Problem
  • Overhead of roughly $25,000/month is known precisely, but there is no stated visibility into margin or actual take-home profit beyond that figure
  • Revenue is trending downward, not upward — from near $1M two years ago to a roughly $500K pace in 2026, driven by the referral slowdown
  • The firm currently spends roughly $6,200/month combined on Spanish Google Ads spend and agency management, on a channel with declining lead quality
  • Overhead is rising with the new hire at the same time revenue is falling, tightening the margin from both directions
Blind Spot: Knowing your overhead number precisely can create a false sense of financial control — without visibility into what each case actually nets after cost, a declining revenue pace can quietly become a declining profit pace too.
The Gap
  • Without margin visibility, it is difficult to know whether the current ad spend is still paying for itself as lead quality declines
  • A rising overhead against falling revenue is a trend that, left unaddressed, compresses profit faster than the topline numbers alone suggest
  • The stated goal of 5+ qualified cases a month at $30K–$50K+ each implies meaningful revenue upside — but only if the cost to acquire and convert each case is understood and controlled
Real financial visibility is what turns a declining revenue pace back into a plan you control — freeing up the mental bandwidth that comes from knowing the numbers, not just guessing at them.
