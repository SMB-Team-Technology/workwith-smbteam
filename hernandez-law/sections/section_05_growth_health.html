Intake

  • Tatiana personally handles most intake, causing delays and missed opportunities during discovery-heavy litigation weeks

Self-Managing Team

A team that can run intake and casework without you is what finally gives you the time back that discovery weeks currently take away.

Profit

  • Overhead is known precisely at roughly $25,000/month, but there is no stated visibility into margin or take-home profit beyond that figure

Knowing overhead is only half the picture — real financial clarity is what turns a declining revenue pace back into a growth plan you control.
