Lead Generation

Filippov Law gets inbound calls and inquiries on the strength of Victoria's reputation, but they are mostly one-time, one-off matters, and the firm did not appear in the Google Maps results for any of about 15 Houston searches we ran. The thin spots in this market, especially outside general counsel and mediation, are where a firm with a 5.0 rating and 17 reviews can compete.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The inquiry form sits mid-page under "Your Trusted Partners In Business Law Solutions" and in the footer, not above the fold
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 76/100 — Desktop 86/100. Mobile largest contentful paint is 5.1 seconds, slower than the roughly 2.5-second mark Google treats as good (lab results from a Lighthouse run on October 15, 2026)
  • There are no pages for exit planning, succession planning or estate planning for business owners, the areas you are moving into
  • There is one Houston location page and no pages aimed at surrounding cities, and no video was observed on the site
Blind Spot: The site was refreshed in 2026, but a business owner searching for exit or succession planning has no page to land on, so the pivot you are building toward is invisible to search engines and to visitors.
The Positive
  • The site is mobile responsive, freshly updated for 2026, and has a clear "Send An Inquiry" button above the fold
  • Attorney bios are strong: 25+ years of Texas practice, 13 years as general counsel for a multinational company, and a licensed mediator credential
The Gap
  • Searchers looking for the services you want more of cannot find a page that speaks to them
  • A mobile load time above 5 seconds can lose visitors before they ever read your bio
  • The blog has not been updated since April 2026, about six months, so the site is not adding fresh pages for search to find
A website that explains your new offers on its own is one more way new clients arrive without you doing all of the selling.

Local SEO

The Positive

The Gap
  • Prospects who start with a Google search see your competitors first and may never learn your firm exists
  • The Yelp listing appears to show a different address (3200 Southwest Fwy) than Google and your website in search snippets, which is worth confirming
Texas Real Estate & Business Law Firm PLLC — 256 Google reviews at 4.8 stars, and it appeared at the top of the business lawyer, business attorney and business contract searches in Houston.
More visibility in the searches where your reputation already outshines the competition is the fastest way to a steady flow of retainer clients.
