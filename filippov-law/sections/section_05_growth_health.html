Lead Generation

  • New business comes from one-off calls, your reputation and planned in-person networking, with no repeatable system for signing retainer clients

Intake

  • There is no dedicated intake person, and AI agents currently handle scheduling
  • The website's inquiry form sits mid-page and in the footer, not above the fold
  • No defined path from first inquiry to signed retainer was described, which matters as you move to flat-fee subscriptions
A simple intake path means new retainer inquiries do not have to land on your desk to be handled.

Self-Managing Team

  • The firm went from about ten employees to a small bench of contractors who are hired as needed
  • Trusted contractors are asking for work, but you have very little to hand them while you run the firm day to day
  • Clients are used to working with you directly, which keeps decisions and delivery tied to your time
Until work moves to other people, you stay stuck running the firm instead of doing the legal work you enjoy.

Profit

  • Revenue has fallen sharply since the firm lost its largest client, and other clients have been sold, restructured or are under financial pressure
  • The flat-fee subscription model is still being built, so there is no recurring revenue base yet
  • No profit plan or margin targets were discussed on the call
Predictable recurring revenue is what turns a firm that runs on your hours into one that can run without them.
