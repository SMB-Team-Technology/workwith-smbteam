Here Is Where Filippov Law Stands Today

Victoria Filippov Nemeth has practiced business law in Houston for more than 25 years, serving mid-size companies as outside general counsel and moving toward flat-fee subscriptions, exit planning and estate planning for business owners. Her 5.0 Google rating, licensed-mediator credential and "well-known name" reputation are real assets, but after losing her anchor client the firm now runs on her own hours plus contractors, with no repeatable system for bringing in new retainer clients.

Stage 6 is where the firm runs on its own with other people, so you are no longer doing every type of day-in, day-out work and can be the face of the business doing the legal work you enjoy.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • The firm has shrunk from about ten employees to a handful of contractors, and you handle most of the work yourself
  • New business comes from one-off calls, your reputation and in-person networking, with no repeatable system for signing retainer clients
  • You are pivoting toward flat-fee subscriptions, exit planning and estate planning for business owners, but the website and local search presence do not reflect that yet
  • Trusted contractors are asking for work while you are still doing the day-to-day yourself

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
You have the reputation and the time right now to build the infrastructure first, which is exactly the order that gets you to a firm that runs without you doing everything.
