Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • Every lead source (referrals, financial-advisor network, seminars) depends on relationships Charles personally built — none of it is a system that runs without him
  • Sage does not appear in organic search results for "estate planning attorney" anywhere in its own Clearfield/Northern Utah market
  • No paid advertising has ever been run on any platform, on any practice area
A firm built to sell for $10M needs demand that shows up on a balance sheet independent of Charles's personal network — referrals alone will not get Sage there.

Intake

  • Seminar attendees generate real interest, but there is no formal lead-nurturing or follow-up sequence, so warm leads go cold
  • No contact form on the homepage — visitors must call or click through to a separate page just to reach the firm
  • Intake is delegated to a receptionist/intake specialist, but without a defined process behind it
Every seminar lead that goes cold is revenue Sage already paid to generate — closing that gap gets Charles closer to $600K without adding a single new lead source.

Self-Managing Team

  • Charles is simultaneously CEO and primary attorney — there is no one else setting strategy, evaluating channels, or making pricing decisions
  • The 6-person team has defined functional roles (paralegal, office manager, marketing specialist, receptionist/intake) but no documented SOPs or role scorecards tying that structure together
  • Charles has stated he wants a 50% CEO / 50% practicing-attorney split — today it is closer to 100% of both, at once
Until someone else owns the CEO function, Charles cannot get the time back he needs to build toward a multi-location firm — he will just keep doing both jobs harder.

Profit

  • Only 5% of revenue is currently set aside, with a stated goal to increase that — but no stated visibility into cost per case or client acquisition cost
  • Pricing on both core services (estate plans, business formations) was inherited from Charles's prior family-law practice rather than set against the value Sage delivers today
  • Untapped higher-margin opportunities (annual compliance packages, an estate administration/probate practice) have been identified but not built out
A $10M sale is built on margin, not just revenue — re-pricing today's services is the fastest lever Charles has to increase what the firm actually keeps.
