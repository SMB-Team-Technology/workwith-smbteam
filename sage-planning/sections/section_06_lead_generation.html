Lead Generation

Sage Estate & Business Law has generated $160,000 in its first four months entirely through referrals, a financial-advisor and accountant network, and monthly seminars — with zero paid advertising and no SEO strategy behind any of it. That means every dollar of growth so far has been personally sourced by Charles, with no predictable, scalable system standing behind it.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • No traditional contact form on the homepage — visitors must call or click through to a separate contact page just to reach the firm
  • PageSpeed mobile and desktop scores could not be captured this session — the public API returned quota errors across 5 attempts; a live manual test is recommended before finalizing any conversion-speed claims
  • Attorney bio photos for Charles Ahlstrom and Gavin West appear to be placeholders rather than real headshots — undercutting the trust a four-month-old brand most needs to build
  • No blog, no video content, and no geo-targeted landing pages for any of the surrounding cities (Ogden, Layton, Kaysville, Salt Lake City) the site claims to serve
Blind Spot: Because the site reads like a freshly built launch site, first-time visitors have no way to see that Charles brings 20+ years of legal experience to the table — the copy leads with the new brand instead of the proven track record that would build instant trust with a stranger.
The Positive
  • Clear calls to action above the fold — a "Schedule a free planning call" button and a visible phone number, so visitors who are ready to act have an obvious next step
  • Transparent, flat-fee pricing across three named tiers starting at $1,500 — most estate-planning competitors hide pricing behind a "call us" wall, and up-front pricing tends to convert better with planning-driven consumers
The Gap
  • Without a homepage contact form, an unknown number of visitors who would have filled out a quick form are instead required to call — and simply leave instead
  • No geo-targeted content means the site cannot rank at all for searches happening in Ogden, Layton, Kaysville, or Salt Lake City — markets the firm claims to serve
  • Missing PageSpeed data is itself a red flag: without a baseline, there is no way to know how many mobile visitors are abandoning the site before it even loads
A website that generates its own leads — not just Charles's personal reputation — is a core piece of the asset SMB Team can help him build toward that $10M sale.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Sage's current Google star rating and review count could not be independently verified this session — flagged for manual confirmation, but given the firm is only four months old it is reasonable to assume the review base is still thin
  • Sage does not appear in organic search-snippet results for "estate planning attorney Clearfield Utah," "trust attorney Ogden Utah," or "wills and trusts lawyer Kaysville Utah" — the three core searches in its home market
  • No geo-targeted landing pages exist for any of the surrounding Wasatch Front cities despite the site's own claim to serve "families throughout Utah, including the Salt Lake Valley"
Blind Spot: Every major legal directory — Avvo, Justia, Martindale-Hubbell, Lawyers.com — still lists Charles exclusively under his old family-law firm, Sentinel Law Group, at a defunct Farmington address. Anyone who searches his name directly right now is routed away from Sage, not toward it.
The Positive
  • No negative reviews or reputation issues to overcome — Sage is starting this channel from a genuinely clean slate
The Gap
  • Two named local competitors are already capturing the exact searches Sage's future clients are running
  • Every day the directory identity crisis goes unaddressed is another day Google has no consistent, updated signal that Sage Estate & Business Law exists
Sandberg, Stettler & White (Kaysville, UT) — 93 Google reviews at 4.8 stars and dedicated estate/elder-law service-area content make them the default local answer for exactly the searches where Sage does not appear.
Fixing the directory identity crisis alone is one of the fastest, lowest-cost ways to reclaim visibility Charles has already earned over 20+ years — before spending a single new dollar on marketing.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • This firm does not appear to be running Google search ads for estate planning or business formation terms in its market — consistent with the transcript's description of a referral-and-seminar-only lead model, with no paid marketing mentioned for any service line
  • No sponsored listings for Sage Estate & Business Law surfaced in any search run this session, for either practice area
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Ascent Law LLC — broad multi-practice digital presence competing for estate-planning search traffic across the greater Salt Lake/Wasatch Front area; specific ad copy could not be confirmed without a live rendered SERP
  • Sandberg, Stettler & White — dedicated elder-law/estate-planning content and geo-pages positioning them to win paid and organic clicks alike in the Kaysville/Northern Utah market
The Opportunity
  • Estate planning attorney — Clearfield, Ogden, and the broader Northern Utah / Wasatch Front market
  • Trust attorney — same Northern Utah geography, capturing search intent Sage's own seminars are already creating
  • Business formation attorney Utah — a practice area with no confirmed competitor ad presence identified this session
  • Probate / estate administration attorney Utah — a market Charles himself believes is underserved, with no dominant paid competitor identified
Blind Spot: Because no one has ever run paid search for Sage, there is no data yet on what a lead actually costs in this market — making it hard to know whether referrals are truly the cheapest channel available, or just the only one currently being measured.
