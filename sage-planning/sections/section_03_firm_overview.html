Here Is Where Sage Estate & Business Law Stands Today

Charles Ahlstrom launched Sage Estate & Business Law in April 2026 after 20+ years practicing family law, building a trust-based estate and business planning practice in Clearfield, Utah. In just over four months, the firm has generated $160,000 in revenue entirely through referrals, a financial-advisor and accountant network, and monthly seminars — with no paid marketing, no updated digital footprint, and no formal system to follow up on seminar leads. The single most important finding: Charles is running the firm as both its CEO and its primary attorney at the same time, and that dual role is now the hard ceiling on how fast Sage can reach its $600K 2026 goal.

Stage 6 is where a firm runs without its founder chained to every case — and it is the only path toward the multi-location, $10M firm Charles has set his sights on by age 61.

Where You Are Today

STAGE 3: SOLO PRACTITIONER
  • New clients come almost entirely from referrals (from Charles's prior family-law practice) and a small network of financial advisors and accountants — with no paid or scalable digital channel behind it
  • Monthly seminars generate real interest, but there is no formal follow-up system to turn attendees into signed clients, so warm leads go cold
  • Charles is simultaneously the firm's CEO and its primary attorney — every strategic decision competes directly with his caseload
  • Pricing on both major service lines ($2,500 average estate plan, $700–$800 average business formation) was inherited from Charles's prior firm and has never been re-evaluated against the specialized value Sage now delivers

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
Closing that gap starts with taking the CEO role off Charles's plate long enough for him to build the systems, pricing, and follow-up process the firm needs to hit its $600K goal this year — not by working more hours, but by working differently.
Overall Growth Stage Assessment
Sage Estate & Business Law
Sage Estate & Business Law is operating at Stage 3 — a young firm whose founder is still personally carrying both the CEO and primary-attorney roles just four months after launch. The single most urgent constraint is that structural bottleneck: as long as Charles is the one making every strategic call, evaluating every marketing channel, and reviewing every price, the firm's growth rate is capped at what one person can personally push through. Until that changes, the jump from $160K in year one to the firm's stated $600K 2026 goal — let alone the long-term vision of a $10M sale by 61 — will keep asking Charles to personally do more, rather than build a firm that can do more without him.
