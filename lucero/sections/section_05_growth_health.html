Lead Generation

Every dollar going to an unprofitable PPC campaign is a dollar that is not funding the associate hire that would finally get Christopher out of court every day.

Intake

Fixing intake can add private cases without spending another marketing dollar — money that goes straight toward the associate hire and the freedom that comes with it.

Self-Managing Team

  • Lindsey supports intake and operations, but there is no additional staff or delegation structure in place to absorb growth
Until Christopher is not the only person who can show up in court, the scalable, resilient firm he described stays a plan instead of a reality.

Profit

  • No annual revenue figure was tracked or discussed on the call — only monthly case counts and goals
  • The firm continues paying Convert It for a $5,800/mo combined engagement despite it being described as unprofitable

Knowing the real return on every dollar spent is what lets Christopher confidently invest in the associate hire and the freedom a resilient firm brings.
