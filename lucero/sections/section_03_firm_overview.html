Here Is Where Lucero, PC Stands Today

Christopher Lucero founded Lucero, PC in 2015 as a criminal and DUI defense practice in La Verne, California, personally handling a mix of private-pay and court-appointed cases with one operations team member, Lindsey Lucero, and no associate attorney yet. The firm's organic blogging is described as "the primary driver of new clients," while its only paid channel — a Convert It PPC campaign — burns through its full monthly budget in 3-4 days and is producing only about one private client a month. The single most important finding: close rate has fallen sharply month over month, from roughly 22-33% of calls converting in July to about 7% in August, well below the 15-25% benchmark for firms at this stage.

Stage 6 is the stage where the firm no longer depends on Christopher walking into court every day — where the associate hire he is already planning becomes a step toward a self-sustaining team, not a stopgap that still requires him to manage every case personally.
