Lead Generation

Lucero, PC's only paid lead source is a Convert It PPC campaign that the firm itself describes as unprofitable, exhausting its full monthly budget in 3-4 days for roughly one private client a month. Meanwhile, named local competitors have built dedicated local landing pages and multi-channel social presences that Lucero, PC does not currently match on any single channel.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • No web-based contact form exists anywhere on the site — the firm is phone-first, funneling every visitor through a single number
  • PageSpeed scores could not be verified this pass — the PageSpeed Insights API returned repeated rate-limit errors in this research environment; a live Core Web Vitals check is recommended before finalizing any website work
  • No live chat and no video content anywhere on the site
  • Only Christopher Lucero has a published attorney bio — no team page or staff introduction, reinforcing a one-person-shop impression even though the firm also handles court-appointed matters
The Positive
  • Christopher Lucero's bio is a strong trust asset — former Southern California Deputy District Attorney, "Top 40 Under 40" and "Rising Star" recognition, and a claimed undefeated record on DUI, domestic violence, and homicide cases while a prosecutor
  • The blog is live and actively publishing practice-specific legal content, giving the site a genuine foundation to build on
The Gap
  • Prospects who prefer or need to reach out in writing — especially after hours — have no way to do that on the site today
  • A strong personal story (former prosecutor, undefeated track record on serious cases) is underused as a trust-builder with no video to bring it to life
  • Unverified site performance is a real risk in a market where named competitors have already invested in dedicated local landing pages
A website that only accepts phone calls is a website that keeps funneling every new client back through Christopher personally — the opposite of the scalable, associate-supported firm he's trying to build.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • Direct Google Business Profile data could not be verified this pass (Maps and Search rendering were blocked in this research environment); available third-party review data shows Lucero, PC's review volume is far thinner than several named local competitors, regardless of exact platform-to-platform counts
  • The firm's own site displays 213-668-7569 as its "Call 24/7" number, while its BBB listing shows (213) 668-7779 — a confirmed NAP (Name/Address/Phone) inconsistency that directly hurts local map-pack ranking
  • No dedicated geo-targeting landing pages exist for Pomona, Claremont, San Dimas, or Rancho Cucamonga, despite the firm regularly appearing in those courts per its own /results page
Blind Spot: Most owners never think to check whether their phone number matches everywhere it's listed online — but Google treats that consistency as a trust signal, and right now Lucero, PC's own site and its BBB listing show two different numbers.
The Positive
  • The firm shows up in multiple directory listings (Justia, FindLaw, LawInfo, BestLawyers, SuperLawyers) for La Verne + practice-area searches, and its blog is actively adding fresh, practice-specific content
The Gap
  • Thin review volume and a NAP mismatch both shape a prospect's first impression before they ever pick up the phone
  • No sub-practice-area content exists the way at least one named competitor has already built — the site does carry a full Personal Injury practice section (auto, truck, motorcycle/bicycle accidents, medical malpractice, premises liability) that wasn't part of this call but represents an underused SEO asset worth a look separately
Poseidon Defense, A.P.C. — has built La Verne-specific landing pages down to the sub-practice-area level (theft/shoplifting, fraud/embezzlement), a level of local SEO investment Lucero, PC's site does not currently match.
Closing the local SEO gap is how Lucero, PC starts capturing the clients search already recognizes it deserves — instead of losing them to firms with less experience but more visible pages.

  • Direct ad-slot rendering could not be captured this pass (search tools were blocked); this firm does not appear to be running visible search ads for standard criminal defense or DUI queries for most of each month, which may reflect the exhausted Convert It budget rather than a deliberate targeting choice
Competitor Ad Landscape

  • Confianza Legal — pairs dedicated city landing pages with active paid-style social promotion, suggesting a coordinated, always-on approach rather than a budget that runs dry mid-month
The Opportunity

  • "criminal defense attorney Pomona Claremont" and "DUI attorney La Verne" — neighboring-city terms where Poseidon Defense and Confianza Legal already have dedicated landing pages

Blind Spot: A PPC account that exhausts its budget in a few days every month isn't just underperforming in the moment — it can also be actively teaching Google's algorithm that these keywords don't convert for this account, making it more expensive to build real momentum later.
