Website Audit

The Problem

  • PageSpeed scores: not independently measurable in this research pass (tooling was rate-limited) — recommend pulling a live PageSpeed score before the proposal call
  • No live chat and no video content anywhere on the site
  • Blog is linked in navigation, but post dates and previews could not be confirmed as current in this pass
Blind Spot: Because there is no web contact form anywhere on the site, every visitor who does not want to pick up the phone — common for people reaching out about a criminal matter for the first time — currently has no way to contact the firm at all, meaning some share of that traffic never becomes a lead in the first place.
The Positive
  • Attorney bio leads with Christopher's "Former Deputy District Attorney" credential — a strong trust signal for criminal defense prospects
  • Site displays a 2026 "Best Lawyers" badge, suggesting the site is actively maintained at least annually
The Gap
  • No contact form means every lead who prefers reaching out digitally instead of calling has no path to do so
  • No live chat or simple digital intake option leaves after-hours prospects with nothing but a phone number to try
  • A phone-first funnel puts more pressure on the phone line at the exact time close rates are falling
A website that only captures leads by phone, at the exact moment the phone line is stretched thin by a stalled close rate, is standing between Christopher and the extra private cases that fund the associate hire he is working toward.

Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Named competitor presence in LSA could not be independently observed in this pass — live LSA widget rendering was not reachable with available tools, so no competitor should be cited as holding or not holding LSA spots based on this alone
  • What is confirmed is that LSA rewards exactly the 3 R's discussed on the call — Responsiveness, Radius, and Reviews — and Lucero, PC's current thin review base (2 known reviews on Avvo) means the Reviews leg needs active building regardless of what competitors are doing

  • Google Screened status, earned through a background check, is a strong trust signal for criminal defense prospects who are often calling for the first time in a moment of crisis
Blind Spot: Because LSA weights Responsiveness heavily, Lucero, PC's declining close rate and phone-only intake could work against it in the LSA algorithm unless intake speed improves alongside launching the channel.
