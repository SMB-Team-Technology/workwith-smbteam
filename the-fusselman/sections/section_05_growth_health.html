Pillar Health Scorecard

Lead Generation

  • The best clients are found through relationships and direct outreach, not through a repeatable system that works every month
  • The Google Business Profile shows 4 reviews at a 2.8 rating and links to a different website than the firm's main site
  • The blog has not been updated since November 2024, and the site scores 40 on mobile speed
A targeted, intentional way to reach the right commercial clients is what lets Christopher choose the work he prefers and move away from the files that drain his time.

Intake

  • New-file documents are read, summarized, and turned into petitions and affidavits largely by hand, which takes weeks of attorney time over a year
  • Forms go out with errors, and payment reporting is entered twice across systems
  • Screening rules for files that are too small or have too many parties are a wish-list item, not something that happens automatically
Every hour of paper work removed from intake is an hour Christopher gets back for negotiating, depositions, and trial.

Self-Managing Team

  • Procedures live in a binder and drift from the important steps over time, so they have to be pulled back by the owner
  • Post-judgment steps still wait for a person to decide to start them, and there are no KPIs to manage the team by
  • The business runs through Christopher, including weekend work
A firm that works for Christopher, rather than the reverse, needs structure that runs without his daily decisions.

Profit

  • Revenue has held in a range of roughly $550,000 to $650,000 for five to six years
  • Files range from about $20,000 to $600,000 in size while the work required is similar, so small files take a large share of time
  • No KPIs are tracked, so it is hard to see which files and steps earn their keep
Getting paid for judgment and expertise instead of volume is what turns a plateaued firm into one that builds real value.
