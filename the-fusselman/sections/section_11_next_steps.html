AI-Assisted New-File Document Preparation
Intake Quick Win
Why It Matters
Each new file means reading documents and building petitions, affidavits, damages figures, and a list of who was involved by hand, which Christopher estimates costs weeks of time every year.
The Opportunity
Using AI to turn a new file's documents into draft work product, with lawyers still reviewing the larger files, could return weeks of attorney time each year to the work Christopher prefers.
Automate the Post-Judgment Steps
Team Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Steps such as waiting out the 30 days to final judgment, filing the abstract of judgment to perfect the lien, and moving to a writ of execution and officer sale currently wait for a person to decide to start them.
The Opportunity
Triggers and reminders that move each judgment to its next step on schedule could keep collections moving without Christopher or his team having to remember each one.
Track a Short List of KPIs and Remove Duplicate Reporting
Profit Quick Win
Why It Matters
With no KPIs and payment reporting duplicated across systems, it is hard to see which files and steps are worth the time they take.
The Opportunity
A handful of numbers, such as file size, hours spent, and collected amounts, fed by a single payment-reporting path, could show where time earns the most and which files to move away from.

The Dushkin Law Firm — an AV-rated Houston firm that publishes dedicated judgment-enforcement content aimed at the same work.

Automated Screening for New Placements
Intake Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Files range from about $20,000 to $600,000 in size with similar work behind them, and small files take a large share of Christopher's time.
The Opportunity
Rules such as a minimum balance or a maximum number of parties could screen placements before they reach a lawyer, so time goes to files the firm actually wants.
Turn the Procedure Binder Into Living Checklists
Team Long-Term Play
Why It Matters
Procedures kept in a binder are flexible, but they drift away from the steps that matter most and have to be pulled back by the owner.
The Opportunity
Converting the key procedures into checklists tied to KPIs and automation could keep the firm's standards in place without Christopher acting as the quality-control system.
