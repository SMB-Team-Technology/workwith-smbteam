Lead Generation

The Fusselman Law Firm has built its best business through reputation and direct relationships, serving commercial clients and insurers who are not searching Google for a collections attorney. That makes the firm's online presence less about volume and more about being easy to verify and easy to find for the right clients, which is where today's website and Google profile fall short.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The blog has not been updated since November 2024, and all six visible posts are from 2024, so the site shows no activity for about two years
  • Lighthouse speed scores: Mobile 40/100 — Desktop 77/100
  • The contact form sits in the middle-to-lower part of the homepage rather than above the fold
  • There is no video and no city-specific page for Houston or other Texas markets, because the site is positioned as serving "Across Texas"
Blind Spot: A prospect with a judgment worth a couple of million dollars told Christopher that ChatGPT named him as a top commercial collection and judgment enforcement attorney in Texas. AI tools build their answers from the website and profiles they can read, so a stale blog and a Google profile that points to a different website give them weaker material than this firm's track record deserves.
The Positive
  • Attorney bios present Christopher's credentials clearly, including his 1995 licensure and AV Preeminent rating
  • The layout is responsive and a clear "Help Starts Here" button appears above the fold
  • The site states the fee model plainly: "we do not collect a fee until we put money in your pocket"
The Gap
  • A mobile score of 40 means visitors checking the firm from a phone, often right after a referral, wait on a slow page
  • A site with no recent content gives referral sources and AI tools little new proof of the firm's current expertise
  • Without video or location pages, the firm's specialty and its Houston base are harder to understand at a glance
A website that does the explaining and the proving for you is one more step toward a firm that works without Christopher in every conversation.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The Google Business Profile shows 4 reviews at a 2.8 star rating
  • The profile's website field points to texasrecoveries.com instead of thefusselmanlawfirm.com
  • A second, older MapQuest listing shows a different address at 1717 Saint James Pl
Blind Spot: Many prospects look a firm up on Google before they call, even when they were referred. A profile with few reviews that sends them to a different website can create doubt at the exact moment someone is deciding to trust you with a large file.
The Positive
  • The firm has established profiles on Martindale-Hubbell, Avvo, Justia, and FindLaw, with its AV Preeminent rating noted
The Gap
  • With only four Google reviews, the profile gives little social proof to anyone who checks the firm on Google
  • Conflicting website and address details across directories make it harder for Google and AI tools to trust a single version of the firm's information
  • Local map results for Houston collection and enforcement searches were not checked in this review
Lone Star Lawyers — a Houston post-judgment enforcement specialist that promotes its Google reviews on its own website, turning client feedback into a trust signal for the same enforcement work.
A clean, consistent profile means the reputation Christopher has earned over more than two decades is easy for the right clients to find and trust.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Paid search activity for commercial collection, judgment enforcement, and subrogation searches in Houston was not observed in this review, so no conclusion is drawn about the firm's ad activity.
  • Christopher noted that the niche commercial work he prefers does not show up as an advertisement, so a broad search campaign is not where his best clients are found.
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • The Weisblatt Law Firm — competes for Houston-area business collections work; paid search activity was not observed in this review
  • The Dushkin Law Firm — competes for collections and judgment enforcement work; paid search activity was not observed in this review
The Opportunity
  • Searches such as "judgment enforcement attorney Houston" and "post-judgment collections Texas" are where a business that already holds a judgment would look for help
  • Terms tied to specific steps, such as abstract of judgment and writ of execution, speak to clients who already know what they need
  • Becoming a recommended answer in AI tools like ChatGPT is a non-obvious opening, since at least one client has already found Christopher that way
  • Reaching specific commercial niches such as remediation and utility or facilities contractors through direct, targeted messaging matches how Christopher wants to find work

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • Local Service Ads results for the firm's practice areas were not observed in this review, so Google Screened status and LSA review counts are not recorded.
  • On the discovery call, Christopher and Jacob agreed that the firm's target clients are not looking for this work through local service ads.
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • Houston collection and enforcement firms — LSA listings for these practice areas were not observed in this review
The Opportunity
  • Because the firm's best clients are businesses and insurers, LSAs are not a priority channel today
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • If LSAs are ever tested, the firm's current Google review base of 4 reviews would be the first thing to strengthen
