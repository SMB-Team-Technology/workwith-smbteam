Here Is Where The Fusselman Law Firm Stands Today

Christopher Fusselman has run The Fusselman Law Firm in Houston since 2002, handling commercial collections and post-judgment enforcement for businesses and insurance companies on a contingency basis. Revenue has held in a range of roughly $550,000 to $650,000 for five to six years, and the most important finding is that the firm still runs through him: the paperwork, the follow-through on judgments, and the decisions all land on his desk instead of in a system.

Stage 6 is the stage where the firm works for you instead of the other way around — where the paper work runs on process and automation, and your time goes to the negotiating, depositions, and trial work you actually want to be doing.

Where You Are Today

STAGE 3: SOLO PRACTITIONER
  • Revenue has plateaued for five to six years, and the firm's business runs through you personally
  • Document-heavy work, from new-file intake to post-judgment steps, still depends on a person deciding to start each step
  • Procedures live in a binder and drift over time, with no KPIs tracking how the firm is performing
  • Your best clients are found through relationships and direct outreach rather than a repeatable system

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between a firm that depends on you and a firm that works for you is a structure gap, not a talent gap — and structure is exactly what automation and a clear plan can build.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Intake Assessment

At The Fusselman Law Firm, intake means taking in a new file full of documents and turning it into petitions, damages calculations, and a record of who was involved, and today that work is done largely by hand. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating — without spending another dollar on marketing.

Status: AMBER
"If AI could take all those documents and turn them into actionable product that I could use to create petitions and affidavits … it would save me weeks of time every year."
The Problem
  • New-file documents are reviewed and turned into petitions and affidavits by hand, with damages and the names and roles of the people involved pulled together manually
  • Forms go out with errors that have to be caught and corrected
  • There is no automatic screening for files that are too small or have too many parties, so every placement gets attention before anyone knows if it fits
  • Records are saved to both FileVine and a backed-up office server, which means the same file is handled twice
Blind Spot: Files can range from about $20,000 to $600,000 while the work to open and prepare them is similar. Without front-end screening, a small file can use as much of Christopher's time as a large one before anyone realizes it.
The Positive
  • FileVine and QuickBooks Online are already integrated, which gives any automation a solid base to build on
  • Saving to both systems protects bar record-keeping if either one fails, a requirement any new process should keep
The Gap
  • Weeks of attorney time each year go to preparing documents instead of negotiating, depositions, and trial work
  • Manual preparation makes errors more likely and slows the start of each file
  • Without screening rules, time goes to files the firm would rather not take
Moving document preparation off Christopher's desk is the fastest way to get his time back for the work he wants to do.

Website Contact Forms & Live Chat
