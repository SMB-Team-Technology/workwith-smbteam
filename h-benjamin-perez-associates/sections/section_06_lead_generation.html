Lead Generation

H. Benjamin Perez & Associates has built genuine credibility over the years — a 4.9/5.0 Avvo rating and Super Lawyers recognition among them — but that reputation isn't translating into visibility where new clients are actually searching. Across divorce, family law, and criminal defense, the firm does not surface in a single organic search where its named competitors consistently do.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The site has not had a substantial refresh in recent memory — no blog activity since June 2023, and a content structure showing its age
  • PageSpeed scores could not be captured this session (requires a live browser-rendering tool that wasn't available) — recommend a live check before finalizing any website scope
  • No visible contact form on the homepage — the site relies entirely on phone-first calls-to-action
  • Mobile responsiveness could not be confirmed this session — flagged as a research gap, not a confirmed problem
Blind Spot: A NAP (name/address/phone) inconsistency was found — hbplawyers.com lists (646) 760-6590 as the primary number, but at least one indexed Yelp listing shows a different number, (646) 770-0989, for the same business. Inconsistent contact info like this can quietly split local search signal.
The Positive
  • Every attorney has an individual bio page with photo, and the firm maintains a deep, comprehensive practice-area content library across divorce, family law, and criminal defense
  • The firm already maintains a full Spanish-language section ("En Español") — a real asset most competitors in this comparison don't appear to have
The Gap
  • Without confirmed PageSpeed and mobile data, it's unclear how many mobile visitors are bouncing before they ever see a phone number
  • No visible contact form means every visitor who doesn't want to call first has no other way to reach the firm
  • Stale blog content signals inactivity to both prospective clients and search engines alike
Every visitor who leaves without a way to reach the firm is one more case that ends up in a competitor's hands — and one more reason the manual, one-at-a-time way this firm runs today won't get Hector to 3x.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • A live Google Business Profile pull wasn't possible this session, but the firm's best available proxy — Avvo (4.9/5.0, 30 reviews) — trails named competitors like Richard Roman Shum (217 reviews on Birdeye) by a wide margin
  • The firm does not surface in organic "best divorce/family/criminal defense lawyer NYC/Manhattan" searches in any of its three practice areas
  • No borough-specific geo-landing pages exist despite the firm marketing itself across eight boroughs and counties
Blind Spot: Because the site's content is organized entirely by practice area rather than geography, the firm is likely invisible for hyper-local searches like "Brooklyn divorce lawyer" or "Bronx family lawyer" — exactly where at least one competitor already has dedicated pages.
The Positive
  • The firm has real review quality where it counts — a 10.0 "Superb" Avvo rating in both Divorce and Criminal Defense, plus an A+ BBB rating. Reputation is not the problem here.
The Gap
  • Every organic search where the firm doesn't surface is a client actively looking for representation right now, going to a competitor instead
  • The existing content investment — dozens of practice-area sub-pages — isn't being maintained or expanded, so its search value is likely eroding over time
Stephen Bilkis & Associates, PLLC — has built dedicated borough-specific landing pages (e.g. "Brooklyn Divorce Lawyer," "Bronx Divorce Lawyer") directly targeting the same eight boroughs and counties H. Benjamin Perez & Associates already claims to serve.
Closing this gap doesn't require reinventing the practice-area content the firm already built — it requires putting it back to work.
