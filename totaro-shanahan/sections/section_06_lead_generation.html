Lead Generation

Totaro & Shanahan has built decades of Chapter 11 business on attorney referrals, with no digital advertising and a radio campaign that has since wound down. The Chapter 7 market Michael wants to grow into is already being captured online by competitors with hundreds of Google reviews and active paid placements in Los Angeles, Orange County, and San Diego.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • The homepage headline, "Overcome Debt & Reclaim Your Financial Freedom," speaks to consumers in debt — Chapter 11, the work behind most of the firm's current revenue, appears only on an inner services page
  • PageSpeed scores: Mobile 88/100 — Desktop 98/100 (Lighthouse engine). Mobile largest-contentful-paint is 3.7 seconds, slower than Google's 2.5-second "good" threshold
  • Chapter 7 location pages exist for Los Angeles, Long Beach, Glendale, and Orange County, but there is no San Diego page — where Michael serves clients — and no Chapter 11 location pages anywhere
  • No video, no blog, and attorney credentials limited to a "Meet Our Team" link and an About page — little content to build trust with someone comparing several firms
Blind Spot: Attorneys who refer Chapter 11 matters will often look a firm up before sending a client. A homepage aimed at consumers in debt, with a single online review behind it, may undersell the specialty that earns those referrals.
The Positive
  • A modern, mobile-responsive design with clear "Get Free Consultation" calls to action at the top of the page
  • Fast desktop performance and a clean base to build on — this does not look like a site that needs to be rebuilt from scratch
The Gap
  • Chapter 7 searchers in San Diego, and Chapter 11 searchers anywhere, have no page written for them — nothing to rank in search and nothing to send paid traffic to
  • Without video or educational content, a visitor who finds the firm has little reason to choose it over a competitor showing hundreds of reviews
  • The Chapter 11 specialty, which carries the firm's revenue today, is under-presented to anyone who finds the site directly
A website that turns Chapter 7 searchers into consultations is the foundation that lets Michael hand marketing to someone else and get back to the legal work.

Local SEO

The Problem
  • The Google Business Profile shows 5.0 stars on just 1 Google review, while the firms filling the Los Angeles map results hold 231, 357, and 641
  • The firm was not observed in the local map results for any Chapter 7 or Chapter 11 search in Los Angeles, Orange County, or San Diego — it appears only for a very narrow "bankruptcy attorney pacific palisades" search
  • Listing details are inconsistent across directories: a street address on Google and MapQuest, a P.O. Box on Martindale-Hubbell, Justia, and Birdeye, and several variations of the firm name
  • Michael agreed on the call that the profile photo should be replaced with a professional headshot
Blind Spot: The Pacific Palisades result is real, but almost no one searches for a bankruptcy attorney that way. The searches that bring in clients are for Los Angeles, Orange County, and San Diego — and the firm is not showing up for those.
The Positive
  • A 5.0-star Google rating and existing profiles on Justia, Martindale-Hubbell, and the California State Bar — the quality is there, the volume of proof is not
The Gap
  • A searcher comparing firms sees 1 review next to hundreds and often chooses before ever reading the website
  • Without location-specific pages and consistent listings, Google has little reason to show the firm in Los Angeles, Orange County, or San Diego results
The Attorney Group — 4.9 stars with 641 Google reviews, and listed first in the Los Angeles map results on three of the five Los Angeles searches we tested.
A strong review base and a consistent local presence are what let Chapter 7 clients find Michael without him having to go find them.

Google Ads

Ad Presence
  • Chapter 7 / consumer bankruptcy: Google search ads for the firm were not observed on any search in Los Angeles, Orange County, San Diego, or Pacific Palisades — consistent with Michael's comment on the call that the firm has never advertised digitally
  • Chapter 11: no firm ads were observed on Chapter 11 searches in Los Angeles, Orange County, or San Diego either — the firm's core practice is currently invisible to anyone searching for it
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • OakTree Law — sponsored top-of-page placement in Los Angeles and Orange County, leading with "No fee unless we win" and "A+ BBB rating for over 10 years"
  • KT - Tang & Associates — sponsored placement in Los Angeles and Orange County with "Flat rate pricing" and "Se habla español"
  • Law Offices of Adele Schneidereit — sponsored placement in Los Angeles and Orange County offering a "Complimentary 30-min consultation"
  • $795 Bankruptcy Lawyer — Orange County text ad: "$795 Lowest Fee Guaranteed. Affordable Payment Plans Available."
  • Bankruptcy Law Center and Bankruptcy Law Offices of Mark L. Miller — sponsored placements on San Diego Chapter 7 searches, the latter with "Flat rate pricing" and "Beat or match price"
The Opportunity
  • Chapter 7 searches by city — "chapter 7 bankruptcy attorney" paired with Los Angeles, Orange County, and San Diego — where competitors are already paying for the click
  • Chapter 11 searches for individuals and businesses in the same three markets, where paid competition looks far lighter than for Chapter 7
  • Urgency searches such as stopping a foreclosure or creditor harassment — Michael has said the firm will take a case within two days of a foreclosure, a strength worth putting in front of people at the moment they search
  • Competitor ads lead with flat fees, free consultations, and "no fee" promises — the firm's flat-fee structure gives it a message to match that, with ad copy built to turn clicks into phone calls rather than rankings
Blind Spot: Michael's Chapter 11 specialty faces very little local paid competition. The only sponsored result we saw on a Los Angeles Chapter 11 search came from an out-of-state firm, so a local specialist may be able to claim that space early.

Local Service Ads

LSA Presence
  • The firm was not observed in the Local Service Ads block on "chapter 7 bankruptcy lawyer los angeles" or "bankruptcy attorney near me"
  • No LSA review history or Google Screened status was observed for the firm, so this channel is an open opportunity rather than a confirmed gap
Competitor LSA Landscape
  • OakTree Law — 4.8 stars with 357 Google reviews
  • KT - Tang & Associates — 4.9 stars with 220 Google reviews
  • Law Offices of Adele Schneidereit — 4.7 stars with 47 Google reviews
The Opportunity
  • The firms in this block change with time and location, but a firm with 47 reviews held a spot alongside firms with hundreds — a firm does not need 600 reviews to compete, but it does need more than one
  • LSAs appear above everything else on Google — above paid ads, above the map pack, above all organic results — and you only pay when a real prospect calls you directly.
  • With 1 Google review today, building a review base is the first step toward competing in this block

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Ad Presence
  • A Meta Ad Library search for the firm returned no active ads, and Michael said the firm has never used Facebook or Instagram
  • Michael also mentioned that Facebook shows the firm as banned, so account access will need to be sorted out before any campaign can run
Competitor Ad Landscape
  • Competitor activity could not be reliably established from the Meta Ad Library, so none is cited here — what is clear is that the firm has no presence of its own on these platforms
The Opportunity
  • Retargeting — website visitors who did not convert are leaving with no follow-up
  • Video ads featuring the attorney — builds trust before a prospect ever calls
  • Educational content ads — capture leads earlier in the decision process
  • Michael raised interest in social media and Instagram video on the call — short explainers on how Chapter 7 and Chapter 11 work are a natural fit for a practice built on trust
